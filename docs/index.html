Owner-led, in person, donut drop, 15-minute meeting. No sales rep. The agent's name on the line is what makes this work, which is exactly the reason a PE platform with a centralized call center cannot run this play.

Who they are

Big Sky Roofing LLC was founded on May 20, 2021, and is operated as an LLC by owner Seth Hobbs, headquartered in Brownsburg, Indiana.

The company has built a reputation as a go-to provider of residential and commercial roof replacement, storm damage restoration, siding installation, and gutters across the Indianapolis metro area.

They self-identify as a locally family-owned business known for integrity and craftsmanship.

ZoomInfo estimates the company at 5–9 employees with under $500K in revenue,

making this a lean owner-operated shop punching well above its weight class in review velocity.

On the product side, they lean on Owens Corning for roofing and have partnered with CertainTeed and James Hardie for siding

— solid mid-tier brand alignment.

They are also a member of the Greater Brownsburg Chamber of Commerce,

signaling intentional local community entrenchment. At 323 Google reviews and 4.9 stars with a 10% monthly velocity, this is a small operator running a disciplined reputation play.

What's driving them

The 10% monthly review velocity — 32 new reviews on a base of 323 in 30 days — is abnormally high for a sub-10-person shop and points to a deliberate, systematized ask engine, not organic luck. The strongest signal is their use of GuildQuality for post-project customer satisfaction surveying.

GuildQuality collects verified customer feedback through an impartial third-party survey process on Big Sky's behalf, which ensures customers have the opportunity to express their true feelings and that the contractor gets accurate feedback — structured workflow that almost certainly feeds into a review ask sequence.

Their own website states that "almost 80% of our business comes through referrals from previously satisfied customers,"

which suggests a tight post-job follow-up loop that converts happy customers into both referrals and reviews.

One GuildQuality reviewer noted that "Big Sky replaced my neighbor's roof and after watching their performance, I was sold" — evidence that job-site visibility in Brownsburg's tight-knit Hendricks County neighborhoods is a direct referral driver.

Their 24/7 emergency repair services

also likely generate high-intensity, emotionally charged customer moments — exactly when a well-timed review ask converts at peak rates.

On Facebook, active posts lean into responsiveness messaging ("Tired of contractors who never call back? Big Sky believes communication is part of the job"),

reinforcing the brand story that drives 5-star sentiment. The velocity is a referral-flywheel plus GuildQuality survey pipeline — not a paid media blitz.
