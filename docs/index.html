It is a woman-owned business

They are a family-owned and operated roofing company based in Brownsburg, Indiana.

Their service footprint spans

Noblesville, Westfield, Carmel, Plainfield, Zionsville, Greenwood, Fishers, and Avon

— essentially the full Indianapolis metro ring.

2. What's Driving the Review Acceleration

There are three clear engines. First, active door-knocking after storms:

"so glad they knocked on our door after a hail storm, would never have known about the work needing done and now we will have a safe home for another 20+ years"

— this tactic is showing up repeatedly across review platforms. Second, a named referral program:

customers explicitly call out that "their referral program is topnotch" and that "it's easy to tell others about having such a great experience."

Third, a multi-platform review push:

the company actively encourages customers to check ratings on Google, Yelp, Angie's List, and BBB

, which compresses word-of-mouth into measurable review volume. The combination of storm-triggered door-knocking + a structured referral program + deliberate multi-platform prompting is what's compounding the velocity.

3. One Thing Raptor Could Copy

The referral program combined with post-job texting is the single highest-leverage move here.

Multiple reviewers explicitly mention they're recommending Indy Rooftops to realtors and neighbors

, and the company's response strategy amplifies every testimonial.

Customers note they "communicate easily and clearly via text, which is a millennial's dream for project management"

— a simple post-job text with a direct Google review link and a referral incentive would replicate exactly what Indy Rooftops appears to be running.

4. One Weakness Raptor Could Exploit

Indy Rooftops is a very young company with shallow institutional roots and documented subcontractor risk.
