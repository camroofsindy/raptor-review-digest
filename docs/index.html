Indy Rooftops

+19 this week
482 reviews · 5.0 stars · +52 in 30 days

Hey Cameron — here's a tight brief on Indy Rooftops (indyrooftops.com) for you:

1. Who They Are

Indy Rooftops was established in 2022 by Mitchel Dotson and Cheryl Edens, originally started for Mitchel's dad, Kevin Dotson, who had been a roofing salesman at another local company for many years.

They position themselves as a woman-owned business, blending personalized attention with the backing of national companies and products.

They serve Indianapolis and surrounding areas including Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Westfield, Avon, Brownsburg, Plainfield, Zionsville, and Greenwood.

Despite being a newer business, they are already BBB accredited with an A+ rating.

2. What's Driving the Review Acceleration

There's no single disclosed ad campaign, but the review velocity is almost certainly the result of a multi-pronged, ground-level push.

Customer reviews specifically mention reps "knocking on our door after a hail storm" — proactive post-storm canvassing is clearly part of their playbook.

They also deploy individual named reps (e.g., "Carl Smith") who reportedly worked with customers for over 14 months fighting insurance denials — the kind of white-glove insurance advocacy that earns personal, name-dropping reviews.

They also actively funnel customers to leave reviews across Google, Yelp, Angie's List, and BBB

, which compounds review velocity across platforms. The formula: door-knock after storms → do the insurance paperwork → ask for the review by name. It's a rep-driven referral machine.

3. One Thing Raptor Could Copy

Indy Rooftops advertises an online tool for an "instant ballpark estimate" directly on their website (indyrooftops.com, phone: 317-617-3928)

— a low-friction top-of-funnel move that most smaller roofing contractors skip entirely.

Yahoo Local specifically cited this instant estimate capability as a key differentiator, naming them a top recommendation for homeowners who prefer quick online estimates.

Cameron, if Raptor doesn't have a self-serve estimate tool on its site, that's a fast conversion advantage to steal.

4. One Weakness Raptor Could Exploit

Indy Rooftops is only three years old and is built heavily around a small team of named reps — Mitchel Dotson, Kevin Dotson, and a handful of others who appear by first name in nearly every review.

They describe themselves as "family-owned and operated," small enough to provide personalized attention

— which is a strength, but also a scalability ceiling. A 52-reviews-in-30-days pace at a company this size likely means they're stretching thin.

Their model also relies on subcontracted roofing crews, with only an in-house manager on-site per job

— an opening for Raptor to hammer quality control and crew consistency as a contrast message in your market. If a subcontractor causes a bad job, the name-brand rep model crumbles fast.
