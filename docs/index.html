Who they are

Indy Rooftops was established in 2022 and was originally started by Cheryl Edens and Mitchel Dotson for Mitchel's father, Kevin Dotson, who had been a roofing salesman at another local company for many years.

Kevin passed away suddenly in September 2022, and his legacy in the roofing business is now carried on by his son Mitchel, who shares his commitment to customer care.

As a woman-owned business, they blend personalized attention with the support of national companies and products.

They are headquartered at 4254 N Raceway Rd, Indianapolis, IN 46234.

Indy Rooftops serves Indianapolis and surrounding areas including Carmel and Fishers,

and their service area pages extend to

Noblesville, Westfield, Plainfield, Zionsville, and Greenwood

— a wide suburban footprint for a company barely three years old.

Their service menu has expanded well beyond roofing to include siding replacement, gutter installation, window replacement, soffit and fascia repair, attic insulation, and specialty systems including EPDM, TPO, built-up, metal, and flat roofing.

They earned BBB accreditation as of January 27, 2023

— fast-tracking credibility signals within their first year.

What's driving them

485 Google reviews at 5.0 stars with +55 in 30 days on a three-year-old company is not organic drift — that is a systematic, operationalized review request cadence, almost certainly triggered at project close via SMS through a tool like NiceJob, Birdeye, or Podium. The smoking gun is the multi-platform strategy they explicitly promote:

they actively encourage customers to check their ratings on Google, Yelp, Angi, and BBB

— four platforms simultaneously, which signals deliberate reputation stack-building, not passive word of mouth. The second velocity driver is a door-knocking, storm-chasing acquisition engine:

one Angi reviewer notes, "so glad they knocked on our door after a hail storm, would never have known about the work needing done."

A third driver is a realtor referral channel that is clearly active and warm:

Finally, their insurance-advocacy positioning —

spending "several hours on the phone with my insurance to make sure they pay every dollar owed"
