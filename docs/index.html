Big Sky Roofing

+9 7d ·323 total reviews ·4.9★ ·+32 in 30d
Who they are

Big Sky Roofing LLC is a Brownsburg, Indiana-based operation founded May 20, 2021, structured as an LLC and owner-operated.

They position themselves as the go-to provider of residential and commercial roof replacement, storm damage restoration, siding installation, and gutters in the Indianapolis metro area.

They self-describe as a locally family-owned business known for integrity and craftsmanship.

They carry light commercial capability as well, offering TPO and EPDM for flat roofing systems.

On the exterior side, they've partnered with CertainTeed and James Hardie to install premium vinyl and fiber cement siding systems.

At only four years old with 323 Google reviews at 4.9 stars — and a 10% velocity spike in a single month — this is a lean owner-operated shop punching well above its weight class in the western Indianapolis suburbs (Hendricks County), with clear territorial momentum.

What's driving them

The 10% review velocity in 30 days is the most important signal here, and the evidence points to a multi-layered referral engine rather than a paid media blitz.

Big Sky openly states that almost 80% of their business comes through referrals from previously satisfied customers

— a number they actually publish on their service area page, which is an unusual and confident move.

GuildQuality-verified reviews reveal neighbor-to-neighbor influence at work: one customer explicitly noted that "Big Sky replaced my neighbor's roof and after watching their performance, I was sold."

This is yard-sign-and-neighbor-visibility fueling organic demand, not Google LSA spend.

Big Sky Roofing LLC is an active GuildQuality member, using their satisfaction surveying platform to collect and publish verified customer feedback

— a tool that systematically generates review requests post-job and feeds Google review velocity.

They also offer 24/7 emergency repair services and active insurance claims guidance

, which creates a high-urgency, high-gratitude customer touchpoint — precisely the moment when a review request converts at its highest rate. The combination of GuildQuality's automated post-job survey flow, a storm-damage-forward service model, and genuine neighbor-to-neighbor visibility in tightly packed Hendricks County HOA neighborhoods is the engine.

Tactic to steal

Big Sky is using GuildQuality as an automated post-job satisfaction survey tool that feeds verified reviews to their Google Business Profile. Raptor should deploy this within 30 days — sign up for GuildQuality (or use a comparable tool like NiceJob or Birdeye if already in-stack), configure an automated SMS/email survey trigger that fires within 24 hours of job completion, and include a direct Google review deep link in the survey close. The execution sequence: (1) set the trigger in your CRM tied to job-closed status, (2) write a single plain-language SMS template from the owner's personal number — not a marketing alias — (3) include one tap to the Google review page. At Raptor's current job volume, even a 30% conversion on that trigger should produce 15–25 new reviews per month, matching Big Sky's velocity and threatening their rank position in the Hendricks County and West Indianapolis Google Maps pack within 60–90 days.
