Section 2

2. Tactic of the Week

Deploy FAQ Schema on the Five Highest-Traffic Service Pages This Week

The WHY

AI engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and others pull data from trusted sources to determine which roofing companies to recommend, and AI rewards companies with consistent, real-world proof across every platform.

One of the fastest-acting structural signals you can deploy is FAQ schema, because it feeds two channels simultaneously: it earns Google rich-result enhancements in traditional SERPs (FAQ dropdowns that expand below your listing, increasing click-through rates), and it feeds the exact question-and-answer format that AI engines pull from when composing AI Overview and Perplexity responses.

FAQ schema is a special markup added to your web page that tells Google your page contains official answers to common questions; when used correctly, it can display your answers directly in search results, improving click-through rates by 20-30% and qualifying you for AI-generated answer cards in tools like Perplexity or ChatGPT.

The mechanism is direct: AI engines, including Google's AI Overview and Perplexity, are extracting structured question-and-answer data from web pages when constructing their summaries.

AI pulls from contractor websites, review platforms, pricing data, and industry sources; if your website shows transparent information and positions you as the honest expert who educates rather than just quotes, you get recommended.

Right now Raptor's pages have no FAQ schema. Every competitor page with FAQ schema is more likely to be pulled into AI answers than a Raptor page without it, even if Raptor's service quality is equivalent.

The HOW

Step 1: Identify the five pages to instrument. Prioritize: (1) roof replacement Indianapolis, (2) roof repair Indianapolis, (3) Carmel roofing, (4) storm damage roof repair, (5) insurance claim roofing Indianapolis. These map to the highest-volume commercial intent queries in the market.

Step 2: For each page, write 4-6 FAQ pairs in plain conversational language. Match the format homeowners actually use when querying AI — "How long does a roof replacement take in Indianapolis?", "Does homeowner's insurance cover storm damage to my roof?", "What is the average cost of a roof replacement in Carmel Indiana in 2026?", "How do I know if I need a full replacement or just a repair?".

These are the types of queries homeowners are typing into ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode.

Raptor should own the answer to each one.

Step 3: Implement the schema. Use Google's Structured Data Markup Helper or add the JSON-LD block manually to the <head> of each page. Format: @type: FAQPage, with mainEntity arrays containing Question and acceptedAnswer objects. Do not nest more than 10 Q&A pairs per page — Google's guidance caps rich-result eligibility at a manageable number.

Step 4: After publishing, submit each updated URL via Google Search Console URL inspection > Request Indexing. This accelerates the crawl.

Step 5: In the same session, add LocalBusiness schema to the homepage with NAP (name, address, phone), service hours, geo coordinates, areaServed listing each city in Raptor's service area explicitly, and priceRange. Add Review/AggregateRating schema pulling Raptor's current Google review average and count. These three schema types together — FAQPage, LocalBusiness, AggregateRating — constitute the minimum viable schema stack for AEO eligibility in this vertical.

Implementing schema markup on your site is a smart move — add LocalBusiness schema with your NAP and opening hours, add Review schema for testimonials, and FAQ schema for any Q&A sections; schema provides explicit clues to search engines about your content and can lead to rich snippets which improve visibility.
