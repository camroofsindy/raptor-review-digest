Section 2

2. Tactic of the Week

Implement RoofingContractor + FAQPage Schema on Every Suburb Service Page — Today

The WHY

AI engines — Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT with Browse — do not guess at what your business does or where it operates.

Schema markup tells Google and AI systems exactly what your business is in machine-readable code. Without it, search engines and AI tools have to infer your business type from content — and they sometimes get it wrong or simply skip you.

For Raptor, "skipping" is the operative word: zero named citations in the six AI-style queries tested this week. The structural reason is almost certainly missing or incomplete structured data combined with a thin review count. Schema is the half you can fix in 48 hours.

The critical schema types for roofers are: LocalBusiness/RoofingContractor on the homepage, Service on service pages, Review/AggregateRating on review sections, and FAQPage on FAQ sections. Benefits include improved search result appearance via rich snippets, better local search rankings, and increased AI citation likelihood.

Clear NAP and hours in schema reduce mismatches and help map visibility. As answer engines pull factual data directly, structured data boosts your chances of being cited or synthesized in AI responses.

That last sentence is the AEO mechanism: AI Overviews and Perplexity pull from structured, parseable data when composing contractor recommendations. Raptor is invisible to that pull right now.

The HOW — five steps, execute by Friday

  1. Audit current schema state. Open Google's Rich Results Test and run raptorroofing.com's homepage and two suburb service pages (e.g., Carmel, Fishers). Screenshot the output for baseline. Expected finding: missing or incomplete AggregateRating and FAQPage blocks.

  2. Build a master JSON-LD block for the homepage using the RoofingContractor type. Include name, url, telephone, address with full Indianapolis metro areaServed array (Indianapolis, Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Greenwood, Westfield, Zionsville, Avon, Plainfield), aggregateRating pulling your current Google review count and star average, and sameAs links to GBP, BBB, and Yelp. Use the Merkle Schema Markup Generator for the JSON-LD template.

Prefer RoofingContractor over the generic LocalBusiness type when applicable.

  1. Build a Service schema block for each suburb service page.

Map one service page to one Service schema — do not cram everything into a single block.

Each page gets serviceType ("Roof Replacement"), areaServed (specific suburb), and provider linked back to the root RoofingContractor entity.

  1. Add FAQPage schema to each service page. Write three to five questions that reflect real search intent: "How much does a roof replacement cost in Carmel Indiana?", "Does homeowner's insurance cover hail damage in Noblesville?", "How long does a roof replacement take?". These questions feed directly into the AI Overview answer format.

FAQPage schema increases AI citation likelihood

because answer engines extract FAQ blocks as pre-formed Q&A pairs.

  1. Validate every page with Google's Rich Results Test and Schema.org Validator before pushing to production.

Test after publishing using Google's Rich Results Test or Schema Validator to check for errors — broken schema provides no benefit.

The SUCCESS METRIC — measure at 30 days

Pull Google Search Console's "Enhancements" tab. You are looking for: (1) zero schema errors on the five highest-traffic service pages, (2) FAQ rich snippet impressions appearing in the Performance report for suburb-level queries, and (3) a baseline AEO check — re-run the six queries from Section 3 manually in Google (AI Overview), Perplexity, and Bing Copilot and record whether Raptor is now named. Schema alone will not close the full AEO gap in 30 days, but it is a mandatory prerequisite: you cannot be cited by AI systems that cannot parse your entity data.
