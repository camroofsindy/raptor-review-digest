1. Who They Are

Indy Rooftops is a family-owned and operated roofing company based in Brownsburg/Indianapolis, Indiana, deliberately sized to offer personalized homeowner attention while still having the support of national suppliers and product lines.

They were established in 2022 by Cheryl Edens and Mitchel Dotson — who originally built the business around Mitchel's father, Kevin Dotson, a longtime roofing salesman at another local company.

Their service menu covers roof replacement, repair, maintenance, inspections, leak repairs, siding, gutter installation, window replacement, soffit/fascia, attic insulation, and specialty roofing systems including EPDM, TPO, metal, and flat roofing.

Their physical address is 4254 N. Raceway Rd., Indianapolis, IN 46234 — phone: (317) 617-3928 — website: indyrooftops.com.

2. What Is Driving the Review Acceleration

Three specific engines are visible in their public footprint: (a) active post-storm door knocking —

one Angi reviewer specifically credited them with knocking on their door after a hail storm, saying they "would never have known about the work needing done" without it

; (b) a named referral program —

an Angi reviewer explicitly called out their referral program as "topnotch," saying "it's easy to tell others about having such a great experience"

; and (c) a multi-platform review push —

their own website actively directs every customer to rate them on Google, Yelp, Angie's List, and BBB

, creating a coordinated volume-building loop.

Despite being a newer business, they are already BBB A+ accredited and have stacked reviews across Google (428+ at time of that report), Angi, and Yelp.
