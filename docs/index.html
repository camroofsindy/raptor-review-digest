This is a precision storm-canvassing play — they identify hail-impacted ZIP codes using tools like HailTrace or Hi-Marley and deploy field reps with a Nextdoor posting as the digital air cover.

— this positions every sales rep as a claims concierge, not just an estimator, which dramatically shortens the close cycle and generates ecstatic reviews.

The goal is to be the first brand a homeowner sees — on their phone, on their neighborhood app, and on Google — within 48 hours of a storm.
