Who they are

Big Sky Roofing, LLC was founded on May 20, 2021, and operates as a limited liability company headquartered at 311 E Main St, Brownsburg, IN.

Big Sky Roofing was developed by two co-founders and is owner-operated, with a stated philosophy of putting the customer first.

The founding team brings an unusual combination of backgrounds to the trade:

one founder holds a construction management degree with an extensive background in real estate, while the other has a finance degree and helped build a digital media tech company from the ground up

— a combination that signals this is not a typical owner-operator shop.

They describe themselves as a locally family-owned business known for integrity and craftsmanship.

Their product mix spans residential and commercial replacement using Owens Corning, 24/7 emergency repair, insurance claim navigation, and premium siding through CertainTeed and James Hardie.

At roughly four years old with 325 Google reviews at 4.9 stars, they are punching well above their weight class for a Hendricks County startup.

What's driving them

The +34 reviews in 30 days (a 10% monthly velocity) is the clearest signal here, and the engine behind it is almost certainly a disciplined referral-and-review flywheel, not paid media.

Big Sky openly states that almost 80% of their business comes through referrals from previously satisfied customers

— a remarkably high referral rate that they are apparently converting into review requests at a high clip. The co-founder with a digital media background is almost certainly running a structured post-job review-request sequence, likely via SMS through a tool like NiceJob or Broadly, timed to hit within 24–48 hours of project completion.

GuildQuality-verified reviews reflect a "watching the neighbor's roof get done" pattern — neighbors observing the crew, then converting — a classic neighborhood visibility play.

Their insurance claim assistance model, where they advocate on behalf of the homeowner and your only out-of-pocket cost is the deductible, dramatically lowers the friction to sign

— which shortens the sales cycle and gets more jobs closed, meaning more review requests hit the street faster. Their Facebook page is also actively posting content around the "communication is part of the job" message,

positioning responsiveness as a core differentiator

— a message engineered to resonate in the Nextdoor and neighborhood Facebook group environment where referral conversations actually happen.

Tactic to steal

Big Sky is using GuildQuality as a verified third-party survey platform that feeds sentiment data and drives secondary reviews — a layer most competitors ignore entirely. Raptor should deploy GuildQuality (or its nearest competitor, Avid Ratings) within 30 days as a post-job survey tool, with the survey firing automatically via JobNimbus or AccuLynx integration 48 hours after a job is marked complete. The GuildQuality profile becomes a trust artifact usable in sales presentations ("our satisfaction scores are independently verified, not self-reported"), and the survey completion rate naturally surfaces your happiest customers for a follow-on Google review request via SMS. This creates a two-stage review acceleration loop — verified satisfaction data followed by a public Google review ask — which is almost certainly the mechanism behind Big Sky's outsized velocity. Execute the integration in week one, run a re-engagement campaign to past 90-day customers in weeks two and three, and you will close the review gap within a single billing cycle.
