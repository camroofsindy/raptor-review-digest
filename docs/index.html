Indy Rooftops

+8 7d ·524 total reviews ·5.0★ ·+64 in 30d
Who they are

Founded in 2022, Indy Rooftops was started by Mitchel Dotson and Cheryl Edens specifically to create a business for Dotson's father Kevin, a veteran roofing salesman who had spent many years at another local company.

The company is woman-owned and roots itself in Brownsburg, Indiana, positioning its identity around personalized, trust-first service.

Their service scope is notably broad: roof replacement, repair, maintenance, inspections, leak repairs, siding, gutter installation, window replacement, soffit and fascia, attic insulation, and specialty systems including EPDM, TPO, built-up, metal, and flat roofing.

They achieved BBB Accreditation by January 2023

— within months of launch — signaling an early, deliberate credibility play. At 524 Google reviews and a maintained 5.0, they are punching well above their age for a sub-4-year-old operation in a crowded Indianapolis market.

What's driving them

The +64-reviews-in-30-days surge (12% velocity on a 524-review base) is almost certainly not organic drift — it is a systematic post-job review-request cadence, likely automated through a tool like NiceJob or Broadly.

Customer reviews explicitly note that the team "communicates easily and clearly via text" and that "they instantly answer the phone no matter the hour,"

which indicates a texting-first workflow that easily appends a review request link at job close. The second major driver is active insurance claim advocacy —

reviews describe Indy Rooftops spending "several hours on the phone with my insurance to make sure they pay every dollar owed," with customers noting the company has "a great legal footing to ensure insurance are held liable."

This insurance-handling capability functions as a referral accelerant: delighted policyholders who felt protected against their insurer become vocal brand advocates.

One HomeAdvisor reviewer specifically credited door-knocking after a hail storm as how they discovered the company

— confirming a canvassing component is active, not just inbound lead gen.

Multiple Angi reviews are written by realtors who are actively referring the company to clients

, pointing to a real estate professional referral channel that is compounding the review engine.

Their weakness

Indy Rooftops maintains only 42 Yelp reviews

despite their 524-review Google profile — a dramatic platform imbalance that signals their reputation is fragile on any channel they don't actively manage. More critically,

third-party aggregators note that "some reviews indicate the work may be subcontracted,"

and

warranties are not discussed in their customer reviews at all.

In a sales conversation, Raptor should drive directly at this: "With Indy Rooftops, you may not know who is actually getting on your roof — and there's nothing in their public record about what warranty backs the install. With Raptor, you get [X-year workmanship warranty], our own trained crew, and you can verify every license before we start." The subcontractor ambiguity plus the warranty silence is a two-punch close that peels off any homeowner who did their homework.
