Hey Cameron — here's what you need to know about Indy Rooftops:

1. Who They Are

Indy Rooftops is a family-owned, woman-owned roofing company based in Brownsburg/Indianapolis, Indiana.

Founded in 2022, the business was started by Mitchel Dotson and Cheryl Edens, originally for Mitchel's father Kevin Dotson, who had been a roofing salesman at another local company for years.

Kevin Dotson was the cornerstone of the team, but passed away suddenly in September 2022; his legacy is now carried on by his son Mitchel.

Their services include roof replacement, repair, maintenance, inspections, leak repairs, shingle replacement, emergency services, siding replacement, gutter installation, window replacement, soffit and fascia repair, and attic insulation.

They're operating out of 4254 N Raceway Rd, Indianapolis, IN 46234 (317-617-3928) and serving the broader Indianapolis metro including Carmel and Noblesville.

Even as a newer business, Indy Rooftops is already BBB A+ accredited, with five-star ratings across Google (428+ reviews at the time of that report), Angi, and Yelp.

2. What's Driving the Review Acceleration

Three overlapping engines are at work. First,

customer reviews explicitly note that Indy Rooftops canvasses neighborhoods after storms — one reviewer noted "so glad they knocked on our door after a hail storm"

— this door-to-door storm-response model floods them with fresh completed jobs fast. Second,

their referral program is a direct driver — customers specifically call it out, saying "their referral program is top-notch" and that "it's easy to tell others about having such a great experience."

Third,

Indy Rooftops actively directs customers to leave reviews on Google, Yelp, Angie's List, and BBB

— a systematic multi-platform push that most smaller competitors skip. The combination of storm-chasing lead generation (volume of jobs), a formalized referral program, and a post-job review ask across every platform is the engine behind 47 reviews in 30 days on a ~480-review base.

3. One Thing Raptor Could Copy

Indy Rooftops offers an online instant ballpark estimate tool embedded directly into their website (indyrooftops.com), giving homeowners a price range without a phone call or appointment.

This is a conversion rate lever most local roofers ignore — it lowers friction for prospects who won't pick up the phone, captures their contact info, and positions the company as more transparent than competitors. Cameron, if Raptor doesn't have one of these, it's a straightforward add-on (tools like Roofr's Instant Estimator embed directly into your site) and it feeds your review pipeline by increasing job volume.

4. One Weakness Raptor Can Exploit

Indy Rooftops works with subcontracted roofing crews, with an in-house manager on-site for each job

— that's a reputational vulnerability. Subcontractor models create consistency risk, and homeowners increasingly research this. If Raptor uses an in-house, W-2 crew model, lead with that contrast aggressively in your ads, your Google Business Profile, and your door-to-door pitch: "Our crew is our crew — not a subcontractor."

Indy Rooftops only offers a three-year workmanship warranty, and even that comes with the caveat that they may address issues outside the warranty period on a case-by-case basis

— if Raptor offers a longer or more clearly guaranteed workmanship warranty, that's a direct, specific point of differentiation you can put on every estimate you hand a homeowner.
