1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010, Cochran Exteriors has built its reputation around exterior renovation in Indiana.

The company is co-owned by Ross Cochran and John Cochran

, making it a family-owned operation with a named Marketing Manager (Andrew Beck) already on staff — a signal they take brand seriously.

They operate out of Indiana, Illinois (Schaumburg), and Kentucky (Lexington)

, positioning them as a regional multi-state player, not just an Indy shop. Their primary pitch is full exterior scope:

roofing, siding, windows, doors, gutters, and more

, which dilutes their roofing identity but expands average job value.

Cochran's web presence is structured with intent.

Their LinkedIn content cadence includes recurring #TipTuesday and #ServiceSpotlight posts, indicating a managed social/content calendar, not ad hoc posting. 7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cochran beats Raptor: Review volume (1,173 vs. 664), multi-state brand presence, full exterior scope (windows/siding/doors adds cross-sell revenue), dedicated marketing staff, and a named Brand President driving growth strategy. Their cost calculator and localized pricing pages are ahead of most Indy competitors on AEO structure.

Where Raptor beats Cochran: 5.0 stars vs. 4.7 is a real and meaningful gap at scale. Raptor's accountability positioning is cleaner and more defensible. Cochran's BBB complaints and HomeAdvisor reviews document post-sale communication breakdowns and workmanship disputes that Raptor can own against.

Most exploitable weakness: Cochran's owner response rate of 0.94 looks engaged, but the pattern of BBB and HomeAdvisor complaints about upselling, poor communication post-contract, and warranty disputes creates a credibility ceiling that Raptor — with a 5.0 rating and a "we don't outsource accountability" brand promise — can directly target in paid and social messaging.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The roof cost calculator.

Cochran deploys a free Indiana roof cost calculator on their site

that captures mid-funnel leads before a homeowner ever picks up the phone. Raptor should build or license an equivalent tool and place it above the fold on the homepage and roofing service pages. It is one of the highest-converting lead gen tools in residential exterior, and Cochran has it; Raptor does not appear to.

  1. Localized cost content pages for suburban markets. Cochran has dedicated roofing pages for the Carmel, Fishers, Greenwood, and Indianapolis markets with specific price ranges baked in. These pages directly feed AI citation engines and cost-intent Google queries. Raptor should be building or refreshing city-level pages for every primary suburb with hyperlocal pricing data — this is the single fastest path to AEO visibility in 2026.

  2. Monitor the Sales Manager hire.

Cochran is actively seeking a Sales Manager in Indianapolis.

When that role is filled, expect a more aggressive canvassing and D2D push in overlapping Raptor territories — particularly Greenwood (they have an office there) and Carmel/Fishers. Track job board fulfillment and watch for uptick in Nextdoor mentions in those zip codes as an early warning indicator.
