Competitive Intelligence Brief: Cochran Exteriors

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated April 26, 2026

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010, Cochran Exteriors has positioned itself as a full-service exterior remodeling company serving Indianapolis and the broader Indiana market.

Co-owners are Ross Cochran and John Cochran, operating the company as a family-owned business with a named marketing manager, Andrew Beck, on record at the BBB.

Their service offering spans roofing, siding, windows, doors, and gutters

— making them a multi-trade exterior company, not a roofing-first specialist, which is their core brand differentiation and also their operational liability.

2. Estimated Scale

PitchBook lists Cochran Exteriors at 43 total employees.

Confidence: medium.

ZoomInfo notes Cochran received an SBA PPP loan for $1–2 million from Regions Bank, supporting the retention of 47 jobs

, which is a reasonable corroborating headcount signal. On revenue:

RocketReach estimates Cochran Exteriors annual revenue at $6.8 million in 2025

, while

CIENCE data puts the range at $10M–$25M.

Confidence: low — these third-party estimates diverge significantly; the $6.8M figure from RocketReach is likely understated for a 40+ employee operation with dual offices. Real revenue is probably in the $10M–$18M range.

They operate two locations: the main office at 8461 Castlewood Dr, Indianapolis, and a second office at 48 N Emerson Ave, Ste 600, Greenwood, IN 46143.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Two active hiring signals visible on LinkedIn as of this brief cycle:

Cochran is actively recruiting a Commercial Estimator in Indianapolis, Indiana.

They are also recruiting a Residential Sales Consultant in Indianapolis, Indiana.

These two simultaneous postings — one commercial, one residential — signal that they are trying to grow both revenue streams in parallel. The Commercial Estimator role is the more telling post:

Cochran recently highlighted a commercial project at an apartment complex in Greenfield, IN

as a "Project of the Month," suggesting the commercial push is active and resourced, not aspirational.

Greg joined the team in 2025 as Brand President, with experience in home services and a focus on client experience

— a brand-level leadership hire that signals a deliberate repositioning effort, not just organic growth.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp lists Cochran Exteriors at 8461 Castlewood Dr with 54 reviews as of April 2026.

No star rating was surfaced from Yelp directly in search results — 54 reviews against 1,173 on Google is a classic review-funneling signature. Customers are being routed to Google and away from Yelp, where review content is harder to manage.

On BBB, Cochran holds an A+ rating with accredited status.

However, the BBB complaints page surfaces unresolved disputes including workmanship and warranty denials —

one reviewer wrote "Avoid this company. They will weasel out of poor workmanship. Warranty isn't worth the paper it was written on."

On Nextdoor, Cochran comes up positively recommended for roofing, windows, gutters, and siding, with customers praising the team's professionalism.

No Facebook Ad Library activity was surfaced in these results — their paid social footprint, if active, is not generating earned mentions or viral engagement.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cochran's web infrastructure is more mature than most local competitors.

They hold both Owens Corning Platinum and GAF Certified Contractor designations

, which feed credible backlink and trust-signal pipelines. Their site architecture is built around city/service page combinations (e.g., /in/roofing, /in/roofing/flat, /commercial/) — a clear programmatic local SEO play targeting neighborhood and suburb queries.

Their roofing pages include specific cost data — stating roof replacement ranges from $16,000 to $24,000 depending on complexity

— a tactic designed to capture high-intent cost-query traffic and anchor price expectations before a sales call. They also run a roof cost calculator on-site. The domain has been live since 2010, giving them a meaningful age and backlink head start.

They hold Angie's List Super Service Awards (2012, 2013), BBB Accreditation, and an Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor designation

, all of which create citation and trust-signal density. Schema markup and local structured data are likely in place given the sophistication of their page build, though no specific schema confirmation was returned in search results.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Cochran Exteriors has meaningful signals that would likely get them cited in AI-generated answers for Indianapolis roofing queries. Their 1,173 Google reviews (4.7 stars), multi-year domain authority, dual office locations, Owens Corning Platinum status, and presence on BBB, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Houzz, Yelp, Nextdoor, and Procore create the kind of cross-platform citation density that large language models use to surface local service recommendations.

At least one third-party site describes them as the trusted and experienced local choice for "more than 10,000 customers in Indianapolis,"

which is the type of superlative claim that can bleed into AI training data and generative responses. Raptor currently has 664 reviews at 5.0 stars — the volume gap (509 reviews) is the primary handicap in AI citation likelihood. Cameron should treat review volume as an AEO lever, not just a reputation metric.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Cochran beats Raptor: Raw review volume (1,173 vs. 664), domain age and SEO infrastructure, multi-trade offering (windows, doors, siding) that generates cross-sell revenue and broader keyword coverage, and a named marketing manager plus a new Brand President signaling real marketing investment. Their commercial division is actively scaling with a dedicated ops manager and new estimator hire.

Where Raptor beats Cochran: Star rating — 5.0 vs. 4.7 is not a small gap at this review volume, and Raptor's 0.95 owner response rate vs. Cochran's mixed engagement on third-party complaint sites signals tighter accountability.

Negative reviews on HomeAdvisor describe Cochran customer experiences as "difficult, frustrating, and stressful" from sales through project completion

— a post-sale delivery gap Raptor can exploit in messaging.

One BBB reviewer noted a Cochran installer asked them to leave a good review so he could get "stars" and a bonus

— a review-incentivization red flag that undermines their credibility and is exactly the behavior Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning counters directly.

Most exploitable weakness: Cochran's reviews reveal a recurring pattern of aggressive sales follow-up, upselling, and post-sale communication breakdowns — the exact gap Raptor's premium, accountable positioning is built to own.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The cost calculator and price-anchoring content.

Cochran publishes specific cost ranges ($15,900–$24,400) and offers a free roof cost calculator, paired with free inspections and flexible financing

— this is high-intent SEO content that intercepts buyers at the research stage. Raptor should build comparable transparent pricing content to compete for those queries before the lead ever calls Cochran.

  1. The dual-office geographic positioning.

Cochran's Greenwood office at 48 N Emerson Ave directly covers the Johnson County/south suburban market

— a move that expands their GBP radius and local pack eligibility. Cameron should evaluate whether a satellite office or registered secondary location could extend Raptor's local pack coverage into Greenwood, Whiteland, or Bargersville.

  1. The programmatic city-service page architecture. Cochran has built location-specific URL structures (/in/roofing/flat, /commercial/) targeting granular service + geography combinations. Raptor should audit which suburb-specific pages Cochran ranks for that Raptor does not yet have, and prioritize those gaps as a content build-out roadmap for Q2–Q3.
