Competitive Intelligence Brief: Cochran Exteriors

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed: May 21, 2025

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010, Cochran Exteriors has built its brand around exterior remodeling across Indiana.

Co-owners are Ross Cochran and John Cochran, with Andrew Beck listed as Marketing Manager on the BBB profile.

They position as a full-service exterior contractor across roofing, siding, windows, doors, and gutters

— and have expanded beyond pure roofing to include a commercial division, making them a broader competitive threat than a single-trade shop.

2. Estimated Scale

PitchBook lists Cochran Exteriors at 43 total employees.

Confidence: medium.

ZoomInfo notes they received an SBA PPP loan for $1–2 million from Regions Bank, supporting the retention of 47 jobs,

which corroborates the headcount range. Revenue estimates conflict:

RocketReach puts annual revenue at $6.8 million for 2025,

while

CIENCE estimates $10–25 million.

Confidence: low on the exact figure — call it $7–12M with the CIENCE upper bound likely reflecting gross contract value, not net revenue. Either way, they are running significantly more volume than a boutique operator and have the payroll infrastructure to sustain an aggressive sales force.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Cochran's LinkedIn page has active postings for both a Commercial Estimator and a Residential Sales Consultant in Indianapolis.

ZoomInfo also flags Cochran as currently seeking a Sales Manager in Indianapolis.

Three concurrent revenue-side roles — commercial estimator, residential sales consultant, and sales manager — signals an intentional push to expand both sales headcount and commercial pipeline simultaneously. There are no visible marketing, ops leadership, or digital roles posted, suggesting the in-house marketing function (Andrew Beck) is not being scaled at this time.

4. Off-Google Presence

Cochran holds a BBB A+ rating and is a BBB Accredited Business.

They list two physical locations: the Castlewood Drive HQ and a Greenwood office at 48 N Emerson Ave, Ste 600.

On Yelp,

they have 54 reviews on record,

a thin number for a 15-year-old company with 1,189 Google reviews — a clear review-funneling pattern toward Google.

On Nextdoor, Cochran was a Neighborhood Favorite in 2021, 2022, and 2023

— a meaningful trust signal in residential zip codes they serve.

They currently hold 152 Faves on Nextdoor.

Facebook shows a page at cochranexteriorsindy with a modest following. No confirmed active Meta Ad Library campaigns were surfaced, suggesting limited paid social investment at this time. The BBB complaints file contains workmanship and warranty disputes, including at least one unresolved complaint where

a customer alleged they would "weasel out of poor workmanship" and that the warranty was not honored.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cochran runs a structured, content-active website at cochranexteriors.com.

They maintain an active blog covering roofing, siding, and windows with tips from exterior remodeling experts.

Their roofing page targets transactional queries directly, publishing cost benchmarks like "the average cost of a roof replacement in Indianapolis ranges from $16,000 to $24,000."

They also deploy a free Indiana roof cost calculator

— a high-conversion AEO asset that feeds answer-engine citations.

They claim both Owens Corning Platinum and GAF Certified Contractor status,

which are legitimate backlink and trust signals. The site has geo-targeted service area pages (e.g., /in/roofing) indicating deliberate local SEO architecture.

Their known tech stack includes reCAPTCHA, Cloudflare CDN, and YouTube integrations.

Their domain has age authority (est. 2010), consistent blog publishing, and a cost calculator — a three-leg AEO stool Raptor does not currently have in that combination.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on available query signals: Cochran Exteriors is well-positioned to appear in AI-generated answers for Indy roofing queries. The combination of a 15-year-old domain, a published cost calculator with specific Indianapolis price ranges, active blog content, BBB A+ accreditation, and 1,189 Google reviews gives AI engines multiple citation anchors. Their cost page specifically — publishing "$16,000–$24,000" and "$15,900–$24,400" benchmarks with named service context — is the exact structured, quotable data that ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity pull into responses.

The interactive roof cost calculator

likely earns direct crawl priority from AI indexing systems looking for authoritative local pricing data. Cochran is almost certainly being cited in Gemini and Perplexity responses for "roof replacement cost Indianapolis" queries. Raptor has no equivalent owned cost-data asset to compete for those citations.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cochran beats Raptor: Raw review volume (1,189 vs. 675) gives them dominant GBP shelf presence; multi-service positioning (roofing + siding + windows + commercial) increases wallet share per customer; a long-tenured domain with a cost calculator and active blog creates compounding AEO presence Raptor lacks. Where Raptor beats Cochran: A perfect 5.0 rating versus Cochran's 4.7 is a meaningful quality signal in premium positioning; Raptor's owner response rate of 0.96 is elite; BBB complaints alleging

a crew member asking customers to leave good reviews for a "stars" bonus

is a reputation liability Raptor can weaponize with a "we don't solicit reviews" narrative. Most exploitable weakness: The documented pattern of post-sale communication failures, warranty disputes, and upsell pressure complaints across BBB, HomeAdvisor, and Angi creates a clear opening for Raptor to own "the contractor that doesn't disappear after the check clears."

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Build a roof cost calculator page. Cochran's free Indiana roof cost calculator is a direct AEO citation magnet. A Raptor-branded version with Indy/Carmel/Fishers/Noblesville/Greenwood geo-variants would compete head-on for the exact structured-data snippets AI engines are pulling for cost queries — and it positions Raptor as the premium transparent option.

  2. Replicate their Nextdoor Neighborhood Favorite strategy.

Cochran earned the Neighborhood Favorite badge three consecutive years (2021–2023).

That badge is earned through neighbor votes, not paid placement. Raptor should run a focused push through the Nextdoor Business page and encourage existing customers in specific high-value neighborhoods (Carmel, Zionsville, Fishers) to favorite and recommend — the badge is a powerful AEO and social proof signal in hyper-local conversations where GBP doesn't dominate.

  1. Monitor the Sales Manager and Commercial Estimator hires closely. Three concurrent revenue-side hires signal Cochran is about to increase outbound sales volume, likely with a heavier storm-chasing and insurance-claim workflow. Cameron should track whether these hires trigger a surge in Cochran's review velocity (which currently sits at 37/month) — and prepare Raptor's counter-positioning around accountability and quality over volume before that sales force is fully ramped.
