Competitive Intelligence Brief: Cochran Exteriors

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed: June 9, 2025

1. Who They Are

Cochran Exteriors was founded in 2010 and has built its brand on exterior remodeling across Indiana.

Co-owned by Ross Cochran and John Cochran, with Andrew Beck serving as Marketing Manager.

The critical flag for this brief:

Cochran was acquired by Infinity Home Services (IHS), a portfolio company of North Branch Capital, as part of IHS's national consolidation strategy in the non-insurance, retail residential exterior market.

This is no longer a true independent — it is a PE-backed roll-up brand operating under a multi-state platform now backed by Freeman Spogli & Co. and LightBay Capital.

2. Estimated Scale

PitchBook lists Cochran Exteriors at 43 total employees

— confidence: medium.

ZoomInfo notes a PPP SBA loan from Regions Bank supporting the retention of 47 jobs

, which triangulates closely. On revenue, sources diverge:

RocketReach pegs annual revenue at $6.8M for 2025

(confidence: low), while

CIENCE places them in the $10M–$25M range

(confidence: low). Given their employee count, review velocity, multi-line service offering (roofing, siding, windows, gutters, doors), two Indiana locations, and PE platform backing, $10M–$15M in annual revenue is the most defensible working estimate. They are meaningfully larger in headcount and service breadth than Raptor's pure-roofing focus.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Active and aggressive.

LinkedIn shows an open Commercial Estimator posting in Indianapolis.

A Residential Sales Consultant role is also live on LinkedIn in Indianapolis.

As recently as 2025, they onboarded a new Sales Representative (David VanOstrand)

— suggesting continuous sales force expansion.

Their HR/Marketing crossover role (Sarah Budzinski, joined 2023) draws from a Disney operations and training background, signaling investment in systematized employee onboarding and customer experience frameworks.

The Commercial Estimator hire is the most telling signal: they are pushing harder into commercial revenue, likely to diversify beyond storm-season residential volatility.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp lists them at 54 reviews

— a near-invisible footprint relative to their 1,198 Google reviews. That is a textbook review-funneling operation: homeowners are being routed exclusively to Google, and Yelp is an afterthought.

BBB carries an A+ accreditation

with a second location at

48 N Emerson Ave, Suite 600, Greenwood, IN.

The BBB profile surface includes both glowing testimonials and at least one complaint flagging workmanship accountability.

Glassdoor employee ratings sit at 3.7/5.0 across 17 anonymous reviews

— not alarming, but not strong. One reviewer noted

"the turnaround rate is terrible because of the lack of training, organization, professionalism, and employee development"

— a potential internal fragility worth watching as IHS scales.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cochran is investing seriously in structured content. Their site runs geo-targeted service pages (e.g., cochranexteriors.com/in/roofing) with suburb-specific URL architecture — a deliberate play for long-tail local queries across Fishers, Carmel, Greenwood, and Greenfield.

They have published an online Indiana roof cost calculator

, which is a high-intent AEO/FAQ asset designed to capture "how much does a roof cost in Indianapolis" queries in AI answers.

Their tech stack includes reCAPTCHA, Cloudflare CDN, and YouTube integrations

, suggesting active video content and performance optimization. The IHS platform almost certainly gives them shared SEO infrastructure, link equity from multi-state brand pages, and a centralized content team — advantages no independent operator in the Indy market replicates. Their domain authority signal benefits from PE-level investment; Cameron should monitor whether IHS cross-links Cochran from its main domain, which would be a meaningful DA boost.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Cochran Exteriors is well-positioned to surface in AI-generated answers for Indianapolis roofing queries. Their combination of 1,198 Google reviews (4.7 stars),

BBB A+ accreditation

, dedicated geo-targeted service pages, a roof cost calculator, and belonging to a nationally recognized platform (IHS was cited on Qualified Remodeler's Top 100 list) gives them the citation signals that LLMs pull from. They are likely appearing in ChatGPT and Gemini answers for "best roofers Indianapolis" and "roof replacement Carmel Indiana" — primarily because they have the review mass, schema-friendly site structure, and third-party publication mentions that feed AI retrieval. Raptor's 677 reviews at a perfect 5.0 is strong but the volume gap is the core vulnerability in AEO. AI models weight review density heavily in local authority signals. Closing that gap — not just maintaining the star rating — is the path to displacing Cochran in generative results.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cochran beats Raptor: Review volume (1,198 vs. 677) is the single biggest lever — they will outrank Raptor in AI citations and map pack impressions on volume alone. Their multi-service model (windows, siding, doors) creates longer customer lifetime value and larger average tickets. PE backing means corporate marketing budget, shared SEO infrastructure, and a dedicated marketing manager Raptor cannot easily replicate dollar-for-dollar. Their two-location footprint (Indy + Greenwood) gives them southern suburb coverage.

Where Raptor beats Cochran: A 5.0 star rating vs. 4.7 is a meaningful differentiator in premium positioning — and Raptor's review response rate of 0.95 signals genuine owner engagement that Cochran's corporate structure makes harder to replicate authentically. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" message is a direct counter to the inherent weakness of any PE roll-up brand: the original founders may not be running your job.

Most exploitable weakness:

Internal reviews flagging high turnover and lack of training

create a real credibility gap Cameron can exploit — a PE-owned company where crew quality and accountability are inconsistent is a story that writes itself in comparison marketing.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Roof cost calculator page.

Cochran's free Indiana roof cost calculator

is purpose-built to capture AI-answer and featured-snippet real estate for high-intent cost queries. Raptor should build and publish a competing Indianapolis roof cost calculator before Cochran's version becomes the default AI citation for that query class.

  1. Geo-targeted URL architecture. Cochran runs separate landing pages per suburb (e.g., /in/roofing with Greenfield commercial callouts). Raptor should audit whether it has suburb-level pages for Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, Carmel, and Greenwood that are indexed and optimized — or it will cede those long-tail map pack and AI placements by default.

  2. Monitor IHS platform integration.

IHS's national consolidation strategy

means Cochran will receive centralized marketing resources, cross-brand backlinks, and national PR that independent operators cannot match organically. Cameron should watch whether IHS begins pushing Cochran harder on paid search (Google Ads/LSA) in Indy, which would signal a coordinated budget push — and should be ready to counter with tighter geo-targeting on LSA bids for the suburbs where Raptor's 5.0 rating is the differentiator.
