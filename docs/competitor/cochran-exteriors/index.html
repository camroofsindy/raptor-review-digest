Cochran Exteriors — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Refreshed: May 27, 2025
Competitor: Cochran Exteriors | 8461 Castlewood Dr, Indianapolis, IN 46250

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010, Cochran Exteriors has been operating in Indiana for 15 years

, run by

co-owners Ross Cochran and John Cochran.

They are a full exterior remodeling contractor covering roofing, siding, windows, doors, and gutters

, competing on breadth of service rather than roofing-only specialization.

They operate two locations — the Castlewood Dr. HQ in Indianapolis and a second office at 48 N. Emerson Ave., Suite 600 in Greenwood

— giving them a dual-market footprint across Marion and Johnson Counties.

2. Estimated Scale

PitchBook lists 43 total employees

(confidence: medium — reflects a point-in-time snapshot). Revenue estimates diverge:

RocketReach pegs annual revenue at $6.8 million for 2025

, while

CIENCE places it in the $10M–$25M range.

The midpoint — call it $8M–$12M — is the most defensible working estimate given the 43-person headcount and dual-location structure (confidence: low).

ZoomInfo notes Cochran received an SBA PPP loan supporting retention of 47 jobs

, corroborating headcount in that range.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

LinkedIn shows active postings for a Commercial Estimator in Indianapolis

, which signals a push to grow commercial revenue.

A Residential Sales Consultant posting also appeared on LinkedIn

— consistent with ongoing sales team churn.

Glassdoor reviewers flag poor training, no growth opportunities, and minimal benefits

, which explains the perpetual sales rep recruiting cycle. No marketing-specific leadership hire detected in the last 30 days.

The named Marketing Manager on BBB is Andrew Beck

— no indication of a replacement or upgrade hire in progress.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp shows 54 reviews

— tiny relative to 1,190 on Google. This is a textbook review-funneling pattern: their team is clearly steering satisfied customers to Google and largely ignoring Yelp.

BBB rating is A+, accredited

, which they lean on in sales conversations. However,

BBB complaint text includes reviews calling them out for poor workmanship and a warranty that is "not worth the paper it was written on."

Nextdoor presence exists and is functional

, with reps posting storm-damage lead gen content. No Facebook Ad Library data was surfaced in results, but their Nextdoor and BBB activity suggests the bulk of their paid/social effort is storm-chase and insurance-claim focused rather than brand-building.

HomeAdvisor reviews include notably negative accounts describing the overall experience as "difficult, frustrating, and stressful."

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cochran has made a real SEO investment.

They maintain an active blog covering roofing, siding, and windows

, targeting informational queries. Their site deploys geo-specific subdirectory architecture (cochranexteriors.com/in, /in/roofing, /in/service-areas), which is a legitimate local SEO play for capturing suburb-level queries.

ThreeBestRated.com lists them as one of the top 3 roofing contractors in Indianapolis

— a third-party citation that passes link equity and is likely a deliberate link-building acquisition.

They hold both Owens Corning Platinum and GAF Certified Contractor status

, which they use as on-page trust signals and likely feed into structured data.

A 50-year warranty claim appears across multiple service pages

, functioning as both a conversion tool and an AEO-ready answer to warranty queries. Raptor should note their pricing page (cochranexteriors.com/pricing) — publishing cost ranges is a deliberate tactic to capture "how much does a roof cost in Indianapolis" queries.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Cochran has meaningful AI engine exposure.

They appear by name on ThreeBestRated.com's "Top 3 Roofing Contractors in Indianapolis" list

, which is a frequently cited source in ChatGPT and Perplexity responses to "best roofers Indianapolis" queries. Their dual-certification status (Owens Corning Platinum + GAF), BBB A+ standing, and volume of structured third-party citations (ThreeBestRated, Houzz, Angi, Nextdoor, BBB) all feed AI retrieval.

A 2026 "Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis" roundup describes them as integrating roofing with siding, windows, and curb-appeal-driven projects

— the exact type of long-form listicle that LLMs pull from. Raptor is not currently named in the sources surfaced in this research, which is the gap. Cameron and Patrick need to prioritize placement on ThreeBestRated and equivalent roundup sites to enter the AI citation pool.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cochran beats Raptor: Raw review volume is their biggest weapon — 1,190 Google reviews vs. Raptor's 675 creates a perceived authority gap in Maps rankings and AI answers, even though Raptor's 5.0 is factually superior to their 4.7. Their multi-service model (roofing + siding + windows + gutters + doors) also allows larger average ticket sizes and cross-sell opportunities Raptor does not currently pursue. Their SEO infrastructure — geo subdirectories, pricing pages, active blog — is more built-out.

Where Raptor beats Cochran: Star rating is not close. 5.0 vs. 4.7 sounds minor but at scale it is structurally meaningful, especially when Cochran's BBB complaint file,

employee reviews soliciting five-star ratings in exchange for bonuses

, and Angi/HomeAdvisor criticisms are visible to anyone who looks past Google. Raptor's owner response rate and quality signal genuine accountability; Cochran's 0.97 rate is strong but the content of their responses often reads templated.

Most exploitable weakness:

Internal Glassdoor reviews flag a high turnover rate tied to poor training and broken growth promises

— meaning Cochran is perpetually onboarding new sales reps, creating inconsistent customer experiences that Raptor can weaponize in any "why us" sales conversation.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Geo subdirectory SEO architecture. Cochran's use of cochranexteriors.com/in/roofing and /in/service-areas is a replicable structure. Raptor should build equivalent pages for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Greenwood with unique content — not just city-name swaps — to compete for suburb-specific queries where Cochran's pages are thin.

  2. Pricing transparency page.

Cochran publishes cost ranges ($16K–$24K for a roof replacement)

as a deliberate SEO and AEO play. Cameron should consider a Raptor pricing page that answers the same query but pairs it with a premium positioning angle — "here is what quality costs and why" — rather than competing on price alone.

  1. Third-party roundup placement for AI visibility.

Cochran's appearance on ThreeBestRated.com

gives them recurring AI citation coverage. Raptor should pursue placement on ThreeBestRated, Expertise.com, and similar structured listicles that LLMs index heavily. These are acquirable through outreach and are the fastest path to appearing in generative engine answers for "best roofer Indianapolis" and suburb-level queries.
