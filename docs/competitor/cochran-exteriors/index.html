Competitive Intelligence Brief — Cochran Exteriors

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated: May 19, 2025

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010, Cochran Exteriors has built its reputation as a central Indiana exterior remodeling contractor.

The company is co-owned by Ross Cochran and John Cochran

, with

Brad Baugh serving as Brand President

— a title that signals an Infinity Home Services-era executive layer above the founding family.

Cochran Exteriors was acquired by Infinity Home Services in June 2022

, making it a platform company asset, not an independent operator — a fact they do not prominently advertise to consumers.

Their service scope covers roofing, siding, windows, doors, and gutters across Indianapolis and central Indiana.

2. Estimated Scale

PitchBook lists Cochran Exteriors at 43 total employees

, while

ZoomInfo brackets them at 11–50 employees at their Indianapolis headquarters.

Confidence: medium. On revenue, sources diverge:

RocketReach estimates 2025 annual revenue at $6.8 million

, while

CIENCE puts the range at $10–25 million.

The spread reflects the opacity of a private subsidiary. Given the 1,187 Google review count, two physical office locations, and national Top 100 Roofing Contractor recognition, the $8–12M working estimate is most defensible. Confidence: low. Notably,

Cochran Exteriors received an SBA PPP loan of $1–2 million from Regions Bank, supporting retention of 47 jobs

— the strongest independent headcount signal available.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Two active hiring signals found on LinkedIn within recent months:

Cochran posted for a Commercial Estimator in Indianapolis

, and

also posted for a Residential Sales Consultant in Indianapolis.

The Commercial Estimator post is the more strategically significant signal — it confirms they are pushing harder into the commercial segment, likely driven by Infinity Home Services' margin requirements. No marketing, ops leadership, or digital/AEO roles were identified in current postings, which is a notable gap given the scale of their review velocity.

4. Off-Google Presence

Cochran Exteriors holds BBB Accreditation with an A+ rating

and

maintains two physical locations: 8461 Castlewood Dr, Indianapolis, and 48 N Emerson Ave, Suite 600, Greenwood.

They have been named one of the Top 100 Roofing Contractors in the nation

— a credentialing claim they deploy across Yelp, Nextdoor, and their own site. On Yelp, they have only 54 reviews (cochranexteriors-indianapolis-2) against 1,187 on Google — a textbook review-funneling gap. The BBB complaint file is worth watching:

at least one reviewer states "Avoid this company. They will weasel out of poor workmanship. Warranty isn't worth the paper it was written on."

Separately,

a BBB reviewer noted that a crew member asked them to leave a good review so he could get "stars" and a bonus

— a structurally risky incentive model that inflates volume at the cost of authenticity.

On Nextdoor, Cochran comes up positively recommended for roofing, windows, gutters, and siding, with customers praising the team's professionalism.

Their tech stack includes Google Ads Conversion Tracking and Microsoft Clarity

, confirming active paid media and on-site behavioral tracking.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cochran's domain (cochranexteriors.com) is a 15-year-old root domain with consistent content investment.

They maintain an active blog covering roofing, siding, and windows, positioning themselves as exterior remodeling educators.

They hold both Owens Corning Platinum and GAF Certified Contractor status

— dual manufacturer certifications that generate authoritative backlinks and trust signals AI engines weight heavily. Their service area pages are geo-segmented (e.g., /in/roofing) and their Houzz profile lists neighborhood-specific landing pages (Castleton, Crows Nest, Rocky Ripple, Williams Creek, etc.) — a hyperlocal SEO strategy Raptor has room to counter.

They also carry Angie's List Super Service Awards from 2012–2013 and NRCA membership

, which contribute to their backlink profile. No evidence of schema markup issues surfaced, and their use of Cloudflare and Bootstrap suggests a modern technical foundation.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Cochran Exteriors has meaningful AI citation exposure based on their profile: a 15-year-old domain, Top 100 Roofing Contractor designation, dual manufacturer certifications (Owens Corning Platinum + GAF), BBB A+ accreditation, and 1,187 Google reviews are exactly the structured trust signals that ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity pull when generating "best roofers Indianapolis" responses. Their blog content targets long-tail queries that AI engines scrape for sourcing. They are likely cited in generative results for broad Indianapolis roofing queries and storm damage/insurance claim contexts given how extensively their insurance claim assistance process is documented across review platforms. Raptor's 5.0 rating at 673 reviews is a stronger quality signal, but Cochran's volume and manufacturer credentials currently give them the AEO edge. Closing that gap requires schema-marked FAQ content, manufacturer credential pages, and press placements — none of which require ad spend.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Raw review volume (1,187 vs. 673), multi-location physical footprint, dual manufacturer certifications (Owens Corning Platinum + GAF), Top 100 national ranking, and a 15-year domain with hyperlocal SEO pages covering neighborhood-level queries Raptor has not yet captured.

Where Raptor beats them: 5.0 star rating vs. 4.7 (the difference matters at the premium price point), no corporate parent diluting the local accountability narrative, and zero credible evidence of incentivized review solicitation — which is a live liability for Cochran.

Most exploitable weakness:

The documented practice of crews asking customers for reviews in exchange for "stars" and a bonus

is a structural integrity problem that Raptor's owner-response model and authentic review velocity directly counters in any head-to-head sales conversation.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Neighborhood-level landing pages. Cochran has built out pages targeting specific Indianapolis neighborhoods (Castleton, Rocky Ripple, Williams Creek, Wynnedale, etc.) with service-specific URL structures. These are low-cost AEO and local SEO assets. Raptor should build equivalent pages for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Greenwood with schema markup before Cochran's domain age advantage compounds further.

  2. Dual manufacturer certification as a content pillar.

Their Owens Corning Platinum and GAF Certified Contractor credentials

are cited across every major directory profile and generate backlinks from manufacturer websites. If Raptor holds or can obtain comparable certifications, those credential pages need to be live, linked, and schema-marked — they are direct inputs into how generative AI ranks contractor recommendations.

  1. Commercial estimator expansion as a lead. The active Commercial Estimator hire signals Cochran is moving budget and capacity toward commercial work. That creates a potential gap in their residential pipeline responsiveness — specifically in the suburban markets (Carmel, Fishers, Westfield) where Raptor competes on premium positioning. Cameron should monitor review cadence in those zip codes; a volume dip from Cochran in the next 60–90 days would confirm the commercial pivot is pulling attention away from residential.
