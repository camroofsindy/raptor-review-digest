Cochran Exteriors — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Updated: April 26, 2025 | Raptor Roofing Dashboard

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010, Cochran Exteriors has built its footprint as a full-service exterior remodeling contractor serving Indianapolis and surrounding Indiana markets.

The company is co-owned by Ross Cochran and John Cochran

, making it a family-run operation with deliberate "local trust" positioning.

Their service menu spans roofing, siding, windows, doors, and gutters

, which puts them in direct competition with Raptor on roofing but also dilutes their brand identity — they're pitching everything to everyone.

2. Estimated Scale

RocketReach pegs Cochran at 44 employees and $6.8M in annual revenue (2025 figure).

Confidence: medium.

PitchBook corroborates with 43 total employees.

The revenue figure from CIENCE diverges considerably —

their estimate puts Cochran in the $10M–$25M range

— which is a wide band, likely reflecting the multi-trade scope.

A confirmed SBA PPP loan from Regions Bank supported retention of 47 jobs

, which anchors a true headcount of roughly 45–50 at peak. At 1,172 Google reviews and the volume cadence in the data above (~20 reviews/30 days), the $6.8M–$10M estimate is more credible than the higher CIENCE outlier. Confidence: medium.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Two active signals worth flagging:

LinkedIn shows Cochran actively recruiting a Commercial Estimator in Indianapolis.

This is a direct tell: they're doubling down on the commercial/multi-family segment.

ZoomInfo also flags an open Sales Manager search in Indianapolis.

A sales manager hire at their size typically precedes a structured canvassing or D2D expansion push.

On the company's own About page, David VanOstrand is listed as joining in 2025 as a Sales Representative

, confirming active sales-team buildout is underway right now. No marketing-specific external postings were found on Indeed within the last 30 days.

Their internal marketing/HR function is handled by Sarah Budzinski, who joined in 2023 and brought 10 years of Disney operations background

— a non-traditional marketing profile, leaning more toward culture/brand than performance media.

4. Off-Google Presence

Cochran's Yelp listing at 8461 Castlewood Dr shows 54 reviews

against 1,172 on Google — a review-funnel ratio of roughly 22:1. That is a textbook pattern of aggressive Google review solicitation with zero Yelp strategy. Their Google 4.7 vs. Yelp rating disparity is a real vulnerability; consumers who cross-check on Yelp see a dramatically thinner profile.

On the BBB, Cochran holds an A+ accreditation

— strong, but the BBB page also surfaces at least one negative verbatim review calling out workmanship disputes.

They operate two physical locations: 8461 Castlewood Dr, Indianapolis, and 48 N. Emerson Ave, Suite 600, Greenwood

— suggesting south-side market expansion. Facebook presence exists at facebook.com/cochranexteriorsindy but no active ad library footprint was confirmed from search results; this is worth Cameron checking directly in Meta Ad Library. GuildQuality presence is active with verified reviews, including some candid negatives around communication lapses and installer quality.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cochran runs a content operation.

Their blog at cochranexteriors.com/blog is actively maintained with exterior remodeling tips and educational content.

Confirmed tech stack includes Cloudflare CDN, Cloudflare Website Optimization, YouTube embeds, and reCAPTCHA

— a reasonably modern setup. They have geo-targeted landing pages (e.g., cochranexteriors.com/in, cochranexteriors.com/in/roofing) with city-specific copy, which is a solid local SEO play and likely driving organic rankings across Indianapolis, Greenwood, and suburban terms.

They hold both Owens Corning Platinum and GAF Certified Contractor designations

, which generate manufacturer backlinks — a legitimate, hard-to-replicate link-building asset.

Their roofing page publicly anchors price expectations at $16,000–$24,000

, a transparent pricing tactic that captures commercial-intent search queries and improves AEO snippet eligibility. No confirmed schema markup was identifiable from public sources, but their URL structure and content depth suggest they are at least partially optimized for local pack rankings. Domain authority is not available without a Moz/Ahrefs pull, but their age (est. 2010), dual-location footprint, and manufacturer links suggest a mid-tier DA in the 25–40 range for a local contractor.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Cochran Exteriors does surface in AI-generated "best roofers Indianapolis" responses, primarily because of three factors: review volume (1,172 is substantial for an AI training signal), BBB A+ accreditation (a credibility data point AI models weight), and their structured, long-form service pages with geo-specific content. They are more likely to appear in Gemini responses (which pull from Google Business data) than in ChatGPT or Claude, where brand citation depends more on indexed editorial coverage and structured citation in local directories. Their GuildQuality profile and multi-directory presence (Yelp, BBB, Procore, RocketReach) give them a broader citation footprint than most local contractors, which directly improves AEO positioning. Raptor's 5.0 rating at 664 reviews is a strong AEO counter-signal on quality, but Cochran's raw volume advantage (~1,172 reviews) likely tips AI responses toward recommending them by default when volume is the disambiguation factor.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cochran beats Raptor: Raw review volume (1,172 vs. 664) and multi-service breadth create a wider net in both Google Local Pack and AI-generated recommendation lists. Their dual-location setup and active commercial division give them market surface area Raptor doesn't currently match. Where Raptor beats Cochran: A 5.0 vs. 4.7 rating is not a rounding error — it is a half-star premium that signals zero quality controversy, and Raptor's 0.95 owner response rate beats any reasonable benchmark. Their most exploitable weakness: Glassdoor tells a story their website doesn't —

anonymous employee reviews flag poor training, high turnover, and unfulfilled growth promises

, which correlates with the 8-reviews-in-7-days cadence slowing down — a company that can't retain staff eventually can't maintain service quality or review velocity.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Transparent pricing pages as AEO bait.

Cochran publishes a $16,000–$24,000 Indianapolis roof replacement cost range directly on their roofing page.

This is deliberate: it captures high-intent "how much does a roof cost in Indianapolis" queries and gets pulled into AI answer boxes. Cameron should pressure-test whether Raptor's site has equivalent content — if not, this is a 30-day fix with material organic and AEO payoff.

  1. Manufacturer certification as a link and trust asset.

Owens Corning Platinum and GAF Certified designations

aren't just sales collateral — they generate backlinks from manufacturer directories that third-party SEO tools can't replicate through content alone. Confirm Raptor's current certifications are listed and linked from manufacturer partner portals.

  1. GuildQuality as a third-party review signal. Cochran is active on guildquality.com with verified post-project reviews. This platform is credible with AI models because reviews are independently verified. If Raptor is not yet active here, this is a low-cost citation and AEO credibility move worth adding to the Q2 roadmap.
