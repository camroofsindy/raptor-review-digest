Cochran Exteriors — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Refreshed: Tuesday, July 22, 2025

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010, Cochran Exteriors has built a residential and commercial exterior remodeling operation serving greater Indianapolis.

Cochran Exteriors is an Infinity Home Services brand out of Indiana

— meaning they operate under a private equity-backed roll-up platform that is actively scaling across multiple Midwest states, not an independent family shop.

Their service line spans roofing, siding, windows, doors, and gutters

, making them a full-envelope competitor rather than a roofing-only player.

2. Estimated Scale

RocketReach lists Cochran Exteriors at 44 employees and $6.8 million in annual revenue (2025 figure).

Confidence: medium — third-party data aggregator, not audited.

PitchBook independently lists 43 total employees

, which corroborates the headcount range. Confidence: medium.

BuildZoom shows 467 pulled building permits totaling $585,600 in permit value and notes Cochran ranks in the top 2% of licensed contractors in Indiana

— a useful proxy for job volume and geographic footprint. One contrarian note: with Infinity Home Services backing and an active commercial division, true system-wide revenue may run higher than the $6.8M figure; treat that as a floor.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Two active roles surfaced across LinkedIn and third-party job boards:

a Residential Sales Consultant posting in Indianapolis

and

a Commercial Estimator posting in Indianapolis.

The sales consultant role is the more telling of the two —

the job description frames Cochran as "building and growing our presence in Central Indiana" and specifically calls for candidates to drive growth in home exterior projects.

This is not maintenance hiring; it is a revenue-expansion signal.

A new Brand President joined in 2025, described as having "a wealth of experience in home services" with a focus on superior client experience.

Leadership transition at the top combined with a sales build-out is a telltale PE-playbook move worth watching closely.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp lists the Castlewood Dr. location with 54 reviews

— a thin count relative to 1,215 on Google. This is a textbook review-funneling gap; nearly all review volume is being driven to one platform.

BBB accreditation shows an A+ rating, with 15 years in business.

However, the BBB complaints file tells a different story:

at least one BBB reviewer flatly states "Avoid this company. They will weasel out of poor workmanship. Warranty isn't worth the paper it was written on."

A separate documented complaint details

a covered stove vent and roof leak following a replacement project, with the company failing to respond appropriately after multiple contact attempts.

Another Angi review surfaces a more systemic issue:

a homeowner reports crew members who did not speak English arriving unannounced, with one explicitly stating he was a private contractor — not a Cochran employee — hired to do the roof.

That is a subcontractor accountability exposure that runs directly counter to Raptor's positioning. No Facebook Ad Library data was confirmed in this pull; monitor that separately via facebook.com/ads/library.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cochran's domain (cochranexteriors.com) has dedicated geo-specific landing pages —

with content structured around a "hassle-free approach" guiding homeowners through "every stage of the exterior remodeling process"

across roofing, siding, windows, gutters, and doors. They publish a transparent pricing page with cost range data:

the average cost of a roof replacement in Indianapolis is framed as $16,000–$24,000 depending on size, complexity, and material.

That kind of pricing content earns featured snippet and AEO eligibility.

They hold both Owens Corning Platinum and GAF Certified Contractor status

— dual manufacturer certifications that support backlink acquisition from both OC and GAF supplier ecosystems. Their careers page, about page with named staff bios, and service-specific subpages (/in/roofing, /commercial) signal a reasonably mature site architecture. Weak point: their Yelp review gap and BBB complaint record create negative sentiment that can surface in brand-query SERPs and AI-generated summaries.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on publicly available AI answer behavior as of July 2025: Cochran Exteriors has meaningful generative engine visibility because of their review volume (1,215 Google reviews), manufacturer certifications, and structured site content. AI models frequently cite high-review-count contractors with dual certifications when answering queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" or "roof replacement Carmel Indiana." Their Infinity Home Services affiliation and Top 100 Roofing Contractor recognition (

Infinity Home Services landed at #8 on the Top 100 Roofing Contractor list

) likely amplifies brand mentions in training data. Raptor's counter-opportunity: Raptor's 5.0 star rating on 690 reviews is a stronger quality signal per-review than Cochran's 4.7 on 1,215 — but Raptor's lower raw volume means less AI citation frequency. Closing that volume gap is the single highest-leverage AEO move available.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cochran beats Raptor: Raw review volume (1,215 vs. 690) drives more AI citation frequency and more GBP trust signals; multi-trade scope (siding, windows, doors) means a higher revenue-per-customer ceiling; Infinity Home Services PE backing means access to capital, shared marketing infrastructure, and national recruiting pipelines.

Where Raptor beats Cochran: 5.0 stars vs. 4.7 is a meaningful delta at the decision point — nearly every major review platform surfaces star ratings in aggregate search results; Raptor's accountability positioning ("we don't outsource accountability") directly counters the documented Cochran pattern of subcontracted crews with no job superintendent on site; owner response rate of 0.6 at Cochran is near-zero engagement, versus Raptor's active owner presence.

Most exploitable weakness: Cochran's own BBB and Angi paper trail documents subcontracted labor showing up unannounced with no English-speaking superintendent — Raptor should make "every crew is ours, every job is owned" a paid media and Nextdoor message before Cochran's new Brand President tightens that up.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Transparent pricing pages.

Cochran publishes a specific cost range ($16,000–$24,000) for Indianapolis roof replacements and invites homeowners to use a free cost calculator.

This content earns featured snippets and AEO placements. Raptor should build a comparable pricing/cost page with Indy-specific data — it is one of the fastest paths to appearing in AI-generated answers for "how much does a roof replacement cost in Indianapolis."

  1. Dual manufacturer certification leverage.

Cochran holds both Owens Corning Platinum and GAF Certified Contractor status

and references these certifications across landing pages. These certifications generate authoritative backlinks from manufacturer directories. Cameron should audit which certifications Raptor currently holds and ensure each one has a live, indexed backlink pointing to the Raptor domain.

  1. Commercial estimator expansion. Cochran's active commercial estimator hire and

positioning as a leading commercial roofing company in Indianapolis specializing in steep-slope, low-slope, and flat roofing systems

signals deliberate commercial revenue diversification. If Raptor is not currently pursuing commercial, watch whether Cochran uses that margin to cross-subsidize residential pricing — that is a PE-playbook move that changes the competitive pricing environment within 12–18 months.
