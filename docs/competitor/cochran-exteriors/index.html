Cochran Exteriors — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Updated: May 19, 2025 | Raptor Roofing Dashboard

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010, Cochran Exteriors has built its reputation on exterior remodeling across Indiana.

Co-owners are Ross Cochran and John Cochran, with Andrew Beck serving as Marketing Manager.

They are a full-service exterior contractor covering roofing, siding, windows, doors, and gutters

— and critically,

on July 21, 2022, Cochran Exteriors was acquired by Infinity Home Services

, a private equity-backed roofing platform, which changes the competitive calculus significantly. They are no longer an independent operator — they are a roll-up asset with national capital behind them.

2. Estimated Scale

RocketReach pegs Cochran at $6.8 million in revenue and 44 employees

(confidence: medium — third-party data model, not audited).

PitchBook independently lists 43 total employees

, which corroborates the headcount.

Cience.com, however, estimates revenue in the $10M–$25M range

(confidence: low — likely inflated by the Infinity Home Services parent). The PPP loan signal is meaningful:

Cochran Exteriors received an SBA PPP loan for $1–2 million from Regions Bank, supporting the retention of 47 jobs

— suggesting the 40–50 headcount figure has been consistent for several years. Call it $8M–$12M in likely annual revenue given review velocity, multi-location ops, and commercial work.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

LinkedIn shows an active posting for a Commercial Estimator in Indianapolis

— a direct signal they are pushing harder into commercial work.

They have also recently posted for a Residential Sales Consultant in Indianapolis

. On the internal build side,

David VanOstrand joined in 2025 as a Sales Representative

, confirming active sales-floor expansion in the current calendar year. The commercial estimator hire is the most strategically relevant: it signals a deliberate move to grow the commercial segment, not just residential storm work. Indeed shows no live Cochran-specific postings for marketing or ops leadership at this time.

4. Off-Google Presence

On Yelp, Cochran has 54 reviews at their 8461 Castlewood Dr location

— against 1,187 Google reviews. That's a 22:1 Google-to-Yelp ratio, which is a textbook review-funneling pattern. They almost certainly hard-redirect satisfied customers to Google and let Yelp starve.

On BBB, Cochran Exteriors LLC holds an A+ rating and is BBB Accredited

.

They operate two locations: 8461 Castlewood Dr, Indianapolis and 48 N Emerson Ave, Ste 600, Greenwood

— meaning they have a south-side GBP presence that competes directly in Raptor's territory. No Facebook Ad Library data was recoverable from the search results, suggesting either minimal paid social spend or a dark/boosted post strategy without declared ad campaigns.

On Glassdoor, Indianapolis-based employees rate Cochran 3.7 out of 5 stars across 17 reviews

— mediocre for a company claiming premium positioning.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cochran's site architecture is notably aggressive on geo-targeted landing pages: they have built out distinct URL structures for /in/, /in/roofing, /in/roofing/commercial and comparable paths, signaling a deliberate city/service-page SEO strategy.

Their roofing page publishes specific price ranges — "the average cost of a roof replacement in Indianapolis ranges from $16,000 to $24,000"

— which is a direct answer-engine optimization play targeting cost-intent queries in AI Overviews and featured snippets.

They also offer a free Indiana roof cost calculator

, a conversion tool that doubles as a low-funnel SEO asset. They have a named Marketing Manager (Andrew Beck, per BBB) and a Disney-trained internal ops/marketing hire (Sarah Budzinski, since 2023), suggesting a more professionalized content operation than a typical indie roofer. No third-party backlink profile data was surfaced, but the multi-location GBP setup and commercial roofing content depth suggest domain authority likely in the 30–45 range for a local contractor.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Cochran does not appear to be cited by name in AI-generated answers for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville" based on current results. Their volume of reviews (1,187) gives them a strong entity signal, but their 4.7 rating and the Infinity Home Services acquisition create noise — AI models tend to weight consistency of brand identity and review quality, not just raw count. The price-range content on their site ($16,000–$24,000) is the one element most likely to pull them into AI Overviews for cost-intent queries. Raptor has a clear opening here: a purpose-built AEO content strategy targeting suburban Indy queries (Fishers, Zionsville, Noblesville, Greenwood) where Cochran's generic city pages have thin local depth.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cochran beats Raptor: Raw review volume (1,187 vs. 673) gives them more Google Map Pack authority and more AI entity weight. Their dual-location GBP setup covers north and south Indy simultaneously, and the Infinity Home Services PE backing means they have capital for paid media that an independent operator cannot easily match. Their multi-service model (windows, siding, doors) also drives cross-sell revenue per customer that a roofing-focused shop foregoes.

Where Raptor beats Cochran: A perfect 5.0 star rating against Cochran's 4.7 is a meaningful signal gap — in premium suburban neighborhoods, that delta gets noticed by high-intent buyers. Raptor's accountability positioning ("we don't outsource accountability") directly exploits the one structural weakness that a PE roll-up like Cochran will always carry.

Glassdoor reviewers note "terrible turnnaround rate because of the lack of training, organization, professionalism, and employee development"

— internally, this is a company with churn problems dressed in a polished exterior.

Most exploitable weakness: A PE-owned company with a 3.7 Glassdoor score and documented employee churn cannot credibly own an "accountability" or "family business" message — Raptor should own that positioning loudly and specifically.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Cost-intent landing pages with real price ranges. Cochran's published $16,000–$24,000 range on their roofing page is directly feeding AI Overviews. Cameron and Patrick should build equivalent pages for Raptor with defensible price anchors, schema markup (FAQPage + Service schema), and suburb-specific variants (Carmel, Fishers, Zionsville) to cut into Cochran's AI citation advantage before it compounds.

  2. Dual-location GBP strategy.

Cochran's second location at 48 N Emerson Ave, Greenwood

is a direct play for south Indianapolis Map Pack real estate. If Raptor has legitimate operational presence or a service hub in any suburban corridor, a second GBP profile is the single highest-leverage local SEO move available.

  1. Commercial estimator expansion as a demand signal. Cochran hiring a Commercial Estimator right now means they are actively upselling into apartment complexes, churches, and commercial strip work —

they recently highlighted commercial upgrades to an apartment complex in Greenfield, IN

as a featured project. Cameron should monitor whether Cochran is pulling commercial leads that previously went to smaller specialty shops, and evaluate whether a targeted commercial roofing content campaign (HOA roofing, property management roofing) makes sense for Raptor's pipeline mix.
