Competitive Intelligence Brief: Amos Exteriors

Raptor Roofing — Cameron Blakely, CMO Dashboard
Refreshed: Thursday, May 22, 2025

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1971, Amos Exteriors is a leading exterior remodeling company trusted by homeowners and businesses throughout Central Indiana.

Family-owned and operated, they serve both residential and commercial customers as a GAF Master Elite Roofing Contractor, and hold memberships in good standing with the BBB (A+), the NRCA, and the Indianapolis Apartment Association.

They now operate with locations in both Indianapolis, IN and Lafayette, IN

, making them a multi-market operator — a meaningful scale distinction from most Indy competitors. Their core differentiator is a full-service exterior platform: roofing, siding, windows, doors, gutters, masonry, and commercial painting all under one roof, with a heavy lean into insurance claims navigation.

2. Estimated Scale

RocketReach puts annual revenue at $28.6 million (2025 estimate) with 57 employees.

Prospeo independently estimates approximately 51–100 employees.

Confidence: medium. Revenue figures from third-party data aggregators carry noise, but the $12M–$29M range across multiple sources is plausible for a 50+ year old multi-trade operation with a 3,000+ review velocity.

A PPP loan of $584,200 from Stock Yards Bank resulted in the reported retention of 40 jobs

, which provides a floor on their W-2 headcount.

Known leadership includes a Director of Sales, a VP of Construction (Todd Danbom), and a Marketing Director (Charles Crowley).

3. Recent Hiring Signals

ZipRecruiter shows an open Team Lead, Field Marketing role in Indianapolis.

Their own careers page is actively recruiting Home Improvement Sales Consultants, trained to "out-sell the competition," running both company-provided leads and self-generated referrals.

ZoomInfo also flags an open Estimator & Project Manager role in Indianapolis.

The Field Marketing Team Lead posting is the one to watch — it signals they are expanding or restructuring canvassing operations, which is their dominant top-of-funnel model. A marketing specialist role was also recently active per Glassdoor. No C-suite or digital marketing director-level hires detected in the last 30 days.

4. Off-Google Presence

Amos Roofing & Exteriors holds only 2.5 stars on Yelp

against their 4.9 on Google — a significant platform disparity that is a textbook review-funneling signal. They are clearly driving customers to Google and not managing Yelp proactively.

Facebook shows 1,383 likes with only 23 people "talking about this"

— minimal organic engagement for a company of their scale, suggesting Facebook is largely dormant. No active Facebook Ad Library presence was confirmed.

BBB-accredited since September 1993

with an A+ rating and active recent reviews.

On Angi, they carry a 4.5 overall rating

— solid but not perfect. GuildQuality shows verified reviews including some negative feedback around siding quality and communication, concentrated in 2017–2018 but visible in search.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Amos Exteriors runs on WordPress with PHP, jQuery 1.11.1, and MySQL

— a legacy tech stack that likely limits Core Web Vitals performance and structured data execution. They have built out a deep location/service page architecture (e.g., amosexteriors.com/locations/indianapolis-in, amosexteriors.com/service-areas/indianapolis-roofing) which generates decent long-tail coverage across Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Greenwood. They are ranking for core Indy roofing queries on the strength of domain age (54 years) and review volume. However, the jQuery 1.11.1 version is outdated by roughly a decade, which is a schema implementation and page speed liability. No evidence of a robust backlink strategy from local press or Indy-area publications. Their blog exists but content cadence appears irregular. The GAF Master Elite designation earns them a high-authority inbound link from gaf.com which Raptor should match or counter.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on current AI engine behavior for prompts like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," and "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville": Amos Exteriors is likely to appear in AI-generated answers by virtue of their review volume (3,098 Google reviews), domain age, multi-location page structure, and GAF Master Elite certification — all of which are strong citation signals for LLMs pulling from structured web data. Their 54-year operating history and BBB A+ accreditation since 1993 further boost entity authority in knowledge graph indexing. Raptor, with 675 reviews and a 5.0 rating, is a thinner entity profile at this stage. The gap is not about quality — it is about entity depth and crawlable citation surface area. Raptor should be building AEO-optimized FAQ schema, press mentions, and third-party citation anchors (NRCA membership, GAF certification page, local news features) to close this AI visibility gap.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Amos beats Raptor: Raw review volume (3,098 vs. 675), domain age, multi-trade service breadth, Lafayette market expansion, and an in-house marketing department with named Director of Marketing and Marketing Manager on the BBB profile. Their insurance claims navigation operation is a well-oiled machine with dedicated reps named throughout reviews. Where Raptor beats Amos: Raptor holds a 5.0 vs. 4.9 — the cleanest rating in the Indy top 10 by volume — and runs a premium, accountable positioning that Amos cannot credibly claim given Glassdoor complaints about sales rep quality, canvassing no-shows, and a 2.5 Yelp rating. The most exploitable weakness:

Amos canvassers are reportedly setting appointments where "the homeowner either doesn't know you're coming, or doesn't understand why you're there"

— a trust and brand integrity gap Raptor can hammer directly in paid and social messaging.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Location-specific service page architecture.

Amos explicitly builds out named suburb service areas including Zionsville, Carmel, Noblesville, Westfield, Fishers, Geist, Plainfield, Avon, Greenwood, Franklin, and Anderson

with dedicated URLs. Raptor should audit whether it has comparable depth — each suburb page is an independent AEO and local SEO asset.

  1. Multi-trade upsell model.

Amos installs GAF asphalt shingles, slate, cedar shake, and flat systems as a GAF Master Elite Contractor with System Plus Warranty coverage

, and upsells into siding, gutters, windows, and masonry on the same job. Monitor whether their average ticket size is growing through multi-trade attachment — this is the revenue-per-customer lever Raptor should have an opinion on.

  1. GuildQuality third-party review pipeline. Amos pipes verified customer surveys through GuildQuality, which generates an independent review corpus that feeds AEO signals. Cameron should evaluate whether adding a GuildQuality or similar verified-survey layer gives Raptor an additional citation source that AI engines pull when surfacing contractor recommendations.
