🦅 Raptor Roofing — Competitor Intelligence Brief

Competitor: Bone Dry Roofing (Indianapolis HQ)
Brief Date: April 25, 2026 | Refreshes Tue/Thu

1. Who They Are

Bone Dry Roofing was founded in 1989 by Gene Judd, who started the company with just a truck and a garage office shortly after graduating from Indiana University.

Headquartered in Indianapolis, they've expanded to include offices in Ohio, Kentucky, Missouri, Tennessee, Colorado, and Florida.

Their growth has included mergers and acquisitions of aligned companies, with the Bone Dry Family of Companies now spanning eight states and 15+ locations, including G&W Roofing, Ravlin's Roofing, RCV Roofing, Roof One, and others

— making them a regional roofing roll-up, not just a contractor.

Bone Dry was named Roofing Contractor Magazine's 2023 Residential Roofing Contractor of the Year.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Structured referral partnership with a real estate network.

Bone Dry has held FC Tucker's "Tucker Top Tier Company" award 2009–2022

and is embedded in Tucker Home Services as a preferred vendor. Raptor should identify an equivalent Indy real estate brokerage (e.g., Carpenter, Highgarden, or a top-10 KW team) and formalize a referral arrangement — even 1–2 closings per month from agent referrals compounds fast.

  1. On-site production supervisor as a marketable differentiator.

Bone Dry keeps a production supervisor on-site for every project's duration, serving as the main homeowner point of contact.

They market this explicitly. Raptor should match and out-message it: if Cameron or the lead PM does this, build a campaign around it — "You'll meet the person whose name is on the warranty before we touch your roof."

  1. Post-job photo/video documentation as a review trigger.

Bone Dry provides detailed photos and videos during inspection and installation

— and customers mention this unprompted in reviews. This is a systematic review-velocity tactic: photo delivery via text → warm ask for review. Raptor should systematize this sequence (job-completion photo text → 24-hour follow-up review ask) and track conversion rate. It's why Bone Dry is generating 93 reviews/90 days; Raptor needs a parallel engine to close the 2,379-review gap.
