Refreshed: Tuesday, April 25, 2026 | Next update: Thursday, April 27

1. Who They Are

Richmond Exteriors is a family-owned business founded in 1987 by William F. Richmond II as Richmond Guttering.

Now operating as a second-generation, family-owned and -operated exterior remodeling company serving the area since 1987

, with

Jerrad Richmond serving as Sales & Marketing Manager

per their BBB profile. Their key differentiator is breadth:

with immense growth in residential contractor services, Richmond Exteriors has expanded its efforts to fulfill the residential needs of Metro Indianapolis & Central Indiana and to provide superior quality work to commercial clientele throughout the Midwest

— roofing is one product line among many (gutters, siding, fencing, windows, doors), which dilutes their roofing-specific brand identity and is their most exploitable structural weakness against a focused roofing specialist like Raptor.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Richmond's domain (richmondguttering.com) carries genuine age authority —

BBB-accredited since 1994

means the domain has been in market for 30+ years. Their on-page SEO strategy is keyword-dense with heavy geographic targeting:

service area pages span Avon, Brownsburg, Carmel, Fishers, Bloomington, Danville, Greenfield, Shelbyville, Speedway, Westfield, and more

. This is a classic location-page farm — effective for long-tail local queries, but the copy quality on many subpages is noticeably templated and thin (the Speedway roofing page, for example, reads as boilerplate with light localization). They run WordPress and use reCAPTCHA.

Their confirmed tech stack includes Facebook Pixel and Facebook Web Custom Audiences

but no evidence of advanced schema, structured FAQ markup, or an active blog publishing cadence. Their backlink profile benefits from longevity (Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB, BirdEye) but there are no visible local press mentions or Indianapolis Business Journal placements. Raptor opportunity: outflank them on freshness — a consistent blog with storm-season content, schema-marked FAQ pages, and IBJ/IndyStar earned media would differentiate Raptor in 2025 SERPs where E-E-A-T is weighted heavily.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Tested across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini using: "best roofers Indianapolis," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," and "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville."

Richmond Exteriors is not being cited by name in AI-generated answers for any of these queries. The AI responses for Indianapolis roofing queries tend to surface Bone Dry Roofing (highest brand-recall signal in training data), Indy Rooftops, and generic advice to check Google Maps and the BBB. Richmond's age and volume of online mentions should theoretically help, but their brand name split ("Richmond Guttering" vs. "Richmond Exteriors") creates entity confusion for LLMs — the domain is richmondguttering.com but the business operates as Richmond Exteriors. This brand fragmentation is likely suppressing their AEO visibility. This is Raptor's clearest AEO opportunity: a single, consistent brand entity name + structured data (LocalBusiness schema with sameAs markup, FAQ schema on key pages, and press mentions that use the exact brand name "Raptor Roofing") will train LLMs to cite Raptor ahead of fragmented competitors.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Richmond beats Raptor:

38 years in business with BBB accreditation since 1994

gives them deep institutional credibility and a multi-decade referral base. Their multi-trade offering (gutters, fencing, siding, windows) enables upsell revenue streams Raptor doesn't currently touch. Their service area geography is expansive, covering markets from Bloomington to Lafayette.

Where Raptor beats Richmond: Raptor holds 664 reviews at a perfect 5.0 — the highest star rating by volume in the Indy top 10 — versus Richmond's 610 at 4.5, with only 3 new reviews in the past 7 days (a near-stalled cadence). Raptor's premium "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is sharper and more defensible than Richmond's generic multi-trade messaging. Richmond's Angi rating of 4.2, Yelp presence of only 23 reviews, and a BBB complaints file with documented legal action all create trust-gap ammunition.

Most exploitable weakness: Richmond's brand-name fragmentation (richmondguttering.com vs. "Richmond Exteriors") and multi-trade dilution make them invisible in AI-generated answers — Raptor can own the AEO/GEO layer before Richmond even recognizes the channel exists.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Commercial estimator push.

Richmond is actively hiring a Commercial Project Estimator

. If they're building a commercial revenue line, they may pull sales resources from residential. Cameron should track whether their residential review cadence drops further — 3 reviews in 7 days is already thin — as a signal that sales attention is shifting. Raptor can double down on residential urgency messaging and storm-season advertising during this window.

  1. Location page architecture.

Richmond's service area page structure covers 20+ Indianapolis-area cities

. The strategy is sound even if execution is thin. Raptor should build high-quality, original-content location pages for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Greenwood — not boilerplate — with real project photos, neighborhood-specific storm history data, and genuine E-E-A-T signals. Beat them on content quality, not just coverage.

  1. Leverage their financing hook against them.

Richmond promotes 12 months no payments/no interest on roof replacement

as a homepage offer. This is a table-stakes move in this market. If Raptor doesn't have an equally prominent financing CTA, this is a conversion gap. More importantly, consider promoting a better term (e.g., 18-month same-as-cash) or wrapping financing messaging in premium language — "we make the best roof on the market accessible" — rather than leading with price. Don't compete on discount; compete on confidence.
