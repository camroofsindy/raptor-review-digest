Richmond Exteriors — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing Dashboard | Refreshed: April 26, 2025

1. Who They Are

Richmond Exteriors is a second-generation, family-owned and operated exterior remodeling company serving the Indianapolis area since 1987.

Jerrad Richmond holds the title of Sales and Marketing Manager

, signaling active family involvement in growth strategy.

With growth in residential remodeling services, Richmond has expanded to fulfill the residential needs of the Indianapolis area and Central Indiana, as well as commercial clientele throughout the Midwest.

Their core differentiator is breadth — roofing is one of at least six exterior trade lines including siding, windows, gutters, fencing, and awnings — which makes them a general exterior contractor as much as a roofing company.

2. Estimated Scale

Richmond Guttering (the operating entity) received an SBA PPP loan of $686,499, resulting in the retention of 65 jobs.

Confidence: medium — PPP data is 2020-vintage but is the most reliable third-party headcount anchor available.

ContactOut lists 30 employees

, likely reflecting current office/admin staff only, not field crews. Splitting the difference, a working estimate of 40–65 total employees is reasonable. At mid-market exterior contractor revenue benchmarks of $150K–$200K per employee, rough annual revenue sits in the $6M–$10M range. Confidence: low — no verified revenue figure is publicly available.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on current AI query behavior for terms like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," and "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville": Richmond Exteriors has minimal generative engine presence. Their domain name confusion (richmondguttering.com for a roofing query) actively works against them in AI-cited results, where LLMs favor topically consistent, well-structured domains. 7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Richmond beats Raptor: Longevity and name recognition — 38 years in market with a BBB A+ seal and multi-trade bundling capability (gut replacement + roof + fence in one contract) lowers friction for project-heavy homeowners. Their financing offer of

payments of $89/month or 12 months of no payments and no interest

gives them a close tool Raptor should have a ready counter for.

Where Raptor beats Richmond: Review quality and recency. Raptor's 5.0 at 664 reviews versus Richmond's 4.5 at 610 is not a close race in the mind of a homeowner doing a side-by-side comparison — it's a disqualifier. Richmond's off-Google platforms are a liability; Raptor's are not. Richmond has no discernible brand positioning beyond tenure.

Most exploitable weakness: The HomeAdvisor "no warranties" flag combined with documented BBB legal disputes creates a direct, verifiable accountability gap that Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning was built to exploit.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Commercial division build-out. Richmond is actively hiring commercial estimators and has a dedicated commercial GM. If they land institutional or property management roofing contracts, they can smooth seasonal revenue variance in ways Raptor currently cannot. Cameron should assess whether a targeted commercial lane — even just property managers and small HOAs — is worth piloting.

  2. Financing as a close tool. Richmond is front-paging 12-month no-interest financing prominently across their site. In a high-rate environment, this is a conversion lever, not just a nice-to-have. If Raptor's financing offer isn't as prominent or as aggressive in the sales process, that gap costs closes.

  3. Multi-trade upsell structure.

Richmond's own testimonials reference customers who came for gutters and then asked for a window quote.

Their breadth of trades creates natural upsell opportunities within the same customer relationship. Raptor should evaluate whether a strategic partnership with a gutter or siding subcontractor — presented under Raptor's brand and accountability standard — captures the same wallet share without diluting core roofing quality.
