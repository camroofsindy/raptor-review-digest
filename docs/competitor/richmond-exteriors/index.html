1. Who They Are

Richmond Exteriors is a second-generation, family-owned and operated exterior remodeling company that has served the Indianapolis area since 1987.

Jerrad Richmond is listed as Sales & Marketing Manager on the BBB profile

, suggesting the family holds both ownership and marketing leadership.

Their service mix spans roofing, fencing, gutters, windows, siding, and more

— a true multi-trade exterior contractor, which dilutes their roofing-specific brand authority but broadens their revenue base and referral surface area.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Richmond Exteriors operates under the domain richmondguttering.com — a legacy domain name rooted in their guttering origin, which creates immediate brand confusion against their positioning as a full exterior remodeler. Their site runs on WordPress with a clearly paginated service architecture (dedicated URLs for /roofing/, /roofing/repair/, /commercial/roofing-systems), which is structurally sound. Content is high-keyword-density but thin — the roofing pages read as keyword-stuffed circa 2019, with no visible bylines, no cited data, and no original blog content published recently.

Their stack includes ClickCease, indicating active paid search spend with click-fraud protection in place

, meaning they are running Google Ads competitively. No evidence of local press backlinks, schema markup beyond basic business NAP, or AEO-optimized FAQ content was found. Their domain authority is almost certainly in the 20–30 range — competitive enough to rank for mid-tail "Indianapolis roofer" terms but not a structural threat to a well-optimized competitor on high-intent terms like "roof replacement Carmel Indiana."

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Richmond Exteriors does not appear to be cited by name in AI-generated answers for key Indy roofing queries ("best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana"). Raptor's 5.0 / 677-review profile is already a stronger citation signal than anything Richmond Exteriors has built for generative engines.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Richmond beats Raptor: Longevity and name recognition —

Richmond Exteriors has maintained a presence in the Greater Indianapolis market since 1987

, giving them deep brand recall with older homeowners and a large installed base for repeat/referral work. Their multi-trade scope (gutters, fencing, siding, windows) gives them more touchpoints per household than a roofing-focused operator.

Where Raptor beats Richmond: Review quality and integrity — Raptor's 5.0 / 677 vs. Richmond's 4.5 / 616 is a meaningful gap on the metric buyers check first. More importantly, Richmond's off-Google collapse (2.9 Angi, 2.5 YP, BBB complaints) creates a credibility ceiling that Raptor's consistent quality cannot be undercut by.

Most exploitable weakness: Richmond's 0.6 owner response rate on Google combined with a 2.9 Angi rating signals a customer service accountability gap that Raptor can weaponize directly in sales conversations and paid ad copy.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Financing front-and-center:

Richmond promotes 12 months no payments/no interest on roof replacements and $89/month payment options prominently on their homepage.

This is a high-converting offer for mid-market homeowners. If Raptor isn't matching this with an equally prominent financing CTA in paid ads and on the estimate page, revenue is being left on the table.

  1. Multi-trade upsell surface: Richmond's gutter + roofing + siding + fencing bundling gives them a higher average ticket per customer visit. Cameron should evaluate whether adding a vetted gutter or siding referral partner creates a similar cross-sell moment without compromising Raptor's focused brand.

  2. Exploit the Angi/off-Google gap in paid search: Richmond's 2.9 Angi rating is a live target. A Google Ads or LSA strategy that intercepts homeowners searching "Richmond Exteriors reviews" or comparison terms and redirects to Raptor's 5.0 profile is low-cost, high-intent, and directly attacks their weakest flank. Add a landing page showing the side-by-side review comparison across platforms — it writes itself.
