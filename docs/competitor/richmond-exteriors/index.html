Richmond Exteriors was founded in 1987 by William F. Richmond II, originally operating as "Richmond Guttering" with just one full-time employee.

Their service suite spans roofing, fencing, gutters, windows, siding, and more

— making them a multi-trade exterior contractor, not a roofing-focused specialist like Raptor. Their key differentiator claim is longevity and "family-built" trust, not precision or premium positioning.
