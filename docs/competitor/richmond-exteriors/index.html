Raptor should develop a partnership or referral relationship with a guttering or siding specialist to capture that second-visit revenue without diluting focus — or at minimum, build co-marketing content around "roof + gutter" bundled inspections.

38-year BBB accreditation

as a trust anchor in their sales process. Raptor should audit whether its own BBB profile is fully optimized and whether Patrick's sales team is actively referencing third-party credentialing in estimates — especially with older homeowners who still weight BBB heavily.

  1. Location landing page architecture. Richmond has built out discrete pages for Indianapolis-area suburb queries (Carmel, Greenwood, etc.) that capture long-tail organic traffic. Raptor's SEO team should audit whether comparable suburb-specific pages exist and are substantive enough to rank — and if not, prioritize Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Westfield given Raptor's northern-market positioning.
