Competitive Intelligence Brief — Richmond Exteriors

Refreshed: Tuesday, July 1, 2025 | Prepared for: Cameron Blakely, Raptor Roofing

1. Who They Are

Richmond Guttering Company, now operating as Richmond Exteriors, is a family-owned business that has been serving the Indianapolis area since 1987, originally focused on gutters before expanding into roofing, siding, fencing, and windows for both residential and commercial clients.

The BBB lists Jerrad Richmond as Sales & Marketing Manager

, suggesting the founding family is still deeply embedded in day-to-day operations. Their web domain remains richmondguttering.com, a legacy URL that dilutes their roofing brand identity and creates ongoing SEO ambiguity they have never fully resolved.

2. Estimated Scale

Richmond Guttering received an SBA PPP loan of $686,499 from Centier Bank, retaining 65 jobs at the time of the loan.

That is the highest-confidence headcount data point available.

ContactOut lists 30 employees

, likely reflecting only office/admin staff visible on LinkedIn — the 65-job PPP figure is a more realistic total including field crews. Confidence: medium. On revenue, a 65-person exterior remodeling operation in a mid-size market with a multi-trade service menu points to an estimated $8M–$14M annually; no public revenue figure is available. Confidence: low.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

ZoomInfo notes that Richmond Guttering has been expanding and recently hired new employees

, but no specific open roles surfaced on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter in a targeted search within the last 30 days.

Steve Walker is listed on LinkedIn as General Manager of Richmond Exteriors' Commercial Division

, indicating a deliberate push into commercial work that warrants watching. No marketing, sales leadership, or ops director postings detected at time of this brief.

4. Off-Google Presence

Richmond Exteriors shows 23 Yelp reviews at their 3510 S Keystone Ave address.

That is a meaningful red flag: 619 Google reviews versus 23 Yelp reviews is a classic review-funneling signature — they are aggressively pushing satisfied customers to Google and largely ignoring or suppressing the Yelp channel.

The BBB profile includes at least one reviewer who states they took legal action against Richmond and received a $65K settlement for damages caused.

Richmond Exteriors holds a BBB A+ rating and has been BBB Accredited since September 19, 1994.

No active Facebook Ad Library campaigns surfaced. LinkedIn presence is thin —

only 176 followers on LinkedIn

— indicating minimal B2B or talent brand investment.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Richmond Exteriors operates on WordPress (

confirmed via ZoomInfo tech stack, which shows WordPress.org among their active technologies

) and their domain is richmondguttering.com — a structural SEO liability for anyone searching "roofing" rather than "guttering." Their site does have service-specific landing pages (roofing, siding, repair, etc.) and appears to target transactional terms like "roof repair Indianapolis" and "roof replacement Indianapolis" with dedicated URLs. However, the root domain name mismatch means they are almost certainly leaking domain authority on pure roofing queries to competitors with cleaner root domains.

They deploy ClickCease, suggesting they are running Google Ads and actively trying to block click fraud

— a signal of meaningful paid search investment. No evidence of a blog content engine producing fresh, indexable content was found. AEO schema markup was not confirmed.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Richmond Exteriors does not surface prominently in generative AI results for key Indy roofing queries such as "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville," or "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana." AI engines like ChatGPT and Gemini tend to cite contractors with strong, content-rich domains, consistent NAP signals, and recent press mentions — Richmond's legacy domain name ("guttering"), thin blog content, and low LinkedIn presence all reduce their AEO footprint. This is an area where Raptor has a clear lane: structured content, FAQ schema, and local citation consistency built explicitly for AI retrieval will outpace a competitor still running on a 1987 brand name in their URL.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Longevity narrative (1987 founding) and multi-trade breadth — gutters, fencing, siding, windows, and roofing under one roof gives them a strong repeat-customer and upsell surface that a roofing-specialist firm cannot match on volume alone. Their 619 Google reviews give them respectable social proof at-a-glance.

Where Raptor beats them: Raptor's 681 reviews at a clean 5.0 star rating versus Richmond's 4.5 on 619 reviews is a decisive trust signal in a side-by-side GBP comparison. Raptor's owner response rate of 1.0 (100%) versus Richmond's pattern of minimal engagement is a visible accountability gap any consumer can see in under 10 seconds. The "we don't outsource accountability" positioning cuts directly against a company that carries a BBB complaint involving a $65K legal settlement.

Most exploitable weakness: Richmond's legacy domain (richmondguttering.com) creates permanent brand confusion between their guttering roots and their roofing ambitions — Cameron should target "roof replacement Indianapolis" and "Indianapolis roofing company" with content that explicitly contrasts a roofing-specialist identity against generalist exterior contractors.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Commercial division build-out.

Richmond has a named General Manager running a dedicated Commercial Division

— a structured move that creates recurring contract revenue outside storm-cycle dependency. Cameron should evaluate whether a defined commercial sales lane is the right next org build for Raptor.

  1. Multi-trade upsell sequencing.

Richmond has grown from gutters into roofing, siding, fencing, and windows

and uses each trade as a re-entry point with existing customers. Raptor should audit whether post-job gutter or attic insulation referral offers are being captured systematically or left on the table.

  1. ClickCease / click-fraud defense on paid search.

Richmond is running ClickCease

, meaning they are spending enough on Google Ads to justify fraud protection. If Raptor is not already running click-fraud filtering on its paid campaigns, Richmond's presence in the same auctions is a direct cost-per-lead inflator worth addressing immediately.
