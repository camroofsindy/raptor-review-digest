Richmond Exteriors — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Updated: July 22, 2025

1. Who They Are

Richmond Exteriors is a family-owned exterior remodeling company with more than 35 years of experience serving customers in the Greater Indianapolis area,

founded in 1987, offering roofing, fencing, gutters, windows, siding, and more.

Their primary domain is richmondguttering.com — a legacy brand signal that splits their identity between "Richmond Exteriors" and "Richmond Guttering," creating brand confusion across directories.

They serve both residential and commercial clients, covering siding, gutters, roofing, windows, doors, and fencing for commercial demands as well.

2. Estimated Scale

Richmond Guttering received an SBA PPP loan of $686,499 from Centier Bank, resulting in the retention of 65 jobs

— the most authoritative headcount signal available.

Indeed independently lists Richmond Exteriors at 51 to 200 employees.

Confidence: medium. The PPP data anchors a real-workforce figure near 65 field and office staff as of 2020; current headcount is likely in that same band given no major expansion signals. Rough annual revenue estimate: $8M–$14M based on PPP loan size, employee count, and multi-trade scope. Confidence: low — no public financials available.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Indeed currently lists 3 open jobs at Richmond Exteriors.

The Indeed company page and the careers page at richmondguttering.com/careers do not show specific role titles publicly without login.

Indeed rates Richmond Exteriors at 3.3 out of 5 stars

as an employer — a below-average score that signals potential retention problems. No marketing, sales director, or ops leadership postings were surfaced in the last 30 days on LinkedIn or ZipRecruiter. The open roles appear to be field/install level based on their careers page structure. No red-flag leadership hiring detected at this time.

4. Off-Google Presence

The cross-platform rating picture is damaging. Google shows 623 reviews at 4.5 stars — but that's where the story ends cleanly.

Richmond Exteriors is rated 2.9 out of 5 on Angi.

They are rated 2.5 stars on YellowPages.

Yelp carries 23 reviews with visible hostile sentiment, including a top Q&A response mocking the company's workmanship.

BBB lists them as A+ accredited

, but

at least one BBB review references a customer who took legal action and received a $65K settlement over damages caused during a siding project.

This is a textbook review-funneling operation: they drive happy customers to Google and let negative experiences accumulate everywhere else. Owner response rate of 0.9% on Google confirms near-zero engagement with reviewers. No Facebook Ad Library activity was confirmed in search results. Their Facebook handle appears to be facebook.com/IndianapolisExteriors.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Richmond Guttering uses ClickCease, Cloudflare Rocket Loader, WordPress, and reCAPTCHA

— indicating active paid search spend (ClickCease is a click-fraud defense tool, meaning they're running Google Ads) and a WordPress CMS that can support blog content. Their site at richmondguttering.com has geo-targeted service pages for roof repair, roof replacement, and storm damage, plus a dedicated /roofing subdirectory. The dual-domain situation (richmondguttering.com vs. richmondexteriors.com) almost certainly dilutes their domain authority — backlink equity is split. No evidence of local press backlinks, schema markup confirmation, or a structured blog program surfaced. Their service areas page exists but appears thin. AEO posture is weak: no structured FAQ schema was confirmed, and the brand name confusion between "Richmond Guttering" and "Richmond Exteriors" undermines consistent entity recognition in search engines. Raptor's tighter single-brand identity is a structural SEO advantage here.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on available search intelligence, Richmond Exteriors does not appear to be a consistently cited entity in AI-generated answers for Indianapolis roofing queries ("best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville"). Their brand fragmentation — operating under both "Richmond Guttering" and "Richmond Exteriors" with two separate domains — creates inconsistent entity signals that generative engines struggle to resolve confidently. Companies that dominate AI answers in home services typically have a single authoritative domain, consistent NAP data across directories, and structured content (FAQs, how-to content, schema). Richmond Exteriors fails on all three. This is a wide-open opportunity for Raptor to own AI answer surfaces by consolidating entity authority under one brand name with properly marked-up content.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Longevity narrative (39 years in market vs. Raptor's newer entry), broader multi-trade service offering (fencing, siding, windows, gutters alongside roofing) that enables higher average ticket and cross-sell, and a larger installed customer base for repeat/referral volume.

Where Raptor beats them: 5.0 vs. 4.5 Google rating with 690 reviews — higher trust signal by volume and perfection of score. Owner response rate of 0.9% at Richmond vs. Raptor's active engagement posture is a visible accountability gap any prospect researching both companies will notice immediately. Raptor's single-brand identity, premium positioning, and "we don't outsource accountability" message directly counters Richmond's documented history of workmanship complaints and legal disputes.

Most exploitable weakness: Richmond's off-Google reputation is quietly toxic — a 2.9 on Angi, 2.5 on YellowPages, and a $65K legal settlement cited on BBB — and they are doing nothing to address it.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. ClickCease deployment on paid search.

Richmond is running ClickCease

, meaning they're protecting Google Ads spend from click fraud. If Raptor is scaling paid search, this is a non-negotiable add — it directly improves ROAS by filtering invalid clicks. Evaluate ClickCease or a competitor like TrafficGuard before next campaign flight.

  1. Multi-trade cross-sell as a retention moat. Richmond's ability to offer gutters, fencing, siding, and windows after a roofing job creates repeat revenue streams Raptor currently leaves on the table. Even a light referral partnership with a trusted gutter or siding sub — co-branded and accountable — could increase customer LTV without adding operational complexity.

  2. Exploit the brand fragmentation gap in AEO now. Richmond's two-domain, two-name problem is a gift. Cameron should accelerate any structured content and schema buildout on raptor's primary domain — FAQ schema, local business schema, service-area schema — targeting the exact queries where Richmond's entity confusion leaves AI answers unresolved. The window to own Indianapolis roofing in ChatGPT and Gemini answers is open and will not stay open indefinitely.
