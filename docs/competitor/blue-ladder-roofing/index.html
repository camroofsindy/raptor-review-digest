Blue Ladder Roofing — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated April 26, 2025

1. Who They Are

Blue Ladder Roofing was founded December 31, 2019, and is owned and operated by Austin Chastain as a sole proprietorship headquartered in Fishers, IN.

The company provides expert roofing services across Indianapolis, St. Louis, and the Ozarks

, making them one of the few Indy-area residential roofers actively operating in multiple states.

They hold IKO Craftsman Premier contractor status and cover residential, commercial, and solar segments.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: low. No LinkedIn company page with a verifiable employee count was surfaced.

BuildZoom lists them at a Counselors Row address in Indianapolis with a BuildZoom score of 0

— meaning no verified permit pull history is on record in Indiana, which is a meaningful data gap for a company claiming significant volume. Based on 385 Google reviews accumulated since late 2019, a door-knocking/inspection-driven sales model, and multi-state expansion, a rough estimate is 10–25 field and sales staff generating $3M–$6M in annual revenue. Treat that estimate loosely until permit data or a LinkedIn headcount becomes verifiable.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Blue Ladder is actively posting outside sales roles — specifically a Zionsville territory listing — with all sales representatives structured as 1099 independent contractors.

A separate Greenwood outside sales listing is also live, again on a 1099 basis.

The pattern is clear: they are expanding their storm-chase sales canvassing footprint into Raptor's southern (Greenwood) and northwest (Zionsville) corridors using commission-only, non-employee reps. No marketing, ops leadership, or production management roles were found — consistent with a founder-led org that hasn't formalized its back office.

4. Off-Google Presence

Facebook shows 96% recommendation rate from only 14 reviews

against 2,847 page likes — a thin social proof layer relative to their Google review volume of 385. The page is active, posting weather-driven educational content (ice dam videos) within the last week.

BBB accreditation is in place since March 2022 with an A+ rating, but the entity is registered as a sole proprietorship

— structurally limiting on credibility for commercial bids.

Yelp presence is minimal — the listing is largely unclaimed in terms of engagement, with no review count visible

, indicating they are not funneling customers there. No Facebook Ad Library activity was confirmed in search results, but their consistent canvassing model and Facebook-sourced lead generation (customers citing finding them via Facebook posts) suggests organic social over paid. Owner response rate on Google: 0.0% per Cameron's supplied data — a hard stop worth exploiting.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Blue Ladder operates at least two active domains — blueladderroofs.com and blueladderroofingindy.com — which is a textbook domain authority dilution mistake.

The primary site has dedicated city landing pages (e.g., /fishers-in) built around local weather pain points

, which is a competent local SEO structure.

Their services page claims they have "rapidly grown to become a trusted leader in roofing and exterior solutions."

Schema markup and blog depth are not evident from surfaced content — the site reads as a lead-capture funnel, not an authority content play.

BuildZoom score of 0 means no permit history is indexed

, which is a structural weakness for any algorithm or AI model that uses permit data as a trust signal. No backlinks from local Indianapolis press were found. Domain authority is likely sub-20.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on available search data, Blue Ladder Roofing does not appear to have significant structured citation presence that would drive consistent AI engine mentions. Their split domain strategy, near-zero press backlinks, and lack of schema-rich content make it unlikely they are cited prominently in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini responses for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" or "roof replacement Carmel Indiana." Their IKO Craftsman Premier status and Roofing Insights affiliation (noted on their site) are the only third-party authority signals that could pull them into AI-generated answers. This is an open lane for Raptor: a company with 664 reviews at 5.0, press mentions, and structured schema has a materially stronger AEO foundation right now.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Blue Ladder beats Raptor: Multi-state expansion signals organizational ambition and gives them a larger total addressable market to grow into. Their 1099 canvassing army in Zionsville and Greenwood means they will touch doors Raptor may not be actively working. Their financing messaging ($97/month) is front-and-center on their homepage — a clear conversion hook for price-sensitive buyers that Raptor's premium positioning does not directly counter.

Where Raptor beats Blue Ladder: 664 reviews vs. 385. 5.0 stars vs. 4.8. Owner response rate that is not zero. No BuildZoom permit data on file and a 0.0% Google response rate are accountability gaps that Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" brand was built to exploit.

Most exploitable weakness: A 0.0% owner response rate on Google combined with zero reviews in the last 7 days signals an operation that has deprioritized reputation management — run direct contrast ads or Nextdoor posts that highlight Raptor's documented response behavior while Blue Ladder's silence is on public record.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Storm-triggered social content cadence. Blue Ladder is posting educational short-form video tied to real-time weather events (ice dams, hail) within days of the weather occurring. This is the right playbook. Raptor should systematize a weather-trigger content workflow — when NWS issues a Hamilton County severe weather watch, a Reels-format video should be live within 48 hours.

  2. City landing page architecture. Their /fishers-in, /springfield-mo, /st-louis-mo structure is the correct local SEO pattern. Raptor should audit whether it has equivalently optimized, schema-marked pages for Carmel, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Greenwood — each with unique content, not boilerplate swaps.

  3. IKO vs. GAF/OC contractor status as a differentiator.

Blue Ladder leads with IKO Craftsman Premier status

in every channel. Raptor should ensure its own manufacturer certification tier is equally prominent in GBP, homepage headers, and review request follow-up emails — manufacturer badges are one of the clearest trust signals AI engines pull when generating contractor recommendations.
