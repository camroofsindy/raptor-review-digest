Competitive Intelligence Brief: Blue Ladder Roofing

Refreshed: May 19, 2025 | Prepared for: Cameron Blakely, Raptor Roofing

1. Who They Are

Blue Ladder Roofing was founded December 31, 2019, is structured as a sole proprietorship, and is owned and operated by Austin Chastain out of Fishers, IN.

They provide expert roofing services across Indianapolis, St. Louis, and the Ozarks

, making them a multi-state operator, not purely a local Indy competitor.

They hold IKO Craftsman Premier status

and have built their market identity almost entirely around storm damage insurance claims as the primary conversion hook.

2. Estimated Scale

ZoomInfo lists Blue Ladder Roofing's corporate office at 11722 Allisonville Rd Ste 103, Fishers, and records 6 employees.

That figure almost certainly undercounts field crews and 1099 sales reps.

Indeed reports salary data estimated from 211 employees, users, and past and present job advertisements

— a sign of meaningful historical hiring volume but not necessarily current headcount. Confidence: low. Revenue is difficult to pin down without permit data; given 390 Google reviews accumulated since late 2019 and a multi-market footprint across Indiana and Missouri, a rough estimate of $3M–$6M annual revenue is plausible. Confidence: low.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

A LinkedIn job posting for an Outside Sales Specialist at Blue Ladder Roofing was listed approximately one month ago.

This is the only active posting surfaced. The Outside Sales Specialist role is consistent with their canvassing/door-knock model — they are hiring commission-based salespeople, not marketing or ops leadership. No marketing, project management, or operations director roles are currently posted. That absence suggests they are not building internal marketing infrastructure and remain reliant on field-driven lead generation.

4. Off-Google Presence

Blue Ladder's Facebook page shows 96% recommendation rate from 14 Facebook reviews

— a thin sample that is easy to manufacture. Yelp presence is nominal:

their Yelp listing exists under "Indianapolis" but shows no published star rating or substantive review count, and the "how do I make an appointment" FAQ has zero answers posted

— a dead page that nobody is managing.

BBB accreditation was granted March 4, 2022

, and

their current BBB rating is A+

. No Nextdoor business account was found in search results. The core discrepancy: 390 Google reviews vs. essentially zero managed presence on Yelp and Nextdoor. Classic review-funneling to Google only — a playbook that inflates GBP scores while leaving off-Google reputation entirely undefended.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Blue Ladder operates across at least three domains: blueladderroofs.com, blueladderroofingindy.com, and blueladderroofingin.com. Running parallel domains for the same brand is a split-authority problem that dilutes link equity across all three.

The Fishers location page on blueladderroofs.com does attempt geo-specific content, addressing local weather issues like humidity, mold, and drainage

— a reasonable local SEO tactic, but the copy is generic and templated, not authoritative.

BuildZoom shows a score of 0 with no verified permit history surfaced, and no BuildZoom reviews exist for the entity

— a gap that weakens their E-E-A-T signals. No evidence of local press backlinks or editorial coverage in Indiana media was found. Their IKO Craftsman Premier badge does earn them a listing on the IKO contractor finder, which is a minor but real backlink. Schema markup and AEO-specific FAQ/HowTo structured data were not confirmed present. Their multi-domain fragmentation is their single biggest organic liability.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

No confirmed citations of Blue Ladder Roofing by name were surfaced in AI-generated results for "best roofers Indianapolis," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." Their split-domain architecture, thin editorial backlink profile, and absence of authoritative local press coverage all work against AI citation. Generative engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) favor entities with consistent NAP signals, strong review velocity, and third-party editorial mentions — none of which Blue Ladder has cleanly consolidated. This is a live opportunity for Raptor to own AEO positioning in the Fishers/Carmel/Noblesville corridor before Blue Ladder gets their domain strategy sorted.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Blue Ladder beats Raptor: Door-to-door canvassing infrastructure and multi-state market reach give them volume lead generation that a premium-positioned competitor does not match. Their insurance claim framing —

"we handle the paperwork, work with your provider, and maximize your coverage"

— is a highly effective conversion message for storm-damaged homeowners who feel overwhelmed.

Where Raptor beats Blue Ladder: 673 reviews at a perfect 5.0 vs. 390 at 4.8 is a meaningful GBP signal gap. A 0.0% owner response rate on Blue Ladder's Google reviews is a glaring accountability deficit that Raptor's premium "we don't outsource accountability" positioning directly exploits. Raptor's review velocity (12 in 30 days) is competitive with Blue Ladder's (12 in 30 days), but Raptor's rating integrity is untouchable.

Most exploitable weakness: Blue Ladder's 0.0% owner response rate on Google, combined with their fragmented multi-domain SEO and zero Yelp/Nextdoor presence, makes them easy to outflank on the trust-and-transparency axis that AI engines and premium buyers increasingly weight.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Insurance claim positioning as a front-door message.

Blue Ladder leads with "insurance claim experts — guidance through the claim process to maximize coverage"

on every geo page. This is the right message for storm-season lead gen. Raptor can own the same frame with a credibility layer Blue Ladder cannot match: a named owner, a 5.0 rating, and a verifiable response record.

  1. Geo-specific landing pages. Blue Ladder has built out individual pages for Fishers, Indianapolis, Carmel, and other cities on blueladderroofs.com. The pages are thin, but the structure is right. Raptor should audit whether it has equivalent dedicated pages for Carmel, Noblesville, Zionsville, Greenwood, and Fishers, and ensure each one has schema markup and genuine local content — not just a city name swap.

  2. Facebook social monitoring.

At least one verified customer review notes that Blue Ladder's reps actively monitor Facebook community posts about roofing inspections and respond directly

— a low-cost social listening tactic that generates warm inbound leads. Cameron should evaluate whether Raptor has a system for monitoring Nextdoor and local Facebook group posts for storm damage or roof questions in the primary service zip codes.
