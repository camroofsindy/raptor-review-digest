Competitive Intelligence Brief: Blue Ladder Roofing

Updated: Tuesday, July 22, 2025 | Fishers, IN Market

1. Who They Are

Blue Ladder Roofing & Solar was founded December 31, 2019, is structured as a sole proprietorship, and is owned and operated by Austin Chastain.

The company provides roofing solutions for homes and businesses in Indiana, Central Missouri, and the New Ozarks

— a multi-state footprint that is notably aggressive for a six-year-old shop.

They position themselves as insurance claim specialists and licensed professionals

, making storm-restoration and insurance navigation their core go-to-market identity rather than premium craftsmanship.

2. Estimated Scale

ZoomInfo lists Blue Ladder Roofing at 6 employees at their Fishers headquarters.

Confidence: low — the ZoomInfo count almost certainly undercounts field reps and subcontractors. More useful:

their own Indeed job posting states 2025 revenue of $8 million in restoration projects, with a projection to reach $20 million-plus in 2026.

Confidence: medium — this is self-reported in a recruiting ad, so treat it as aspirational, but the $8M 2025 baseline is plausible for a storm-chaser with 400+ reviews. Annual revenue estimate: $6M–$9M at present run rate.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Blue Ladder is currently recruiting 1–2 outside sales reps on Indeed to close out storm restoration season, focused on handling insurance claim volume from recent hail and wind events.

A Glassdoor listing from January 2025 shows they also posted a Customer Success Manager role to support outside sales, covering lead intake, scheduling, follow-ups, and CRM management.

Glassdoor currently shows 2 open positions.

Pattern read: they are building a classic storm-chaser engine — outside canvassers who write claims, backstopped by a thin inside operations layer. No marketing, tech, or ops leadership hires detected.

4. Off-Google Presence

Blue Ladder holds a BBB A+ rating (Indiana entity) and has been BBB-accredited since March 4, 2022.

However, the BBB complaints file is where it gets interesting:

one filed complaint documented that Blue Ladder failed to install adequate flashing on a roof corner, causing post-installation leaks.

A separate BBB review reads:

"We have requested to stop being contacted by these salesman countless times over the last year. Never are they adhering our request."

That is a door-knock / aggressive canvassing complaint — a reputational liability their Google review page doesn't reflect.

Their Missouri BBB entity (Wentzville, MO) carries only a B rating with 4 complaints filed.

Yelp presence exists but shows only 15 photos and minimal review volume — classic review-funneling to Google, with essentially no Yelp footprint built. No Facebook Ad Library activity was confirmed in this pull. Facebook page is active with organic posts but no documented paid campaigns detected.
