Competitive Intelligence Brief: Kingdom Roofing Systems

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely | Refreshed: April 28, 2025

1. Who They Are

Founded in the spring of 2012 by Daniel Young and Ryan Martlage

, Kingdom Roofing Systems is a multi-location Central Indiana exterior remodeling contractor.

They operate three registered locations: Marion (HQ), Bloomington, and 6964 Corporate Dr, Indianapolis, IN 46278.

They hold GAF Master Elite status — an honor held by only 2% of roofing contractors nationwide — and are also an Owens Corning Platinum Preferred contractor

, giving them dual top-tier manufacturer credentialing that is a core pillar of their pitch.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: medium.

PitchBook lists Kingdom Roofing Systems at 40 total employees.

An older profile from Zionsville Monthly reported closer to 30, suggesting modest but real growth over time.

By their own account, Kingdom installs approximately 500 roofs and 700 total jobs annually — 40% commercial, 60% residential.

At an average residential ticket of $12,000–$15,000 and commercial work layered in, rough annual revenue likely falls in the $7M–$10M range. Confidence: low — no public financials exist, but job volume and headcount both point to this band.

A critical new data point:

Kingdom is now part of the Allstar Home Services family — described as "a fast-growing national network of leading exterior remodeling brands backed by Morgan Stanley Capital Partners."

This is a private equity backstop that changes the competitive calculus entirely. They now have institutional capital behind them.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Active recruiting is concentrated on the revenue side.

ZipRecruiter shows an open role for a "High-Income Sales Opportunity — Roofing and Exterior Remodeling Sales Representative" in Indianapolis, advertising uncapped commission, paid training, and pre-qualified leads.

A commercial sales representative role is also posted statewide with a $60K–$70K base plus up to 5% commission, alongside a commercial site supervisor opening.

Daniel Young's LinkedIn confirms they are also actively recruiting residential sales reps in Bloomington and Indianapolis.

No marketing, ops leadership, or digital roles spotted in the last 30 days — their hiring is entirely front-of-funnel sales-focused, which suggests they are trying to accelerate top-line revenue post-PE acquisition rather than build internal infrastructure.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp:

The Indianapolis location at 6964 Corporate Dr has 54 photos posted

but only 16 reviews — thin for a company this size. No active Q&A. The Marion location has 3 reviews. Minimal Yelp investment overall.

BBB:

BBB Accredited since July 8, 2016

, rated A+.

Four total complaints in the last 3 years, with 1 complaint closed in the last 12 months

— one of which involved a customer alleging harassment via repeated calls, emails, and door knocks after asking to be left alone. That complaint pattern is consistent with an aggressive storm-chasing sales culture.

Facebook:

The Facebook page lists 88% recommendation rate across 100 reviews

— meaningfully lower than their Google presentation.

Angi/HomeAdvisor: Mixed signals. Positive installation reviews sit alongside complaints about lawn damage, wrong nail sizing, and shingles coming loose the day of installation. At least one reviewer on Angi gave a 1-star account citing failed warranty follow-through and a $795 inspection fee demand. The gap between their 4.7 Google score and the rougher texture on Angi and BBB indicates review-funneling toward Google.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Kingdom has an established SEO history.

Their past agency (Paper Box SEO) documented that by optimizing their Google Business Profile and acquiring more reviews, Kingdom achieved a 219% increase in organic leads over one year.

They have dedicated location-specific service pages (e.g., kingdomroofingsystems.com/indianapolis-roof-replacement/) and a separate landing page domain at kingdomgreatdeals.com — a classic tactic to run conversion-optimized paid landing pages separately from the main domain.

They also have a branded mobile app for project tracking, warranty storage, and referral rewards

— a sticky post-sale retention tool that contributes to backlink and brand signal diversity. Blog content appears thin and not recently updated. Schema markup on service pages is present but AEO optimization (FAQ schema, conversational query targeting) is not evident. Their dual-manufacturer certification pages (GAF + Owens Corning) create authoritative content nodes that likely pull manufacturer backlink equity.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Kingdom Roofing Systems does surface in AI-generated responses for general Central Indiana roofing queries, primarily due to their volume of third-party citations across BBB, Angi, Owens Corning's contractor directory, and their Zionsville Monthly press feature. In tests for "best roofers Indianapolis" and "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," they are mentioned alongside other established local names, largely because their dual GAF Master Elite + Owens Corning Platinum Preferred credentials are machine-readable and frequently cited. They are not dominant in AI answers for suburban submarkets like Fishers, Noblesville, or Zionsville — those results skew toward competitors with more hyperlocal page content and recent review velocity. Their near-zero recent review activity (1 in 90 days) is a direct AI-visibility liability: generative engines weight recency of social proof signals.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Kingdom beats Raptor: Dual manufacturer elite status (GAF Master Elite + Owens Corning Platinum Preferred simultaneously) is a credential Raptor cannot currently match on both fronts, and Kingdom deploys it loudly. Their branded customer app and PE-backed scale give them operational infrastructure advantages for multi-location coverage. Their commercial division (40% of volume) gives them revenue diversification Raptor doesn't have.

Where Raptor beats Kingdom: 664 reviews at a true 5.0 vs. Kingdom's 304 at 4.7 — that's a meaningful trust gap, especially in AI-cited results. Kingdom's off-Google review profile is inconsistent (BBB complaints, Angi negatives, 88% Facebook rec rate) suggesting the 4.7 is their ceiling, not their floor. Kingdom's review velocity is essentially dead (1 review in 90 days) while Raptor's pipeline remains active.

Most exploitable weakness: Kingdom's aggressive door-to-door and follow-up sales culture is generating documented harassment complaints on BBB — Raptor should make "no-pressure, no-chase" a specific, named differentiator in ads and on the website while Kingdom's complaints are live and searchable.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The dual-domain paid landing page play. Kingdom runs kingdomgreatdeals.com as a stripped-down conversion page separate from their main domain — isolating paid traffic from organic and enabling aggressive A/B testing without touching core SEO. Cameron should evaluate whether Raptor's paid campaigns warrant a dedicated CRO-optimized landing domain or at minimum isolated landing pages with independent conversion tracking.

  2. The branded customer app as a referral engine.

Kingdom's app lets customers track projects, store warranties, and make referrals to earn rewards

— turning every completed job into a passive lead-gen node. Raptor doesn't need a custom app to replicate this; a well-structured referral program with text/email delivery and a reward mechanism (gift card, check, or Raptor-branded perk) would capture the same mechanic at a fraction of the build cost.

  1. Monitor their PE-backed expansion closely.

Kingdom is now inside the Allstar Home Services / Morgan Stanley Capital Partners portfolio

— meaning they have access to centralized marketing resources, brand playbooks, and acquisition capital. Watch for sudden jumps in their ad spend on Meta Ad Library, new submarket GBP listings (Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville), or a rebrand push. When PE money hits a regional home-services brand, the first 12 months usually bring a paid media surge. Raptor needs to be fortified in those suburban GBP rankings before that budget arrives.
