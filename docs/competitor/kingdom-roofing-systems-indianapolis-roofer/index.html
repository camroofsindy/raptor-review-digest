Competitive Intelligence Brief — Kingdom Roofing Systems

Refreshed: Tuesday, July 28, 2026 | Prepared for Cameron Blakely

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010 by Daniel Young — originally out of Marion, Indiana — with a second location later added in the Park 100 area of northwest Indianapolis.

Leadership consists of Daniel Young (President) and Kristi Crisp (COO).

Kingdom operates as an exterior remodeling and restoration contractor across Marion, Kokomo, and Indianapolis, specializing in roofing, windows, and siding.

Their market identity is built around dual manufacturer elite status —

GAF Master Elite Contractor, an honor held by only 2% of roofing contractors nationwide

— and a hard insurance-claim-assistance posture targeting storm-hit homeowners.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: Medium. Multiple data sources land in a similar range but diverge enough to flag.

Growjo estimates annual revenue of $15.7M and 60 employees.

PitchBook lists 40 total employees.

RocketReach puts headcount at 43.

Critically,

Growjo notes Kingdom's employee count shrank by -30% last year

— a meaningful contraction signal.

ZoomInfo flagged a planned office move in December 2025 affecting approximately 15 employees at the Indianapolis location.

Best estimate: 40–50 active employees, $10M–$16M revenue. The contraction warrants watching.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

ZoomInfo notes Kingdom has more open Customer Service/Support roles than at any point in the prior 12 months, and is actively seeking a Project Manager in both Indianapolis and Marion.

No marketing, growth, or ops-leadership postings were surfaced from Indeed or LinkedIn in the last 30 days. The pattern — PM hiring plus CS backfills — suggests they're rebuilding field capacity after the headcount contraction rather than investing in brand or lead-gen infrastructure. No CMO, marketing manager, or digital ad roles detected. That gap is telling.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Kingdom has invested meaningfully in SEO infrastructure.

Kingdom Roofing Systems is a confirmed client of Roofing Webmasters

, a niche roofing SEO agency.

Roofing Webmasters uses a proprietary tool called DataPins that tags completed jobs with photos, geo-coordinates, and schema markup to build location-specific content across service areas without separate landing pages.

This is a legitimate, above-average SEO play for a home services contractor. Their site runs on

WordPress with Cloudflare optimization.

They have dedicated city landing pages (kingdomroofingsystems.com/cities-served/) and a local press backlink from Carmel Monthly Magazine.

A prior SEO engagement produced a 219% increase in organic leads over one year.

Their title tag — "Indianapolis Roofer | Top 2% GAF | Kingdom Roofing" — is keyword-dense and credentialed. Schema and AEO posture are likely handled via the Roofing Webmasters/DataPins stack, which

explicitly targets AI-generated search results including AI Overviews and ChatGPT.

This is the most sophisticated SEO setup among Indy competitors reviewed to date.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Kingdom has structural advantages that make AI citation probable. They appear in a third-party "best of" roundup —

a 2026 rankings article from indyroofandrestoration.com calls Kingdom "a powerhouse of modern technology," noting their Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status and use of advanced software to justify insurance claims.

That type of editorial content — named, specific, published in 2026 — is exactly what LLMs pull from when generating local service recommendations. Combined with the Roofing Webmasters/DataPins stack explicitly built for AI Overview optimization, Kingdom is likely appearing in generative answers for insurance/storm-damage roofing queries in Indy. Their brand name is well-distributed across Yelp, BBB, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Directorii, and Google — which builds the entity footprint that AI engines use to corroborate citations. Raptor should assume Kingdom ranks in AI responses for "storm damage roof Indianapolis" and "roof replacement Indianapolis" and treat that as table stakes to displace.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Kingdom beats Raptor: SEO infrastructure is more developed — dedicated agency, DataPins geo-tagging, city pages, and a local press backlink. They also carry dual elite manufacturer status (both GAF Master Elite and Owens Corning Platinum Preferred), which is a credentialing one-two punch that Raptor should audit its own counter-narrative against. Their branded customer app adds a retention and referral layer Raptor does not currently have publicly.

Where Raptor beats Kingdom: 692 reviews vs. 305 — more than double the volume, and a clean 5.0 vs. 4.7. Zero reviews in the last 90 days (Kingdom) vs. Raptor's active velocity signals that Kingdom's review engine has stalled badly. Owner response rate of 0.9 (90%) from Cameron is far superior to Kingdom's near-dormant engagement on recent reviews. Raptor's positioning is cleaner and does not carry the legacy entity-name baggage (Three Amigos / Allstar Home Services LLC) that Kingdom does.

Most exploitable weakness: Kingdom's review velocity has flatlined — 0 in the last 30 days — which will erode their Google Map Pack ranking in coming months; Raptor should accelerate review acquisition now to widen the gap before the next storm cycle.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. DataPins / geo-tagged job content. Kingdom is using Roofing Webmasters' DataPins tool to auto-publish geo-stamped job completions with schema markup across their service footprint. This is a concrete, repeatable tactic for both local SEO and AI entity building. Cameron should evaluate whether Raptor's current SEO partner offers an equivalent capability or if DataPins can be licensed directly.

  2. Dual manufacturer elite status as a credential stacking story. Kingdom leads with both GAF Master Elite and Owens Corning Platinum Preferred in the same breath. If Raptor holds one or more manufacturer certifications, the brief needs to be built into every landing page title tag, ad headline, and AI-visible editorial asset — not buried in an "About" page.

  3. The branded customer app.

Kingdom's app provides project notifications, warranty storage, and a referral rewards program.

This is a low-cost retention and referral mechanic that generates ongoing touchpoints after the job closes. Cameron should assess whether a white-labeled equivalent (JobNimbus, Hatch, or a custom build) could be deployed to Raptor's existing customer base as a post-install referral driver before a competitor in the 5-star tier picks this up first.
