White Oak Construction -- Competitive Intelligence Brief

Updated: May 19, 2025 | Prepared for Cameron Blakely

1. Who They Are

White Oak Construction was incorporated in October 1986, with roots going back to a business start date of October 1, 1982, making them a 40-plus-year operator in the Indianapolis market.

Leadership per BBB: James "Sam" Luppino serves as President and David Wilson as Vice President.

They are a full-service exterior and interior remodeling company -- roofing is one product line among many, sitting alongside windows, siding, doors, gutters, kitchen/bath remodels, and new construction.

They operate from four Indiana locations: Indianapolis (HQ), Lafayette, Sellersburg, and Warsaw

, giving them a multi-market footprint that is meaningfully broader than a roofing-focused competitor.

2. Estimated Scale

LinkedIn shows 618 followers and the company self-reports a team of "over 150+ trained employees."

Confidence: medium -- this is a self-reported marketing figure.

Indeed salary data is estimated from 104 employees, past and present

, which loosely corroborates a workforce in the 75--150 active range. With four locations, diversified trades, and a high-volume canvassing sales model, a rough revenue estimate of $15M--$25M annually is plausible -- though roofing likely represents less than 30% of that total. Confidence: low -- no public financials available.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Indeed currently lists 5 open jobs at White Oak Construction.

Active postings visible on Indeed include an Event Services Associate ($16--$17/hr), a Survey Collection Specialist ($16--$17/hr), and a Sales Representative ($100,000+/year) all in Indianapolis.

Additional open roles include Surveyor positions in Warsaw and Sellersburg, and an Installers role (Windows, Siding and Gutters) in Sellersburg at $19.22--$22.41/hr.

The concentration of canvasser/surveyor/event associate postings confirms a door-to-door and event lead generation model as the primary top-of-funnel. No marketing manager, digital lead, or ops director postings are visible -- the hiring posture is purely field-level sales and labor.

4. Off-Google Presence

The cross-platform rating picture is damaging relative to their 4.2 Google profile.

Facebook shows 263 reviews with only 60% recommending them

-- a stark gap versus their Google volume of 1,043.

BBB complaint records include a customer reporting a two-hour in-home sales presentation that yielded a $92K roof quote, then dropped to $65K "if you agreed now" -- a classic high-pressure closer tactic that drew a sharp negative response.

BBB rates them A+ and they are BBB Accredited

, which they promote, but the complaint thread reveals recurring issues with wrong product orders, unfulfilled commitments, and slow resolution timelines. Yelp shows 75 reviews at whiteoakconstruction on Yelp without a visible star average surfaced, but the gap between Google scale and Yelp/Facebook sentiment strongly suggests they funnel satisfied customers to Google and let negative experiences fester elsewhere.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Their homepage targets "Indianapolis to Carmel and Plainfield" in the title tag and intro copy

, signaling basic geographic SEO intent.

The Indianapolis location page is optimized with "over 40 years" local trust language and targets exterior remodeling keywords broadly

, but the site architecture is designed to serve a multi-trade general contractor, not a roofing specialist. This is a structural SEO weakness -- they cannot own "Indianapolis roofing" queries the way a roofing-only brand can.

They hold a GAF Master Elite certification

, which earns them a listing on GAF's contractor finder -- a legitimate mid-funnel backlink source.

Their roofing page promotes CAT4 wind-rated systems with a 50-year warranty covering materials and labor

, which is a concrete product differentiator they are actively messaging. Blog content exists on whiteoakconstruction.com but is thin and keyword-scattered across multiple trades. No evidence of schema markup sophistication, local link building from Indy press, or AEO-specific FAQ content targeting voice or AI queries.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on current AI answer patterns for Indianapolis roofing queries ("best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville"), White Oak Construction does not consistently surface as a named recommendation in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini outputs. Their multi-trade identity likely works against them here -- generative engines tend to surface specialist roofing brands with concentrated review signals and tightly topical content. Their GAF Master Elite status is the one credential that could earn citation in AI answers if properly marked up and referenced in crawlable on-page content, but there is no evidence they are leveraging it that way. This is an open lane for Raptor: a roofing-only brand with a 5.0 rating and tighter topical authority is more likely to be cited in AI summaries if structured data and AEO-focused FAQ content is deployed.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Raw review volume (1,043 vs. 673), four-location geographic footprint, GAF Master Elite credential, a 50-year labor-and-materials warranty as a conversion tool, and an aggressive outbound canvassing machine that manufactures leads at scale. Where Raptor beats them: 5.0 vs. 4.2 star rating -- a meaningful gap at the premium buyer tier; single-trade specialization that reads as expertise vs. White Oak's generalist positioning; far cleaner cross-platform reputation (60% Facebook recommendation rate is a liability White Oak cannot quietly fix); and zero evidence of the high-pressure in-home sales tactics that are generating BBB complaints. Most exploitable weakness:

The documented pattern of $90K+ in-home quotes pressure-dropped to $65K on the spot

is a sales-culture red flag Cameron can neutralize with transparent, published pricing ranges and a "no-pressure" positioning message aimed directly at homeowners who have already been burned by this kind of close.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The 50-year labor-and-materials warranty as a headline offer.

White Oak promotes "CAT4 wind-rated" systems with "an unparalleled 50-year warranty that covers materials AND labor"

as a front-page conversion hook. This is strong consumer messaging. Raptor should audit its own warranty story and determine whether it can be presented with equal or sharper specificity on the homepage and in sales decks.

  1. GAF Master Elite as a trust anchor and AI citation trigger.

GAF entrusts Master Elite contractors to offer their strongest warranties including the Golden Pledge Limited Warranty

. This certification earns directory backlinks and manufacturer-page citations that feed domain authority and generative engine training data. If Raptor holds or can pursue comparable manufacturer certification, the structured deployment of that credential across the site is a direct counter-move.

  1. Multi-location event and canvassing signals worth monitoring. White Oak's posting volume for canvassers, surveyors, and event associates across Indianapolis, Warsaw, and Sellersburg suggests a coordinated seasonal lead-gen push.

Sales leads reportedly run inside a 105-mile radius territory of Indianapolis.

If White Oak is activating canvassers in Raptor's core zip codes this spring/summer, Cameron should have Patrick accelerate any pending Nextdoor or neighborhood-specific retargeting campaigns to counter the door-knock with a digital presence the same homeowner sees within 24 hours.
