Competitive Intelligence Brief: White Oak Construction

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated: April 28, 2026

1. Who They Are

White Oak Construction Corporation was founded October 1, 1982, incorporated in 1986, and is BBB-accredited since August 19, 1992.

Leadership is James (Sam) Luppino (President) and David Wilson (Vice President), with April Roberts as Office Manager.

They provide roofing services across Indianapolis, Lafayette, Sellersburg, and surrounding cities including Carmel, Fishers, Brownsburg, Noblesville, Zionsville, Terre Haute, Fort Wayne, and South Bend

— a geographic footprint far wider than a typical residential roofer. Their differentiation play is longevity plus product breadth:

they offer CAT4 wind-rated roofing systems with a 50-year warranty covering materials AND labor.

2. Estimated Scale

White Oak Construction's annual revenue was reported at $28.1 million in 2025

across multiple data aggregators. Confidence: medium — Datanyze, RocketReach, and NeverBounce all converge on this figure but none are audited financials.

Their LinkedIn self-description claims a team of 150+ trained employees

, though

Datanyze pegs the employee count at 89

. Confidence: low — the 150+ figure appears to be aspirational marketing copy; 89 is more consistent with the revenue estimate.

They operate four registered locations: Indianapolis HQ, Lafayette, Warsaw, and Sellersburg, IN.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

White Oak Construction currently has 5 open jobs on Indeed.

Glassdoor lists 7 open positions in Indianapolis specifically.

Specific role titles were not surfaced in public job listings at the time of this pull. The volume of simultaneous openings across Indiana locations is consistent with a sales-force-heavy model — typical for a company running in-home appointment canvassing. One internal Indeed review flags that

leads are inside a 105-mile radius territory of Indianapolis, but management over three locations is described as creating a toxic work environment, with ownership looking the other way.

That culture signal is a recruiting vulnerability Raptor can exploit.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp lists White Oak at 10967 E US Hwy 136, Indianapolis, with 75 reviews and 141 photos as of April 2026.

Their Yelp star rating is not confirmed positive — the page exists but the rating is not surfaced prominently, suggesting it underperforms their Google number. Their Google profile sits at 4.2 stars on 1,042 reviews — a meaningful gap vs. Raptor's 5.0. On BBB,

they hold an A+ accredited rating

, which they actively trade on. The BBB complaint thread, however, contains multiple damaging signals: one reviewer alleged a

$92K roof estimate that dropped to $65K on the spot when declined — a classic high-pressure sales tactic

, and another described

a same-day-only pricing ultimatum followed by delivery of the wrong product and a refund fight.

On HomeAdvisor/Angi,

they are rated 4.7 out of 5

— higher than Google, suggesting curated referral-platform management. No Facebook Ad Library data was accessible in this pull; monitor Meta Ad Library directly for White Oak.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

White Oak's site (whiteoakconstruction.com) targets a wide keyword set across roofing, windows, siding, gutters, doors, and interior remodeling.

They publish blog content — e.g., "8 Tips for Choosing the Best Roofing Company in Indianapolis" dated November 2025

— signaling an active content strategy targeting informational queries. Their GAF certified contractor profile (gaf.com listing) provides a quality backlink from a high-DA manufacturer domain. Their BAGI directory listing also provides a local citation. The spread across five-plus service categories dilutes topical authority for roofing specifically — a structural SEO weakness Raptor can exploit with tighter roofing-only content clusters. Schema markup and AEO optimization were not confirmed on their site.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

White Oak Construction's name and longevity claim ("43 years," "50-year warranty," multiple Indiana markets) give them plausible citation material for AI engines responding to broad Indy home improvement queries. However, their 4.2 Google rating and volume of publicly visible complaints create a credibility floor that limits how confidently AI systems will recommend them vs. cleaner-rated competitors. Raptor's 5.0 rating at 664 reviews is a stronger trust signal for AI citation in queries like "best roofer Indianapolis" or "top roofing contractor Carmel Indiana." Cameron and Patrick should ensure Raptor's GAF certification page, BAGI listing, and any local press coverage are live and indexable — those are the backlink types AI engines currently weight for home services citations.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where White Oak beats Raptor: Volume of Google reviews (1,042 vs. 664), multi-market geographic reach, 43 years of operational history, a 50-year labor-and-materials warranty product, and a broader service menu (windows, siding, doors, interiors) that enables cross-sell and repeat customer LTV. Their BBB A+ and GAF certification also provide institutional credibility signals.

Where Raptor beats White Oak: Star rating is not close — 5.0 vs. 4.2 is a full category difference in consumer perception and AI ranking signals. White Oak's BBB complaint thread documents confirmed high-pressure sales tactics and product delivery failures, both of which are radioactive in today's review-aware market. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is a direct counter to White Oak's documented execution gaps.

Most exploitable weakness: White Oak's in-home sales model generates revenue but also generates their worst reviews — the pressure-sell complaints are specific, credible, and publicly indexed, making a Raptor "no-pressure, transparent pricing" message a surgical competitive counter.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The 50-year labor-and-materials warranty.

White Oak markets a CAT4 wind-rated system with a 50-year warranty covering both materials and labor.

If Raptor's manufacturer partnerships allow a comparable warranty tier, this needs to be front-page messaging — it is a direct purchase trigger for homeowners comparing quotes.

  1. Multi-platform citation building. White Oak has active profiles on GAF, BBB, BAGI, Angi/HomeAdvisor, Houzz, and Yelp. The multi-platform citation density feeds both traditional SEO and generative engine visibility. Raptor should audit which of these directories have thin or missing profiles and fill them immediately.

  2. Canvassing/lead-gen territory model.

Reviewers note it is reasonable to earn low six figures and that the company sells a superior product within a 105-mile Indianapolis radius territory.

White Oak is running a structured canvassing operation. Raptor should track whether White Oak canvassers are working Raptor's core suburban zip codes (Carmel, Fishers, Zionsville, Greenwood) and be prepared with a rapid-response counter-offer strategy — a leave-behind piece, a door-hanger, or a "get a second opinion" campaign timed to storm events.
