Competitive Intelligence Brief: White Oak Construction

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed: Tue, July 1, 2025

1. Who They Are

White Oak Construction was founded in 1982 by Sam Luppino (President) and the late Jerry Sobota, and is headquartered at 10967 US Hwy 136, Indianapolis, IN 46234, with additional locations in Lafayette, Sellersburg, and Warsaw, Indiana.

They operate as a broad home improvement contractor covering roofing, gutters, siding, windows, doors, and interior remodeling, with over 43 years in the market.

Their distinction is scale and breadth — they are a full multi-trade contractor that uses roofing as a door-opener but upsells aggressively into windows, siding, and interior remodels; they are not a roofing-specialist firm.

2. Estimated Scale

A Towne Post Network profile corroborates 124 employees across Indianapolis, Lafayette, Sellersburg, and Warsaw.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Raw review volume (1,052 vs. 681) gives them more AI entity mass and GBP ranking weight on volume-sensitive queries. Four physical locations and a 43-year operating history signal institutional permanence to both search engines and homeowners doing due diligence. Their canvassing infrastructure means they are actively generating demand rather than waiting for inbound.

Where Raptor beats them: Raptor's 5.0 rating against White Oak's 4.2 is a 0.8-star delta that is decisive in AI ranking logic and in consumer psychology at the final comparison stage.

White Oak's complaints about high-pressure, same-day-only pricing are a documented trust gap

that Raptor can own with a transparent, pressure-free positioning message. White Oak is a generalist; Raptor is a roofing specialist — in a category where expertise signals matter, that is a legitimate differentiator.

Most exploitable weakness: Their 0.9 owner response rate on Google reviews — combined with a 4.2 star average and a documented BBB complaint pattern around sales tactics — is an open window for Raptor to run a direct contrast campaign on transparency and accountability.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The 50-year labor-and-materials warranty play.

White Oak's CAT4 wind-rated, 50-year warranty covering both materials and labor

is a powerful close at the kitchen table. If Raptor does not have a comparable warranty story front-and-center on the website and in sales collateral, this is the next thing to build. Homeowners comparing the two companies online will see this and it will move decisions.

White Oak explicitly calls out Carmel, Fishers, Brownsburg, Noblesville, Zionsville, and others on their roofing page.

Raptor should have standalone, schema-tagged location pages for each of these suburbs that are deeper and more specific than White Oak's thin multi-city coverage — this is a direct SEO and AEO gap to exploit.

  1. Monitor their canvassing territory expansion.

Their sales territory runs inside a 105-mile radius of Indianapolis

, which means they are knocking doors in Raptor's primary service area right now. If White Oak begins hiring canvassers aggressively on Indeed, that is a leading indicator of a neighborhood-level push into Raptor's best zip codes — watch the job postings weekly.
