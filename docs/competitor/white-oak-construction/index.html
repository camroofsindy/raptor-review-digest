White Oak Construction — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated August 13, 2026

1. Who They Are

White Oak Construction was founded October 1, 1982, incorporated October 22, 1986, and is BBB accredited since August 1992.

They operate out of Indianapolis with additional offices serving Lafayette and Evansville, Indiana, and Sellersburg, Kentucky.

They are a broad-scope home improvement contractor — roofing is one of several verticals alongside windows, siding, doors, gutters, and interior remodeling — which means roofing is not their singular focus, and their sales model is heavily canvasser-driven rather than referral- or reputation-first.

2. Estimated Scale

LinkedIn lists White Oak as a privately held company founded in 1982 with 51–200 employees, headquartered in Indianapolis.

NeverBounce's data intelligence profile pegs them at 89 employees and $28M revenue

, while

CIENCE estimates revenue in the $50M–$100M range.

The spread is wide. Given the multi-office footprint, the canvassing army, and 1,088 Google reviews accumulated over 43 years, a realistic working estimate is $15M–$30M revenue with 75–120 active employees — confidence: medium. The high-end data intelligence figures almost certainly conflate revenue across all White Oak-branded entities (White Oak Commercial Group, White Oak Window & Door, White Oak Remodels).

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Indeed currently shows 4 open positions at White Oak Construction.

Active postings visible on Indeed include Sales Representative roles in Indianapolis ($100K+) and Lafayette, plus Installers (Windows, Siding, Gutters) in Sellersburg.

A Sales Representative posting specifically targets their new Warsaw office, advertising $100K+ earnings with a structured training program.

No marketing, ops leadership, or digital roles are visible. The hiring pattern is classic door-to-door sales expansion: they are opening territory, not building infrastructure. That is a tell — growth is being funded by canvasser volume, not brand equity.

4. Off-Google Presence

On Yelp, White Oak Construction carries 79 reviews at their US-136 location.

The star rating is not confirmed in search results but the gap between 79 Yelp reviews and 1,088 Google reviews is significant — a 14:1 ratio that is a textbook review-funneling signature. Their owner response rate of 1.0 on Google (per Cameron's data) is essentially non-existent, which compounds the reputational gap.

On BBB, a filed complaint from late 2024 involves door-to-door salesmen who sold a retired woman gutter guards and a bathroom window, followed by a multi-month defective product saga involving three incorrect window deliveries.

BBB lists multiple DBA entities: White Oak Remodels, White Oak Commercial Group, and White Oak Window & Door

— brand fragmentation that dilutes review equity and creates confusion for consumers searching online. No Facebook Ad Library activity was confirmed in results, and no notable Nextdoor presence surfaced.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Their homepage targets the Indianapolis-to-Carmel and Plainfield corridor with language around roofing, siding, and windows.

They push a proprietary angle with CAT4 wind-rated roofing systems and a 50-year warranty covering both materials and labor

— this is their primary differentiator in web copy. Their blog exists (whiteoakconstruction.com) with posts on topics like fascia/soffit and roofing cost estimating, but content depth and publishing cadence appear thin. No confirmed schema markup, local press backlinks, or domain authority signals surfaced in results.

They have appeared on local media (FOX59's Indy Now) tied to a $10K giveaway promotion

, which is their clearest earned-media signal and a tactic worth noting. GAF Master Elite certification gives them a credentialed anchor page on gaf.com, which carries real domain authority for roofing-adjacent queries.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on available search data, White Oak Construction does not appear to be prominently cited in AI-generated answers for key Indy roofing queries ("best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville"). Their broad GC positioning — roofing sits alongside windows, doors, and kitchen remodels — works against them in AI citation engines, which tend to surface specialists. Their 4.2 Google rating at 1,088 reviews is also a structural liability: generative engines frequently weight star rating alongside volume, and a 4.2 from a broad-scope remodeler will consistently lose citation priority to a focused roofer with a higher rating. This is a confirmed open lane for Raptor.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Raw review volume (1,088 vs. 699), 43 years of brand age, multi-market office presence in Lafayette and Warsaw, GAF Master Elite certification with its own indexed page, and a structured in-home sales machine with canvassers that generates consistent pipeline at scale.

Where Raptor beats them: 5.0 vs. 4.2 star rating — an 0.8-point gap that is enormous in both local pack ranking algorithms and AI citation logic. Raptor's owner response rate and review recency (0 responses, 3 reviews in 90 days for White Oak) signals a company coasting on legacy volume.

Internal Indeed reviews flag management as "manipulative" and note "constant churn and turnover"

— a structural weakness Raptor can exploit in both recruiting and brand positioning.

Most exploitable weakness: Their 1.0 owner response rate and 3 reviews in 90 days on a 1,088-review profile signals a review acquisition machine that has gone cold — Raptor should be loudly competing on recency and responsiveness, not just total stars.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The FOX59/Indy Now media play.

White Oak has used local TV appearances — including a $10K giveaway announced on FOX59 — to generate brand impressions.

Raptor should pursue a paid or earned segment on a comparable local outlet. The giveaway mechanic is replicable and produces both press mentions (backlinks) and social content simultaneously.

  1. The 50-year labor + materials warranty positioning.

White Oak leads with a CAT4 wind-rated roofing system backed by a 50-year warranty covering both materials and labor

as a homepage hero message. If Raptor's GAF Golden Pledge or equivalent warranty can match or beat this, it needs to be the first line of every landing page and every AI answer — not buried in fine print.

  1. The Warsaw/multi-market expansion signal. White Oak is actively opening new sales territory in Warsaw. Raptor should map which northern Indiana suburbs are losing their incumbent provider to this kind of canvasser-driven generalist — those homeowners are primed to switch to a premium, referral-driven specialist when the canvasser experience disappoints.
