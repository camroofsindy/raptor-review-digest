1. Who They Are

West Shore Home was founded in 2006 by B.J. Werzyn, a 1999 Penn State graduate; the company began as a window and door replacement business operating from a single office in central Pennsylvania.

The Indianapolis branch opened in June 2022, serving homeowners across the metro region, and specializes in window, door, and bath remodeling with most projects completed in just one day.

Their core differentiator is that installation crews are full-time employees — not subcontractors — which they use to market consistency and accountability at every job site.
