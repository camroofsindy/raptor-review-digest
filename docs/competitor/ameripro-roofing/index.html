1. Who They Are

Proliance General Contractors, Inc. is owned by Thomas Self (Owner/CEO) and managed by Alan Perales.

Established in 2004, the Self family has been in general contracting and remodeling for a combined 50 years.

They operate out of three markets — Indianapolis, IN; Westmont, IL; and Brookfield, WI

— positioning themselves as a multi-state storm-restoration and insurance-claim contractor rather than a pure-play local roofer. Their distinguishing play is the dual-entity model:

Proliance Public Adjusting and Proliance General Contractors appear to be the same company — a combination that a BBB complainant flagged as potentially illegal under Indiana Code IC 27-1-27-2.

2. Estimated Scale

ZoomInfo lists Proliance's employee count at 11–50 company-wide, headquartered in Downers Grove, IL.

BuildZoom permit data shows their typical Indianapolis project size is approximately $12,221
