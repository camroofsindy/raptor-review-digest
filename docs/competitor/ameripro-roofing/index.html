AmeriPro Roofing — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Refreshed: Tuesday, April 29, 2025 | Location on File: 11711 N Pennsylvania St Suite 105, Carmel, IN 46032

1. Who They Are

AmeriPro Roofing was founded in 2000 in Downers Grove, IL, originally identifying a gap in storm restoration services in the Chicagoland market.

The company was built on the vision of CEO Mike Gray, who came from the restoration industry and saw the opportunity to serve homeowners navigating weather-damage claims.

It is a family-owned business with a 20-plus-year track record specializing in storm and natural disaster restoration of roofs, siding, and gutters, now operating a Carmel, IN office at the Pennsylvania Street address that serves the broader Indianapolis market.

2. Estimated Scale

Roofing Contractors Magazine ranked AmeriPro as the #1 largest residential roofer in the country and the #9 largest roofing company nationally, with over 500 employees claimed on their own site.

Third-party growth data from Growjo estimates enterprise-wide revenue at approximately $135.9M with 375 employees.

Confidence: medium — these are national figures; the Carmel branch is a satellite office in a suite, so the Indiana-specific headcount is a fraction of the total.

AmeriPro operates 21 offices across 14 states

, so Indiana is one of many markets competing internally for corporate attention and budget.
