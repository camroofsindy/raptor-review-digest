Cox Roofing — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Updated: July 21, 2025 | Prepared for Cameron Blakely
Raptor benchmark: 690 reviews / 5.0 stars
Cox current position: 521 reviews / 4.8 stars

1. Who They Are

Cox Roofing is a family-owned business serving Indiana and Colorado since 2013, operating as an Owens Corning Platinum Preferred contractor with an A+ BBB rating.

The company is owned and operated by Justin Cox, with Mishell Cox serving as Manager of Business Development.

The brand leans on the owner's background as a firefighter — "locally owned and operated by a Firefighter" — as a trust signal, and their service lines have expanded well beyond residential roofing to include commercial, multi-family, siding, gutters, painting, and drywall.

2. Estimated Scale

Cox Roofing currently has 22 open jobs listed on Indeed

, suggesting active scaling.

ZipRecruiter job listings describe Cox Roofing Systems as "one of the fastest-growing roofing companies in the country, recognized on the Inc. 500 list multiple years in a row."

Their most recent Inc. 5000 rank is No. 3,681 (2025) reflecting 103% three-year growth.

LinkedIn shows approximately 11–25 employees in the company profile, but active hiring across two states and a COO-level operations post suggests the true headcount — field crews plus office — is likely 30–60 across Indiana and Colorado. Confidence: medium. Revenue estimate based on Inc. 5000 placement and multi-state footprint: $5M–$12M annually. Confidence: low (no public filing found).

3. Recent Hiring Signals

As recently as this week, Cox posted a "Roofing Foreman" position on Facebook.

ZipRecruiter shows an active listing for a Field Marketing role at Cox Roofing

— that is the most important signal here. A field marketing hire at this stage typically means they are building a canvassing/door-knock operation or storm-chasing infrastructure.

Indeed also shows an active Sales Manager posting, with the role focused on directly impacting results and growing the business while embracing Cox's core values.

The combination of field marketing + sales manager postings simultaneously is a textbook storm-season surge-hiring pattern. Watch for a ramp-up in canvassing activity across Westfield, Fishers, and Noblesville this quarter.

4. Off-Google Presence

Cox's Facebook page sits at 2,873 likes with 21 currently talking about the page

— low engagement relative to their Google review volume, indicating Facebook is not an active community-building channel for them. No verified active Facebook Ad Library spend was confirmed in search results.

Their Yelp listing at 10837 Andrade Dr, Zionsville shows only 24 photos and no visible review volume

— a meaningful gap given their 521 Google reviews. This is a classic review-funneling pattern: strong Google numbers, thin Yelp presence.

BBB-accredited since April 14, 2017

with an A+ rating, which they lean on in marketing copy.

Houzz shows community involvement claims — Cox states they actively participate in and donate to local organizations — but their Houzz profile photo count and review volume are not competitive.

No meaningful Reddit presence was surfaced.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cox runs a structured multi-state domain at coxroofingsystems.com with dedicated state landing pages (/indiana/) and city-specific service area pages (/indiana/service-areas/indianapolis-in/), which is a solid local SEO architecture.

They are NRCA members and OSHA-certified

— credentials they use on service area pages to build E-E-A-T signals.

Starting in 2024, Cox began publishing educational blog articles on roofing, siding, and guttering topics

, which is a move toward content-driven AEO positioning. However, the content cadence appears sporadic rather than systematic.

Their team is HAAG-certified

, a credential that, if properly embedded in structured data, is a strong AEO signal for insurance-claim-related queries. Domain authority is unconfirmed without a paid Ahrefs/Semrush pull, but their multi-year domain, Inc. 5000 press mentions, and Owens Corning directory listing provide a meaningful backlink floor. No evidence of FAQPage or HowTo schema on primary service pages.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

No direct citation of Cox Roofing by name was returned in search results for Indianapolis-specific AI-answer queries ("best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville").

AI engines pull roofer recommendations from Google Business Profile data, manufacturer-certified-installer directories, third-party review aggregators, Reddit discussions, and structured data embedded on roofer websites

— Cox has most of those boxes partially checked but lacks the structured schema and content depth to reliably surface in generative answers.

When ChatGPT answers "best roofer in a specific town," the AI pulls simultaneously from a company's website, Google Business Profile, reviews on multiple platforms, and any other place the business gets mentioned online

— Cox's thin Yelp presence and absent Reddit footprint are active liabilities here. This is a net opportunity for Raptor, which has a higher review volume, a perfect rating, and — if schema and content are deployed now — can claim AI answer slots before Cox closes the gap.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cox beats Raptor: Multi-family and commercial service lines give them revenue diversification and a B2B sales motion Raptor does not currently have; their two-state footprint signals ambition that creates brand halo in the Indy market; and their Inc. 5000 placement provides third-party credibility that functions as a trust shortcut with commercial property managers.

Their stated COO-level ambition — to position Cox as "the top exterior restoration contractor in the nation by 2030"

— is not just bluster; it signals they will continue to out-invest on headcount and infrastructure.

Where Raptor beats Cox: 169 more Google reviews, a perfect 5.0 versus Cox's 4.8, and a review velocity that — at 10 reviews in 90 days for Cox versus zero in the last seven days — suggests their review engine has stalled or is being managed inconsistently. Cox's owner response rate of 1.0 (meaning 100% response) is strong, but the volume drop is the exploitable weakness.

Most exploitable weakness: Cox's review acquisition has gone flat (10 reviews in 90 days, zero in the last 7), which means any Raptor campaign that drives even a modest weekly review cadence will widen the gap on the most visible trust signal in local search within 60–90 days.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

1. The Field Marketing hire. Cox posting a Field Marketing role signals a canvassing buildout, likely targeting storm-affected neighborhoods. Cameron should geo-fence Cox canvassing zones with targeted Meta and Google Ads — intercept homeowners at the consideration stage before Cox can close. Raptor's 5.0 rating is the better close at the door.

2. The Inc. 5000 / third-party credibility play.

Cox's No. 3,681 Inc. 5000 ranking (2025) gives them a media-grade trust badge.

Raptor should pursue equivalent third-party validation — Indiana-specific business awards, BBB Torch Award, or NARI recognition — that Cameron can embed in sales decks, GBP, and schema markup to match Cox's earned-media credibility stack.

3. Multi-city service-area page architecture. Cox has built out dedicated landing pages for Indianapolis, Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Westfield under a clean URL structure. If Raptor's service-area pages are thinner or consolidated, this is a direct organic search gap to close — especially for Carmel and Fishers queries where Raptor can attach the 5.0 rating to a hyper-local page and overtake Cox in both traditional and AI-driven results.
