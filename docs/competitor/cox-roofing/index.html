Competitor Brief: Cox Roofing

coxroofingsystems.com | Refreshed: Tuesday, May 20, 2025

1. Who They Are

Cox Roofing is a family-owned business serving Indiana and Colorado since 2013.

Mishell Cox and her husband Justin are the driving forces behind the business — Mishell leading business development and customer service, Justin bringing hands-on construction expertise.

They hold Owens Corning Platinum Preferred contractor status, carry an A+ BBB rating, and their team is HAAG-certified

— credentials they lean on heavily in marketing. Their scope has expanded beyond residential roofing to include

siding, gutters, drywall, and painting

, positioning them as a full exterior restoration shop rather than a pure-play roofer.

Their stated COO goal is to become the top exterior restoration contractor in the nation by 2030.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: medium.

Inc. 5000 profiled Cox Residential at 11–50 employees.

That listing dates to 2022, and given their Colorado expansion and active multi-family/commercial division, current headcount is likely in the 30–60 range across both markets.

A LinkedIn post noted the Colorado team alone onboarded 10 new hires in a single week during a hail season push

, suggesting significant surge-hiring capacity. Rough annual revenue estimate: $5M–$12M based on Inc. 5000 growth trajectory and multi-state footprint. Confidence: low — no public financials available.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cox runs a dense location-page strategy at coxroofingsystems.com, with dedicated subpages for Indianapolis, service-specific emergency pages (hail damage, storm damage, tarping), and service-type pages (TPO, EPDM, multi-family).

Starting in 2024, they began publishing informational articles on roofing, siding, and guttering — a content cadence play intended to build topical authority.

Their site covers commercial, residential, and multi-family verticals with separate URL structures, which is architecturally sound for SEO. Schema markup status is not confirmed from surface-level search — their blog content appears thin in depth and does not appear to have earned notable third-party press backlinks.

They have a financing partnership with Momnt

, which gives them a conversion-layer differentiator on landing pages.

They prominently advertise Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status and A+ BBB rating

as trust signals in page headers — a smart on-page credentialing move Raptor should match or counter with its own certifications. Domain authority is estimated low-to-mid range for a regional contractor; no confirmed high-authority local press backlinks were found.

Based on available search data, Cox Roofing does not appear to be a named citation in AI-generated answers for primary Indy roofing queries ("best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville"). This is a market-wide gap: no Indianapolis residential roofer appears to have locked in consistent generative engine citations, which means the AEO window is still wide open for Raptor to own first.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cox beats Raptor: Credential display (OC Platinum Preferred, HAAG certification, A+ BBB prominently above the fold); multi-state footprint story that signals scale to insurance adjusters and property managers; financing integration (Momnt) on their site; and a broader exterior restoration scope (siding, painting, drywall) that enables upsell per job.

Where Raptor beats Cox: 673 reviews at a perfect 5.0 vs. Cox's 508 at 4.8 — Raptor has both higher volume and a cleaner rating, which dominates in map-pack trust signals and AI entity scoring. Raptor's review velocity (5 reviews in 7 days vs. Cox's pace) is stronger. Cox's BBB complaint record and Angi billing dispute create an accountability narrative Raptor can exploit directly.

Most exploitable weakness: Cox's post-job follow-through has cracked publicly — the BBB complaint documenting six broken callbacks and a five-week trim repair is the kind of operational failure that Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning was built to counter directly.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The "IVO" AI persona play on social.

Cox has deployed a named AI guide on Facebook positioned to educate homeowners on roofing decisions.

This is an early-mover content strategy for social AEO. Cameron should consider a Raptor-branded educational persona — grounded in the velociraptor brand identity — that publishes short-form authority content on Facebook and YouTube. It drives entity recognition in AI engines and differentiates from the generic contractor feed.

  1. Owens Corning Platinum Preferred as a front-of-site trust anchor. Cox leads with this credential sitewide. If Raptor holds or can pursue Platinum Preferred status, it should be equally prominent — not buried in an "About" page. In competitive Indy searches, that badge drives conversion at the estimate request stage.

  2. Multi-family and commercial as a parallel revenue lane.

Cox explicitly targets multi-family roofing across Indiana and Colorado

, giving them an insurance-driven B2B pipeline that smooths seasonal residential volatility. Cameron should assess whether Raptor's current capacity can support even a light commercial or HOA play — the margin profile and referral network effects make it worth scoping.
