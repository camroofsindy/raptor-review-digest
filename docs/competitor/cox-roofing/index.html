Competitive Intelligence Brief — Cox Roofing

Refreshed: April 26, 2026 | Prepared for Cameron Blakely

1. Who They Are

Cox Roofing is a family-owned business based out of Zionsville, Indiana, serving Indiana and Colorado since 2013.

Mishell and Justin Cox are the co-founders and driving forces behind the company — Mishell leads business development and customer experience, while Justin brings hands-on construction expertise.

The company has aggressively expanded its national footprint with offices in Florida, Colorado, Kentucky, and Wyoming, and is eyeing further expansion into Illinois with a stated goal of copy-pasting operations across the country.

2. Estimated Scale

Cox's LinkedIn company page lists service lines spanning roof replacement, repair, gutters, siding, exterior painting, masonry, and low-slope roofing.

Their LinkedIn company page shows approximately 11–50 employees for the Indiana entity, though the multi-state expansion complicates headcount. Confidence: low — LinkedIn undercounts field crews in home services by a wide margin.

The company has publicly stated it is on track to have more offices than any other roofing company in the nation

, suggesting an aggressive multi-market growth posture. With a multi-state presence and the volume of named account managers visible across review platforms, a realistic Indiana-market revenue estimate is $3M–$7M annually. Confidence: low — no permit data or ZoomInfo revenue figure confirmed.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Cox's LinkedIn company page has posted open calls specifically seeking candidates with management experience in roofing

, signaling they are building a middle-management layer consistent with franchise-style scaling.

Given their stated multi-state expansion — offices in Florida, Colorado, Kentucky, Wyoming — and sights on Illinois

, expect active recruiting for market managers and production supervisors at the state level. No specific Indianapolis-market job postings were live on Indeed or ZipRecruiter at time of this pull. The more telling signal:

their stated mission is to be the top exterior restoration contractor in the nation by 2030

, which suggests C-suite-level hiring pressure behind the scenes, not just field roles.

4. Off-Google Presence

Cox Roofing's Yelp listing is registered at 10837 Andrade Dr, Zionsville, IN 46077

, but the page shows minimal review traction — no visible star rating surfaced in search snippets, suggesting their Yelp presence is thin and uncultivated relative to their Google volume of 503 reviews. That is a classic review-funneling pattern: all effort flows to Google, Yelp is neglected.

Their Facebook page (Cox Roofing, Zionsville) shows 2,864 likes with 17 people actively talking about it

— low engagement for a company claiming national scale, and no evidence of a paid ad campaign from the Facebook Ad Library at time of search.

They have held BBB accreditation since April 14, 2017

, and

carry an A+ BBB rating and Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status

— two credibility markers they actively promote. No meaningful Nextdoor presence surfaced. Angi/HomeAdvisor profile exists with mixed older reviews.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cox operates under the domain coxroofingsystems.com, which has dedicated city/service-area landing pages —

their Indianapolis-specific page positions them as the local family-owned company serving Indiana since 2013

, a standard but competent local SEO play. They have structured service pages for residential, commercial, multi-family, and specific material types (TPO, EPDM, PVC, metal), which is a solid topical authority structure.

Starting in 2024 they began publishing roofing and siding articles

, so their blog content is relatively new with limited backlink age. No evidence of schema markup sophistication, press coverage from Indy Star or IBJ, or earned backlinks from local news surfaced. Their domain is competing on volume of location pages rather than authority depth — a crawlable structure but not an AEO-optimized one. Towne Post Network (townepost.com) ran a recent feature, which is their most visible earned media link.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on available search results, Cox Roofing does not appear as a named citation in standard AI-generated "best roofers Indianapolis" roundups. The bestroofersindianapolis.com directory — the type of structured, comparison-style page that AI engines like ChatGPT and Gemini frequently pull from for local contractor queries —

operates as an independent, unbiased directory sorted by Google review rating, review count, and years in business

. Cox's 4.8 rating at 503 reviews puts them in a competitive position on that ranking signal, but they are not confirmed as a named result in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini for Indy-area roofing queries based on current research. This is a meaningful gap given their national ambitions. Raptor has a stronger AEO opportunity here given its 5.0 rating and higher review volume relative to rating — the combination that AI engines weight heavily.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cox beats Raptor: Multi-state brand footprint and a clearly articulated national expansion story give them a pipeline narrative that attracts commercial and multi-family clients Raptor does not currently chase.

Their HAAG-certified inspectors

and

financing partnership with Momnt

are consumer-facing conversion tools Raptor should note. Their service breadth — painting, drywall, siding, masonry — positions them as a one-stop exterior contractor, which increases average job value.

Where Raptor beats Cox: 664 reviews at 5.0 stars versus Cox's 503 at 4.8 is not a small gap in AI and GBP ranking logic — a perfect rating with more volume is a dominant trust signal. Raptor's review velocity (and quality positioning) is structurally stronger. Cox's Yelp neglect and thin Facebook ad presence mean their off-Google reputation is porous.

Most exploitable weakness: Cox's review velocity has dropped to 7 reviews in the last 30 days — a rate that, compounded over a year, signals either a slowdown in job volume or a broken post-job review capture process. Either way, Raptor should be pressing hard on velocity right now.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Momnt financing integration.

Cox's partnership with Momnt for consumer financing

is a conversion-layer play that residential competitors increasingly use to close hesitant homeowners. Cameron should evaluate whether Raptor has a comparable financing offer in the sales script and if it is front-loaded in the estimate presentation, not buried.

  1. Dedicated city landing pages with service-line depth. Cox has built out Indianapolis, Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Westfield landing pages with service-specific sub-pages. That architecture captures long-tail queries ("TPO roofing Indianapolis," "metal roof Carmel Indiana") that Raptor should audit against its own site structure.

  2. Named-rep review capture. Multiple Cox reviews call out specific sales reps by name — Kyle, Matt Elias, Sam Forkin — which indicates a systemized post-job review prompt tied to the individual rep. This builds rep-level social proof and Google review density simultaneously. Raptor should evaluate whether its own review request flow is rep-attributed or generic, and shift to rep-specific if not already done.
