Competitor Intelligence Brief — The Heartland Builders

Refreshed: Thursday, April 25, 2025 | Analyst: CMO, Raptor Roofing

1. Who They Are

The Heartland Builders is a minority-owned construction company based in Indianapolis.

Founded in 2019, they were built on a promise to deliver the highest standard in product while providing the best customer experience possible.

Their stated service footprint covers Indianapolis, Carmel, Zionsville, Westfield, Noblesville, Fishers, McCordsville, and Greenwood

— directly overlapping Raptor's core markets. Distinctives include a GAF-certified materials program, BBB accreditation, and a broad scope that spans roofing, interior remodeling, commercial work, and siding — making them a general contractor competing on roofing terms, not a focused roofing-first brand.

2. Estimated Scale

As of September 2025, The Heartland Builders has approximately 11 employees across 2 continents, with key team members including a Director of Construction

— per LeadIQ. Confidence: medium (LeadIQ pulls from LinkedIn headcount signals, which often undercount field crews and 1099 subs). At 11 FTEs + likely subcontractor reliance —

they openly acknowledge subcontracting mechanical services

— estimated annual revenue is $1.5M–$3.5M. Confidence: low (no public financials; BuildZoom permit activity not deep enough to triangulate volume).

3. Recent Hiring Signals

No active job board postings found on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter for "The Heartland Builders Indianapolis" in the last 30 days. However, the most recent significant hire was an ops-side PM:

they welcomed Hilary Parks as their newest Project Manager, with LinkedIn noting she brings experience managing complex residential and commercial projects across Indiana.

Their careers page currently shows only a general "Construction Sales Rep" listing with no date stamp.

The listing calls for someone to identify, develop, nurture, and close construction sales

— suggesting they're still pushing sales capacity over operations depth. No marketing or digital/ops leadership hires visible. Flag: A PM hire without a corresponding marketing or demand-gen hire signals a company scaling production without a top-of-funnel plan.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Their tech stack includes NitroPack, WordPress, Google Cloud, React, Google Tag Manager, and Google Analytics

— a functional but agency-standard build. They have built out dedicated location pages targeting

Carmel, Zionsville, Westfield, Noblesville, Fishers, McCordsville, and Greenwood

— solid geographic keyword coverage. They publish case studies (

e.g., the Medical Mutts roof transformation in Indianapolis and a TPO commercial roof installation for D&E Printing in Brownsburg

), which is a legitimate AEO play — structured project content that AI engines can extract. Domain authority signal is mid-tier for a 6-year-old local site; no evidence of local press backlinks or editorial coverage. No FAQ schema or How-To schema visible in page source.

They do promote GAF Systems Plus Warranty and a 1-year workmanship warranty

prominently — trust content that does support AI extraction. Blog cadence appears sporadic.

What Raptor should counter: Their case study format is clean and crawlable. Raptor has the review volume to outcompete on trust signals, but needs more structured FAQ schema and city-specific project documentation to win AI extractions over them.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Tested across ChatGPT (GPT-4o web search), Claude, and Gemini for: "best roofers Indianapolis," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," and "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville."

Result: The Heartland Builders does not appear by name in any generative AI answer tested. The companies that do surface in AI responses for Indianapolis roofing queries are largely those cited in third-party round-up lists (e.g., Stay Dry's 2026 Top 10, Angi, HomeAdvisor).

Popular AI platforms are directly citing lists when providing answers about the best roofing companies in a city, pulling from results titled "10 best roofers in [city]."

The Heartland Builders' thin off-Google footprint (weak Yelp, dormant Facebook, no press mentions) means

AI platforms pulling from Google Business Profile, Yelp, and Facebook reviews — where roofing companies with reviews across all three platforms are 2.8x more likely to appear in AI search results

— will continue to exclude them. This is an opportunity for Raptor, not a threat.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Broader service scope (interior remodeling, commercial flat roofing, hardscaping) gives them cross-sell angles and a "one-stop" pitch to repeat customers. Their Synchrony financing integration (

0% interest financing

) is a sales conversion tool Raptor should evaluate. They also carry a top-1% BuildZoom score that savvy homeowners may discover.

Where Raptor beats them: Review volume (664 vs. 503), rating (5.0 vs. 4.8), review velocity (their 1 review in the last 7 days vs. their 14 in 90 days signals a stalled review engine), AI visibility, off-platform reputation consistency, and Raptor's singular roofing-first brand positioning.

Most exploitable weakness: Their Angi profile carries a 2.0-star rating — a ticking trust bomb that any comparison shopper or AI engine aggregating third-party signals will eventually surface against their Google 4.8.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Case study content with named commercial clients.

Their Medical Mutts and D&E Printing project case studies

are a smart AEO move — real project names and addresses that AI engines can index and cite. Raptor should build named, city-tagged project case studies at scale (10–15 per quarter) with before/after photo documentation.

  1. The "#YourForeverContractor" lifecycle positioning. Their LinkedIn branding pushes a repeat-customer identity —

BuiltByHeartland #YourForeverContractor

— which is a referral and retention play worth monitoring. If they build a loyalty/maintenance program around this, it competes directly with Raptor's premium homeowner segment.

  1. Minority-owned business certification as a commercial BD lever.

The Heartland Builders is a minority-owned, Indianapolis-based company

, which qualifies them for MBE procurement preferences on certain commercial and municipal contracts. This is a market segment Raptor currently has no stated play in — worth flagging for Cameron to evaluate whether a strategic partnership or subcontracting relationship in commercial could neutralize this advantage rather than compete against it.
