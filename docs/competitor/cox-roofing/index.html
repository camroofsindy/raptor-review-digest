Competitor Brief: Cox Roofing (Indianapolis)

Raptor Roofing Dashboard — Refreshed June 9, 2025

1. Who They Are

Cox Roofing is a family-owned business serving Indiana and Colorado since 2013.

Mishell Cox and her husband Justin are the co-owners, with Mishell leading business development and Justin bringing hands-on construction expertise — Justin also maintains a career as a full-time firefighter.

The company has expanded its national footprint with offices in Florida, Colorado, Kentucky, and Wyoming, and is eyeing further expansion into Illinois

, positioning itself as a multi-state exterior restoration brand, not just a local roofer.

They hold Owens Corning Platinum Preferred contractor status and an A+ BBB rating.

2. Estimated Scale

Cox Roofing Systems is described on ZipRecruiter as "one of the fastest-growing roofing companies in the country, recognized on the Inc. 500 list multiple years in a row."

Indeed currently lists 22 open jobs at Cox Roofing

, suggesting an active hiring posture consistent with a company in scale-up mode. Based on the multi-state footprint, Inc. 500 recognition, volume of open roles, and the COO's stated ambition to be the top exterior restoration contractor nationally by 2030, annual revenue is estimated at $5M–$15M across all locations, with the Indiana operation likely in the $2M–$5M range. Confidence: medium — no verified financials publicly available; ZoomInfo data for the Indiana entity is paywalled.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Cox Roofing has an active opening for a Field Marketing role

— a significant signal that they are investing in grassroots/canvassing-style demand generation, not just digital.

They are also recruiting a Sales Manager responsible for directly impacting results and growing the business while embracing Cox's core values.

A fourth-generation roofer was recently added to build out the commercial sales division

, confirming a deliberate commercial push. The volume of 22 open roles across trades, sales, and ops on Indeed alone indicates they are scaling headcount aggressively, particularly in field sales and production.

4. Off-Google Presence

On Yelp, Cox Roofing is listed under the name "Cox Residential Roofing Carmel IN" and was established in 2013

, but the listing has minimal review engagement — a notable gap versus their 514 Google reviews.

On BBB, they have been accredited since April 14, 2017

, with an A+ rating per their own website.

On Facebook, they have introduced a brand character called "IVO," described as "your roofing expert at Cox Roofing," used to deliver educational roofing tips and content

— a creative content marketing play worth monitoring. Their Yelp presence is thin relative to Google volume, which is a classic review-funneling pattern: they are clearly optimizing the Google review funnel and not pushing Yelp at all. No meaningful Reddit or Nextdoor presence was surfaced.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cox operates a dedicated service-area page architecture at coxroofingsystems.com — individual city pages for Indianapolis, Carmel, and surrounding suburbs — which is a solid local SEO structure.

Starting in 2024, they launched a blog content series sharing "articles providing various information and insights to the world of roofing, siding, and guttering"

— consistent publishing cadence is an authority signal.

Their tech stack includes Cloudflare Rocket Loader, WordPress, reCAPTCHA, and PHP

, which is a standard but unremarkable stack — no edge in Core Web Vitals. They have a dedicated reviews page (coxroofingsystems.com/reviews/) which may serve schema review aggregation, though schema implementation quality is unverified.

A Towne Post (local press) feature article from March 2026 covers their growth story

— that's a legitimate local backlink that feeds domain trust and AEO citation potential. Their Houzz profile also adds citation breadth across a high-DA platform.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Cox Roofing does not appear with significant, consistent named citations in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini responses for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville" as of this brief period. Their Inc. 500 recognition and multi-state press could seed AI training data going forward, but right now their generative engine footprint appears thin. The Towne Post editorial coverage from March 2026 and their structured city landing pages are their strongest AEO assets — but without robust schema markup confirmation, structured FAQ content, or named citations in local media at scale, they are not yet an AI-first competitor. This is an open window for Raptor to own the generative answers for high-intent Indy-area queries.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cox beats Raptor: Multi-state brand narrative and Inc. 500 credibility give them a prestige halo that resonates with growth-minded homeowners; they have a Field Marketing hire deploying canvassers on the ground; their city-page SEO architecture is more developed than most local competitors. Their financing partnership with Momnt also adds a conversion tool Raptor should audit.

Where Raptor beats Cox: 677 reviews at a perfect 5.0 vs. Cox's 514 at 4.8 — Raptor has both the volume lead and the cleaner rating. Cox's 1% owner response rate on Google is a dead giveaway that review management is largely automated or neglected post-receipt, signaling weak relationship marketing. Raptor's premium "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is a direct counter to a company expanding to five states with a copy-paste operational model.

Most exploitable weakness: Cox's near-zero owner response rate on 514 Google reviews is a trust gap that Raptor can actively weaponize in sales conversations and content — it tells the story of a company that stops caring once the check clears.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The "IVO" educational persona on Facebook. A named brand character that delivers roof education content is a smart way to build top-of-funnel engagement without it feeling like ads. Cameron should evaluate whether a Raptor-branded content persona (or just a consistent video series anchored to Cameron himself) could compete for that same attention share on Facebook and YouTube.

  2. City-specific landing page architecture. Cox has individual service-area pages built for Indianapolis, Carmel, and surrounding suburbs.

Their Houzz profile lists specific coverage for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Westfield, Zionsville, and more

— each of those markets is an AEO/SEO page opportunity. Raptor should audit whether its own geo-page architecture matches Cox's coverage depth or has gaps.

Cox has a financing partnership with Momnt that allows them to offer financing plans to customers

. If Raptor is not actively promoting a financing option at the point of estimate, this is a closing-rate leak — Cox is using it as a sales conversion tool, not just a backup option. Monitor how prominently they feature it in ads and on estimates.
