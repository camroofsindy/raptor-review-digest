Indy Rooftops LLC — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Refreshed: August 11, 2026 | Cameron Blakely, Raptor Roofing

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2022 by Mitchel Dotson and Cheryl Edens, Indy Rooftops was built essentially as a vehicle to employ Dotson's father, Kevin Dotson, who had been a roofing salesman at another local company for many years.

The business markets itself as woman-owned and traces its roots to Brownsburg, Indiana, leaning into a "hassle-free experience" brand promise.

Service scope extends well beyond roofing into siding replacement, gutter installation, window replacement, soffit/fascia repair, attic insulation, and commercial systems including EPDM, TPO, and metal.

2. Estimated Scale

ZoomInfo places Indy Rooftops at 11–50 employees, headquartered at 4254 N Raceway Rd, Indianapolis, IN 46234.

Confidence: low — ZoomInfo bands are notoriously broad for sub-50-person shops; field evidence (named reps across reviews: Mitch, Kevin, Zack, Jack, Carl, Max, William) suggests a real headcount closer to 10–18 people including a canvassing team. At a 544-review volume for a company only three years old, revenue is likely in the $3M–$6M range, probably storm-claim-driven. Revenue confidence: low.

BuildZoom scores them 93, ranking in the top 27% of 22,324 Indiana licensed contractors.

Notably,

according to the Indianapolis Department of Business and Neighborhood Services, the status of one of their licenses was at one point cancelled, though this information may have changed.

Worth a periodic recheck.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

No active job postings for Indy Rooftops were found on Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, or any job board as of this refresh. The most telling signal is the presence of a named canvassing team member on Facebook.

A post introduces William as part of their canvassing team, who is preparing to head off to college at the University of Cincinnati — meaning canvasser turnover is baked into their model.

No marketing, sales leadership, or ops hires are posting publicly. This is consistent with a founder-led operation where Mitchel Dotson handles sales and ops himself.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp lists Indy Rooftops at 4254 N Raceway Rd with 380 photos and 51 reviews as of June 2026

— 51 Yelp reviews against 544 Google reviews is a reasonable cross-platform ratio, suggesting no obvious review suppression or platform-funneling issue. Sentiment on Yelp reads positively.

Facebook shows 2,215 likes and 353 people "talking about this,"

which is strong organic Facebook engagement for a three-year-old residential contractor.

BBB lists Mitchel Dotson as Owner and Cheryl Edens as CEO; accreditation was granted January 27, 2023.

No BBB complaints surfaced in visible public data.

They tout Malarkey Premium Contractor, IKO Craftsman Premier Contractor, and BBB A-rating credentials

— all displayed prominently on the website. No Facebook Ad Library data returned in this search; canvassing appears to be their primary lead generation channel, not paid social.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

The website actively aggregates reviews across Google, Yelp, Angi, and BBB

, which is smart structured-content fodder for Local Pack signals.

They hold both a Malarkey Premium Contractor and IKO Craftsman Premier credential

, and both are named on the site — manufacturer backlinks are a legitimate DA booster that many local operators miss.

The site claims full licensing and insurance with language around adherence to "highest safety and quality standards,"

which is standard E-E-A-T filler but present. The domain is indyrooftops.com — no third-party DA data was retrievable in this search cycle. However, the site has location-specific subpages (e.g., /indianapolis/) indicating some geographic page strategy. No evidence of a blog, original editorial content, or press backlinks was found. AEO posture appears thin — no schema markup or FAQ schema is publicly documented.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

No search results in this cycle confirmed Indy Rooftops being specifically cited by name in ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude responses for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." Their lack of original editorial content (no blog, no local press mentions found) is the structural reason they likely do not rank in AI-generated answers. GBP volume and Yelp presence alone are insufficient for generative engine citation in 2025–2026 — LLMs weight authoritative third-party mentions and original content. This is an exploitable gap for Raptor.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Indy Rooftops beats Raptor: They have a three-year head start on storm-claim adjacency messaging and an active canvassing team that generates door-to-door pipeline — a volume play Raptor has not publicly committed to. Their "woman-owned" designation also opens certain municipal and contractor program eligibility doors.

Reviews explicitly highlight their insurance advocacy, with customers noting that Indy Rooftops spent hours on the phone with insurance companies to ensure full claim payouts

— this is a repeatable conversion message they use in sales.

Where Raptor beats them: 697 vs. 544 Google reviews, same 5.0 rating — Raptor is ahead in social proof volume on the platform that matters most. Owner response rate of 0.4 (Indy Rooftops) versus Raptor's engagement signals a founder who is scaling away from reputation management. The most exploitable weakness: their review velocity has flatlined (9 reviews in 90 days) while their canvassing team appears to be a revolving door of college-age part-timers, suggesting inconsistent lead flow and no systematic post-job review solicitation.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Manufacturer credential display as trust architecture.

Indy Rooftops surfaces their Malarkey Premium Contractor and IKO Craftsman Premier credentials

prominently on both the homepage and location subpages. Raptor should audit which manufacturer credentials it holds and ensure they appear above the fold on every location landing page — these credentials generate backlinks from manufacturer directories and carry E-E-A-T weight with AI engines.

  1. Insurance-claim advocacy as a conversion message. Across Angi, BBB, and Yelp, the single most recurring theme in Indy Rooftops reviews is that their reps personally manage the insurance adjuster relationship.

Their site formalizes this: "our roofing technicians meet adjusters on site, document the damage themselves, and stay with the claim through the final invoice."

If Raptor is doing this operationally, it is not being said loudly enough in marketing copy. Steal this framing.

  1. Geographic subpage strategy. Indy Rooftops has built out /indianapolis/ and likely additional suburb-specific pages. Cameron should pressure-test whether Raptor has equivalent, keyword-optimized pages for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Greenwood, and Zionsville — those pages are the infrastructure for both Local Pack and generative engine citations. Indy Rooftops is building the scaffold; Raptor needs to build it faster and with more editorial substance (blog content, neighborhood case studies) to leapfrog them in AEO.
