Competitor Intelligence Brief — Watergate Roofing

Refreshed: Tuesday, April 25, 2025 | Prepared for: Cameron Blakely, Raptor Roofing

1. Who They Are

Watergate Roofing has provided residential and commercial roofing services to the Indianapolis area since 2013, and is a proudly woman-owned Indiana business.

Owner Millie Hindes founded the company in 2013 and has been in the roofing business since 2007, falling in love with the industry as soon as she climbed her first ladder.

Serving Indianapolis since 2013, Watergate also provides roofing and home improvement services in Fishers, Zionsville, Plainfield, Bloomington, and all nearby communities.

Their differentiator is a "quality-control-first" operational model — post-installation inspections on every single job — paired with a woman-owned identity that generates significant organic word-of-mouth in Carmel, Midtown Indy, and Nextdoor-heavy suburban neighborhoods.
