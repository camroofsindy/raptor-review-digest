RAPTOR ROOFING — SELF-INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
Refreshed: June 9, 2026 | Cameron Blakely dashboard

1. Who They Are

Raptor Roofing (formerly Raptor Restoration) was founded June 21, 2021, and rebranded in January 2025 to sharpen focus on roofing and exterior services.

Co-founders Cameron Blakely and Patrick Kinney are Center Grove locals building the operation from a southside Indianapolis base at 5145 S. Meridian Street.

The company positions itself around exceptional customer care and community impact, serving Central Indiana including Indianapolis, Carmel, and surrounding suburbs.

The core differentiator is the "we don't outsource accountability" owner-operated model — both principals are still embedded in day-to-day sales and project delivery.

2. Estimated Scale

LinkedIn lists the entity (still indexed as Raptor Restoration, LLC) at 2–10 employees, founded 2021, structured as a Partnership.

Confidence: low — the LinkedIn profile reflects the pre-rebrand entity and almost certainly understates actual headcount given 677 Google reviews and a multi-county service footprint.

Indeed salary data shows roles ranging from Estimator (~$25K) through Sales Manager (~$116K), with Sales Consultant averaging ~$99K — a compensation structure consistent with a commission-heavy residential sales model carrying 8–15 field reps.

Based on review velocity (677 reviews in roughly 4 years, accelerating), job mix, and Indy market rates, estimated annual revenue is $3M–$6M. Confidence: low — no public permit data or ZoomInfo pull confirmed.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

No active Raptor Roofing-specific job postings were surfaced on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter in the last 30 days as of this refresh.

LinkedIn does show a Field Consultant role occupied by a Greenwood-based rep with a IUPUI background

, confirming the company recruits locally for frontline sales. The absence of open postings is notable: either headcount is stable, hiring is happening through referral/word-of-mouth (common at this stage), or postings were recently filled. Watch for a marketing or ops manager post — that would be the clearest signal of a scale-up push.

4. Off-Google Presence

BBB profile carries an A+ rating, accredited since 9/30/2022, with the file opened October 2021.

The BBB record includes at least one unresolved workmanship complaint — a customer describing unsecured end caps post-install that went unaddressed for two-plus weeks.

That complaint is a visible brand liability since BBB pages rank in Google for brand-name searches.

On Yelp, the company is still indexed under the old "Raptor Restoration" branding at the same Meridian Street address

— meaning anyone searching "Raptor Roofing Yelp" may land on a stale profile with mismatched branding. That is a low-hanging citation cleanup opportunity.

Nextdoor presence is active and organic — a Greenwood neighbor recommended a specific rep by name and extension, and the brand is surfacing in threads about honest, no-hard-sell contractors.

That is earned trust, not paid placement.

Facebook page (RaptorRoofingIndiana) is live and active

, though Facebook Ad Library data was not confirmed in this pull — worth a manual check at facebook.com/ads/library to see if paid social is currently running.

GuildQuality profile also exists under the Raptor Restoration name, showing verified third-party survey reviews from Fishers, Greenwood, and Sheridan customers

— a quality signal most competitors skip entirely.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

The website covers a wide service-area footprint explicitly naming Indianapolis, Greenwood, Franklin, Beech Grove, Center Grove, Columbus, Crawfordsville, Plainfield, Mooresville, Avon, Brownsburg, Carmel, Fishers, and Noblesville

— indicating intentional geo-page strategy.

Dedicated city landing pages exist for Indianapolis with specific content on roof replacement, repair, storm restoration, and exterior upgrades, referencing property age and neighborhood weather exposure.

That is above-average for a sub-$10M contractor.

The site was relaunched in January 2025 as raptorroofing.com

alongside the rebrand, explicitly designed to enhance customer experience and accessibility.

Domain authority is still accruing — the old domain (raptorrestorationusa.com) holds residual citation equity that may not be fully 301'd. Blog content exists but leans promotional (e.g., introindianapolis.com feature is a placement piece). Schema markup and backlink depth from local press are unconfirmed at this pull — recommend a manual Ahrefs or Semrush check.

A directory site, bestroofersindianapolis.com, claims to rank contractors by Google rating, review count, and years in business without paid placement

— confirm whether Raptor Roofing is listed there, as it is an AEO citation source.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on this research pull, Raptor Roofing does not yet appear to be a dominant named citation in AI-generated answers for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." The brand is present on enough directory and review surfaces that it can appear in AI overviews, but the lack of strong third-party editorial coverage (local press features, industry association mentions, .edu or .gov adjacent links) limits consistent AI citation. The Nextdoor organic buzz and GuildQuality verified reviews are the strongest AEO signals currently in play. The gap between 677 Google reviews and thin AI citation is the clearest single opportunity on the board right now.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Raptor leads: 677 reviews at a perfect 5.0 is a moat. The 97% owner response rate signals engagement that AI engines and Google's local algorithms reward. The Nextdoor word-of-mouth is organic and trust-weighted. The GuildQuality third-party verification layer is a differentiator almost no local competitor uses.

Exploitable weakness: The Yelp profile is still indexed as "Raptor Restoration" with a stale brand identity — a searcher doing pre-hire due diligence hits a mismatched result that creates subconscious doubt, and there is at least one visible BBB complaint with no documented public resolution response that any competitor can point to.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Close the Yelp/BBB citation gap immediately. Claim and update the Yelp listing to "Raptor Roofing," add updated photos and service descriptions, and post a public, professional response to the open BBB complaint. These two items likely cost Raptor 2–5 conversions per month from high-intent searchers doing multi-source validation.

  2. Build editorial backlinks for AEO coverage. A single well-placed feature in the Indianapolis Business Journal, IndyStar home improvement section, or a neighborhood publication like the Center Grove/Greenwood community papers creates the kind of named, third-party editorial citation that causes ChatGPT and Gemini to surface a business by name. A press angle already exists: "Local husband-and-wife-owned company earns highest review volume of any Indy roofer at a perfect 5.0" — that is a story.

  3. Systematize GuildQuality (or similar) as a sales-enablement asset.

The existing GuildQuality profile demonstrates verified third-party satisfaction data

— but it is living under the old Raptor Restoration URL. Move that equity, push the verified survey results into proposal decks and the website, and use it as a premium-positioning proof point against competitors who only have Google reviews.
