1. Who They Are

Raptor Restoration, founded June 21, 2021, rebranded to Raptor Roofing in January 2025.

The company is structured as an LLC with Patrick Kinney listed as President/CEO and Cameron Blakely as President, operating out of 5145 S Meridian St Suite B, Indianapolis, IN 46217, and holding BBB accreditation since September 30, 2022.

Serving central Indiana — including Indianapolis, Carmel, and surrounding communities — the brand positions itself around trust, home protection, and community reinvestment.

2. Estimated Scale

LinkedIn (under the legacy "Raptor Restoration, LLC" entity) lists the company size as 2–10 employees, founded 2021, headquartered in Indianapolis.

Confidence: low — the LinkedIn profile reflects the pre-rebrand entity and is likely stale.

Indeed salary data shows roles spanning Estimator ($25,736/yr) through Sales Manager ($116,417/yr), with Sales Consultant averaging $99,829/yr, suggesting an active multi-rep sales org rather than a pure owner-operator.

Based on review velocity (682 Google reviews in ~4 years), James Hardie and Owens Corning certifications, and a multi-suburb service footprint, estimated annual revenue is $2M–$5M. Confidence: medium.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

No active Raptor Roofing job postings were found on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter specific to the Indianapolis location in the last 30 days.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

The web search query targeting ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity citation results for "best roofers Indianapolis" and related suburban queries returned no third-party AI-generated listicles or answer-engine snippets naming Raptor Roofing by name. This is not unusual — most local home-services contractors have near-zero AEO presence in generative engines as of mid-2026. However, the risk is real: AI answer engines increasingly pull from structured review aggregators, local press mentions, and schema-rich service pages. Raptor Roofing's multi-city landing page strategy and blog content put it in a stronger position than most Indy competitors to earn citations, but no confirmed GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) presence was found for queries like "roof replacement Carmel Indiana" or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." This is a greenfield opportunity — the first Indy roofer to earn consistent AI citations at the suburb level will own that zero-click demand.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where they match Raptor: 5-star Google rating, locally owned positioning, insurance claim expertise, James Hardie and Owens Corning certifications, multi-suburb service page SEO. They are, in most respects, a direct mirror.

Where Raptor has a clear edge: 682 reviews vs. the competitor's position — Raptor holds the highest review volume of any 5-star Indy roofer, which is the single most defensible moat in local pack rankings. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" brand line is crisper than anything visible in competitor messaging. The 0.4 owner response rate on Google, however, is a self-inflicted wound that an aggressive competitor could exploit in positioning.

Most exploitable weakness:

Their Yelp listing still operates under the legacy "Raptor Restoration" name

— a brand fragmentation gap that suppresses cross-platform trust for consumers who research beyond Google, and a cleanup they have apparently deprioritized for 18+ months.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. GuildQuality as a third-party trust layer.

GuildQuality collects verified customer feedback via impartial third-party surveys and publishes it publicly

— this is a conversion-rate tool, not just a vanity platform. If a prospect is comparing two 5-star roofers, a GuildQuality profile with 50+ verified survey responses is a legitimate tiebreaker. Cameron should evaluate whether Raptor's review volume justifies the investment.

  1. Suburb-level service page publishing cadence. The competitor is systematically building location pages for every named suburb in the metro.

Their commercial roofing pages are structured around specific buyer intent signals — property managers, active leaks, ponding water, rooftop units — rather than generic copy.

Raptor should audit whether its own location pages match this specificity or are still templated.

  1. Owner response rate as a ranking and trust lever. The 0.4 response rate on Google is the single most actionable internal fix on the table. Google's local ranking algorithm rewards review engagement, and a homeowner browsing three 5-star competitors will notice the one whose owner actually replies. A target of responding to 100% of new reviews within 48 hours is achievable at current velocity (10 reviews/30 days) and directly addresses both the algorithmic and perception gaps.
