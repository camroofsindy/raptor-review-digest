RAPTOR ROOFING — SELF-AUDIT BRIEF
Dashboard Refresh: July 23, 2025 | Compiled for Cameron Blakely

1. Who They Are

Raptor Roofing was incorporated on June 21, 2021, operates as an LLC, and has been BBB accredited since September 30, 2022.

The company rebranded from Raptor Restoration to Raptor Roofing in January 2025, marking a strategic shift away from broad disaster restoration toward a tighter roofing and exterior identity.

The company serves Indianapolis, Carmel, Greenwood, Fishers, Noblesville, and nearby communities

, positioning around low-pressure consultations, clean process, and premium accountability — not volume sales.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: low on headcount.

Indeed salary data (last updated March 29, 2025) surfaces roles including Sales Consultant, Senior Sales Consultant, Sales Manager, Junior Sales Consultant, Estimator, and Accounting Clerk — suggesting a structured but lean team with multiple sales layers.

Based on that role spread, the sales-to-ops ratio, and the volume of Google reviews accumulated, estimated headcount is 8–15 total (owners + field + sales). Confidence: low on revenue. At roughly 692 reviews over approximately 4 years with a residential average ticket of $9,000–$12,000 and volume growth indicators, estimated gross revenue is in the $2M–$4M range annually. No ZoomInfo, BuildZoom permit volume, or verifiable third-party revenue data was surfaced in research.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

No active Raptor Roofing job postings were found on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter within the last 30 days as of this brief. The most recent Indeed salary data reflects roles reported over the past 36 months, not active listings.

The salary profile shows the company has previously hired for Sales Manager ($116K), Senior Sales Consultant ($116K), and Sales Consultant ($99K) roles in Indianapolis

— indicating a commissioned, performance-structured sales team, not hourly canvassers. The absence of current postings either means the team is fully staffed or hiring is being done through referral. Worth monitoring Indeed and LinkedIn weekly — any new ops, marketing, or production manager listing signals a deliberate scale move.

4. Off-Google Presence

Raptor Roofing holds an A+ BBB rating and is BBB accredited

— a trust signal that shows up in paid and organic search snippets.

Raptor appears in Yelp's "Best 10 Roofing in Indianapolis" list as of May 2026

, and

also appears in Yelp's Greenwood roofing results

— meaningful given the southside market concentration. No standalone Yelp review count or star rating was surfaced in research, so a cross-platform discrepancy cannot be confirmed or denied at this time; direct Yelp profile audit is recommended.

The Facebook page (facebook.com/RaptorRoofingIndiana) shows 1,062 likes and 65 people talking about it

— decent engagement for a local contractor but not a dominant organic social presence. Facebook Ad Library was not surfaced in results; a manual Ad Library check at facebook.com/ads/library for "Raptor Roofing" is the next step to confirm whether paid social is active.

Raptor has a verified Nextdoor business listing for Indianapolis

, which is an underused trust channel most competitors ignore.

The company also retains an older GuildQuality profile under the Raptor Restoration name

— a small brand fragmentation risk if homeowners cross-reference that survey platform.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

The raptorroofing.com domain is actively built out with a deep content architecture.

Targeted location pages — such as the Greenwood roof replacement page — carry explicit trust markers including A+ BBB rating, 5-star reviews, and free inspections, clearly written for both ranking and conversion

. The site runs neighborhood-level service pages across Marion County sub-areas

(Broad Ripple, Irvington, Downtown, Castleton, Nora, Southside, Near Eastside, Westside)

— a siloed local SEO architecture that is the correct playbook for competing against regional chains.

The site also deploys conversion-oriented promotional callouts such as "$1,000 off roof replacement"

— indicating CRO-aware page design, not just SEO filler. The site title tag pattern ("Top Rated Roofing Company Near Me Indianapolis") is a blunt keyword-targeting move that signals the team is optimizing for near-me queries. Domain authority was not available in surfaced results; an Ahrefs or Moz spot-check is recommended. AEO schema markup was not verifiable from search results alone — a structured data audit of raptorroofing.com is the right next step.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

A third-party editorial site, introindianapolis.com (published April 30, 2026), ranks Raptor Roofing #1 among top roofing companies in Indianapolis for 2025, noting the company "has quickly risen to the top of the Indianapolis market."

This type of listicle content is exactly the source layer that ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity crawl and cite when generating "best roofers Indianapolis" answers. Raptor is actively building citation equity in the right format. However, no direct confirmation was found that Raptor is currently named by name inside ChatGPT or Gemini generative responses for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" or "roof replacement Carmel Indiana" — that requires live AI engine testing outside search. The introindianapolis.com placement is the most exploitable AEO asset in Raptor's current footprint and should be protected and replicated across additional local editorial outlets (Patch, Indianapolis Business Journal, neighborhood blogs). AI engines weight named citations from third-party editorial sources — Raptor's own site ranking does not substitute for that.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Raptor beats itself (honest read): 692 reviews at 5.0 is the strongest reputation signal in the Indy top 10 by volume — that is a real moat.

The A+ BBB accreditation

plus Nextdoor presence plus the introindianapolis.com #1 ranking creates a multi-channel trust stack that most local competitors do not have. The one exploitable weakness: A 0.0% owner response rate on Google reviews is a visible gap. At 692 reviews with zero public owner responses, any competitor or savvy homeowner who notices will read it as disengagement — and AI engines that surface "responsive contractor" as a ranking criterion for generative answers may discount Raptor on that dimension. That single metric undermines the "accountability" brand pillar more than any competitor action could.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

1. Seasonal dollar-off promotions on location pages.

Raptor's own Greenwood page runs a "$1,000 off roof replacement" seasonal promotion

— this is working creative that should be rotated across all high-traffic location pages with expiring urgency windows, not left as a static evergreen offer.

2. GuildQuality profile consolidation.

The legacy Raptor Restoration GuildQuality profile still lives at the same address and phone number

. Competitors or aggregator AI tools could surface this as a separate, lower-activity entity. Claim it, redirect it, or populate it with post-rebrand reviews to prevent brand dilution in third-party data pulls.

3. Owner response activation on Google, immediately. This is the single highest-ROI action available right now. Responding to even the last 20–30 reviews — specifically the most recent 5 — signals active ownership to both Google's local ranking algorithm and to AI engines that evaluate contractor engagement signals. Cameron answering reviews in his own voice (not templated) is differentiated content that no scaled competitor can replicate authentically.
