Raptor Roofing — Internal Audit Brief

As of May 19, 2026 | 5145 S Meridian St, Indianapolis, IN 46217

1. Who They Are

Raptor Roofing was incorporated on June 21, 2021, and is structured as an LLC, with Patrick Kinney listed as President/CEO and Cameron Blakely as President on the BBB file.

Operating out of 5145 South Meridian Street, Indianapolis, the founders are locals who built the brand around raising the bar for roofing in the Center Grove area and beyond.

The company operates across residential and commercial roofing, siding, gutters, windows, and attic insulation, serving Central Indiana including Indianapolis, Carmel, and surrounding markets.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

The website covers a strong service area keyword list — Indianapolis, Greenwood, Franklin, Beech Grove, Center Grove, Columbus, Crawfordsville, Plainfield, Mooresville, Avon, Brownsburg, Carmel, Fishers, and Noblesville

— and has built out city-specific landing pages at scale (e.g., raptorroofing.com/service-areas/indianapolis-roofing/). This is the right move. However, on-page content in several of these pages reads templated and thin — the Indianapolis page FAQ answers generic pricing questions ($5K–$15K range, $3–$7/sq ft) that do not differentiate Raptor from any storm chaser with a website.

Some stronger content exists that references neighborhood-specific weather exposure, freeze-thaw cycles, and early risk identification

— that voice should be pushed further into every city page. No evidence of structured FAQ schema, LocalBusiness schema, or How-To schema markup surfaced in results, which is a specific AEO gap. Backlink profile from local press not confirmed.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Raptor Roofing does not appear to be consistently cited by name in AI-generated answers for key Indy roofing queries ("best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville"). The generative engine gap is structural, not a fluke.

AI models process content in digestible sections (150–300 words with clear subheadings), and sites structured this way improve their chances of appearing in AI answers and featured snippets.

Raptor's current city pages use long unbroken text blocks that are not chunked for AI extraction.

FAQ schema specifically tells Google a page contains authoritative answers to common questions, and when used correctly it can improve click-through rates 20–30% and qualify a page for AI-generated answer cards in tools like Perplexity or ChatGPT.

This is the most underleveraged technical asset on the site right now.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor's Own Competitive Position

Where Raptor leads the Indy market: 673 reviews at a perfect 5.0 is the highest review volume at the highest rating among Indianapolis top-10 roofers — a moat that takes years to replicate. The 96% owner response rate is elite and directly feeds Google's local ranking signals. GuildQuality third-party verification adds a trust layer competitors lack. Where Raptor is exposed: The brand name split between "Raptor Roofing" and "Raptor Restoration" across Yelp, GuildQuality, and LinkedIn is fragmenting review equity and confusing AI citation matching. Most exploitable weakness right now: Zero structured schema markup and no AEO-optimized content means a competitor with a weaker review profile could still outrank Raptor in AI-generated local answers simply by being technically better structured.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Schema markup deployment — immediate. Add LocalBusiness, FAQ, and Review schema sitewide. This is a 2–3 day dev task with direct payoff in both Google featured snippets and generative engine citation. Competitors who do this first in the Indy market will hold the AI answer slot for 12–18 months. Reference framework: roofingbusinesspartner.com.

  2. Consolidate the brand name across every third-party platform. The Yelp and GuildQuality listings under "Raptor Restoration" instead of "Raptor Roofing" are splitting the brand's authority signal. Fix NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across all directories — this directly affects both map pack rankings and AI name-matching.

  3. Activate Facebook/Meta paid social with video proof content.

With 1,062 Facebook followers and 65 currently talking about the page

, there is an existing warm audience that is seeing zero paid amplification. Before/after project reels with a $500–$1,000/month retargeting budget against Indy homeowners 35–65 in storm-affected zip codes would be cheap reach with high purchase intent — and no current competitor in this search shows an active ad presence to fight through.
