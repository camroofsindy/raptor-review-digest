Competitive Intelligence Brief: Red Bird Roofing

Refreshed: August 4, 2026 | Analyst: Raptor CMO Desk

1. Who They Are

Red Bird Roofing was founded in 2023 by Chad Peterman (of Peterman Brothers), Chris Yano (of RYNO Strategic Solutions), and Billy Elliot — bringing over 20 years of combined home services experience in HVAC and plumbing into Indiana's roofing market.

They operate out of Carmel, IN, serving the greater Indianapolis metro and Lafayette area.

This is not a typical startup — the founding trio brings direct access to Peterman Brothers' existing customer database, RYNO's in-house digital marketing expertise, and the capital to scale fast. That is the single biggest structural threat in this profile.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: low on headcount — no verified LinkedIn employee count is publicly crawlable at this time; their LinkedIn page returns no employee data.

ZoomInfo lists Red Bird Roofing at redbirdroofing.com with an Indiana location but provides no revenue or headcount figure.

Based on 263 Google reviews accumulated since a 2023 founding, active named sales reps (Tyler, Justin, Jordan referenced in reviews), and a dedicated production manager role visible in customer write-ups, a reasonable estimate is 8–15 W-2/1099 staff and $2M–$4M in annual revenue. Confidence: low. They are growing but not yet at Peterman Brothers scale.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

No active job postings for Red Bird Roofing (Carmel, IN) were found on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter in the last 30 days.

Their own careers page at redbirdroofing.com/careers carries a generic "join our team" message with no specific open roles listed.

The absence of active hiring posts is notable — either they are fully staffed for current volume, or volume has plateaued (consistent with zero new Google reviews in the last 90 days). Worth revisiting in 30 days.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Their homepage targets "roofing contractor in Carmel, IN" and calls out Indianapolis and Lafayette as secondary service areas.

They have built out a clear location-page architecture: redbirdroofing.com/roof-repair-indianapolis, /roof-replacement-carmel-in, /roof-inspection-carmel-in, and /metal-roofing-carmel-in — standard but competent local SEO page structure. However, their site has a critical execution gap: multiple service pages still contain placeholder Lorem Ipsum body copy (e.g.,

"Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet consectetur. Mus dui ultricies odio nulla morbi semper cursus vulputate. Quam erat nunc id pulvinar."

— live on their inspection and replacement pages as of this refresh). That is a domain authority anchor dragging their rankings down and a crawlability issue that Google penalizes. Chris Yano's RYNO background means this will likely get fixed — but it hasn't been yet.

They promote financing options and monthly rotating specials

, which is a conversion-page tactic worth watching. No evidence of a blog content program or local press backlinks surfaced.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

No evidence from this search pass that Red Bird Roofing is being cited by name in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini responses for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." Their domain is less than three years old, their backlink profile is thin, and their Lorem Ipsum content issues further limit LLM training signal. They are not yet an AEO player. This is a window — Raptor should be the brand that owns these AI answers before Red Bird's RYNO-connected founders inevitably prioritize it.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Red Bird beats Raptor: Founder network leverage is significant. Chad Peterman's existing homeowner customer base in the Indy suburbs is a built-in warm referral pipeline Raptor cannot replicate overnight.

Their Malarkey Emerald installer status is a genuine product differentiator — Malarkey shingles are regarded as among the best Class 4 hail-resistant products available

, and they lean into it in sales conversations.

Where Raptor beats Red Bird: 695 reviews vs. 263. A perfect 5.0 vs. their 4.9. Zero reviews in 90 days on their side signals a review generation system that has gone cold or a volume slowdown. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is earned through operational consistency — Red Bird is still building that proof base.

Most exploitable weakness: Their website has live Lorem Ipsum placeholder copy on multiple service pages and zero reviews in 90 days — both are signals of a business in a post-launch lull that Cameron can exploit with a targeted Carmel/North Indy ad push right now.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Malarkey vs. Owens Corning as a sales wedge.

Red Bird's Malarkey Emerald status gives them longer warranties than Owens Corning, and reviewers notice it.

Cameron should evaluate whether Raptor's current material story is being told explicitly in the sales process or just assumed — if not, close that gap before Red Bird makes it a head-to-head differentiator in the Carmel market.

  1. Production manager on-site as a proof point.

Red Bird sends an on-site production manager who is present for the entire installation, sends progress photos, and flags discovered issues with drone video.

If Raptor is not documenting and sharing in-progress proof assets (drone shots, timestamped photos) with customers on install day, Red Bird will own that moment-of-truth experience narrative.

  1. Monitor their Facebook Ad Library quarterly. With Chris Yano (RYNO Strategic Solutions) as a co-founder, a paid social ramp is inevitable. The current 61 "talking about this" engagement on their Facebook page is low but could spike fast. Set a quarterly reminder to check facebook.com/ads/library for the "Red Bird Roofing" advertiser before they establish paid reach in Carmel, Westfield, and Zionsville ZIP codes.
