1. Who They Are

Red Bird Roofing was founded in 2023 by home service veterans Chad Peterman (of Peterman Brothers), Chris Yano (of RYNO Strategic Solutions), and Billy Elliott.

That founding team is the single most important fact in this brief — Peterman Brothers is one of the largest HVAC/plumbing operators in Indiana, and RYNO Strategic Solutions is a national home-services digital marketing agency. These are not accidental roofers.

Their stated service area is broad, covering Carmel, Indianapolis, Lafayette, and dozens of surrounding communities including Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, Greenwood, and Westfield.

Their differentiation play is leveraging the Peterman Brothers brand trust and RYNO's digital marketing infrastructure to run a professionally systemized roofing operation in a market that is still largely dominated by owner-operators.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Red Bird beats Raptor: Institutional infrastructure. A co-founder who runs a national home-services marketing agency means their SEO, paid search, and brand architecture are not guesswork — they are systematically built. Their Malarkey Emerald installer status is also a differentiated product credential.

Red Bird holds Emerald status with Malarkey, whose shingles are regarded by experienced roofers as among the best class-4 hail-resistant products available.

Where Raptor beats Red Bird: Volume and credibility of social proof — 687 reviews at 5.0 vs. 259 at 4.9 — and zero review velocity for Red Bird in the last 90 days, which suggests an operational slowdown, a pipeline problem, or an intentional pause. Raptor's owner-led accountability narrative is also harder to replicate when your brand is backed by a private equity-adjacent holding structure.

Most exploitable weakness: Red Bird's Google review engine has gone completely cold for 90 days — zero new reviews — which is the kind of gap that triggers AI engines and local packs to de-prioritize a listing. Hit them on recency.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Malarkey Emerald installer positioning.

Malarkey's class-4 hail-resistant shingles carry warranties "significantly longer" than Owens Corning according to a detailed Angi review.

If Raptor is not already using manufacturer-tier status as a sales proof point on landing pages and in estimates, Red Bird is eating that positioning unchallenged.

  1. Dedicated lead generation function. The Trevor Frank "Lead Generation Specialist" hire signals Red Bird is building an outbound/canvassing machine separate from their inbound funnel. Cameron should watch whether this role is storm-chasing or neighborhood canvassing — both would signal a shift in how they source volume jobs. If canvassing, Raptor's premium brand needs a clear counter-script ready for reps who encounter Red Bird in the field.

Their careers page explicitly references Chad Peterman by name and positions Red Bird as "dedicated to becoming the employer of choice for top talent."

They are recruiting off the Peterman brand halo. Cameron should ensure Raptor's own careers page and Glassdoor presence reflect the premium culture narrative — top installers and sales reps in Indy are choosing between these two brands.
