Competitive Intelligence Brief — Bone Dry Roofing (Loc 2 / Indianapolis HQ)

Refreshed: April 26, 2026 | Dashboard: Cameron Blakely, Raptor Roofing

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1989 by Gene Judd, who got his start swinging hammers alongside his parents out of a small garage in Speedway, Indiana.

Headquartered in Indianapolis, Bone Dry Roofing operates 20 locations and runs an 81% residential / 19% commercial split under Gene Judd as President.

The company has grown aggressively through acquisition, with the Bone Dry Family of Companies now spanning 15 branded roofing locations plus subsidiaries including Roof One, Bone Dry Commercial Roofing, and Nu-Tec Roofing Contractors across eight states.

Their differentiator is institutional scale dressed up as a family operation — not a franchise, but an M&A rollup playing local in every market.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: high on revenue range; medium on employee count given multi-entity structure.

Per Roofing Contractor Magazine's 2023 Contractor of the Year profile, Bone Dry reported 2022 annual revenue of $121 million across 20 locations with approximately 500 employees company-wide.

More recent data pushes that figure higher:

ZoomInfo notes that Bone Dry's 2023 revenue exceeded $183 million,

and

LeadIQ places the current revenue range at $100M–$250M.

Of the 2022 $121M total, approximately $36 million — roughly 30% — came from the original Indianapolis office alone,

making the Loc 2 GBP the single highest-volume location in the network. Applying that 30% share to a ~$183M 2023 base implies the Indy location alone approaches $55M annually. That's not a local competitor — that's a regional institution operating in Raptor's backyard.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Bone Dry currently carries 79 open jobs across Indeed company-wide.

Active Indianapolis-area postings include:

Roofing Service Technician, Roofing Sales Representative (Residential Division), and Sales Representative for the Commercial Roofing Division.

An IEG Sales Representative role (Interior, Exterior, and Gutters) is also live, targeting homeowners across the full exterior service stack.

Notably,

an Accounts Receivable Clerk posting in Indianapolis and an Assistant General Manager search in Detroit are also active,

suggesting back-office scaling alongside field expansion. No marketing-specific or CMO-equivalent hire flagged for the Indy location in the last 30 days —

Matt Tyner holds the CMO title company-wide, focused on new market entry and lead volume growth.

The volume and breadth of sales postings signals an active push to grow the top-of-funnel rep count heading into peak season.

4. Off-Google Presence

Bone Dry's primary Indianapolis location on Yelp (7735 Winton Dr) shows 75 reviews

— a thin count relative to 943 on Google, which is a textbook review-funneling signal. Their Google review machine is clearly optimized to route satisfied customers to Google only, leaving Yelp as an unmanaged channel where disgruntled customers tend to self-select.

BBB profile carries an A+ rating and multiple Angie's List Super Service Award wins on record.

Community-level partnerships are active:

Bone Dry partnered with Indy Humane on a pet microchipping event in the Indianapolis area

and

donated a full roof and labor to a Nashville first responder alongside Owens Corning and the Nashville Sounds.

Facebook Ad Library was not directly searchable within this pull, but their tech stack —

confirmed to include Facebook, LinkedIn, WordPress, and Adobe Premiere Pro

— indicates active paid social production capability. The Yelp gap (75 reviews vs. 943 on Google) is the clearest vulnerability in their off-Google presence.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

bonedry.com is a high-authority domain by home-services standards — multi-state, 35+ years old, Wordpress-based, with dedicated city/location subpages at URLs like bonedry.com/indianapolis/.

Bone Dry was named Roofing Contractor Magazine's 2023 Residential Roofing Contractor of the Year, generating national trade press backlinks

that materially boost domain authority. They operate a blog, email newsletter, and location-specific service pages covering roofing, gutters, masonry, solar, siding, and insulation — a topical authority moat that takes years to replicate.

Their tech stack includes Adobe Premiere Pro, cdnjs, and Webpack,

indicating video production capacity and a JavaScript-heavy front end (which can hurt crawlability if not carefully managed). The "Bone Dry University" brand and the 2023 national award are both organic link-building assets Raptor has no equivalent of yet. AEO risk: their blog and service pages are exactly the type of structured, high-volume content that LLMs index for local contractor answers.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry Roofing is very likely cited by name in AI-generated responses to queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," and "roofing company Fishers Noblesville." The reasons are structural:

their own site claims 4.8 stars across over 5,000 reviews in nine states,

giving LLMs multiple high-confidence data points to pull from. The national trade press citation —

named the 2023 Residential Roofing Contractor of the Year by Roofing Contractor Magazine

— is exactly the third-party authority signal that pushes contractors into AI answer sets. Their multi-location footprint also means they appear in AI answers for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, and Greenwood simultaneously, while Raptor currently competes for those suburb queries with a single GBP. This is the most dangerous asymmetry in the matchup. Raptor needs suburb-specific AEO content (dedicated landing pages, schema markup, localized FAQ content) to compete in AI answer visibility for the collar communities.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Bone Dry beats Raptor: Review volume (943 vs. 664), brand name recognition built over 35 years, national trade press authority, multi-service upsell depth (solar, masonry, commercial, insulation), and a dedicated CMO running coordinated multi-market paid media. Their Bone Dry University recruitment engine also means they can staff faster than a single-location operator.

Where Raptor beats Bone Dry: Rating purity (5.0 vs. 4.9 matters in AI ranking and LSA), owner response rate discipline (Raptor's consistent engagement vs. Bone Dry's institutional size making personal responsiveness harder), and premium positioning — Raptor can credibly claim accountability in a way a 500-person rollup structurally cannot. At Bone Dry's scale, subcontractor and crew consistency questions are real and show up in their longer-tail negative reviews.

Most exploitable weakness: Their Yelp profile has just 75 reviews against 943 on Google — a classic corporate review-funneling gap that signals they are ignoring platforms where unhappy customers actually land, and where Raptor can establish clear visible superiority.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The on-site production supervisor model.

Bone Dry keeps a production supervisor on-site for the duration of every project, serving as the main point of contact for homeowners.

They also deliver

detailed photos and videos throughout the installation process.

Cameron should formalize and market this same practice explicitly — it's a direct counter to the "accountability" story and it's content-rich for social and review prompts.

  1. The community partnership playbook. Bone Dry runs cause-tied visibility plays (Indy Humane, first responder roof donations) that generate local press, social engagement, and backlinks with zero paid media spend. Raptor should identify one Indy-area cause partnership per quarter and treat it as both a PR and link-building asset, not just goodwill.

  2. Suburb-specific landing pages with schema. Bone Dry's domain covers every Indy suburb with dedicated pages. Cameron should prioritize publishing geo-specific pages for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Greenwood — each with LocalBusiness schema, FAQ schema targeting AI answer queries, and a distinct testimonial pool — to chip away at Bone Dry's current AEO advantage in those collar markets before the gap widens further.
