Bone Dry Roofing — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Location tracked: Loc 2 (Indianapolis/Greenwood market) | Refreshed: August 11, 2026

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1989, Bone Dry Roofing has grown into one of the largest residential roofing companies in the Midwest.

The company is family owned and locally operated, led by President Gene Judd, and headquartered in Indianapolis.

Their service footprint now spans Indianapolis, Bloomington, Evansville, Fort Wayne, and Lafayette in Indiana, plus Ohio, Kentucky, Missouri, Tennessee, Colorado, and Florida — making them a genuine regional platform, not a single-market operator.

Their differentiator on paper is institutional scale married to a family-brand story —

Bone Dry was named Roofing Contractor Magazine's 2023 Residential Roofing Contractor of the Year

, a national credentialing win most Indy competitors cannot touch.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry has strong structural credentials that AI citation engines reward: a national award win, a high-domain-authority root domain with city-specific pages, a Wikipedia-style backlink profile from trade press, and volume review counts across multiple platforms. Based on their profile —

an average rating of 4.8 stars across over 5,000 reviews across nine states

, plus the 2023 national contractor of the year designation — they are a likely citation in broad queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" or "top roofing contractor Greenwood Indiana" in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity responses. AI models weight authoritative third-party mentions (trade publications, BBB, volume review platforms) heavily; Bone Dry checks every box. 7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Bone Dry beats Raptor: Raw review volume (968 vs. 697), national brand credentialing, a dedicated CMO and full marketing department, multi-location domain authority, service breadth (solar, masonry, insulation, commercial), and enterprise-level hiring infrastructure. They can outspend on paid media in any given week without blinking.

Where Raptor beats Bone Dry: Star rating — Raptor's 5.0 is a clean differentiator against Bone Dry's 4.9 at this review volume. More critically, Bone Dry's Loc 2 is generating only 10 reviews in 90 days — a stagnation signal that suggests the Indianapolis office may be operating on brand inertia rather than active review generation. Raptor's 1.0 owner response rate signals tighter accountability to individual customers, a message Bone Dry's institutional scale cannot credibly match.

Most exploitable weakness: A 10-review/90-day velocity at nearly 1,000 reviews total means Bone Dry Loc 2 is coasting — Cameron should be calling this out in every sales conversation with the phrase "they've grown past caring about your individual job."

Cameron should identify 1-2 Indy/Carmel/Fishers nonprofits or community events where Raptor can plant a similar flag — these mentions feed AEO citation engines organically.

  2. The "Roof System Protection Plan" as a retention product.

Bone Dry's Roof System Protection Plan is positioned as a long-term peace of mind product promising care, consistency, and accountability after installation.

This is a post-sale retention and referral engine dressed up as a warranty product. Raptor should evaluate building an analogous named program — something that gives past customers a reason to call Raptor first for any future exterior need and to refer neighbors, without competing on price.
