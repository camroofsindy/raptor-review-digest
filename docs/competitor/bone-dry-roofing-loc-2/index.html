Bone Dry Roofing (Loc 2) — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Refreshed: Tuesday, July 22, 2026 | Analyst: Raptor CMO

1. Who They Are

Bone Dry Roofing was established in 1989 and has grown from a garage operation into one of the largest residential, family-owned roofing companies in the area.

The company is headquartered in Indianapolis, founded by Gene Judd, who remains current principal owner and president, with a scope of work that is approximately 81% residential and 19% commercial.

Location 2 specifically operates out of 7304 US 31 S, Indianapolis, IN 46227, phone (317) 375-7740

— the south-side corridor directly competing with Raptor's Greenwood-adjacent customer base.

2. Estimated Scale

This is an enterprise-scale operator, not a local shop.

As of their 2023 Residential Contractor of the Year profile in Roofing Contractor magazine, Bone Dry reported $121M in 2022 revenue across 20 locations and 500 employees.

A subsequent ZoomInfo-cited announcement noted revenue exceeded $183 million in 2023

, and

RocketReach pegs the 2026 annual revenue figure at $191 million.

LeadIQ estimates approximately 250 employees as of June 2026.

Confidence: medium — third-party revenue aggregators vary widely; the $183–191M range for the enterprise is the most credible band. The Loc 2 branch is one of at least five Indianapolis-area addresses and is not independently capitalized, so attributing a standalone P&L is not possible from public data.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Active and aggressive.

Bone Dry posted a full-time SEO Manager role in Indianapolis on ZipRecruiter, paying $66,900–$93,200.

That listing surfaced in late December 2025/early January 2026 and signals a deliberate in-house digital build-out.

On Indeed, open Indianapolis roles include Roofing Repairs Sales Representative, Roofing Sales Representative, and Repair Technician positions.

Commercial Roofing Division Sales Representative and Solar Installer roles are also live.

Indeed currently lists 78 open jobs companywide.

The in-house SEO Manager hire is the most important signal here — they are bringing digital strategy off agency and into headquarters, which will accelerate their content and local SEO velocity.

4. Off-Google Presence

On Yelp, the Winton Drive location (Loc 6) carries 75 reviews

— no star rating surfaced for Loc 2 specifically, but Yelp volume is thin relative to 965 Google reviews, suggesting systematic review funneling to Google. That's a red flag for anyone stress-testing their reputation: the Google number is enormous but not corroborated across platforms.

The Facebook page tied to the Greenwood/south Indy service area has 827 likes and describes itself as a family-owned company proudly serving Greenwood since 1989.

Their tech stack includes HubSpot Analytics and Google Business Profile

, which confirms UTM-tracked social traffic management.

They hold BBB accreditation

with no adverse rating noted publicly. No significant Reddit presence found. Facebook Ad Library activity was not directly surfaced in results but the UTM parameters visible in their Facebook bio (utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=profile-link) confirm active paid/tracked social campaign infrastructure.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Bone Dry is playing enterprise SEO at a level no independent Indy roofer can easily match.

Their Indianapolis landing page covers residential and commercial roofing, gutters, and exterior services comprehensively

, with dedicated city/service subpages that generate long-tail ranking depth.

They hold both Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor and CertainTeed ShingleMaster Select Contractor status

— manufacturer pages link back to their domain, which is a strong local backlink signal.

Their in-house "Bone Dry University" training academy, launched in 2021, has logged over 8,734 hours of live training

— content fodder they are increasingly using for blog and resource pages. The in-house SEO Manager hire (see Section 3) signals they know their content volume is below what their domain authority should be producing. AEO-specific schema is not confirmed from public crawl data, but their HubSpot stack and multi-location GBP management suggest structured data is at least partially deployed.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry Roofing is highly likely to appear in AI-generated answers for Indianapolis roofing queries. The reasons are structural, not speculative: (1) they have a high-authority root domain (bonedry.com) with multi-state coverage and press-cited revenue figures that make them "factually dense" for LLM training sets; (2)

they were named 2023 Residential Roofing Contractor of the Year by Roofing Contractor magazine

, and that award is cited across multiple third-party domains — exactly the kind of cross-referenced, authoritative signal that surfaces in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity answers; (3)

they carry an average 4.8-star rating across over 5,000 reviews in nine states

, a volume metric that AI engines treat as a credibility signal. For hyper-local prompts like "roof replacement Carmel" or "roofer Zionsville Indiana,"

Bone Dry has a location serving the Zionsville area

that gives them local entity presence in those geographies. Raptor is not yet building the citation footprint (press pickups, award pages, third-party directory consistency) that would compete in generative engine results at this scale.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Volume, brand recognition, multi-location GBP coverage, manufacturer co-marketing (OC Platinum + CertainTeed), 965 Google reviews vs. Raptor's 690, and an in-house marketing team with a dedicated SEO hire. Their corporate infrastructure means they can sustain lead flow through storms and slow seasons in ways a single-location operator cannot.

Where Raptor beats them: Raptor holds a 5.0 star rating vs. Bone Dry's 4.9 — a meaningful signal in AI-curated results where perfect scores get cited first. Raptor's owner-response rate and accountability narrative ("we don't outsource accountability") hits directly at Bone Dry's Achilles heel: at enterprise scale, customers frequently report feeling like a ticket number, not a client. Bone Dry's Yelp-to-Google review disparity, and their 1.0/5.0 Indeed ratings in markets like St. Louis and Fort Collins, reveal cultural inconsistency at scale.

Most exploitable weakness: Bone Dry's 30-day review velocity is nearly flat (10 reviews in 90 days companywide for this location) despite 965 total reviews — their review engine has stalled, and Raptor can close the raw volume gap faster than Bone Dry can fix its assembly-line service perception.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. In-house SEO Manager model. Bone Dry is pulling digital strategy in-house at the $66K–$93K salary band. Cameron should evaluate whether Raptor's current agency relationship is producing the content velocity and technical SEO depth that competes with a dedicated internal hire — or whether a fractional SEO specialist embedded in Raptor's workflow would close the gap at lower cost.

  2. "Bone Dry University" as brand and retention tool.

Bone Dry University was created in 2021 as an in-house training academy and has logged over 8,734 hours of live training and more than 18,103 e-learning module completions.

Raptor should develop a visible, named training or standards program — even a lightweight version — that can be used in sales conversations and on-site messaging to signal crew quality without relying on headcount scale.

  1. Manufacturer co-marketing leverage.

Bone Dry's dual status as an Owens Corning Platinum Preferred and CertainTeed ShingleMaster Select contractor

earns them backlinks, co-branded marketing assets, and manufacturer referral leads. Cameron and Patrick should audit Raptor's current manufacturer certification level and push for the highest tier available — the backlink alone is worth it for AEO/GEO visibility.
