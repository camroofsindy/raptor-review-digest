Competitive Intelligence Brief — Bone Dry Roofing (Loc 2)

Refreshed: Tuesday, May 20, 2025 | Analyst: Raptor CMO

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1989 by Gene Judd, who started the company out of a garage in Speedway, Indiana as a true family affair.

Headquartered in Indianapolis and operating across 20 locations, the company runs 81% residential and 19% commercial work.

Bone Dry has grown aggressively through M&A and now operates a family of companies spanning eight states — including G&W Roofing, Roof One, QRS, Ravlin's, RCV, and Nu-Tec — making it the dominant multi-brand platform in the Midwest roofing space.

2. Estimated Scale

Per Roofing Contractor Magazine's 2023 Contractor of the Year profile, Bone Dry reported $121M in 2022 revenue with approximately 500 employees companywide.

ZoomInfo's reported figures put 2023 revenue at over $183 million

, and

RocketReach pegs the 2026 annual revenue estimate at $191 million.

Confidence: medium — these are third-party estimates extrapolated from public filings and data aggregators, not audited statements.

LeadIQ reports approximately 240 employees as of March 2026

, though that figure almost certainly undercounts field crews and subcontractors. For Indianapolis Loc 2 specifically,

the original Indianapolis office contributed roughly $36M (about 30%) of the company's $121M 2022 total

— call the Indy Loc 2 contribution roughly $15–20M annually. Confidence: low on the location-specific split.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Active and broad.

Bone Dry is currently running a Management in Training (MIT) program and actively hiring for it on ZipRecruiter.

They are also recruiting Commercial Sales Representatives specifically targeting the Indianapolis area.

A separate Indianapolis Southside/Greenwood-area sales rep posting is live

, which signals deliberate penetration of Raptor's core suburban geography.

Bone Dry Solar is actively recruiting a Solar Systems Electrician in Indianapolis via ZipRecruiter

, pointing to continued solar division build-out.

An Assistant Production Manager role is also posted, suggesting ops infrastructure growth.

The Greenwood sales hire is the one to watch — that's a direct encroachment on Raptor's strongest market.

4. Off-Google Presence

On Yelp, the Loc 2 address (7735 Winton Dr) shows 75 reviews

— a thin number relative to 953 Google reviews. That's a classic review-funneling pattern: Google gets the push, Yelp is virtually abandoned.

Their US-31 South Yelp listing (tagged as Loc 2 on Yelp's own URL) shows only 1 review, despite the area generating 1,684 roofing searches per month within 25 miles.

BBB maintains an A+ rating and the company has historically won Angie's List Super Service Awards for consecutive years.

BBB lists a full C-suite including President Gene Judd, CMO Matt Tyner, COO Billy Smith, and CFO Tony Merlie

— a marketing executive at the top-company level means paid media and brand strategy are centrally coordinated, not location-by-location. Recent BBB complaint activity is notable:

a June 2025 complaint details a $4,000 tarp job where the roof was not fully covered after a tornado, causing further water damage

— execution gaps at scale are showing up in the complaint log. Facebook Ad Library activity was not surfaced in available data; monitor directly at facebook.com/ads/library.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Bone Dry operates on WordPress with a well-structured multi-location site architecture at bonedry.com.

Their known tech stack includes Adobe Premiere Pro, cdnjs, WordPress, and Module Federation

— indicating video content production and a modular front-end build.

They hold the 2023 Roofing Contractor Magazine Residential Contractor of the Year award

, which generates a high-authority editorial backlink from a trade publication — that alone meaningfully boosts domain authority. Dedicated location pages exist for bonedry.com/indianapolis/ with service-specific subpages, strong signals for local pack ranking. They also operate a newsletter subscription program, indicating content marketing depth. The multi-brand acquisition strategy (Roof One, QRS, etc.) means they are likely capturing keyword traffic under multiple domains simultaneously — a tactic Raptor cannot easily replicate but should be aware of.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry Roofing is consistently cited in AI-generated answers for Indianapolis roofing queries. Their combination of 35+ years in market, a national trade award, 5,000+ cross-platform reviews, Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status, and a well-structured multi-location website gives large language models abundant citation fodder.

Their own site claims an average of 4.8 stars across over 5,000 reviews

— that aggregate review volume is exactly the data signal that feeds AI answer engines. For Raptor, the AEO gap is that Bone Dry's brand appears in more third-party editorial contexts (trade press, award coverage, partnership announcements). Raptor's 5.0 star average and premium positioning are not yet surfacing in generative answers because there is insufficient third-party corroboration — no trade press features, no award citations, no partnership announcements that AI engines can pull from.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Bone Dry beats Raptor: Raw review volume (953 vs. 673), multi-location brand presence saturating Indianapolis suburbs including Greenwood, a dedicated CMO and internal marketing department, national trade award credibility, and dual manufacturer certifications (Owens Corning Platinum + CertainTeed ShingleMaster) that provide warranty upsell leverage.

Their in-house Bone Dry University, launched in 2021, has logged over 8,734 hours of live training

— that's a talent retention and quality consistency moat. Where Raptor beats them: Star rating (5.0 vs. 4.9), premium single-trade accountability positioning vs. Bone Dry's sprawling multi-brand complexity, and a tighter owner-accountability narrative that resonates with high-value homeowners who distrust large corporate operators. Most exploitable weakness: The BBB complaint pattern — execution failures on tarp jobs and work performed outside scope — signals that at $190M+ in revenue, field quality control is slipping. That's a direct opening for Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" message.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

1. The dual-cert warranty play.

Bone Dry holds both Owens Corning Platinum Preferred and CertainTeed ShingleMaster Select status

, which they market as a premium differentiator in their sales process. Cameron should evaluate whether a second manufacturer certification creates a tangible close-rate advantage worth pursuing.

2. Greenwood/Southside sales rep deployment. Bone Dry just posted a sales rep role specifically for Greenwood and the Indy Southside. Watch whether they begin canvassing or direct-mail blitzing Raptor's core zip codes. Raptor should preempt with a hyper-local review push and geo-targeted paid ads in those markets before Bone Dry's new rep gets their pipeline warm.

3. Community partnership PR for AEO lift.

Bone Dry has executed visible charity partnerships — including with Indy Humane for microchipping events and with Owens Corning and the Nashville Sounds to donate a roof to a first responder.

These generate local press backlinks and third-party brand mentions that feed generative engine citations. Raptor needs equivalent press-worthy partnerships in Indy to close the AEO visibility gap.
