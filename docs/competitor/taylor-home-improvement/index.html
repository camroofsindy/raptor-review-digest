Refreshed: April 26, 2025 | Noblesville, IN

1. Who They Are

Taylor Home Improvement is owned by father-and-son team Steve and Adam Taylor, operating as a family-owned company based in Noblesville, Indiana, serving all of central Indiana.

BBB records show a file opened in March 2008, putting the business at roughly 17 years old.

Their primary differentiator is multi-trade breadth — roofing, siding, gutters, remodeling, painting, windows, and doors — combined with a GAF Master Elite credential that they actively market as a scarcity play.

ZoomInfo lists Taylor Home Improvement at 20 employees at their Noblesville headquarters.
