1. Who They Are

Taylor Home Improvement is owned by a father-and-son team, Steve and Adam Taylor, operating out of Noblesville, Indiana and serving all of central Indiana.

The company is family-owned and locally operated, specializing in exterior and interior remodeling including roof replacement and repair, siding, windows, doors, gutters, and gutter guards.

Their differentiator on paper is a stack of manufacturer credentials:

they claim membership in an elite group of only 13 roofing contractors in Indiana awarded GAF Master Elite status

they hold Owens Corning Preferred Contractor status and are trained and supported by James Hardie Company for fiber cement siding.
