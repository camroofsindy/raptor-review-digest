Taylor Home Improvement — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Refreshed: August 13, 2025 | Prepared for: Cameron Blakely

1. Who They Are

Taylor Home Improvement is owned by father-and-son team Steve and Adam Taylor, a family-owned company based in Noblesville, Indiana, serving all of central Indiana with roofing, siding, gutters, painting, remodeling, and windows/doors.

Their BBB file opened in March 2008, putting them at roughly 17 years in business, with BBB accreditation since 2017.

Their key differentiator is multi-credential manufacturer status —

they claim to be among only 13 roofing contractors in Indiana awarded GAF Master Elite status, placing them in the top 2% nationwide.

2. Estimated Scale

ZoomInfo lists Taylor Home Improvement's corporate office at the Noblesville location with 20 employees

, while

ContactOut independently pegs them at 23 employees.

Confidence: medium — both are third-party estimates; no verified headcount is publicly disclosed.

Their Noblesville address is listed as their corporate office and original location, with a second location in Carmel and a stated expansion into Fishers

, suggesting a multi-location structure that would support that 20–25 headcount range. Estimated annual revenue: $3M–$6M based on headcount, service mix, and multi-location presence. Confidence: low — they are private and no revenue figure is publicly filed.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Their LinkedIn company page shows only 96 followers

, and no active job postings were surfaced on LinkedIn, Indeed, or ZipRecruiter in the last 30 days across any searches conducted for this brief. No marketing, sales ops, or field leadership roles are currently listed. This is either a lean org that promotes from within or a company that hires through referral. Either way, no growth signal is present right now — but the multi-location push into Carmel and Fishers (per their own website) suggests field hiring could come in Q4.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp lists them at 11790 IN-32 with 18 reviews

— versus 280 on Google. That ratio is a clear sign of deliberate review-funneling to Google, with zero effort to build Yelp presence.

Facebook shows 90% recommendation rate across 30 reviews

, and

1,185 page likes with only 14 currently talking about the page

— an under-engaged audience for a 17-year-old company. No Facebook Ad Library activity was surfaced, suggesting no paid social spend at this time.

BBB rates them A+ and they are accredited

;

the BBB also recognized them for a Complaint-Free Year

, which they actively publicized on LinkedIn — a smart credibility move. No Reddit presence was found. No meaningful Nextdoor footprint was detectable in search.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Taylor Home Improvement operates taylorhomeimprovement.net and has built out location-specific pages for Noblesville, Carmel, and Fishers — a correct local SEO structure.

Their BuildZoom score of 109 ranks in the top 5% of 22,324 Indiana licensed contractors

, which generates a credible third-party backlink.

They are also listed as a member of F.C. Tucker Home Services

— a referral partnership that drives real estate agent-to-homeowner pipeline and provides a high-authority local backlink. Their blog content appears dated and thin based on page metadata; the most recent indexed content crawled for roofing services pages shows no substantive long-form content. Schema markup presence is unconfirmed but likely minimal given the overall site structure. No local press backlinks were found. Domain authority is estimated in the 20–30 range — functional but not dominant.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Taylor Home Improvement does not appear to be surfacing in generative engine results for the primary Indy-market queries — "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." Their GAF Master Elite credential and multi-location setup give them the raw ingredients to appear in AI-cited answers, but without structured content, authoritative press mentions, or citation-worthy long-form pages, they are not pulling citations from ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude in tested queries. This is a mutual gap in the market — neither they nor Raptor should assume AI presence without actively building for it.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Multi-manufacturer credentialing (GAF Master Elite + Owens Corning Preferred + James Hardie-trained) gives them a wider materials pitch and lets them compete across roofing and siding in a single sales conversation. The F.C. Tucker partnership is a legitimate referral engine Raptor does not currently have a public equivalent to.

Where Raptor beats them: 699 reviews at 5.0 vs. Taylor's 280 at 4.6 is not a close contest at the bottom of the funnel — a half-star gap at meaningful volume is decisive in consumer psychology. Taylor's 0 new reviews in the last 90 days signals a broken or passive review collection process. Owner response rate is also zero, meaning they are leaving trust-building on the table on every review page that surfaces in search. Raptor's premium positioning and active reputation management are measurably superior.

Most exploitable weakness: Taylor's review velocity has completely flatlined — zero in 90 days — while they are actively expanding into Carmel and Fishers, meaning they are walking into new markets with a stale review profile and no owner response record.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. F.C. Tucker Home Services partnership model.

Taylor is a listed member of F.C. Tucker's Home Services program, which positions contractors to capture referrals before, during, and after a home sale.

Raptor should evaluate a comparable real estate brokerage partnership — Tucker, Carpenter, or RE/MAX — as a lead channel, particularly in Hamilton County where resale volume is high.

  1. BBB Complaint-Free Year as earned media.

Taylor actively promoted their BBB Complaint-Free Year recognition on LinkedIn as a trust signal for their family-owned brand.

Cameron should identify equivalent third-party validations — whether GAF Triple Excellence, BBB recognition, or a local "Best of" award — and build a content push around each one rather than letting it sit as a logo on the website.

  1. Multi-location SEO architecture. Taylor has built dedicated location pages for Noblesville, Carmel, and Fishers. The structure is correct even if the content is thin. Raptor should audit whether its own location-specific pages are winning the right suburb-level queries (Zionsville, Westfield, Greenwood) before Taylor thickens up its content and closes the gap.
