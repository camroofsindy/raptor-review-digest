Competitor Intelligence Brief — Taylor Home Improvement

Updated: April 26, 2025 | HQ: 11790 IN-32, Noblesville, IN 46060

1. Who They Are

Taylor Home Improvement is owned by a father-and-son team, Steve and Adam Taylor, operating as a family-owned company based in Noblesville, Indiana, serving all of central Indiana.

They offer a full range of home improvement services including roofing, siding, gutters, interior and exterior painting, remodeling and repairs, and windows and doors.

Their sharpest differentiator on paper is manufacturer credentialing:

they are in an elite group of only 13 roofing contractors in the state of Indiana and only 2% nationwide awarded with the GAF Master Elite status.
