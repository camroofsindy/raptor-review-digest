Competitive Intelligence Brief — Taylor Home Improvement

Refreshed: Thursday, May 22, 2026 | Prepared for Cameron Blakely

1. Who They Are

Taylor Home Improvement is a family-owned company based in Noblesville, Indiana, owned by father-and-son team Steve and Adam Taylor, serving all of central Indiana with roofing, siding, gutters, painting, and remodeling.

The BBB file opened in 2008, putting them at roughly 17 years in business, with BBB accreditation since March 2017.

Their core differentiator is a dual credential play:

they claim membership in an elite group of only 13 roofing contractors in Indiana awarded GAF Master Elite status

, paired with

an on-staff HAAG Certified roofing inspector and Adam Taylor's 15+ years working insurance claims for hail, wind, and storm damage.

2. Estimated Scale

ZoomInfo lists Taylor Home Improvement's headcount at approximately 20 employees

(confidence: medium — ZoomInfo counts are notoriously stale for small operators; actual W-2 count is likely 10–15 with subcontractor crews making up the rest).

They operate from a Noblesville headquarters with a secondary location in Carmel, Indiana

, suggesting deliberate northern-suburb expansion. No public revenue figure exists; at 20 employees and a roofing-primary, multi-trade model, estimated annual revenue is in the $3M–$6M range (confidence: low — no permit volume data or financial filings found).

3. Recent Hiring Signals

A LinkedIn job posting for an Outside Sales Representative at Taylor Home Improvement was found at linkedin.com/jobs/view/outside-sales-representative-at-taylor-home-improvement-4222336365

— this is the most substantive signal.

LinkedIn also shows a Director of Sales role held by Randolph Fuller, indicating a formal sales leadership layer exists.

No marketing, ops-leadership, or project management postings were found on Indeed or ZipRecruiter within the last 30 days. The outside sales rep post is notable: it signals they are trying to scale lead generation through feet-on-the-street canvassing, not digital — a structurally expensive and increasingly inefficient channel in a post-storm market.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

The site runs on WordPress with Cloudflare CDN and reCAPTCHA

— a generic, low-investment stack.

They have built city-specific location pages, including a dedicated Carmel, Indiana page

, alongside a Noblesville page, suggesting basic local SEO page templating. The site has service-specific URLs (e.g., /roof-repairs-and-replacement/, /locations/noblesville/) but no evidence of a blog with consistent publish cadence, no visible schema markup targeting FAQPage or LocalBusiness with review aggregation, and no backlinks from local press found.

Their BuildZoom score of 109 ranks in the top 5% of Indiana licensed contractors

, which does earn them a legitimate third-party citation signal. The GAF contractor directory listing at gaf.com is a meaningful domain-authority backlink Raptor should audit against its own GAF profile. Overall SEO posture: moderate local presence, no content moat, no AEO-optimized FAQ schema.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on this research pass, Taylor Home Improvement does not appear to carry the citation profile necessary to surface in AI-generated answers for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." The signals AI engines use — consistent NAP across high-DA directories, active press mentions, volume of recent structured reviews, schema-marked FAQ content — are all weak or absent here. Their GAF listing and BuildZoom score are the two most AI-legible authority signals they hold. Neither is strong enough to drive named citations in LLM outputs without supporting content volume. This is an open lane for Raptor.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: GAF Master Elite designation is a real credential with tangible warranty leverage —

GAF entrusts Master Elite contractors to offer their strongest warranties including the Golden Pledge Limited Warranty on qualifying roof systems

— and their insurance-claim expertise with a HAAG Certified inspector on staff is a differentiated sales tool in a hail-prone market. They also have a physical Carmel location, giving them a GBP footprint in a high-income zip Raptor should monitor.

Where Raptor beats them: 675 reviews vs. 274, a full point higher rating (5.0 vs. 4.6), zero owner response rate on their end vs. Raptor's active engagement, and a review pipeline that has gone completely dark for 90+ days. The most exploitable weakness is their review velocity collapse — a competitor with zero new Google reviews in 90 days is invisible to both the algorithm and AI engines, regardless of credential quality.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The insurance-claim positioning: Taylor leans hard into HAAG certification and storm-damage claim navigation as a sales differentiator —

Adam is experienced in roofing, siding, gutters, and emergency repair assessments and is an expert in facilitating and negotiating insurance storm damage claims.

If Raptor has similar capability or can certify a team member, this is a trust signal worth owning explicitly in ad copy targeting post-storm search spikes.

  1. The F.C. Tucker partnership:

Taylor Home Improvement is a member of F.C. Tucker's Home Services vendor network

, which places them in front of buyers and sellers during real estate transactions — a warm, pre-qualified referral channel most roofing contractors ignore entirely. Cameron should audit whether Raptor has a comparable real estate agent partnership pipeline and, if not, pursue it.

  1. City-specific landing page templating: Taylor has built location pages for Noblesville and Carmel. Monitor whether they expand to Fishers, Westfield, or Zionsville — those pages will compete for the exact geo-modified queries where Raptor should be dominant. If Raptor's own location pages are thinner, this is a near-term SEO task worth prioritizing before Taylor fills the gap.
