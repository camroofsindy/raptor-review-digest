Competitive Intelligence Brief: Big Sky Roofing

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed May 5, 2025

1. Who They Are

Big Sky Roofing LLC was founded on May 20, 2021, and is owned and operated by Seth Hobbs.

The company was co-developed by Seth Hobbs and Mitch Widau — Seth holds a construction management degree with a background in real estate, while Mitch brings a finance degree and experience building a digital media tech company.

Based in Brownsburg, Indiana, Big Sky positions itself as the go-to provider of residential and commercial roof replacement, storm damage restoration, siding, and gutters across the Indianapolis metro area.

2. Estimated Scale

ZoomInfo estimates 5–9 employees with under $500K in annual revenue, with a corporate footprint of 1–10 people at their Brownsburg office.

Confidence: low — ZoomInfo's sub-$500K figure is almost certainly stale and understates actual throughput for a company with 323 Google reviews in under 4 years of operation.

BuildZoom records show 13 permitted projects and approximately 11 projects over the past two years

, suggesting the majority of their volume is residential and likely unpermitted or pulled under the homeowner's name, which is common in Hendricks County. A more realistic revenue estimate based on review velocity and ticket size norms for the Indy market: $1.5M–$3M annually. Confidence: medium.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Indeed shows salary data for a Residential Supervisor role in Brownsburg, with an estimated annual pay of approximately $44,725

— suggesting they have posted or filled this role within the past 24 months.

ZoomInfo notes that Big Sky Roofing is expanding and has recently hired new employees

, though specific role titles are paywalled. No active job postings were found on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter as of this refresh. The Residential Supervisor data point is the most actionable signal: it implies they are trying to build a supervisory layer between Seth and the install crews — a classic sign of early scaling friction. Watch for a sales rep hire next.

Their Yelp profile exists but shows minimal review activity — no discernible Yelp rating volume to compare against their 323 Google reviews, which is a textbook review-funneling pattern: they are very deliberately steering customers to Google only.

Facebook shows 523 page likes with 11 currently talking about the page

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Querying ChatGPT and Gemini for "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville," and "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana" does not surface Big Sky Roofing by name in AI-generated answers. Their geographic anchor (Brownsburg/Hendricks County) limits their entity footprint in generative results, which tend to favor contractors with broader metro service area signals, editorial backlinks, and structured FAQ content. This is not surprising given their lack of a blog or local press mentions — AI engines have nothing to cite. This is a clean opportunity for Raptor, which has a stronger review volume base and can build AEO-optimized content targeting the suburb-level queries Big Sky is not answering.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Big Sky beats Raptor: Tighter geographic identity in Hendricks County — they own the Brownsburg/Avon homeowner's mental real estate in a way that requires Raptor to earn ground west of I-465. Their co-founder Mitch Widau's digital media background suggests a more sophisticated approach to web presence than the typical owner-operator, even if execution has been uneven. Their 0.76 owner response rate on Google reviews signals disciplined reputation management.

Where Raptor beats them: 664 reviews vs. 323 — nearly double the social proof. 5.0 vs. 4.9 — a full tenth-star gap that matters in Google Map Pack ranking signals and AI citation preferences. Raptor has no BBB accreditation gap to defend. Raptor's premium positioning is clearly articulated; Big Sky's "value-driven" and "outstanding prices" language is a race to the bottom that will eventually compress their margins.

Most exploitable weakness: Big Sky has zero blog content and no suburb-level landing pages outside Brownsburg — a 6-month content sprint by Raptor targeting Avon, Plainfield, and Zionsville could cut off their westward expansion before it starts.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Owens Corning Preferred status as a backlink engine.

Big Sky's Owens Corning Preferred Contractor profile generates a direct link from owenscorning.com.

Cameron should confirm Raptor's manufacturer certifications are fully leveraged for contractor directory backlinks — GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed all offer this. Each is a high-authority inbound link that Big Sky already has and Raptor should match or exceed.

  1. Extended-hours availability as a positioning signal.

Big Sky lists availability 6:30 AM to 8:30 PM, seven days a week.

Whether real or just a listing setting, it shows up prominently in search results and signals accessibility. If Raptor's GBP hours are narrower, closing that perception gap costs nothing.

  1. Facebook content focused on communication as a differentiator. Big Sky's most recent Facebook post explicitly called out competitors who don't call back, positioning responsiveness as a brand pillar. This is a smart move in a category with poor baseline communication. Raptor should be doing this more aggressively — video testimonials specifically about responsiveness and follow-through would reinforce the "we don't outsource accountability" message at the top of the funnel where Big Sky is actively fishing.
