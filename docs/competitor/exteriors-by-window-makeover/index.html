Competitive Intelligence Brief: Exteriors by Window Makeover

Raptor Roofing | Updated: Tuesday, May 20, 2025

1. Who They Are

Window Makeover has been serving central Indiana for over 23 years

, with the roofing and exteriors arm — Exteriors by Window Makeover, LLC —

incorporated June 20, 2022, with business operations formally started July 22, 2022.

Co-owners are Craig Harrell and Dave Benigni

, operating out of 6802 Hillsdale Ct, Indianapolis, IN 46250.

The company positions itself as a full-service exterior and interior home improvement provider, spanning windows, doors, roofing, siding, and gutters

— making roofing a relatively recent add-on to an established window brand with significant legacy trust equity.

2. Estimated Scale

BBB lists 17 employees for the Window Makeover parent entity

(confidence: medium — BBB figures are self-reported and may not fully capture the Exteriors LLC headcount separately).

LinkedIn lists the company size at 11–50 employees

. Given the multi-trade model and a VP of Sales on staff (see Section 3), a working estimate of 20–30 total staff across both entities is reasonable. Annual revenue is likely in the $3M–$6M range across all trades, with roofing representing a minority share given it was added post-2022 — confidence: low; no ZoomInfo or BuildZoom revenue figure is publicly indexed.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

LinkedIn shows a recently posted "Door to Door Salesperson" listing at Exteriors by Window Makeover

— a canvassing/D2D role signals they are actively building a street-level lead generation operation, a classic storm-chasing playbook move.

A VP of Sales role under the name Gary Ormsby also appears on LinkedIn

, indicating they've brought in senior sales leadership. No marketing, SEO, or ops director postings were found in the last 30 days. The D2D canvasser hire is the most telling signal — they are burning cash on volume acquisition, not brand building.

4. Off-Google Presence

Facebook page shows 388 likes with only 2 people currently talking about it

— low engagement for a company this age. No active Facebook Ad Library presence was surfaced in search results, suggesting limited or no paid social spend.

BBB lists them as accredited with an A+ rating

since September 2023 — clean record, no complaints surfaced.

Nextdoor presence is active, with local homeowners citing them for roofing, siding, gutters, and insurance claim work

, and neighbors specifically noting they value a single-point-of-contact for multi-trade projects. Yelp presence exists at the same address but shows no meaningful review volume or star rating data — the listing exists primarily as a citation anchor rather than an active review channel. The discrepancy between a 4.8 Google rating and a near-dormant Yelp profile is a textbook sign of deliberate Google review funneling with zero investment in platform diversification.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

The company runs two separate domains — windowmakeoverinc.com (the primary, content-heavier site) and exteriorsbywm.com (a thin, near-placeholder domain) — which creates a split authority problem.

ZoomInfo confirms the site stack runs on WordPress with Cloudflare optimization

, but no evidence of structured schema markup, local service schema, or FAQ schema was surfaced.

Their listing on F.C. Tucker's Home Services directory

(homeservices.talktotucker.com) is a legitimate high-DA backlink. Blog content exists on the roofing and repair pages but is generic, thin, and not targeting long-tail Indy suburb queries (Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville).

Their Andersen Windows certified dealer status since 2019

gives them one strong manufacturer backlink — a citation type Raptor should be replicating via GAF, Owens Corning, or CertainTeed programs.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

No citation of Exteriors by Window Makeover was returned in any AI engine search result for "best roofers Indianapolis," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." The AI search results returned ChatGPT tool pages, not local business citations — confirming that neither this competitor nor most Indy roofing contractors have broken through into GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) citation territory. This is a wide-open lane. The company's split-domain strategy, thin suburb-specific content, and lack of schema markup are structural reasons they will not appear in AI-generated answers anytime soon. Raptor's opportunity to own this space before any competitor gets there is live right now.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Legacy brand recognition from 23+ years under the Window Makeover name gives them cross-sell trust with homeowners who first came to them for windows — a warm pipeline Raptor cannot easily replicate. Their F.C. Tucker partnership is also a referral channel Cameron should be watching closely.

Where Raptor beats them: 673 reviews at 5.0 stars vs. 321 reviews at 4.8 is not even a close comparison — Raptor has more than double the review volume and a perfect score. Review velocity is also diverging sharply: 0 new reviews in the last 7 days and only 3 in the last 30 for Window Makeover signals either customer fatigue with leaving reviews or a stalling post-job follow-up system. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" premium positioning is also meaningfully differentiated from Window Makeover's multi-trade generalist model.

Most exploitable weakness: Their D2D canvassing hire and generalist positioning make them look like a volume shop — Cameron can run direct contrast messaging around the single-trade specialist authority angle and own it before Window Makeover can pivot.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. F.C. Tucker Home Services directory listing.

Window Makeover's listing on Tucker's home services network

is a high-trust referral channel tied directly to real estate transactions. Cameron should pursue a comparable listing or partnership with Tucker or a competing real estate brokerage — this is a warm lead pipeline that converts well at point-of-sale for new homeowners.

  1. Andersen/manufacturer certification backlink model.

Their certified Andersen Windows Dealer status

earns a manufacturer-grade backlink and adds credibility signals. Raptor should be maximizing GAF Master Elite or Owens Corning Platinum preferred contractor status for equivalent domain authority and AEO trust signals — AI engines weight manufacturer citations heavily.

  1. Monitor their D2D canvassing territory. A new door-to-door salesperson hire means they are about to knock doors in storm-affected Indy zip codes. Cameron should geo-target paid search and LSA spend in the same zip codes preemptively — when a homeowner gets a D2D pitch from Window Makeover, Raptor should already be showing up on their phone when they Google to verify.
