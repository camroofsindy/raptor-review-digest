Rees Restoration — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Updated: April 26, 2025

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2014 by Johnny Rees

,

Rees Restoration is a family-owned company

headquartered in Greenwood, IN.

They specialize in roofing, siding, windows, and storm damage restoration, serving both residential and commercial clients across Central Indiana

. Their primary differentiator is a multi-trade exterior model — they position themselves as a one-stop shop and lean heavily into insurance claims navigation and storm restoration as lead acquisition channels.

2. Estimated Scale

Salary.com estimates 11–20 employees at Rees Restoration Inc.

Confidence: low (self-reported data range is broad; actual field headcount is likely higher given review volume and multi-rep sales model).

Revenue is estimated at $1M–$5M annually according to Salary.com.

Confidence: low — this bracket almost certainly understates actual revenue for a company with 579 Google reviews and a documented multi-rep sales force; real revenue likely runs $4M–$8M.

BuildZoom shows 32 permitted projects totaling $1,081,000 in permit value

— a floor number only, since storm/insurance replacements frequently go unpermitted in Indiana.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Rees Restoration's careers page is actively posting a Field Estimator role, with applications directed to info@reesrestorationindy.com.

Indeed reflects only 5 past and present job postings historically

, suggesting they run a lean hiring operation that likely relies on word-of-mouth recruiting within the sales rep community. No marketing, ops leadership, or digital/SEO roles were found active in the last 30 days on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter. The Field Estimator post is notable: it signals either volume growth requiring faster quoting capacity or rep turnover — both worth watching.

4. Off-Google Presence

Rees Restoration has been BBB-accredited since September 25, 2015, and is listed out of Greenwood, IN.

They claim an A+ BBB rating and an Angi "Super Service Award."

However, the BBB complaint file tells a different story.

At least one complainant states they are at the point of suing Rees Restoration over a roof replacement.

A BBB reviewer writes they were sold the wrong shingle color and the gutter issue was never resolved, and that the company does not return phone calls.

ZoomInfo confirms they use Facebook Pixel and Facebook Web Custom Audiences

, indicating active Facebook ad spend, though no specific creatives are publicly visible. No significant Yelp or Reddit presence was surfaced. The gap between 4.7 on Google (579 reviews) and active BBB complaint patterns is a classic review-funneling setup — reps push satisfied customers to Google, dissatisfied ones end up on BBB.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Rees operates a multi-page, service-area-structured site at reesrestorationindy.net with dedicated location pages (e.g., /indianapolis-in/, /indianapolis-in/roofing/replacement/) — a solid local SEO architecture.

They are publishing blog content tied to their HOVER tool, targeting Central Indiana homeowners with how-to visualization content.

Their blog cadence appears thin — one confirmed post from September 2025 on the HOVER tool.

BuildZoom scores them at 105, ranking in the top 8% of Indiana licensed contractors

, which aids E-E-A-T signals. No evidence of structured schema markup, press backlinks, or AEO-optimized FAQ content was found. Their site's domain name (reesrestorationindy.net — a .net, not .com) is a persistent authority disadvantage versus competitors on .com.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

No independent citations of Rees Restoration by name were found in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini responses to the target queries ("best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana"). Their thin blog output, .net domain, near-zero press backlinks, and absence of structured FAQ/schema content mean they have essentially no AEO footprint. This is a clean opening for Raptor: structured content with proper schema, authoritative local press mentions, and entity-building across AI-indexed sources can capture the generative engine layer that Rees is completely ignoring.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Rees beats Raptor: Raw review volume (579 vs. 664 is close, and they have a multi-year head start). Their multi-trade model (siding, windows, gutters, roofing under one roof) lets a single sales rep generate a larger average ticket and more referral surface area per job. Their HOVER technology claim —

"The Only Roofer in Central Indiana Using HOVER Technology"

— is an aggressive tech differentiator in the estimate/trust phase, regardless of whether the exclusivity claim is strictly accurate.

Where Raptor beats Rees: A 5.0 star rating at 664 reviews is categorically cleaner than a 4.7. Owner response rate of 0.52 at Rees is mediocre and signals management inattention post-sale. BBB complaint patterns and the warranty-dispute paper trail are exploitable in sales conversations. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is a direct surgical strike at Rees's documented post-install responsiveness failures.

Most exploitable weakness: Their 0.52 owner response rate combined with active BBB complaints about unanswered calls and warranty disputes signals a sales-first, service-second operation — exactly the trust gap Raptor should be naming directly in sales enablement and comparison landing page content.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The HOVER technology play.

Rees markets HOVER as delivering "fast, accurate roof measurements without climbing on your home," creating 3D models from photos for transparent estimates.

Whether Raptor adopts HOVER or a competing tool (Hover, Roofr, EagleView interactive), Rees has proven this is a conversion and differentiation lever worth owning. If Raptor can claim this or a comparable tool, the "only roofer" exclusivity Rees promotes disappears.

  1. Insurance claims navigation as a top-of-funnel content pillar. Review after review on Rees's site features reps walking homeowners through the adjuster process. This is intentional: storm damage + insurance claims is their primary lead engine. Raptor should build dedicated content (blog posts, videos, landing pages) around "how to file a roof insurance claim in Indianapolis" — a query with high commercial intent that Rees is capturing relationally but not yet owning in organic search.

  2. Monitor their BBB complaint trajectory.

At least one BBB complaint shows the business failed to respond to a dispute.

If that pattern accelerates, it becomes a direct sales tool: Cameron and Patrick should have a one-page leave-behind ready that contrasts Raptor's documented response rate and 5.0 rating against Rees's 4.7 and active warranty complaints — without naming Rees by name, but letting the numbers speak.
