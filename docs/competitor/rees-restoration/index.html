Competitive Intelligence Brief: Bone Dry Roofing

1. Who They Are

Bone Dry Roofing was founded in 1989 by Gene Judd, who started the company out of a small garage in Speedway, Indiana.

President Gene Judd leads the operation alongside CIO Judd Haag, CMO Matt Tyner, COO Billy Smith, and CFO Tony Merlie.

Headquartered in Indianapolis, they now operate 11 total offices across five states — family-owned and not a franchise — and have built one of the largest residential roofing companies in the Midwest.

Their differentiation play is brand ubiquity and multi-service bundling (roofing, gutters, masonry, insulation, solar, siding), which positions them as a one-call home-services company rather than a specialist roofer.
