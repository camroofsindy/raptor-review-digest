Competitive Intelligence Brief — ARAC Roof It Forward

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed: May 19, 2025

1. Who They Are

In business since 2005, ARAC Roof It Forward has many years of experience offering high-quality roofing services to local residential and commercial customers.

They operate out of

15499 Stony Creek Way, Noblesville, IN 46060

and are a franchise/multi-market operation —

founded in 2005, ARAC Roof It Forward is based out of Woodstock, Georgia

, with the Indianapolis location functioning as a regional branch. Their core differentiator is cause-based marketing:

through their Roof It Forward program they have made a commitment to give back to the community a portion of every dollar that comes into the company through partnerships with local charities.

On the Indianapolis-specific site, that charity is

Peyton Manning Children's Hospital.

, suggesting they bootstrapped most of the operation and the Indiana branch is not a separately capitalized entity.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

ARAC runs a notably aggressive geo-page SEO strategy. Their domain roofitforward.com hosts dedicated landing pages for Indianapolis, Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Greenwood, Zionsville, Brownsburg, Plainfield, Anderson, and Greenfield —

covering a sprawling service area across all major Indy suburbs.

They also operate a second Indiana-specific domain, roofitforwardindiana.com, creating potential duplicate-content risk but also two shots at ranking.

They are an Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor

, which earns backlinks from the Owens Corning contractor directory — a legitimate DA-boosting link. Their site copy is repetitive and thin on blog/thought-leadership content; no evidence of schema markup investment or AEO-targeted FAQ structures was found.

Their free video inspection landing page framing ("we bring the roof down to you") is a conversion hook

that could rank for intent-rich queries — Raptor should audit whether that page is outranking equivalent Raptor content.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on current AI training data and search patterns for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," and "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville," ARAC Roof It Forward has moderate AI citation risk due to three structural advantages: (1) their multi-domain, multi-suburb page setup provides the geographic specificity that LLMs use to match local queries; (2)

they are a Super Service Award winning company on Angi

, a platform LLMs frequently pull contractor citations from; and (3) their Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status is a certification signal that appears in structured directories. However, their stagnant Google review velocity (2 reviews in 90 days) and absence of recent press or blog content means their AI footprint is not growing — it is coasting on legacy citations. Raptor's 673 reviews at 5.0 stars with an active cadence is a stronger AEO signal if properly structured with FAQ schema and location-specific content.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where ARAC beats Raptor: Cause-marketing narrative is deeply embedded —

a portion of every dollar goes to fund a local beneficiary, and this is not a one-time deal but a percentage of revenue from every completed job.

That story is emotionally sticky and gives sales reps a closing tool Raptor does not currently have at scale. They also have a wider suburban page footprint and Owens Corning Platinum status as a third-party credibility anchor.

Where Raptor beats ARAC: 673 reviews vs. 310, a full 0.3-star gap (5.0 vs. 4.7), active review velocity vs. ARAC's near-flatline (2 reviews in 90 days), and no credible post-install complaint record visible for Raptor vs. active 2025 BBB complaints for ARAC. Raptor's accountability positioning is substantiated; ARAC's is contradicted by its own BBB file.

Most exploitable weakness: ARAC's canvassing-heavy, sales-rep-driven model generates exactly the kind of subcontractor accountability gaps documented in their BBB complaints — Cameron should be running those complaint narratives as indirect contrast positioning in sales conversations immediately.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The video inspection UVP.

ARAC offers a free video inspection where they "bring the roof down to you," and the interaction can be done remotely.

This is a strong friction-reduction tactic that converts risk-averse homeowners who won't schedule an in-person visit. Raptor should evaluate a comparable remote-first inspection offer with a distinct name and hook.

  1. Owens Corning Platinum leverage.

ARAC markets their Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status as something that lets them offer extended warranties that 99% of other roofers cannot, and directly challenges prospects to ask competitors if they hold the same status.

This is a sharp competitive wedge. If Raptor holds comparable certifications, the same challenge-to-compare framing should be on every estimate and sales page.

  1. Cause-marketing architecture.

ARAC's mission of donating a portion of every dollar earned to local charities through their Roof It Forward program

is baked into the brand name itself — impossible to ignore. Raptor does not need to copy the charity angle, but a similarly named and tangible community commitment (tied to a Noblesville/Hamilton County cause) would neutralize this advantage in head-to-head sales situations and give Cameron a story that AI engines will pick up as a local relevance signal.
