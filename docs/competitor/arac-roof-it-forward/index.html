ARAC Roof It Forward — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated August 11, 2025

1. Who They Are

ARAC Roof It Forward has been in business since 2005

, operating out of 15499 Stony Creek Way, Noblesville, IN 46060.

They describe themselves as a "trusted, faith-based local roofing contractor" specializing in full-service residential and commercial roofing, exterior remodeling, roof repair, and complete roof replacement.

Their differentiation anchor is a cause-marketing model:

they donate a portion of every dollar earned to local charities through their Roof It Forward program.

They are a franchise-style multi-market operation headquartered in Woodstock, GA, with the Indianapolis location functioning as one of several regional branches.

2. Estimated Scale

SimplyHired/Indeed data places the company at 51–200 employees enterprise-wide and revenue of $25M–$100M (USD) across all markets, with corporate headquarters listed at 296 Bell Park Dr, Woodstock, GA 30188.

The Indiana branch specifically is a subset of that. Confidence: medium — these figures reflect the full ARAC national entity, not the Noblesville location in isolation. Local branch headcount is likely 10–25 field and sales staff based on job volume and permit activity. Revenue attribution for the Indianapolis branch alone is not publicly broken out.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

The clearest active signal is in field sales and canvassing leadership.

ARAC is actively recruiting an experienced Canvassing Manager — described as "the field general for the canvassers" — responsible for managing a canvassing team and ensuring they hit goals.

The role involves creating and implementing canvassing strategies and reporting weekly progress to senior leadership.

Indeed currently shows only 1 open job nationally,

but

Glassdoor lists 24 open positions across all ARAC markets.

The canvassing management hire is the most strategically relevant signal — they are doubling down on door-knocking as a primary lead gen channel rather than pulling it back.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

ARAC operates two Indiana-facing domains — roofitforward.com/indianapolis and a standalone roofitforwardindiana.com — which creates a split-domain authority problem. The main domain has dedicated location pages for Noblesville, Indianapolis, and other submarkets, and produces city-specific content (completed project pages by shingle color/address).

They self-describe as a BBB-accredited roofer with "over 1,000 positive reviews on Google Reviews, Angi, and more," holding Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status, and claim to be "among the top 1% of roofers in the country."

Their content strategy leans on product-level landing pages (commercial roofing, roof repair) but the blog and schema signals appear thin — no evidence of structured FAQ schema or AEO-optimized Q&A content surfaced in search results. The split domain is an exploitable SEO liability for Raptor if Cameron pushes consolidated, schema-rich city pages.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on available search data, ARAC Roof It Forward does not appear to have dominant AI citation presence for Indianapolis-area roofing queries. Their brand name "Roof It Forward" is highly distinctive and theoretically memorable to LLMs, but their content posture (thin blog, split domains, minimal third-party editorial coverage) limits citation eligibility. Their Angi and HomeAdvisor profiles are likely their strongest LLM training signals given the volume of structured review data on those platforms. No local press backlinks or editorial mentions surfaced in results that would trigger AI citation. Raptor's path to generative engine dominance is clearer — consolidated domain, premium positioning language, and structured local content will outperform ARAC's fragmented setup in AI answer retrieval.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where ARAC beats Raptor: Their faith-based, cause-marketing identity ("Roof It Forward" / donating to Peyton Manning Children's Hospital and local charities) is a highly differentiated emotional hook that resonates strongly in Hamilton County's demographics — it gives a low-information buyer a feel-good reason to choose them that has nothing to do with quality. Their Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status is also a legitimate warranty differentiator they market aggressively.

Where Raptor beats ARAC: 697 reviews at 5.0 vs. 316 at 4.8 — Raptor wins on both volume and rating. ARAC's review velocity is cratering (0 in 7 days, 10 in 90 days) while their BBB complaints surface real execution concerns around subcontracted crews. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is a direct counter to ARAC's documented on-site labor issues.

Most exploitable weakness: The BBB complaint pattern —

workers throwing materials across the roof and a crew that could not communicate in English with the homeowner

— is a credibility fracture that Raptor can exploit through owned content contrasting in-house crew accountability vs. subcontracted labor.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The Canvassing Manager hire is a volume play — prepare for it. ARAC is building out a structured, managed door-knocking operation. After a storm event, they will flood Hamilton County neighborhoods systematically. Cameron should have a pre-built rapid-response storm canvassing playbook ready so Raptor can be first on doors in Fishers, Noblesville, and Carmel zip codes before ARAC's team activates.

  2. Cause-marketing attribution at the estimate stage.

ARAC's Roof It Forward program commits a portion of every dollar to local charities and community organizations.

They embed this in every sales conversation. Raptor should audit whether its own community giving story is surfacing at the estimate stage — not just on the website — because this is where ARAC converts undecided buyers emotionally.

  1. Owens Corning Platinum co-branding on paid and organic.

ARAC markets its Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status as access to "extended warranty options that 99% of ALL other roofing companies can't" offer.

This is a concrete, defensible claim they run hard in all channels. Cameron should confirm Raptor's OC tier and ensure it is equally prominent in LSA ad copy, Google Business description, and estimate collateral — or identify a comparable manufacturer credential that can fill that trust slot.
