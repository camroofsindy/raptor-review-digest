ARAC Roof It Forward — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated July 21, 2026

1. Who They Are

ARAC Roof It Forward has been in business since 2005

, operating out of

15499 Stony Creek Way, Noblesville, IN, serving the Greater Indianapolis area

.

The Indiana franchise is led by President John Phillips

, with

parent company ARAC headquartered in Woodstock, Georgia

— making this a franchise operation, not a locally owned independent. Their core differentiator is philanthropic brand positioning:

ARAC exists to "honor God through giving," donating a portion of every dollar to local charities through the Roof It Forward program

, with a named partnership with

Peyton Manning Children's Hospital

.

2. Estimated Scale

Growjo estimates annual revenue of $32.8M and 113 employees across the company.

The Indiana franchise footprint is smaller —

VisualVisitor puts the Indiana entity at 48 employees with $10–50M revenue

, while

SimplyHired and Indeed both list 51–200 employees company-wide.

Confidence: medium. These are aggregated third-party estimates across all ARAC markets; the Indiana-specific P&L is not publicly disclosed.

Employee count dropped -7% last year per Growjo

— a meaningful contraction signal worth tracking.

ZoomInfo notes a funding round of under $5M, last closed May 2020.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

ARAC is actively recruiting a Canvassing Manager — responsible for managing a door-knock team and meeting canvassing volume goals.

ZoomInfo flags that ARAC has more open roles in Sales than at any point in the past 12 months.

Glassdoor shows 24 open positions company-wide.

Indeed currently lists 1 open job for the Indiana market.

The pattern is clear: they are doubling down on field sales and canvassing infrastructure — a storm-chaser-style growth model — while Indiana-specific marketing or ops leadership hires are not visible.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

ARAC runs a multi-domain strategy — roofitforward.com/indianapolis as the primary market page and a separate roofitforwardindiana.com domain — which can dilute link equity and create indexation conflicts.

Their own site claims 16+ years of experience and touts reviews across Google and Angi

, but the blog and content depth appear thin. The Noblesville location pages (roofitforward.com/noblesville-in) are essentially photo galleries of completed jobs with product spec copy — low editorial value, weak AEO signal.

Their Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status is front-and-center in their positioning

, and

they claim recognition as "top 1% of roofers in the US" by Owens Corning

— a credential they work into web copy repeatedly. Schema markup and structured FAQ content are not evident from public-facing pages. The split-domain architecture is an exploitable SEO weakness for a focused single-domain competitor like Raptor.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on known AI training signals and the data gathered: ARAC's philanthropic hook ("Roof It Forward," Peyton Manning Children's Hospital donation) gives them a memorable brand narrative that AI models may cite when prompted about "community-focused roofers Indianapolis." However, their review volume (318 at 4.7) and split-domain structure work against them in generative engine citation, where AI tends to surface companies with consolidated authority signals, high review volume on a single platform, and clear geographic schema.

Their own LinkedIn posts note 1,000+ total reviews in Atlanta and 1,300+ combined across all markets

— meaning the Indianapolis market is underdeveloped relative to their home market and unlikely to dominate AI answers for Indy-specific queries. Raptor's 690 reviews at 5.0, concentrated on one market and one domain, presents a stronger citation profile for AEO.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where ARAC beats Raptor: Their philanthropic brand story (Peyton Manning Children's Hospital, documented $600K+ donated) is emotionally resonant and differentiates them in a commodity category — harder for Raptor to replicate without its own giving infrastructure. Their Owens Corning Platinum Preferred credential and Angi Super Service Award give them third-party validation across multiple platforms.

Where Raptor beats ARAC: 690 reviews vs. 318 — more than double the social proof volume, and a 5.0 vs. 4.7 rating that signals tighter quality control. ARAC's franchise structure means local accountability is diffuse; Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is a direct counter. Their Facebook page is functionally dead and their Yelp presence is negligible.

Most exploitable weakness:

ARAC's headcount declined 7% last year

, and their hiring signals are concentrated in canvassing/door-knock roles — a high-churn, low-trust sales model that generates consistent negative word-of-mouth if the canvassers are aggressive, which is a reputation risk Cameron should actively monitor and contrast against Raptor's inbound/referral positioning.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The charity anchor:

A portion of every dollar ARAC earns is directed to one of six local beneficiaries in their markets.

Raptor does not need to match the dollar amount — but formalizing a named local giving partner (announced, trackable, marketed) would neutralize ARAC's biggest emotional differentiator and give Cameron a PR story that AI engines and local press will cite.

  1. Video roof inspection as a lead conversion tool:

ARAC offers a free video inspection where they "bring the roof down to you" and walk the homeowner through findings remotely.

This is a low-friction lead capture mechanism that reduces the barrier to a first appointment — worth building into Raptor's sales funnel if not already present.

  1. Canvassing manager infrastructure:

ARAC is actively building out a managed canvassing operation with dedicated leadership.

Whether Raptor wants to canvass or not, Cameron should understand ARAC's post-storm neighborhood saturation playbook and have a counter-response ready — including timed social ads and Google LSA bid increases — for the same storm-affected ZIP codes ARAC's canvassers are working.
