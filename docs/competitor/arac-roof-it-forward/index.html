Competitor Brief: ARAC Roof It Forward

Raptor Roofing Intelligence Dashboard — Refreshed May 19, 2025
Address: 15499 Stony Creek Way, Noblesville, IN 46060 | Web: roofitforward.com

1. Who They Are

In business since 2005, ARAC Roof It Forward offers high-quality roofing services to local residential and commercial customers.

The Indiana entity is owned by John Phillips (Owner) and Craig Stevens (Co-Owner), with Kim Stevens as Office Manager and Mike Susits as National General Manager

— a structure that suggests a franchised or multi-market operation rather than a true local independent.

ARAC's defining brand move is a faith-based mission, committing to give back a portion of every dollar through partnerships with local charities

, with the Indiana market specifically tied to

Peyton Manning Children's Hospital as a named beneficiary.

2. Estimated Scale

Growjo estimates ARAC Roof It Forward at approximately $35.1M in annual revenue with 121 employees across the enterprise.

The Indiana market is a branch of a larger national operation headquartered in Georgia.

SimplyHired categorizes the company at 51–200 employees and $25M–$100M in revenue

, while

VisualVisitor lists 48 employees and $10–50M revenue for the Indiana entity, with John Phillips listed as President.

Confidence: medium. Figures span a wide range across data providers due to the franchise structure; the Indiana-specific headcount is likely in the 40–60 range.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

ARAC is actively recruiting a Canvassing Manager on ZipRecruiter, responsible for managing a team of canvassers and ensuring they meet their goals.

ZoomInfo flags that ARAC has more open Sales roles than at any time in the past 12 months, with technologies including Salesforce and Google Reviews cited in postings.

Indeed currently lists 1 open role company-wide.

The Canvassing Manager hire is the most strategically significant signal — it means they are formalizing and scaling door-to-door operations heading into storm season, which is their primary customer acquisition engine. No marketing, ops leadership, or digital-specific hires were identified.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

ARAC operates two domains — roofitforward.com and roofitforwardin.com — which splits link equity and creates brand confusion. The primary site has city-specific landing pages (Indianapolis, Noblesville, Carmel, Fishers) with repetitive, thin content — each page is largely a loop of Owens Corning product specs and identical CTAs.

They do list service cities explicitly including Noblesville, Carmel, Fishers, Greenfield, Indianapolis, Greenwood, Plainfield, Anderson, and Zionsville

, which covers geo-SEO basics. However, there is no evidence of a genuine blog strategy, earned local press backlinks, or structured FAQ/schema content targeting AEO queries.

They claim Owens Corning recognition as one of the top 1% of roofers in the U.S.

, which is a trust signal they embed sitewide — a tactic worth noting. Their domain authority relative to Raptor is unverified from available data, but the dual-domain split is a structural weakness any competent SEO should exploit against them.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on query patterns across ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini for terms like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," and "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville": ARAC Roof It Forward does appear in some AI-generated lists, driven primarily by their volume of third-party platform presence (Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB). However, their brand surfaces inconsistently — the dual-domain structure, thin page content, and near-total absence of authoritative local press coverage limits their AEO footprint. They are not a dominant AI-cited brand for the Hamilton County suburbs (Carmel, Fishers, Zionsville) — the exact geography where Raptor has the strongest positioning. No strong evidence of structured FAQ content, local citation schema, or AEO-optimized long-form content that would anchor them in LLM responses.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Scale and geographic saturation — a multi-state operation with a National General Manager, an established Angi/HomeAdvisor pipeline, Owens Corning Platinum certification, and a charity-based brand story that resonates with values-driven homeowners in the suburbs. Their door-to-door canvassing infrastructure is active and scaling.

Where Raptor beats them: Review quality and recency are not even close — 673 reviews at 5.0 vs. 310 at 4.7, with zero ARAC Google reviews in 90 days. Raptor's 5.0 rating is the sharpest trust signal in the Indy top 10. BBB complaints about non-English-speaking crews and unresolved property damage directly undercut their "accountability" positioning in a way Raptor can own authentically.

Most exploitable weakness: Their Google review engine has flatlined — zero reviews in 90 days signals operational stagnation or a broken post-job follow-up process, and Cameron should target that exact gap in conquest messaging.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The named charity anchor.

ARAC donates a portion of every job to Peyton Manning Children's Hospital

— a nationally recognized Indy institution. The specificity of naming one marquee charity (vs. generic "giving back") creates a memorable, repeatable sales story. Raptor should evaluate whether it has an equally specific, named community giving commitment that sales reps can deploy at the door and in proposals.

  1. Owens Corning Platinum as a sales weapon.

ARAC leans hard on Platinum Preferred status as a warranty differentiator, actively prompting prospects to ask any roofer whether they hold that status.

This is clean sales enablement — pre-arming prospects with a disqualifying question for competitors. Cameron should audit whether Raptor's manufacturer certifications are being deployed with the same specificity and aggression in the sales process.

  1. Canvassing Manager infrastructure.

ARAC is actively hiring a Canvassing Manager to oversee door-to-door teams and implement strategies to increase canvassing effectiveness.

If storm events hit Hamilton County this summer, their boots-on-ground capacity will outpace any digital-only response. Raptor should have a documented playbook for rapid neighborhood canvassing post-storm — even if the model is leaner — before that gap becomes a closed-deal gap.
