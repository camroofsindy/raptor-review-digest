ARAC Roof It Forward — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely | Refreshed: June 30, 2026

1. Who They Are

ARAC was founded by John Phillips, a Coles College of Business graduate, on the philosophy of doing what they say with excellence.

In business since 2005, ARAC Roof It Forward serves local residential and commercial customers from their Noblesville, IN office at 15499 Stony Creek Way.

Their stated mission is to "honor God through giving," with a commitment to donate a portion of every dollar earned to local charities through the Roof It Forward program

— a faith-based charitable brand wrapper that functions as their primary market differentiator.

The BBB lists Craig Stevens as Co-Owner

, placing two named principals in the organization alongside founder Phillips.

2. Estimated Scale

As of August 2024, LeadIQ estimated approximately 72 employees, covering roofing, siding, gutter, and painting services across Greater Indianapolis.

Confidence: medium (LeadIQ data is ~10 months stale).

Indeed independently brackets them at 51–200 employees.

ZoomInfo lists 96 employees total across the corporate entity, headquartered in Woodstock, Georgia

— the Indiana operation is a franchise/branch unit of that larger national ARAC platform. Revenue for the Indy branch is not publicly disclosed; at typical residential roofing revenue-per-head benchmarks ($200K–$250K/employee), a 72-person operation suggests a rough Indianapolis-market revenue run rate of $14M–$18M annually. Confidence: low — this is a modeled estimate with no verified filing.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

ZoomInfo flags that ARAC currently has more open Sales roles than at any point in the prior 12 months, with Salesforce and Google Reviews cited as active tech stack tools.

ZipRecruiter shows an active posting for a Canvassing Manager — responsible for managing a door-to-door team and reporting weekly progress to senior leadership.

Indeed shows 1 open role indexed to the national entity.

The Canvassing Manager hire is the most strategically significant signal: they are scaling D2D lead generation, which is a high-volume, low-yield play typically used to prop up a pipeline that isn't converting organically at scale. Watch for increased neighborhood canvassing in Hamilton County storm corridors.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

ARAC holds Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor status, which they lean on heavily as a warranty differentiator across all web content.

Their site at roofitforward.com runs geo-targeted landing pages for Indianapolis, Noblesville, Carmel, Fishers, Greenwood, and surrounding markets — a standard but competent local SEO architecture.

Their confirmed tech stack includes Google Tag Manager, Google Maps, jQuery, New Relic, and Slick

— a mid-tier setup with analytics instrumentation but no evidence of advanced schema or AEO-specific structured data.

LinkedIn activity shows prior radio advertising on Extra 106.3 FM's All Four Seasons Home Show

— an offline channel that boosts branded search volume and can improve AI citation frequency. Their content volume is high by quantity but thin by depth; most pages are product/city pages, not authoritative long-form content that earns backlinks. No visible local press backlinks surfaced in search results.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

ARAC Roof It Forward does not appear to be a dominant name-drop in AI-generated answers for key Indy roofing queries tested (best roofers Indianapolis, roof replacement Carmel Indiana, roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville). Their multi-market ARAC parent brand has national name equity, but the Indiana franchise operates under a secondary domain (roofitforwardin.com) alongside the primary roofitforward.com — that domain fragmentation hurts consolidated authority signals that LLMs use for citation. The faith-based giving narrative is a distinctive brand signal that could get them cited in "socially responsible contractor" queries, but only if that content is structured and linked at sufficient depth. Right now, it reads as website copy, not as authoritative content. This is an open lane for Raptor to own the AI answer layer first.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where ARAC beats Raptor: Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status gives them a manufactuer-backed warranty story Raptor cannot match without the same certification; the faith/charity brand is emotionally resonant in Hamilton County's demographic and hard to out-narrative directly. Their multi-city landing page infrastructure and radio presence give them broader passive reach.

Where Raptor beats ARAC: 681 reviews vs. 314 — Raptor has more than double the Google review volume; 5.0 vs. 4.7 is a visible gap at the zero moment of truth; Raptor's 0.9% owner response rate vs. Raptor's premium engagement posture signals a care gap ARAC is actively leaving on the table. Review velocity is nearly dead — only 7 reviews in the last 90 days is a flatline for a company claiming thousands of happy customers.

Most exploitable weakness: A 0.9% owner response rate combined with only 7 Google reviews in 90 days tells the market that ARAC's review engine has stalled — Cameron should make Raptor's review velocity and response discipline a public-facing talking point.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Canvassing Manager hire as storm-season signal. ARAC is actively building a structured D2D canvassing operation. Post-storm, they will flood Hamilton County subdivisions. Raptor needs a rapid-response protocol — whether that's your own canvass team, a hyper-local Google PMA push, or a same-week door-hanger drop in hail-impacted zip codes — to intercept leads before ARAC's boots hit the ground.

  2. Charity tie-in as brand architecture, not just PR.

ARAC publicly announced Q1 2026 donations including $3,262.50 to Camp Sunshine, $150 to a children's cancer center, and $325 to Nemours Children's Health.

These are small dollar amounts but they publish them with specificity, which creates trust and local press hook opportunities. Raptor should consider a similar quantified giving disclosure — tied to a named Indy-area cause — to neutralize this brand moat.

  1. Radio + AI visibility compounding. ARAC's prior use of local radio (Extra 106.3 FM home show) is a tactic worth modeling: radio drives branded search queries, branded search queries feed Google's entity recognition, and entity recognition feeds LLM citation probability. A targeted Indy radio or podcast sponsorship — even a single recurring segment — would accelerate Raptor's generative engine presence faster than blog content alone.
