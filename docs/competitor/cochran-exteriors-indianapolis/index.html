Cochran Exteriors — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated: April 26, 2026

1. Who They Are

Founded in 2010, Cochran Exteriors has built its identity around full exterior remodeling

across the greater Indianapolis market.

The business is co-owned by Ross Cochran and John Cochran, with Andrew Beck serving as Marketing Manager

— meaning they have dedicated in-house marketing leadership, not an agency-only relationship.

Cochran positions itself as a design-forward remodeler, integrating roofing with siding, windows, and curb-appeal-driven projects

, which gives them a larger average ticket and a stickier multi-trade customer relationship than pure-play roofers.

2. Estimated Scale

PitchBook reports 43 total employees.

CIENCE pegs the headcount band at 11–50 and annual revenue in the $10M–$25M range.

RocketReach is more conservative,

placing 2025 annual revenue at $6.8 million.

ZoomInfo notes they received an SBA PPP loan for $1–$2 million from Regions Bank, supporting the retention of 47 jobs

— which corroborates the 40–50 headcount range during peak periods. Confidence: medium. Revenue spread between sources is wide ($6.8M vs. $10M–$25M); the true number is likely $8M–$12M given employee count and volume.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Two active signals worth flagging.

LinkedIn shows an open Commercial Estimator posting in Indianapolis

— a sign they are deliberately growing the commercial side, not just handling opportunistic B2B work.

ZoomInfo flags a Sales Manager opening in Indianapolis

as well, suggesting they are building a middle layer of sales leadership above their consultant ranks.

A prior Residential Sales Consultant post also appeared on LinkedIn

, consistent with a team running a dedicated canvassing/D2D operation. No marketing or ops VP listings found in the last 30 days — Andrew Beck appears to still own that function internally.

4. Off-Google Presence

The cross-platform picture is materially weaker than their Google profile suggests.

Yelp shows 54 reviews at the same Castlewood Dr. address

— that is a 95%+ gap vs. their 1,171 Google reviews, a textbook review-funneling pattern where customers are driven hard to Google and Yelp is left to collect organic (often dissatisfied) reviews unmanaged.

BBB lists them as A+ accredited

, which is clean, but

they carry two BBB office locations — Indianapolis HQ and 48 N. Emerson Ave., Ste. 600, Greenwood, IN 46143

— indicating a deliberate south-side/Greenwood market push.

On Houzz, they carry credentials including Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor, Certified Vinyl Siding Installer, and NRCA membership

, which are legitimately defensible trust signals.

Nextdoor presence is active, with customers praising the team's professionalism and diligence

. BBB complaint thread reveals at least one unresolved workmanship dispute involving a leaking roof and a blocked stove vent —

the business "responded to the dispute but failed to make a good faith effort to resolve it,"

per the BBB's own finding.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Cochran's SEO investment is visible and intentional. Their site runs

Cloudflare CDN and Cloudflare Website Optimization

, indicating performance infrastructure that helps technical Core Web Vitals. They have city/service-specific URL architecture (e.g., /in/roofing, /in/roofing/flat, /service-areas) — solid local landing page coverage.

They publish an interactive Indiana roof cost calculator as a conversion tool

, which captures informational-intent traffic and likely generates a meaningful share of organic leads. They appear in at least two third-party "best roofers Indianapolis 2026" roundups —

ThreeBestRated.com names them in the top three Indianapolis roofing contractors

, and

a Stay Dry Roofing-authored top-10 list includes them with a favorable positioning note

. These third-party citations are exactly what AEO/LLM training data picks up. Domain authority is not confirmed without Moz/Ahrefs access, but the multi-page content depth and third-party link profile suggest they are above-average for an Indy roofing operator.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Cochran is earning meaningful AI citation exposure. The Stay Dry Roofing top-10 list (published January 2026) and the IndyRoof top-10 list (updated March 2026) both name Cochran explicitly with descriptive positioning language —

noted as an Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor with detailed design consultations as a differentiator

. These structured, recent, third-party roundups are precisely the signal sources that ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity draw from when answering "best roofers Indianapolis." Cochran is likely cited by name in at least some AI answers for broad Indianapolis queries. Their cost calculator and suburb-specific service area pages (/service-areas) also improve the odds of AEO snippet capture for queries like "how much does a roof replacement cost in Indianapolis." Raptor has no equivalent third-party roundup citations confirmed at this time — that is an immediate gap.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Cochran beats Raptor: Raw review volume (1,171 vs. 664), multi-trade service breadth (windows, siding, doors in addition to roofing), in-house marketing manager, active commercial division, and confirmed AI/roundup citation presence. Their Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status and NRCA membership are credentialed trust signals Raptor should match or counter on the website. Where Raptor beats Cochran: A perfect 5.0 star rating vs. Cochran's 4.7 — at scale, that 0.3-point gap is significant and signals a real quality control problem Cochran has not solved. Raptor's owner response rate (0.95) is elite and meaningfully differentiates accountability positioning. BBB complaint history, HomeAdvisor complaints about post-sale communication breakdown, and the workmanship dispute ruling all expose Cochran's weakest flank. Most exploitable weakness: Their own BBB file contains a finding that they failed to resolve a workmanship complaint in good faith — that is a surgical contrast point Raptor can run with in sales conversations and review responses without naming Cochran directly.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

1. The roof cost calculator.

Cochran runs a free Indiana roof cost calculator that delivers instant price estimates

— this is a high-intent lead capture tool that intercepts homeowners in the research phase before they ever request a quote. Cameron should prioritize building a Raptor-branded version targeting Indy-metro zip codes.

2. Multi-location BBB listing strategy.

Cochran runs separate BBB profiles for their Indianapolis HQ and their Greenwood, IN location

. If Raptor plans any geographic expansion toward the south side or Hamilton County, establishing a second BBB presence is a low-cost trust signal with local GBP reinforcement value.

3. Owens Corning Platinum Preferred positioning.

Cochran prominently carries Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor status

— one of the highest manufacturer tiers available and a known AI/roundup citation magnet. If Raptor can attain or already holds an equivalent manufacturer certification, it needs to be front and center in metadata, schema, and every third-party profile — because that is the exact language third-party roundup authors and LLMs pull when deciding whom to include.
