Competitive Intelligence Brief: Trojan Roofing

indytrojanroofing.com | (317) 442-9480 | Indianapolis, IN
Refreshed: June 9, 2025

1. Who They Are

Trojan Roofing, Inc. was incorporated November 5, 2020, with a business start date of January 1, 2020, and is owned and operated by Tyler Morgan.

They have been serving residential and commercial clients throughout the Indianapolis area since 2019.

Their primary positioning is storm/insurance restoration-led — door-to-door canvassing after hail events is a documented acquisition tactic — supplemented by a multi-location digital footprint that lists both downtown Indianapolis (333 Alabama St) and South Side (2345 S Lynhurst Dr) addresses.

They are authorized installers of IKO Dynasty and Owens Corning.

2. Estimated Scale

The indytrojanroofing.com team page lists one Founder/Owner and six Project Manager roles

— but that almost certainly understates actual headcount given active multi-role hiring (see Section 3).

Glassdoor has 12 Indianapolis-area employee reviews on file

, suggesting a team of 15–35 when you include field and sales staff. Confidence: low. Revenue estimate: at 641 Google reviews and a storm-restoration close model, a rough estimate puts them at $3M–$6M annually — plausible for an insurance-claims-heavy shop of this size. Confidence: low. No ZoomInfo or Dun & Bradstreet public revenue data found.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

ZipRecruiter shows active postings for a Field Tech Inspector and Outside Sales Representatives in Indianapolis.

A Recruiting Manager role (Maxwell Bunch) is listed on LinkedIn

, signaling they have internalized their recruiter function — a move typical of companies scaling a commission-based canvassing/sales force fast. No marketing, ops director, or digital lead hires surfaced. The hiring pattern is almost entirely sales and field inspection, which is consistent with a storm-chase volume model rather than a brand-building growth stage.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Indytrojanroofing.com has a low-authority but keyword-dense site architecture. They have built out individual service pages (residential, commercial, roof repair, roof replacement) and geo-targeting subfolders (e.g., /roofing/indianapolis/, with Greenwood referenced as a service city).

A dedicated Greenwood, IN service page exists.

The site embeds Trustindex-synced Google reviews directly on the homepage, which is a reasonable schema-adjacent trust signal but not true structured data markup. Blog content is thin — the team page references articles dated April 25, 2025, but nothing substantive surfaced in search.

Their BuildZoom score of 98 ranks in the top 16% of 22,324 Indiana licensed contractors

, which will earn some directory link equity but not meaningful DA. No local press backlinks or co-citation from Indianapolis Business Journal, Fox59, or similar found. AEO posture is effectively zero — no FAQ schema, no structured Q&A content discovered.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

No evidence found that Trojan Roofing is cited by name in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini responses for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." Their content is too thin, their backlink profile too weak, and their off-site reputation signals too mixed for LLMs to surface them confidently. This is a clean opportunity for Raptor to own that generative answer real estate — especially for Carmel, Fishers, Zionsville, and Noblesville — before Trojan invests in AEO infrastructure.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Trojan beats Raptor: Pure volume of review count (641) is close to Raptor's 677, and their Google surface-level rating of 4.9 is close enough that a casual consumer won't distinguish the two. Their door-knocking canvass model lets them intercept storm leads at the property before a homeowner ever searches. They appear to have a built-in recruiter function scaling that machine.

Where Raptor beats Trojan: A full star ahead in trust credibility once a consumer digs deeper. Raptor's 0.15 owner response rate for Trojan (vs. Raptor's documented engagement) is a trust gap Cameron can weaponize.

Trojan's BBB F-rating for failure to respond to complaints

and a 3.5 Yelp score vs. Raptor's clean cross-platform reputation means any mid-funnel comparison shopper lands on Raptor. Trojan has no meaningful AEO footprint and no local press credibility.

Most exploitable weakness: Their BBB F-rating and Yelp 3.5 are publicly searchable — a single well-placed piece of content targeting "Trojan Roofing reviews Indianapolis" comparison queries could intercept their own branded traffic.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Geo-subfolder page architecture. Trojan has built individual city landing pages (Greenwood, Indianapolis proper) under indytrojanroofing.com/roofing/. Cameron should audit whether Raptor has equivalent depth for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Westfield — each with unique content, not thin duplicates — to compete on those high-intent geo queries.

  2. Internalized recruiting function.

Trojan has a dedicated Recruiting Manager on staff

specifically to feed their sales pipeline. If Raptor's growth plan includes adding sales reps, front-loading a part-time recruiter role is worth modeling — it systematizes canvassing-to-hire before you need it, not after.

  1. Monitor their storm-chase activation pattern. Trojan's core GTM is canvassing neighborhoods post-hail event. Watch their ZipRecruiter postings after any significant weather event in Hamilton or Hendricks County — a spike in Outside Sales Rep ads is a leading indicator they are mobilizing into a specific geography, and Cameron should counter with a localized paid search + direct mail surge into the same zip codes within 48 hours.
