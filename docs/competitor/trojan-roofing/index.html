RAPTOR ROOFING — COMPETITOR INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
Subject: Trojan Roofing | indytrojanroofing.com | Updated: May 19, 2025

1. Who They Are

Trojan Roofing, Inc. was incorporated on November 5, 2020 and lists a business start date of January 1, 2020, with Tyler Morgan as owner.

They present as a family-owned and operated business serving the greater Indianapolis area with residential and commercial roofing.

Their public-facing story leans heavily on insurance claim navigation and storm damage, with IKO Dynasty and Owens Corning as their marquee product credentials — a positioning that signals volume storm-chasing more than premium craftsmanship.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: medium.

Glassdoor shows 12 Indianapolis-based employee reviews on record

, suggesting a company somewhere in the 15-30 employee range including commission sales reps and subcontracted crews.

Indeed salary data indicates a pay range of approximately $40,891/year for sales roles up to $103,945 for management.

No ZoomInfo or dedicated revenue filing was surfaced for the roofing entity. Based on 643 Google reviews over roughly five years and a storm-claim-heavy model, a rough revenue estimate of $3M–$6M annually is plausible but low-confidence.

BuildZoom lists Tyler Morgan as the employee of record and notes Trojan holds four licenses, with a BuildZoom score of 98 ranking in the top 16% of Indiana licensed contractors.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Indeed shows 15 Trojan Roofing jobs listed in Indianapolis, IN

, though specific role titles from the last 30 days were not fully surfaced in search results.

One Indeed listing references "career advancement opportunities within Trojan Roofing's growing multi-state operations,"

which is notable — this is the first signal of geographic expansion ambition. No dedicated marketing, ops leadership, or field manager roles were confirmed as recently posted. Watch their Indeed page for any marketing coordinator or territory manager postings, which would signal a structured scale-up.

4. Off-Google Presence

The cross-platform picture is damning relative to their Google surface.

Yelp shows a 3.5-star rating based on 5 reviews.

That is a 1.4-star gap versus their 4.9 Google rating — a textbook review-funneling operation where customers are pushed to Google while dissatisfied customers find their way to Yelp unaided.

The BBB shows they are NOT accredited and have earned an F rating, with failure to respond to 4 complaints filed against the business and advertising issues flagged by the BBB.

Their Facebook page has only 233 likes

and no visible paid ad activity found in the Meta Ad Library. No meaningful Nextdoor presence was surfaced. The combination of a 3.5 Yelp and F-rated BBB against a polished Google profile is an exploitable credibility gap.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

The site at indytrojanroofing.com has built out dedicated service pages for residential roofing, commercial roofing, roof repair, and storm damage — and has geo-targeted location pages including one specifically for Indianapolis and Greenwood. The site uses Trustindex to embed Google reviews directly on service pages, which is a smart schema-adjacent trust signal. However, the content reads as heavily templated and keyword-stuffed rather than authoritative. No original blog content, local press backlinks, or third-party editorial coverage was found.

BuildZoom notes that one of their contractor licenses was at one point cancelled according to the Indianapolis Department of Business and Neighborhood Services

— a detail that, if surfaced in a comparison article or AEO content piece, would undermine their credibility with in-market researchers. Domain authority is estimated low-to-mid range given the absence of press links or editorial backlinks.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on current research, Trojan Roofing does not appear to be meaningfully cited by name in AI-generated answers for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana," or "roof replacement Carmel Indiana." Their thin editorial backlink profile, absence of third-party press mentions, and no visible presence on authoritative local directories beyond basic listings means AI engines have little quality signal to draw from. This is a direct opportunity for Raptor: structured AEO content (FAQ schema, "best roofers in Indianapolis" comparison pages, contractor guides) paired with press placements could dominate AI answer surfaces where Trojan has zero footprint.

Where they beat Raptor: 643 reviews gives them a slight volume edge (Raptor: 673 — nearly matched), and their multi-service website with location-specific landing pages is more SEO-architecturally built out than a single-page or thin-content site would be. Where Raptor beats them: A full star of quality gap in owner response rate (Raptor's engagement vs. Trojan's 0.06 owner response rate is not even a contest), a perfect 5.0 vs. their 4.9, zero reviews in the last 90 days on Trojan vs. Raptor's active velocity, a 3.5 on Yelp and an F at the BBB versus Raptor's clean record, and Raptor's premium positioning vs. Trojan's insurance-volume model.
