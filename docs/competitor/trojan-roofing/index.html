Trojan Roofing — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Refreshed: May 19, 2025 | Analyst: Raptor CMO Dashboard

1. Who They Are

Trojan Roofing, Inc. was founded in 2020 (incorporated November 5, 2020) and is owned by Tyler Morgan.

Since 2019, they have been serving residential and commercial clients throughout the Indianapolis area

, with their registered address at 333 Alabama St Ste #350, Indianapolis, IN 46204.

Their services extend beyond roofing to include siding and gutter solutions, and they are authorized installers of IKO Dynasty and Owens Corning systems.

Their primary differentiator is a high-volume, insurance-claim-driven sales model — canvassing neighborhoods post-storm and funneling prospects through a rep-heavy field team.

2. Estimated Scale

Glassdoor lists 12 Trojan Roofing reviews in Indianapolis

, suggesting a sales-forward organization in the 15–35 employee range when field reps, subcontractors, and back-office are factored in. Confidence: low — no verified LinkedIn headcount is publicly accessible and the company does not appear on ZoomInfo with employee data.

Their BuildZoom score of 98 ranks in the top 16% of 22,324 Indiana licensed contractors

, which implies an active, multi-permit operation. Rough revenue estimate: $3M–$7M annually based on review volume trajectory and insurance-claim business model. Confidence: low.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

LinkedIn shows a "Recruiting Manager" role — Maxwell Bunch — listed at Trojan Roofing

, which signals they maintain internal recruiting infrastructure rather than relying purely on organic referrals.

Indeed references "career advancement opportunities within Trojan Roofing's growing multi-state operations,"

suggesting they are positioning for geographic expansion beyond Indiana. No specific new job postings in the last 30 days were confirmed on Indeed or ZipRecruiter for Trojan-branded roles — the Indeed search returned only competing roofing companies. The presence of a Recruiting Manager is the most meaningful signal: they are actively scaling a sales force, not ops or production.

4. Off-Google Presence

The cross-platform review picture is damning.

Yelp shows a 3.5-star rating from 5 reviews

— compared to their 4.9 Google profile with 643 reviews. That is a textbook review-funneling pattern: satisfied customers get directed to Google, dissatisfied ones find Yelp on their own.

Trojan Roofing is NOT BBB Accredited and carries an F rating, with failure to respond to 4 complaints and advertising issues flagged by the BBB.

BBB complaints include an allegation of a $12K payment with no work performed, and a separate complaint that they installed a roof without obtaining a building permit.

Facebook shows only 233 likes

— minimal organic social investment. No confirmed active Facebook Ad Library spend was surfaced. Nextdoor presence is unconfirmed.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

indytrojanroofing.com has built out location-specific service pages (e.g., /roofing/indianapolis/, /roof-repair/, /roof-storm-damage/, /commercial-roofing/) with keyword-stuffed copy targeting terms like "roof repair Indianapolis IN" and "commercial roofing Indianapolis IN." The on-page strategy is quantity-over-quality: repetitive boilerplate, thin unique content, no identifiable blog or editorial calendar.

They claim IKO Dynasty and Owens Corning Awards

, which are legitimate manufacturer certifications and could generate backlink equity — but there is no visible local press coverage or third-party editorial links. Schema markup status is unconfirmed; the site architecture suggests a template-built CMS with no clear technical SEO investment. They are competing on review volume for Local Pack placement, not organic authority.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on current AI engine behavior as of May 2025: Trojan Roofing does not appear to be a consistently cited name in generative AI responses for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." AI citations in local home services skew toward companies with strong editorial backlink profiles (local press, association mentions, review aggregator prominence) and structured FAQ/schema content — areas where Trojan's template site underperforms. Their Yelp 3.5 and BBB F rating also create negative signal weight that suppresses AI citation likelihood. This is an active opening for Raptor: a single well-placed IndyStar or IBJ mention, combined with FAQ schema, could leapfrog Trojan in LLM outputs for high-value suburb queries.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Trojan beats Raptor: Raw review volume pace — they built 643 reviews in roughly 5 years through an aggressive canvassing-and-close sales model that systematically requests reviews at job completion. They also have more developed service-page architecture targeting long-tail city/suburb keywords.

Where Raptor beats Trojan: Every trust signal that matters to a high-intent homeowner doing due diligence. Raptor holds a 5.0 at 673 reviews vs. Trojan's 4.9 at 643; Raptor's Yelp and BBB exposure is clean; Trojan carries an F-rated BBB profile and a 3.5 Yelp.

A BBB complaint specifically states Trojan installed a roof without obtaining a building permit

— that is liability exposure Raptor can implicitly contrast against without ever naming Trojan directly.

Most exploitable weakness: A 6% owner response rate on Google reviews combined with an F BBB rating and a dead review pipeline (0 reviews in 30 days) signals that Trojan's review velocity has stalled — likely a canvassing team contraction — and the owner is not engaged in reputation defense. Raptor should press this window hard.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Location-page architecture. Trojan built individual pages targeting /roofing/greenwood/, /roofing/indianapolis/, etc. Raptor should audit whether its own suburb-level pages are thinner or less keyword-intentional than Trojan's, then build out superior versions with real project photography, permit data, and testimonial video — not boilerplate.

  2. Manufacturer award leverage.

Trojan claims IKO Dynasty and Owens Corning Awards as credibility signals.

Raptor should ensure every manufacturer certification it holds is prominently featured on service pages and pushed into schema markup — this is free authority that feeds both traditional SEO and AI citation engines.

  1. Monitor their review recovery. With 0 new Google reviews in 30 days, Trojan's canvassing operation has either paused or contracted. If this holds through Q3, their Local Pack position becomes vulnerable. Cameron should track their monthly review cadence and be ready to push Raptor's own review velocity — specifically in zip codes where Trojan was most active — to claim Pack displacement before Trojan restarts its engine.
