Competitive Intelligence Brief: Weathertight Roofing LLC

Refreshed: July 2, 2025 | Address: 8945 South St, Fishers, IN 46038

1. Who They Are

Weathertight Roofing LLC was founded by Corey Cates, who started roofing during a mission trip after Hurricane Katrina in 2006 and formally launched the company in May 2012 after graduating with a Biology degree from Indiana University.

They've grown from a local family-owned operation to a multi-location central Indiana presence, serving Fishers, Monticello, and Muncie.

Their brand hook is the slogan "Roof With The Best Or Leak Like The Rest," backed by GAF Master Elite certification.

2. Estimated Scale

BuildZoom lists named employees Alex Grim, Ashley Leitherer, and Corey Cates, with a General Contractor license and 6 additional licenses per the Indianapolis license board.

Their BuildZoom score of 106 ranks in the top 7% of 22,324 Indiana licensed contractors.

Confidence: Low on headcount — the named roster is thin for a claimed 3-location operation; actual crew count with subcontractors is likely 10–25 field personnel.

BuildZoom records 28 permitted projects totaling $535,800 in declared permit value

, which meaningfully underrepresents residential re-roofing volume (permits are frequently waived on residential). Rough revenue estimate: $3M–$6M annually. Confidence: Low.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

LinkedIn surfaced a Weathertight Roofing Accounts Payable Specialist posting in Fishers, IN, listed approximately one month ago.

That is an internal finance/ops hire, not sales or marketing — suggesting they are building back-office infrastructure, possibly to support multi-location billing or to reduce owner-dependency on financial controls. No marketing, sales leadership, or production manager roles were found on LinkedIn, Indeed, or ZipRecruiter in the last 30 days beyond that single post. Flag: A solo AP hire with no outward-facing sales or marketing roles suggests organic growth plateau or budget tightening on the revenue side.

4. Off-Google Presence

Facebook page (@weathertightroofs) shows only 12 reviews at 100% recommend and lists pricing as "$$".

That is a functionally invisible social proof footprint for a company claiming 297 Google reviews — a clear funnel: they are aggressively directing satisfied customers straight to Google and doing nothing to diversify.

BBB accreditation was granted July 12, 2024 — recent — with an A+ rating, business incorporated May 2012.

Yelp has an active listing at the Fishers address but review content is thin and not publicly surfacing a score, which is consistent with the Google-funneling pattern. No Reddit presence found for brand mentions. No Facebook Ad Library activity was detected during this search cycle, meaning they are running zero paid social as of this brief — a notable gap for a company their size in a storm-restoration market.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

The website deploys HOVER software for 3D roof visualization and estimate accuracy

— a legitimate conversion tool that doubles as a differentiator on-page.

They lean heavily into insurance claim positioning, claiming 20+ years of collective experience navigating the claims process

, and have dedicated service pages structured around storm damage and hail repair. The site at weathertightroofingllc.com has location-specific sub-pages for Noblesville, Muncie, Monticello, and Lawrence — a standard geo-page SEO play. Domain authority signal is moderate for a 13-year-old domain. No evidence of a maintained blog or consistent content publishing cadence was found; their site's metadata is service-page-heavy but thin on educational content. Schema markup for local business and review aggregation is present via their review embed, but AEO-grade FAQ schema targeting AI answer boxes is not apparent.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Multi-location footprint (Fishers + Monticello + Muncie) gives them geographic surface area; GAF Master Elite is a credentialed trust signal that resonates with insurance adjusters; HOVER 3D visualization is a sales-floor closer that few local competitors deploy. Where Raptor beats them: 682 reviews vs. 297 — more than 2x the social proof by volume at the same 5.0 rating; zero review velocity in the last 90 days on Weathertight's side is a brand that has decelerated or stalled its review engine entirely; Raptor's premium, accountability-forward positioning is more defensible against AI citation than a commoditized insurance-claims pitch. Most exploitable weakness: Their Google review engine has completely stalled — zero reviews in 90 days with zero owner responses — which is a live credibility decay problem Cameron should actively contrast in market messaging.

Set a Google Alerts + monthly manual GBP check cadence on Weathertight specifically.
