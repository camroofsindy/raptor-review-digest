Weathertight Roofing LLC — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Updated: June 11, 2026 | 8945 South St, Fishers, IN 46038

1. Who They Are

Owner Corey Cates founded Weathertight Roofing in May 2012 after graduating from Indiana University and started roofing during a 2006 mission trip to Louisiana following Hurricane Katrina.

The company is based in Fishers and serves Fishers, Monticello, and Muncie, covering residential and commercial roofing, gutters, siding, and exterior finishes.

Their market identity leans on GAF Master Elite certification and a self-described 20-year insurance claims specialization, anchored by the slogan "Roof With The Best Or Leak Like The Rest."

Their 20-year insurance claims positioning is a strong storm-season hook

that resonates directly with the Hamilton County homeowner who just had hail damage and wants a guide, not just a contractor.

Where Raptor beats Weathertight: 677 reviews vs. 291 — more than double the social proof at identical star rating. Raptor's review velocity (based on the provided data) is structurally stronger.
