Weathertight Roofing LLC — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Updated: May 19, 2026 | Raptor Roofing Dashboard

1. Who They Are

Owner Corey Cates began roofing in 2006 on a Hurricane Katrina mission trip in Louisiana. He graduated with a Biology degree from Indiana University and founded Weathertight Roofing in May 2012.

Since inception the company has grown from a local family-owned operation to serving Fishers, Monticello, and Muncie.

Their brand anchor is the motto "Roof With The Best Or Leak Like The Rest," backed by GAF Master Elite status and three physical locations with 24/7 emergency availability.

2. Estimated Scale

BuildZoom lists named employees as Alex Grim, Ashley Leitherer, and Corey Cates

— suggesting a very lean core office team. ZoomInfo separately lists a headcount of approximately 4 employees. With three service locations and an insurance-restoration specialty, the actual field crew is likely 10–20 when subs are counted. Confidence: low.

BuildZoom records 28 permitted projects totaling $535,800 in declared permit value.

The typical permit size is approximately $24,950 per job.

Actual revenue is almost certainly higher than permit-declared values suggest; rough estimate is $2–4M annually given permit volume plus unpermitted repair/insurance work. Confidence: low.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

LinkedIn shows a Weathertight Roofing Accounts Payable Specialist posting in Fishers, IN listed approximately one month ago.

No active sales, marketing, or ops leadership roles were found on Indeed or ZipRecruiter in the last 30 days specific to Weathertight. The AP hire is a notable signal — it points to either a bookkeeping gap at current scale or preparation for a volume increase that requires tighter financial controls. No marketing or digital hires detected, which is consistent with a small-owner-operated model.

4. Off-Google Presence

Their Facebook page (facebook.com/weathertightroofs) shows approximately 975 likes and 29 people "talking about this"

No Nextdoor business presence was surfaced. No Reddit threads referencing Weathertight were found. The discrepancy between 291 Google reviews and a near-invisible off-platform footprint is a textbook review-funneling pattern — customers are being directed to Google only.

They feature HOVER software prominently, using it to provide 3D interactive roof models and accurate estimates

— this is conversion-oriented content that functions as both a sales tool and a content differentiator.

Their BuildZoom score of 106 ranks in the top 7% of 22,324 Indiana licensed contractors

Domain authority is estimated low-to-mid for a 13-year-old local site without consistent content production. AEO posture is weak — no FAQ schema, no article schema, and no local press citations detected.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

No AI-generated answer sets (ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude) prominently named Weathertight Roofing in response to queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville" during research for this brief. 7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Three physical locations (Fishers, Monticello, Muncie) gives them geographic breadth and a multi-market permit history Raptor does not yet match. GAF Master Elite is table stakes but their HOVER integration is a legitimate in-home close tool. Where Raptor beats them: 673 reviews vs. 291 is a 2.3x volume advantage at an identical 5.0 star rating — the social proof gap is decisive for homeowners comparison-shopping on Google Maps. Zero reviews in the last 90 days is Weathertight's single most exploitable weakness: their review engine has effectively stalled, which will trigger GBP ranking decay within 60–90 days if it hasn't already.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. HOVER as a close tool. Weathertight is using HOVER's 3D visualization platform in the estimate process. If Raptor isn't already using HOVER or a comparable tool (EagleView, Roofle), adding it creates a premium in-home presentation that directly supports the "we don't outsource accountability" brand and gives sales reps a reason to stay in the room longer.

  2. Multi-location GBP strategy. Weathertight's three-city footprint (Fishers, Monticello, Muncie) means they are pulling GBP map pack impressions across multiple markets simultaneously. Cameron should evaluate whether Raptor's current single-location GBP setup is leaving map pack real estate on the table in Carmel, Noblesville, or Greenwood.

  3. Monitor their review reactivation. Zero reviews in 90 days means their internal follow-up process — text, email, or CRM trigger — has broken down. Watch for a sudden burst of 20–30 reviews, which would indicate they've patched the system and re-engaged a backlog of past customers. If that burst comes, Raptor should accelerate its own cadence to maintain the volume gap.
