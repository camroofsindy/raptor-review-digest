Weathertight Roofing LLC — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed: April 26, 2025

1. Who They Are

Founded in May 2012 by Corey Cates — a central Indiana native who started roofing during Hurricane Katrina relief work in Louisiana before earning a Biology degree from Indiana University — Weathertight Roofing LLC grew from a family-run shop into a multi-location operation serving Fishers, Monticello, and Muncie.

They cover residential and commercial work including roofing systems, gutters, siding, and exterior finishes.

Their primary differentiator on paper is

the GAF Master Elite certification, backed by the tagline "Roof With The Best Or Leak Like The Rest."

Insurance claim advocacy is a second pillar:

they claim over 20 years of experience in handling insurance claims, positioning themselves as guides through the claims process.

2. Estimated Scale

BuildZoom lists named employees Alex Grim, Ashley Leitherer, and Corey Cates

on file — suggesting a lean core office team. ZoomInfo's entry for a Weathertight Roofing entity lists 4 employees, though that record may conflate a different entity.

The company claims three locations and 24/7 emergency availability

, which implies subcontracted or rotating field crews rather than a large W-2 workforce. Confidence: low on headcount — no LinkedIn company page for the Indiana LLC was found.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Weathertight beats Raptor: GAF Master Elite certification gives them a manufacturer-credentialed close when competing on insurance jobs; their HOVER visualization tool is a tangible sales-room differentiator that smooths approvals and reduces buyer hesitation. Their multi-location footprint (Fishers, Muncie, Monticello) also lets them claim broader geographic coverage.

Where Raptor beats Weathertight: 664 reviews vs. 290 — more than double the social proof volume, and Raptor's 5.0 holds at that volume where Weathertight's does not. Raptor's off-Google review distribution (if maintained) is almost certainly less funneled. Weathertight's owner response rate (per provided data: 1.0) is ambiguous — if that means near-zero engagement on reviews, it's a trust signal gap Raptor should hammer in sales conversations.

Most exploitable weakness: Weathertight has zero generative engine presence and a thin off-Google review footprint — a company with 290 Google reviews and no Yelp traction is one negative Reddit thread away from a reputation crater.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

1. HOVER 3D visualization as a close tool. Weathertight is using HOVER to let homeowners visualize their roof before committing.

The interactive 3D model shows homeowners what their home looks like with different products and colors, removing hesitation around the project.

If Raptor isn't already deploying a visualization step in the sales process, this is worth a 30-day pilot — it directly attacks buyer indecision at the estimate stage.

2. Geo-targeted service pages with city-level URLs. Weathertight has built out dedicated pages for Lawrence, Noblesville, Muncie, and Monticello. The execution is thin (no blog depth, no schema), but the architecture is right. Raptor should build tighter, schema-marked versions of these pages for Zionsville, Westfield, Carmel, and Greenwood — and layer in FAQ schema to capture AI Overview real estate.

3. Monitor their GAF Master Elite positioning.

Their entire brand anchor is the GAF Master Elite certification.

If Raptor holds or pursues comparable manufacturer credentials (GAF, Owens Corning Platinum, etc.), that closes the one credentialing gap Weathertight currently uses to justify premium positioning against smaller operators. Watch whether they start co-branding with GAF in paid placements — that would signal a campaign push Cameron should preempt locally.
