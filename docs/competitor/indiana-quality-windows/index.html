Competitor Intelligence Brief: Indiana Quality Windows (a/k/a Indiana Quality Home Exteriors)

Prepared for Cameron Blakely | Raptor Roofing | Refreshed: July 2, 2025

1. Who They Are

Indiana Quality Windows was established in 1987 as a full-service home exterior company

operating out of 1262 S High School Rd on the west side of Indianapolis.

As a family-owned company, they claim to handle every project themselves, never outsourcing to subcontractors

— a direct messaging parallel to Raptor's own positioning.

Their service scope spans doors, windows, siding, roofing, gutters, painting, and drywall

, making them a broad exterior generalist rather than a roofing specialist. The listed member on their BBB profile is Christopher McCarty;

Nita Kelso serves as Office Manager per the BBB listing

.

2. Estimated Scale

BuildZoom shows 95 permitted projects and lists an employee named Brian Baker under a Renovation license

.

Their BuildZoom score of 106 ranks in the top 7% of 22,324 Indiana licensed contractors

— a credibility signal, but not a revenue indicator.

Permit records indicate they have worked on at least 1 project over the past 3 years

, which is a meaningful red flag for a company claiming 30+ years of continuous operation. ZoomInfo does not publish a headcount or revenue figure for them publicly. Based on the single-location operation, low permit velocity, west-side Indianapolis geography, and multi-trade generalist structure, estimated annual revenue is $1M–$3M. Confidence: low. Employee count estimated at 5–15 total, including installers. Confidence: low.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

As of January 13, 2025, Indiana Quality posted on Facebook that they are hiring an "In-Store Product Promoter / Brand Ambassador" — an outgoing, reliable individual to promote their product inside a retail store and generate customer leads.

This is a significant signal: they are pursuing a retail co-location lead-gen strategy, likely inside a Lowe's or Home Depot.

A Birdeye review confirms Indiana Quality is already a Lowe's installation provider for the area

, lending credibility to this interpretation. No marketing, sales ops, or leadership roles found on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter in the last 30 days. The brand ambassador post is the only hiring signal visible.

Bottom line: 4.8 on Google vs. 2.8 on HomeAdvisor and ~3.0 on Yelp is a textbook review-funneling discrepancy. The 1.0 owner response rate on Google (provided) means they are not engaging negative signals anywhere.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Indiana Quality's domain is indianaquality.com.

Their tech stack includes reCAPTCHA, PHP, Google Font API, and Microsoft 365

— a basic, non-sophisticated web setup with no evidence of structured schema markup, blog content production, or local press backlinks visible in search results. The site has a reviews page (indianaquality.com/reviews) but no indexable blog or service-area landing page architecture. They do not appear in any Indy roofing-specific ranking roundups or local media coverage. Their domain likely carries low authority; their organic roofing keyword footprint appears minimal. The site's primary SEO value comes from the age of the domain (est. 1987 company, domain registered in the early 2000s) and their Google Business Profile, not from content investment. Zero AEO signals detected — no FAQ schema, no structured Q&A, no effort to capture featured snippets on roofing or window queries.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Longevity narrative (founded 1987 vs. Raptor's newer entry), multi-trade bundling (a homeowner can get windows, siding, gutters, and a roof in one call), and the Lowe's installation partnership, which generates passive inbound leads Raptor does not have a direct equivalent for.

Where Raptor beats them: Review volume and rating purity (682 at 5.0 vs. 333 at 4.8 Google, with Indiana Quality sitting at 2.8 on HomeAdvisor and ~3.0 on Yelp). Raptor's review response posture is also superior. Raptor is a roofing specialist with zero platform review discrepancy; Indiana Quality is a generalist with a cross-platform reputation problem they are not managing. Raptor's AI/AEO presence, if activated, will dominate Indiana Quality by default.

Most exploitable weakness: Their HomeAdvisor and Yelp reviews are significantly below their Google rating, and their Google review velocity has cratered (1 review in 90 days vs. Raptor's consistent cadence) — a direct attack surface for any homeowner doing multi-platform due diligence before a large purchase.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Lowe's / Retail Co-Location Lead Gen: Indiana Quality's in-store brand ambassador hire signals they are actively mining foot traffic at big-box retail. Cameron should evaluate a similar demo-table or event presence at Lowe's, Home Depot, or a local lumber yard in the Carmel/Fishers/Noblesville corridor — especially post-storm weekends when foot traffic spikes around roofing materials.

  2. Multi-Trade Bundling as a Roofing Upsell: Indiana Quality's entire business model is built on the cross-sell (roof + gutters + siding + windows). Raptor's positioning as a roofing specialist is a strength, but the bundling language on their site ("we handle all home exterior projects") gives Indiana Quality a reason to get called first. Raptor should build a preferred-partner referral program with a window/door company so that roofing leads can be reciprocally exchanged — capturing the bundle value without diluting the specialist brand.

  3. Monitor Their Google Review Velocity: With only 1 review in 90 days, Indiana Quality may be entering a period of operational contraction or post-ownership transition. If their GBP ranking drops, that is a keyword vacancy Cameron can capture with a direct paid or LSA push on west-side Indianapolis roofing terms (High School Rd corridor, Speedway, Plainfield) where Indiana Quality has historically had the most neighborhood name recognition.
