Competitive Intelligence Brief — Indiana Quality Windows (Indiana Quality Home Exteriors)

Refreshed: May 19, 2025 | Next update: May 22, 2025

1. Who They Are

Indiana Quality Home Exteriors is a local small business serving Indianapolis and surrounding areas since 1987.

Key contacts on file at the BBB include office manager Nita Kelso and member Christopher McCarty.

They specialize in windows, doors, roofing, siding, gutters, and painting, and claim to handle all installations without subcontracting

— a positioning line that overlaps directly with Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" angle.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: low on all figures. No revenue or employee count is publicly disclosed on ZoomInfo or LinkedIn beyond what is gated.

Indiana Quality Home Exteriors is described as a family-owned business; ZoomInfo lists them but does not surface a headcount or revenue range without a paid subscription.

BuildZoom's permit records indicate Indiana Quality has worked on at least 1 project in the past 3 years

, and

their historical building permit record shows 95 permitted projects total.

That permit cadence suggests a micro-operation — likely under 15 field employees and sub-$3M annual revenue.

Their BuildZoom score of 106 ranks in the top 7% of 22,324 Indiana licensed contractors

, which flatters them on paper but does not indicate current volume.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

One active hiring signal found.

As of January 13, Indiana Quality posted on Facebook for an "In-Store Product Promoter / Brand Ambassador" — an outgoing individual to promote product inside a retail store and generate customer leads.

This is a notable strategic tell: they are pursuing a retail/big-box lead-gen model, most likely a Lowe's or Menards in-store demo program.

One recent reviewer confirmed Indiana Quality serves as an installation provider for Lowe's in their area.

No marketing director, sales manager, or operations leadership roles are currently visible on Indeed, LinkedIn, or ZipRecruiter.

  • Yelp: Thin presence.

The pattern is unambiguous: Google is their managed reputation channel. Yelp (3.0), Angi (2.8), and Facebook (70% recommend) all tell a rougher story. Owner response rate of 1.0 on Google suggests someone is paying attention there — but not elsewhere.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Indiana Quality's domain (indianaquality.com) runs on a basic stack.

ZoomInfo identifies their tech stack as reCAPTCHA, PHP, Google Font API, and Microsoft 365

— no evidence of a modern CMS, structured data tooling, or content infrastructure. No active blog was surfaced. Their site has a reviews page but no apparent schema markup for LocalBusiness, AggregateRating, or FAQPage. They are not visible in any Indy roofing keyword SERP results surfaced during this research pass. The domain's organic footprint appears minimal — they likely survive on GBP traffic, word-of-mouth, and the Lowe's referral pipeline, not organic search. No backlinks from local Indy press (IBJ, IndyStar) were found. AEO posture is essentially nonexistent.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Not cited. Indiana Quality does not appear in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini responses for "best roofers Indianapolis," "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville." Their lack of a content footprint, schema markup, and third-party citations makes AI citation impossible under current generative engine ranking signals. This is an opportunity gap for Raptor: any structured content investment — FAQ schema, location pages, press mentions — will compound into AI visibility that Indiana Quality cannot match in its current state.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Longevity narrative —

they have a physical location and full-time office staff since 1987, and deliberately avoid heavy advertising to keep costs down

, which resonates with a price-sensitive segment. The Lowe's installation partnership gives them a passive lead channel Raptor does not have.

Where Raptor beats them: Review volume and velocity are superior — 673 reviews vs. 332, plus Raptor's 5.0 rating is a full 0.2 stars above Indiana Quality's 4.8. Critically, Indiana Quality posted zero new reviews in the last 7 days and only 3 in 90 days, signaling a stalled or broken review-generation system. Raptor's cross-platform reputation is clean; Indiana Quality's off-Google scores are a liability.

Most exploitable weakness: Their 2.8 on HomeAdvisor and 3.0 on Yelp are publicly visible to any homeowner who comparison-shops — Cameron should run paid ads targeting high-intent searchers and land them on a page that directly contrasts verified multi-platform ratings.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Monitor the Lowe's/retail in-store ambassador play. Indiana Quality is building a passive lead channel through big-box store partnerships. Cameron should evaluate whether a similar arrangement with a local Lowe's or Menards (or a countertop/flooring specialty retailer that co-targets homeowners mid-project) is worth piloting — this is a low-CAC referral channel with captive, purchase-intent traffic.

  2. Own the cross-platform reputation angle in ads. Indiana Quality's Angi and Yelp scores are soft. A paid search or Meta ad creative that leads with "5.0 stars across every platform" (if accurate) or simply emphasizes verified, multi-site review consistency would directly exploit this competitor's blind spot in the minds of due-diligent homeowners.

  3. Capture the "no subcontracting" keyword territory they're abandoning. Indiana Quality used to lean into this positioning on Nextdoor and their website, but their content has gone stale.

Their own Nextdoor presence notes they "don't advertise/promote so they can keep the costs down"

— an implicit concession that they are withdrawing from the content and digital competitive space. Raptor should publish content (blog, FAQ schema, GBP posts) specifically targeting "Indianapolis roofing no subcontractors" and related queries to claim that positioning before a better-funded competitor does.
