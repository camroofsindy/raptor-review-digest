Competitor Intelligence Brief — Stay Dry Roofing

Refreshed: Tuesday, July 22, 2026 | Prepared for: Cameron Blakely, Raptor Roofing

1. Who They Are

Stay Dry Roofing LLC was founded in 2016 as a residential and commercial exterior contractor.

Josh Lyon is the Principal Owner and President.

They serve Indianapolis and surrounding communities including Carmel, Greenwood, Fishers, Noblesville, Brownsburg, and Plainfield.

Their differentiator on paper is a GAF Master Elite certification and a "family-owned" narrative —

they earned GAF's 2026 President's Club Award, which they position as evidence of top-tier craftsmanship and industry-leading performance.

2. Estimated Scale

Stay Dry Roofing has approximately 31 employees and an estimated $5.5 million in annual revenue, per RocketReach.

Confidence: medium — RocketReach figures are modeled, not audited. However, that number is sharply down from their peak:

the Indianapolis Business Journal reported 2021 revenue of $13.8 million with 81% growth from 2019 to 2021.

The gap between $13.8M in 2021 and ~$5.5M today is a significant red flag worth monitoring — post-storm-cycle contraction or operational pullback.

LinkedIn lists the company at 11–50 employees.

Confidence: medium.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Stay Dry is actively recruiting an Outside Sales Representative (Residential Sales Advisor) focused on roofing, guttering, and siding, with a posting tied to the Westfield, IN market on Indeed.

ZipRecruiter shows approximately 10 Stay Dry Roofing job listings currently active, ranging from $64K to $338K — the top of that range suggesting commission-heavy sales roles.

No marketing, ops leadership, or field management hires detected in the last 30 days. The sales rep push into Westfield/Hamilton County territory is the most notable signal — that is Raptor's backyard.

4. Off-Google Presence

  • Yelp:

The primary Indianapolis listing at 5060 E 62nd St shows only 23 reviews

— a fraction of their 432 Google reviews. This is a textbook review-funneling gap. Customers are being steered exclusively to Google. A separate

Greenwood Yelp listing exists at 3209 W Smith Valley Rd, where they position as "the most trusted Indianapolis roofing company."

  • BBB:

BBB accredited since May 2017.

They earned a "Complaint Free Year" recognition from the BBB in 2023.

A+ rated.
- Facebook:

Their Facebook page shows 1,155 likes with only 6 people currently talking about the page

— low engagement relative to their review volume. No evidence of active Facebook Ad Library spend found in this search cycle.
- Nextdoor:

Stay Dry shows up in Nextdoor recommendation threads, with users citing BBB checks as the validation step before hiring them.

Their reps are posting directly in threads — a grassroots tactic that works in Indy neighborhoods but is easy to track and counter.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Stay Dry has made a calculated SEO bet that most competitors overlook: they publish self-serving list content under their own domain.

Their site hosts a "2026 Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis" post that places themselves at number one.

This is a direct AEO play — if an AI pulls that page as a source, Stay Dry gets cited as the top Indy roofer from their own content.

They also claim "over 9 Indy locations" on their about page

, which is likely a network of location landing pages designed to capture hyper-local queries (Carmel, Fishers, Greenwood, etc.) rather than actual physical offices.

Their homepage claims "over 1,000 five-star reviews" — a figure that conflicts sharply with the 432 Google reviews we're tracking

, suggesting they are aggregating across platforms or inflating the claim. That's worth flagging in competitive sales conversations.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Stay Dry has engineered for AI citation in a way that most Indy competitors have not. Their self-published "Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis" page on staydryroofing.com is indexed, dated January 2026, and structured to look like a third-party editorial list. ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity all pull from exactly this type of structured content when answering "best roofers Indianapolis" queries.

The page positions Stay Dry at the top on the basis of Inc. 5000 recognition and growth metrics.

Their GAF Master Elite status, BBB A+ accreditation, and Inc. 5000 listing are all structured data points that AI models weight heavily as trust signals. Raptor is not currently publishing comparable authoritative list content — this is a direct AEO gap that should be closed in Q3.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Stay Dry beats Raptor: Sheer content infrastructure — location landing pages, self-referential SEO lists, multiple Yelp listings, and a GAF President's Club credential that generates organic press. Their Inc. 5000 history also gives them a credibility timestamp in AI training data that Raptor currently lacks.

They also advertise a 25-year workmanship warranty

, which is a strong sales floor close.

Where Raptor beats Stay Dry: Raptor holds 690 reviews at a clean 5.0 versus Stay Dry's 432 at 4.9. More importantly, Stay Dry's review velocity has collapsed — only 10 new reviews in 90 days is the equivalent of a company that stopped asking. Raptor's owner response rate and volume consistency signal a healthier operational cadence. The claim of "1,000 five-star reviews" on their homepage against a verified 432 on Google is a credibility liability they have not reconciled.

Most exploitable weakness: Their review engine has stalled — 10 reviews in 90 days on a 432-count profile is a momentum problem, and their Yelp presence (23 reviews) exposes aggressive Google-funneling that informed buyers will notice.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The self-published "best of" list tactic. Stay Dry is running a page that functions as AEO bait — ranking themselves #1 in a list they wrote. Raptor should publish a genuinely useful, independently framed "Top Indy Roofers" guide at raptor's domain that passes the credibility sniff test better than Stay Dry's version, includes schema markup, and cites third-party signals (permit data, GAF/OC certification rosters). This is the fastest path to AI citation in 2026.

  2. Hamilton County sales expansion. Stay Dry is pushing a sales rep specifically into Westfield. Monitor whether they open a Westfield or Noblesville Google Business Profile in the next 60 days. If they do, Raptor should respond with a direct campaign in that ZIP targeting homeowners who are currently researching "new roofer near me" queries — before Stay Dry earns reviews in that sub-market.

  3. The 25-year workmanship warranty as a close.

Stay Dry advertises a 25-year workmanship warranty on all roof replacement projects

— prominently, in their FAQ, where buyers are already in decision mode. If Raptor's warranty story is comparable or stronger, it needs to be surfaced at the same point in the buyer journey (FAQ, estimate page, sales script), not buried in fine print.
