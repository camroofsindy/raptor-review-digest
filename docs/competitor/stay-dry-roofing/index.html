Competitive Intelligence Brief — Stay Dry Roofing

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated: May 19, 2025

1. Who They Are

Stay Dry Roofing LLC was founded in 2016 as a residential and commercial exterior contractor, with Josh Lyon serving as Principal Owner and President.

They serve Indianapolis and surrounding communities including Carmel, Greenwood, Fishers, Noblesville, Brownsburg, and Plainfield.

Their primary market differentiator is a GAF Master Elite designation paired with an Inc. 5000 appearance — positioning them as an operationally scaled, credential-heavy competitor rather than a boutique shop.

2. Estimated Scale

RocketReach estimates Stay Dry Roofing at approximately 31 employees and $5.5 million in annual revenue

(confidence: low — RocketReach figures are algorithmically modeled and often lag reality).

More credibly, IBJ reported 2021 revenue at $13.8 million with 81% growth from FY2019 to 2021

, suggesting the RocketReach number is heavily understated.

LinkedIn lists their company size as 11–50 employees

(confidence: medium). Real headcount is likely in the 30–45 range; revenue is plausibly in the $8–12M range given post-pandemic normalization.

They also claim over 9 Indianapolis-area locations

, which signals a distributed sales/ops model, not a single-office shop.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Indeed shows an active posting for a Residential Sales Advisor — assisting homeowners with roofing, guttering, and siding needs — requiring no prior experience, located in Westfield, IN.

ZipRecruiter lists approximately 10 open Stay Dry Roofing positions ranging from $64K to $338K.

The $338K ceiling is almost certainly a commission-based sales role. No marketing, ops leadership, or CMO-level hire is visible. The sales-rep-heavy hiring pattern confirms a volume-outbound model fueled by door-to-door and storm-chasing canvassers, not inbound demand generation. That is a structural vulnerability Raptor does not share.

4. Off-Google Presence

BBB-accredited since May 19, 2017

, with an A+ rating.

They earned a BBB "Complaint Free Year" recognition in 2023.

Yelp presence exists but shows only 26 reviews (yelp.com/biz/stay-dry-roofing-indianapolis) — a meaningful gap versus their 422 Google reviews. This is a textbook review-funneling pattern: all the solicitation energy goes to Google, leaving Yelp thin and vulnerable to a single bad review outsizing organic ones.

They do host video testimonials on their own website

, which is a smart AEO hedge.

At least one BBB reviewer noted hearing about Stay Dry on Indianapolis radio station Q-95

, confirming they run or have run traditional broadcast media buys — a budget signal. No active Facebook Ad Library spend was confirmable from this pull; monitor weekly.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Stay Dry is playing an aggressive content-quantity game.

Their website hosts a self-authored "Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis for 2026" post where they award themselves the top spot

— a transparent but genuinely effective AEO tactic that gets their brand name into AI training corpora for list-style queries.

Their homepage targets hyper-local neighborhood-level language, naming Monument Circle, Broad Ripple, Meridian-Kessler, and Speedway

— structured content designed to capture geo-modified searches and appear in AI answers for neighborhood-level queries.

Their FAQ schema openly answers cost queries, stating roof replacement in Indianapolis typically runs $8,000–$17,000

— exactly the kind of structured answer content that feeds AI overviews.

They hold the GAF 2026 President's Club Award

, which is a third-party credentialing signal that AI engines treat as authority. Their domain (staydryroofing.com) has an Inc. 5000 citation, IBJ press, and BBB backlinks — a meaningful authority stack.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on available data, Stay Dry is likely cited in AI-generated answers for Indianapolis roofing queries. The evidence:

the company has been honored on the Inc. 5000 list, a national ranking that AI engines recognize as a credibility signal

; they publish their own "best of" lists that name themselves first; and their site uses FAQ schema on cost, warranty, and licensing questions — the exact content types that get pulled into ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity responses. Their neighborhood-level landing pages (

Carmel, Greenwood, Fishers, Noblesville, Brownsburg, Plainfield

) mean they have indexed content for every suburb-modified query Raptor should be winning. Raptor's 5.0 rating and higher review count are not yet reflected in AI answers if Raptor lacks equivalent structured content — that is the gap to close immediately.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Stay Dry beats Raptor: Content volume and AEO infrastructure — self-authored "best of" lists, neighborhood landing pages, FAQ schema, and video testimonials give them more AI-retrievable surface area. Their Inc. 5000 and IBJ press citations provide off-site authority signals Raptor currently lacks. They also have a dedicated CMO (Matthew Bullock, per IBJ) which means their marketing is not ad hoc.

Where Raptor beats Stay Dry: 673 reviews vs. 422 — Raptor has 60% more social proof by volume, and a 5.0 vs. 4.9 rating. Review velocity tells the real story: Stay Dry generated only 5 Google reviews in the last 90 days versus a much stronger cadence historically — a significant deceleration suggesting either operational slowdown, review fatigue, or a team in transition. Raptor's premium positioning ("we don't outsource accountability") is more defensible long-term than Stay Dry's scaled-sales model, which Glassdoor employee reviews flag for management inconsistency and pay structure churn.

Most exploitable weakness: Stay Dry's review velocity has nearly flatlined (1 review in the last 7 days, 3 in 30 days) — Raptor should hammer the contrast in review recency in any content or sales collateral comparing the two.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The self-authored "best of" list play.

Stay Dry publishes a "Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis" page where they award themselves the top spot

and control the framing for every competitor mentioned. Cameron should consider an analogous page — not to rank competitors, but to own the "what makes a great Indianapolis roofer" query and answer it in Raptor's voice. That page becomes an AEO asset.

  1. Neighborhood-level FAQ schema.

Their FAQ answers specific cost questions ("roof replacement in Indianapolis typically ranges from $8,000–$17,000") inline on the homepage

, feeding AI overview snippets. Raptor should build suburb-specific FAQ pages for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, and Greenwood with schema markup — cost ranges, permit timelines, material recommendations — before Stay Dry locks in those answer positions.

  1. Radio/broadcast as credibility signal, not just lead gen.

At least one reviewer cited hearing Stay Dry on Q-95 radio

before hiring them — broadcast creates the "I've heard of them" trust that accelerates conversion when a homeowner then finds Raptor online. A targeted Indianapolis drive-time radio buy, even a short run post-storm, builds brand memory that pure digital cannot replicate at the same speed.
