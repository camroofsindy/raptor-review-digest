Competitive Intelligence Brief: Stay Dry Roofing

Refreshed: Tuesday, May 20, 2025 | Analyst: Raptor Roofing CMO

1. Who They Are

Stay Dry Roofing LLC was founded in 2016 as a residential and commercial exterior contractor, led by Principal Owner and President Josh Lyon.

They serve Indianapolis and surrounding communities including Carmel, Greenwood, Fishers, Noblesville, Brownsburg, and Plainfield.

Their differentiator is a GAF Master Elite credential paired with a multi-location footprint —

Stay Dry claims over 9 Indy locations

— positioning them as a scaled local operator rather than a single-shop contractor.

2. Estimated Scale

LinkedIn lists their company size as 11–50 employees.

RocketReach pegs revenue at approximately $5.5 million annually with 31 employees.

Confidence: medium — RocketReach estimates are modeled, not audited. Corroborating signal:

they appeared on the Inc. 5000 list in 2022

, confirming meaningful growth velocity in that period, though velocity appears to have since plateaued based on the review data Cameron provided (only 5 new Google reviews in the last 90 days).

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Indeed currently shows Stay Dry posting an Outside Sales Representative / Residential Sales Advisor role based out of Westfield, IN, focused on assisting homeowners with roofing, guttering, and siding needs.

ZipRecruiter lists approximately 10 active Stay Dry Roofing postings in Indianapolis, with compensation ranging up to $338K

— consistent with commission-heavy sales rep structures.

Their careers page leads with the claim that reps earn the highest commissions in the industry, along with a wrapped truck, iPad, and phone

— a standard aggressive-sales-culture recruitment pitch. No marketing, ops leadership, or digital roles visible. This is a pure sales-headcount build, not a brand investment.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp shows Stay Dry at their 62nd Street address with only 26 reviews

— a stark gap against 422 Google reviews. That ratio (16:1 Google-to-Yelp) is a textbook review-funneling signal; they are aggressively routing satisfied customers to Google and ignoring Yelp.

A second Yelp listing exists at 333 N Alabama St, Indianapolis

, suggesting their multi-location strategy has created duplicate or thin profiles across directories.

BBB-accredited since May 2017

, with

a BBB "Complaint Free Year" recognition earned in 2023.

No public Facebook Ad Library activity was surfaced in search results, and their

LinkedIn company page has only 467 followers

— low for a $5M+ contractor claiming market leadership. Facebook presence exists but appears to function primarily as a content mirror, not a paid acquisition channel.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Stay Dry's SEO posture is notably aggressive and worth respecting.

Their own website hosts a "Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis" post for 2026 in which they rank themselves first

— a self-citation play designed to intercept comparison-shopping queries.

They tout a 2026 GAF President's Club Award

, which they are embedding as structured trust content on the homepage. Their site targets hyper-local landmark language —

referencing Lucas Oil Stadium, Monument Circle, and the Indianapolis Motor Speedway

— which is a clear geo-entity SEO tactic for local search and AI answer engines. They also operate a dedicated commercial subdomain (staydrycommercial.com), splitting domain authority across two properties. Schema markup and FAQ sections are present on the main site based on the FAQ-style content visible throughout. The self-ranking "Top 10" page is the clearest AEO/GEO play in their arsenal.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on available search signals, Stay Dry has built the content architecture to appear in AI-generated answers: their self-ranking "best roofing companies Indianapolis" post, GAF Master Elite credential pages, FAQ-rich service pages, and multi-location service area pages all follow the pattern of content that generative engines pull from. The GAF contractor directory listing at gaf.com provides a high-authority third-party citation. However, their thin Yelp presence and low LinkedIn authority limit their citation diversity — a key factor in AI answer sourcing. Cameron should test current ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity responses for "best roofer Indianapolis" and "roof replacement Carmel Indiana" to confirm whether Stay Dry is being named; their content setup suggests they are at minimum a candidate, if not already present.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Multi-location footprint and service area page volume creates more indexable geographic surface area. The GAF President's Club 2026 award and the self-published "Top 10" ranking page are credibility assets Raptor does not currently counter publicly. Their sales rep compensation package (wrapped trucks, high commissions) likely attracts volume-hungry reps who drive aggressive lead generation.

Where Raptor beats them: 673 reviews at a perfect 5.0 vs. Stay Dry's 422 at 4.9 — and Raptor's review velocity is not collapsing the way Stay Dry's is (5 reviews in 90 days is a serious warning sign of operational slowdown or review-program fatigue). Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is a direct counter to any company running a commission-heavy, high-turnover sales force.

Most exploitable weakness: Stay Dry's Google review velocity has nearly flatlined — 5 reviews in 90 days for a company claiming market leadership is a credibility gap Cameron can weaponize in sales conversations and paid ad copy right now.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The self-ranking "Top 10" content play. Stay Dry publishes a page ranking themselves #1 among Indy roofers. This intercepts high-intent comparison searches and feeds AI citation engines. Cameron should commission a version with Raptor's actual review advantage (673 reviews, 5.0 star, highest volume in the Indy top 10) front and center — with named, verifiable data points that generative engines can quote.

  2. GAF President's Club / manufacturer award amplification. Stay Dry prominently leads with their 2026 GAF President's Club award across the homepage, FAQs, and service pages. If Raptor holds comparable manufacturer credentials, they need to be embedded as structured, named trust signals in schema markup — not just mentioned in passing.

  3. Multi-location service area page architecture.

Stay Dry has built out multiple Indianapolis location pages including Downtown and North Alabama Street, explicitly to respond quickly to roofing needs anywhere in the city.

Raptor should audit whether its own service-area page depth matches or exceeds Stay Dry's across Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Greenwood, and Zionsville — these pages are the primary surface area for both local pack rankings and AI geographic answer pulls.
