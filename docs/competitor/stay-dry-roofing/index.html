Stay Dry Roofing — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Updated: August 11, 2026 | Raptor Roofing Dashboard

1. Who They Are

Stay Dry Roofing, LLC was founded in 2016 as a residential and commercial exterior contractor.

Josh Lyon is the Principal Owner and President.

The company also lists a "Marketing Founder" on its team page, signaling that marketing has been a deliberate, founder-level priority from early on — not an afterthought.

Stay Dry focuses on residential and commercial roof replacement and repairs centralized in Indianapolis, and claims over nine Indy locations to serve the metro area.

Their differentiator is the GAF Master Elite credential combined with an insurance-claim-assistance posture that funnels storm-damage leads directly into their pipeline.

2. Estimated Scale

Stay Dry Roofing is listed at $5.5 million in revenue with 32 employees

per RocketReach.

ZoomInfo independently corroborates the $5.5M revenue figure and 11–50 employee band.

Confidence: medium — both sources are third-party estimates, not audited financials, though cross-source alignment adds credibility.

Inc. 5000 named them a 2022 honoree, founded 2016, with a company size of 11–50.

At ~$5.5M they are likely doing 120–160 roofs per year at average ticket, with a sales team-heavy model given hiring patterns (see Section 3).

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Stay Dry is currently posting for a Residential Sales Advisor — assisting homeowners with roofing, guttering, and siding needs — with no experience required, listed in the Westfield, IN area on Indeed.

ZipRecruiter shows an active posting for a Sales Lead Coordinator role in Indianapolis.

The pattern is telling: entry-level, commission-driven sales reps and a dedicated lead coordinator. This is a volume-canvassing operation, not a premium referral model. No marketing leadership, operations director, or estimating manager roles surfaced in the last 30 days, which suggests their back-office is relatively stable and the current growth push is purely front-line sales headcount.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Stay Dry publishes a self-authored "Updated 2026 Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis" list on their own domain, positioning themselves at the top of a rankings article they wrote.

This is an aggressive AEO/SEO play — building topical authority while manufacturing a citation environment where they rank themselves.

Their homepage claims BBB A+ status and GAF Master Elite certification

, both of which are schema-friendly trust signals.

They are currently promoting a 2026 GAF President's Club Award

, which generates legitimate third-party backlink potential from GAF's own domain. The self-ranking content piece at staydryroofing.com/updated-2026-top-10-roofing-companies-in-indianapolis/ is the single most important SEO asset Cameron should monitor — it directly intercepts high-intent comparison queries.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Stay Dry has positioned for AI citation with several structural advantages: GAF Master Elite status, a 2022 Inc. 5000 listing, BBB A+ accreditation, and self-published "best roofers" content. The Inc. 5000 profile at inc.com/profile/stay-dry-roofing is a high-authority backlink that AI models weight heavily when constructing lists. Their self-authored top-10 list is a direct attempt to be the source that generative engines pull from for "best roofers Indianapolis" queries. With 436 Google reviews vs. Raptor's 697, and a 4.9 vs. Raptor's 5.0, Raptor has a stronger raw signal for AI citation on volume and rating — but Stay Dry's content infrastructure (self-ranking articles, Inc. 5000, GAF landing pages) is more developed. Raptor needs publisher-side placements — local press, home services directories, Indianapolis Business Journal mentions — to compete in AI answer surfaces.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Content infrastructure is more mature — self-published comparison content, an Inc. 5000 credential, and a dedicated "Marketing Founder" title indicate sustained SEO/AEO investment. Their GAF Master Elite + President's Club combination also provides manufacturer-backed social proof Raptor should evaluate closing.

Where Raptor beats them: 697 reviews at 5.0 vs. 436 at 4.9 is a meaningful gap — both in raw trust signal and AI citation weighting. A 0.5 owner response rate on Stay Dry's Google profile is an exploitable weakness: at scale, they are not engaging their own reviewers, which signals operational indifference and gives Raptor a visible differentiation point on accountability. The Yelp abandonment (23 reviews vs. 436 on Google) and thin Facebook engagement suggest a company that built one channel and neglected the rest.

Most exploitable weakness: Their 0.5 owner response rate on Google reviews — against a brand that claims "customer first" positioning — is a direct contradiction Cameron can own publicly and in sales conversations.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The self-published comparison content play. Stay Dry's "Top 10 Roofers in Indianapolis 2026" page intercepts comparison-stage buyers and AI training data simultaneously. Cameron should build a Raptor-authored version — with tighter editorial standards and Patrick's voice — before this becomes a category default.

  2. GAF President's Club promotion.

Stay Dry is actively promoting their 2026 GAF President's Club Award on their homepage.

If Raptor qualifies or can pursue a comparable manufacturer credential (GAF, Owens Corning, CertainTeed), that certification creates backlink inventory from manufacturer domains — exactly the kind of high-DA citation that feeds both Google rankings and generative engine answers.

  1. Dedicated Sales Lead Coordinator role. The active posting for a lead coordinator signals they are building a structured lead-to-appointment pipeline layer between marketing and sales. If Raptor is routing leads directly to sales reps without a coordination function, speed-to-contact metrics are likely lagging. This is a conversion rate fix, not a volume fix, and it's relatively low-cost to implement.
