6. Generative Engine Visibility

Stay Dry's programmatic content strategy is clearly engineered for AI citation. Their self-authored "Top 10 Roofing Companies Indianapolis 2026" page directly targets the prompt structures that ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude pull from when answering queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" or "top roofing contractor near Carmel Indiana." The Inc. 5000 inclusion and IBJ Fast 25 press coverage provide the third-party editorial signals that large language models weight heavily for entity authority. Their multi-location page structure — with suburb-specific URLs hitting Fishers, Noblesville, Carmel, and Greenwood — means they are positioned to surface in geo-modified AI queries ("roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville") where single-location operators get outcompeted. Raptor currently lacks the editorial press coverage and suburb-specific structured content required to compete in AI-generated answer sets at the same volume.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Stay Dry beats Raptor: SEO infrastructure — multi-location pages, self-authored ranking content, and earned press backlinks give them broader organic and AI search surface area. Their GAF President's Club award is a tangible third-party credential that appears prominently in conversion content.

Where Raptor beats Stay Dry: Review integrity and velocity credibility. Raptor's 681 reviews at a true 5.0 versus Stay Dry's 430 at 4.9 — with only 10 reviews in the last 90 days — signals a slowing review engine on their side. Raptor's 0.0 review-platform discrepancy vs. Stay Dry's near-total Google funneling (22 Yelp reviews to 430 Google) is a trust gap a premium buyer will notice if pointed there.

Most exploitable weakness: Their homepage claim of "over 1,000 five-star reviews" is not supported by their Google review count — this is a specific, checkable misrepresentation that Raptor can implicitly counter with clean, verifiable numbers.
