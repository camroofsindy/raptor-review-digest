Competitive Intelligence Brief — Stay Dry Roofing (Indianapolis)

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed: June 9, 2025

1. Who They Are

Stay Dry Roofing LLC was founded in 2016 as a residential and commercial exterior contractor, led by Principal Owner and President Josh Lyon.

They serve Indianapolis and surrounding communities including Carmel, Greenwood, Fishers, Noblesville, Brownsburg, and Plainfield.

They hold GAF Master Elite contractor status and were recognized as a 2026 GAF President's Club Award winner — a designation GAF reserves for contractors who demonstrate excellence in performance, reliability, and service.

Their brand voice leans heavily on "family-owned," professionalism, and insurance-claim navigation, making storm-damage work a core pipeline driver.

2. Estimated Scale

RocketReach estimates Stay Dry Roofing at approximately $5.5M in annual revenue and 31 employees

(confidence: medium — RocketReach figures are modeled, not audited).

LinkedIn lists company size as 11–50 employees

(confidence: medium).

They appeared on the Inc. 5000 list in 2022

, confirming a period of verified rapid growth — though that trajectory may have plateaued given the current slow review velocity (only 8 new Google reviews in the last 90 days).

They claim over 9 Indianapolis-area locations

, which, if real, suggests a distributed sales-office model rather than a single production hub.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Stay Dry is actively posting for a Residential Sales Advisor (Westfield, IN) on Indeed — a role focused on assisting homeowners with roofing, guttering, and siding needs, with no experience required.

ZipRecruiter shows approximately 10 open Stay Dry Roofing positions ranging from $64K to $338K

, suggesting they are actively building out the sales bench. No marketing director, ops leadership, or digital roles were surfaced — their hiring posture is almost entirely front-line sales, which tells you their growth lever is still headcount-driven canvassing rather than inbound infrastructure. That is a structural vulnerability.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Stay Dry's SEO posture is aggressive and worth taking seriously.

They self-publish a "Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis" list on their own domain in which they rank themselves #1

— a classic parasite-ranking tactic that targets high-intent list queries.

Their homepage claims "over 1,000 five-star reviews" across platforms

, which inflates their perceived authority beyond the 425 Google reviews on record. The site runs FAQ schema (confirmed by structured Q&A sections covering cost, timeline, licensing, and warranty) which gives them AEO surface area in featured snippets.

Service area pages explicitly call out Monument Circle, Broad Ripple, Carmel, Fishers, and Lawrence

, indicating geo-targeted landing page buildout. Their domain (staydryroofing.com) also runs a separate commercial subdomain (staydrycommercial.com), splitting authority rather than consolidating it — a copyable countermove for Raptor.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Stay Dry Roofing surfaces in AI-generated answers for Indianapolis roofing queries primarily because of three compounding signals: their Inc. 5000 appearance, GAF President's Club / Master Elite status, and their self-authored "Top 10" list that trains AI crawlers to associate them with authoritative ranking content. Queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" and "roof replacement Carmel Indiana" in ChatGPT and Gemini tend to surface companies with structured data, local press mentions, and third-party directory density — all areas where Stay Dry has invested. Raptor currently lacks the third-party citation layer (local press, Inc.-style rankings, manufacturer award pages) that causes AI engines to treat a company as a named authority rather than a generic local result. This is the most urgent AEO gap to close.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Stay Dry beats Raptor: Total Google review volume (425 vs. 677 — Raptor leads, but Stay Dry claims 1,000+ across all platforms), Inc. 5000 brand credibility, GAF President's Club award as a marketable third-party endorsement, and self-published SEO content that captures list-query traffic. Their multi-location office strategy also gives them broader geographic map-pack footprint.

Where Raptor beats Stay Dry: Raptor holds a perfect 5.0 on Google with 677 reviews — a meaningful signal advantage in conversion. Stay Dry's 4.9 rating is strong but not perfect, and their review velocity (8 in 90 days) is stalling badly compared to what a $5M+ operation should be generating.

Indeed employee reviews note internal management friction, long hours, and a constantly changing pay structure

— the kind of culture signal that eventually shows up in customer experience and retention.

Most exploitable weakness: Their review engine has nearly stalled — 6 reviews in 30 days from a 31-person operation is a system problem, not a seasonality blip, and creates a widening gap Raptor should accelerate into.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The self-published "Top 10" list play. Stay Dry's 2026 Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis page is a direct AI-citation and SEO traffic grab. Cameron should greenlight a Raptor-authored version of this page — written to genuinely serve homeowners, but structured to capture the same list-query traffic and train AI engines to cite Raptor by name.

  2. GAF President's Club / manufacturer award positioning.

Stay Dry prominently leads with their 2026 GAF President's Club Award

as a trust signal on every key page. If Raptor holds comparable manufacturer certifications, those credentials need to be front-and-center in homepage headers and Google Business posts — not buried in an about page.

  1. Radio/broadcast for brand search lift.

A Stay Dry customer explicitly cited Q-95 radio as their discovery channel

, which means traditional broadcast is still driving brand search volume in this market. Even a targeted flight during storm season could measurably lift Raptor's branded query volume — which then compounds SEO and AI-citation authority over time.
