Competitor: Stay Dry Roofing | Updated: Thursday, April 25, 2026
Prepared by: CMO, Raptor Roofing

1. Who They Are

Stay Dry Roofing was founded in 2016 by John Olinger, with a stated focus on bringing a higher standard of professionalism to the residential roofing experience.

The LLC is led by Josh Lyon as Principal Owner & President, and is headquartered at 5060 E 62nd St, Suite 130, Indianapolis, IN 46220.

They operate satellite offices across Carmel, Greenwood, Brownsburg, Noblesville, Fishers, Plainfield, and Fort Wayne

— positioning themselves as the most geographically distributed residential brand in Central Indiana. Their differentiation hook mirrors Raptor's: professionalism over the transactional norm in roofing.

2. Estimated Scale

RocketReach estimates Stay Dry Roofing at approximately $5.5M in revenue with 31 employees.

Confidence: medium — third-party firmographic estimates for private contractors in this revenue band typically lag 12–18 months and often undercount 1099 field crews.

Inc. 5000 lists their company size at 11–50 and recognized them as a 2022 honoree

, suggesting meaningful revenue growth between 2019–2021. Real-world revenue likely runs $6M–$9M when subcontractor volume is factored in. Confidence: medium.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Stay Dry is actively posting for Residential Sales Advisors — outside sales reps assisting homeowners with roofing, guttering, and siding needs, with no prior experience required, based in Westfield, IN.

ZipRecruiter shows 10 active Stay Dry Roofing job postings in Indianapolis ranging from $64K–$338K.

The wide salary band and "no experience needed" messaging is classic insurance-churn sales hiring — they're filling canvass-and-close roles, not building a professional sales team. No marketing, ops leadership, or digital roles were detected in the last 30 days. This is a volume-sales play, not a brand-building investment.

Per a 2021 IBJ profile, Olinger has a minority business partner who runs a digital marketing firm, and SEO has been a deliberate core strategy from early on.

Their documented keyword strategy focused on dominating "roofing," "roof replacement Indianapolis," and "roofing contractors" to rank 1–2–3 in search results.

Their most aggressive current SEO move:

they've published a self-authored "Updated 2026 Top 10 Roofing Companies in Indianapolis" list on their own domain (staydryroofing.com)

— ranking themselves #1, with other competitors listed below. This is a classic parasite-content/entity-authority play designed to intercept high-intent "best roofer Indianapolis" queries.

They maintain an active roofing blog

Their homepage also prominently features GAF Master Elite status and a 2026 President's Club Award win
