Competitive Intelligence Brief: Indy Roof & Restoration

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed: June 30, 2025

1. Who They Are

Aaron Christy founded Indy Roof & Restoration in 2018.

The company is led by COO Adam Kaminski alongside Christy,

and

Operations Manager Greg Russell also holds a named leadership role per their BBB profile.

They serve residential and commercial customers across a wide Indiana footprint and lean heavily into insurance claim assistance as a core differentiator.

Their brand identity is built almost entirely on review volume and storm-restoration expertise — with 2,258 Google reviews at 4.9 stars, they are the highest-volume competitor in the Indianapolis market by a significant margin.

2. Estimated Scale

Clodura.AI lists 11–50 employees, with named roles including a Marketing Director (Nick Segar), Sales Manager (Daniel Ralston), and Branch Manager (Sam Frisby).

Confidence: medium.

Growjo estimates annual revenue at $18.8M with 72 employees for the parent "Indy Roof Company" entity,

while

Prospeo estimates annual revenue at approximately $2.4M

— a wide spread that reflects the difficulty of pinning down a private residential contractor. The Growjo figure ($18.8M) is more consistent with a 2,258-review operation running a named sales team, dedicated marketing director, and multi-line restoration services. Revenue confidence: low. The truth likely sits in the $8M–$18M range.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Their own careers page is actively recruiting Field Inspectors whose primary role is conducting roof and exterior inspections, helping homeowners navigate the insurance process, with a clear path to climb the sales team ladder.

Indeed also shows active Indy Roof & Restoration postings for Water & Mold Restoration Sales roles in Indianapolis, requiring prior experience in water damage restoration or mitigation sales.

The dual push into field sales and restoration mitigation sales signals they are expanding their revenue-per-job model beyond just roofing. No senior marketing or ops leadership postings found in the last 30 days — the named Marketing Director (Nick Segar, per Clodura) appears to be an existing seat, not a new hire.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp has placed a Consumer Alert on their Indy Roof & Restoration page to warn users about abnormal activity or attempts to mislead them

— this is a significant flag. They have only 28 Yelp reviews despite 2,258 on Google, a classic review-funneling pattern.

Their Facebook page has 4,783 likes with only 23 people currently talking about the page,

suggesting low organic Facebook engagement relative to their Google presence.

They have held BBB accreditation since July 5, 2019,

and carry certifications including Owens Corning Preferred, Malarkey Certified, IKO Certified, MuleHide Certified, and Duro-Last Certified.

However, the BBB complaints page contains detailed allegations including

post-work skylight damage, claims of being misled into gutter upgrades by citing false building code requirements, and failure to submit documents to insurers.

The 0.0% owner response rate on Google reviews — combined with the Yelp consumer alert — is a structurally exposed flank.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

ZoomInfo's tech stack data shows they run Facebook Pixel, Facebook Web Custom Audiences, WordPress, and PHP

— a standard but not sophisticated paid/organic stack. Their domain (indyroofandrestoration.com) operates a blog and location-specific service pages covering Carmel, Fishers, Greenwood, Brownsburg, Westfield, and Zionsville.

They hold Owens Corning Preferred and Malarkey Certified contractor status,

both of which generate manufacturer backlinks — a meaningful local SEO signal. They have been cited in trade press:

Aaron Christy was quoted by name in Roofing Contractor magazine's 2026 State of the Industry report discussing metal roofing growth,

which delivers a high-authority backlink. Their site has editorial, fact-checking, corrections, and accessibility policy pages listed in the footer — signals of an intentional AEO/E-E-A-T content strategy, likely coached by an agency.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Indy Roof & Restoration punches well in generative engine results. Their review volume (2,258 at 4.9 stars) is the primary citation trigger for AI tools — ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity tend to surface businesses with the highest review counts when answering "best roofers Indianapolis" or "top roofing contractor near Greenwood Indiana" style queries. Their WISH-TV segment coverage, Roofing Contractor magazine quote, manufacturer certifications, and BBB A+ accreditation all feed the structured data signals that LLMs prioritize for local service recommendations.

A WISH-TV lifestyle segment featuring both Christy and COO Kaminski

adds a local news backlink that specifically boosts generative engine trust signals. Raptor's absence of similar press coverage is an exploitable gap for AEO positioning.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Raw review volume (2,258 vs. 681) dominates AI citation, Google Local Pack ranking, and social proof at-a-glance. Their named marketing director, gamified internal sales culture (

a custom-built app running a "75 Hard"-style daily performance challenge tracking claims filed and contracts signed

), and manufacturer certification depth create a well-systematized growth operation. Where Raptor beats them: 5.0 vs. 4.9 on Google, a 0.0% owner response rate on their end (vs. Raptor's active engagement), a Yelp consumer alert signaling possible review irregularities, and active BBB complaints pointing to post-sale accountability gaps. Most exploitable weakness: Their 0.0% owner response rate combined with a Yelp consumer alert gives Raptor an opening to own the "accountability after the job" narrative that Indy Roof explicitly cannot claim right now.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Gamified sales floor with a custom tracking app.

Christy built an internal app that tracks daily activity points and revenue points (claims filed, contracts signed) on two separate leaderboards.

Cameron should evaluate whether a lightweight version of this — even a shared Google Sheet with daily rep standings — would accelerate Raptor's pipeline velocity without the overhead of custom software.

  1. Earned media as AEO fuel. Indy Roof has a WISH-TV segment and a Roofing Contractor magazine quote working as high-authority backlinks. Cameron and Patrick should pursue at least one local TV lifestyle segment and one trade publication quote in Q3 — these are the exact inputs that move the needle for generative engine citations on "best roofers Indianapolis" queries.

  2. Community initiative as a branded content loop.

Christy's annual backpack drive has scaled from 500 bags to a 2026 goal of 1,500–2,500 bags, done publicly with business partner matching and social media updates.

Raptor should identify one signature annual community initiative and build a documented content series around it — this is not charity for charity's sake, it is the exact type of locally-relevant, recurring content that earns Nextdoor mentions, local press pickups, and AI citation trust signals simultaneously.
