Competitive Intelligence Brief: Indy Roof & Restoration

Updated: May 19, 2026 | Prepared for Cameron Blakely

1. Who They Are

Aaron Christy founded Indy Roof & Restoration in 2018.

The company is led by founder Aaron Christy and COO Adam Kaminski

, with

Greg Russell serving as Operations Manager per BBB filings.

They serve residential and commercial customers across a wide Indiana service area, leaning heavily into insurance claim facilitation and real estate agent referral relationships.

Their positioning centers on storm restoration with a full-exterior scope —

roofs, gutters, siding, interior work, and anything damaged during a storm

— making them a broader restoration play than a pure roofing shop.

2. Estimated Scale

Prospeo estimates 21–50 employees

;

SignalHire pegs headcount at approximately 30.

Clodura lists 11–50 employees with named roles including a Branch Manager, Marketing Director, Sales Manager, and Project Manager.

Revenue estimates diverge significantly:

Prospeo estimates annual revenue at approximately $2.4M

, while

Clodura brackets them in the $11M–$100M range

— the latter almost certainly over-inflated for a single-market residential operator. The $2.4M figure is also likely low given 2,202 Google reviews. A realistic estimate given their volume and review velocity is $8M–$15M annually. Confidence: low — no audited financials available; all figures are third-party model estimates.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Indy Roof & Restoration is actively posting on Indeed for a Water & Mold Restoration Sales role, paying $900–$1,300 per week, requiring prior water damage restoration or mitigation sales experience.

Their careers page is actively recruiting Field Inspectors, framing the role around insurance claim navigation with a direct path into commission-based sales leadership.

A named Marketing Director (Nick Segar) and Sales Manager (Daniel Ralston) appear in their org chart via Clodura

, suggesting those functions are staffed in-house — not outsourced. No C-suite or ops leadership openings detected in the last 30 days, which indicates the leadership layer is stable. The restoration-sales hire is the signal to watch: they are deliberately expanding beyond roofing into water/mold, which broadens their average job ticket and stickiness with insurance carriers.

4. Off-Google Presence

On Yelp, they have only 28 reviews and have received a Consumer Alert from Yelp flagging abnormal activity or attempts to mislead users

— a material red flag given their 2,202 Google reviews. That 79:1 Google-to-Yelp ratio is a textbook review-funneling signal; they are clearly directing every post-job review touch to Google and ignoring or suppressing the Yelp channel.

On Facebook they have 4,777 likes with 52 people currently talking about the page

— modest engagement relative to their review volume.

Facebook lists only 86% recommendation rate across 48 reviews

, again pointing to platform concentration on Google.

BBB accredited since July 2019

with an A+ rating, but

at least one detailed BBB complaint from April 2025 alleges forged signatures on paperwork and a $1,500 cash demand with no follow-through

— this is not a resolved PR issue; it is live and indexed. No meaningful Reddit presence detected.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Their domain (indyroofandrestoration.com) is a WordPress install

using Facebook Pixel and Facebook Web Custom Audiences

, indicating active paid social retargeting.

They hold Owens Corning Preferred, Malarkey Certified, IKO Certified, MuleHide Certified, and Duro-Last Certified status

— each of which typically provides a manufacturer directory backlink, a legitimate local SEO trust signal. Their service-area coverage copy spans

Carmel, Geist, Fishers, Greenwood, Brownsburg, Westfield, and Zionsville

, meaning they have suburb-level landing pages or at minimum keyword-stuffed copy targeting Raptor's exact suburban markets. Their careers page, about page, and homepage all show content depth. The site has an Editorial Policy, Corrections Policy, and Fact-Checking Policy listed — this is deliberate E-E-A-T engineering for Google's Helpful Content standards, not boilerplate. That is a sophisticated SEO signal most local roofers do not execute. No evidence of a structured blog content calendar or local press backlinks found in this pull.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Review volume (2,202 vs. 673) provides substantially more surface area for long-tail keyword indexing in Google Maps and generates stronger AI training signal at scale. Their full-exterior/restoration scope (water, mold, siding, gutters) extends the average customer relationship and LTV well beyond a single roof. COO-level leadership and a named in-house Marketing Director indicate a more formalized operational structure.

Where Raptor beats them: Raptor's 5.0 rating is objectively cleaner and harder to attack in premium positioning; Indy Roof's 4.9 with a live Yelp Consumer Alert and an indexed BBB complaint alleging forged signatures creates a reputational vulnerability that a well-run content or paid campaign can exploit. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" message is a direct counter to the field-rep accountability complaints surfacing in Indy Roof's off-Google reviews.

Most exploitable weakness: The BBB complaint alleging forged signatures and a $1,500 cash demand — combined with Yelp's Consumer Alert — is a trust gap that Raptor can own in paid ad copy and in AEO content structured around "how to choose a trustworthy Indianapolis roofer."

Indy Roof's site includes an Editorial Policy, Fact-Checking Policy, and Corrections Policy — these are signals engineered specifically for Google's Helpful Content algorithm and for LLM citation eligibility. Cameron should have Patrick audit whether Raptor's site carries equivalent trust signals; if not, a one-hour dev task closes that gap.

Their certifications include Owens Corning Preferred, Malarkey Certified, IKO Certified, MuleHide Certified, and Duro-Last Certified

— each carries a directory listing and backlink from a high-DA manufacturer domain. Raptor should audit which certifications it holds vs. which it qualifies for and has not yet claimed; each unclaimed cert page is a free backlink and an AI citation signal sitting on the table.

  1. Earned media as AEO fuel.

Indy Roof earned a WISH-TV segment in July 2025 through COO Adam Kaminski

by tying community sponsorships (Little League, Habitat for Humanity, Beech Grove High School) to a local news pitch. Cameron should map Raptor's existing community involvement to a targeted local press outreach calendar — one WRTV or IBJ placement generates the type of authoritative backlink and brand mention that LLMs weight heavily in "best roofer" generative answers.
