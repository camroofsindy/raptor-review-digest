Competitive Intelligence Brief: Indy Roof & Restoration

Refreshed: Tuesday, May 20, 2025 | Subject: indyroofandrestoration.com

1. Who They Are

Aaron Christy founded Indy Roof & Restoration in 2018.

The company is led by founder Aaron Christy and COO Adam Kaminski

, with

Greg Russell serving as Operations Manager per BBB records.

Their core identity is storm restoration and insurance advocacy —

they lean heavily on working with insurance companies and positioning themselves as trusted by real estate agents and residential and commercial customers throughout central Indiana.

Structurally they operate as a full exterior GC:

roofs, gutters, siding, interior work, and anything else damaged during a storm.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: medium. Multiple data sources converge in the 25–50 employee range.

Clodura.AI lists them at 11–50 employees total

, while

SignalHire estimates approximately 30 employees.

Growjo estimates annual revenue of $10.9M with 47 employees

— the most aggressive estimate in the dataset, though Growjo skews high.

Prospeo pegs annual revenue closer to $2.4M

, which appears to undercount significantly given their review velocity and GC scope. A $5M–$10M ARR range is the most defensible read.

Growjo did note they grew employee count by 38% in the prior year

— a meaningful signal regardless of which revenue figure is correct.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Their

careers page actively solicits "driven individuals" emphasizing personal and professional growth

, and

commission-based Field Inspectors are a standing open role, with the primary function of roof and exterior inspections and guiding homeowners through the insurance process.

Indeed also shows an active Water & Mold Restoration Sales posting, requiring experience in water damage restoration or mitigation sales

— a signal they are pushing deeper into the interior restoration/mitigation vertical.

Clodura's org chart shows a dedicated Marketing Director (Nick Segar) and Sales Manager (Daniel Ralston)

4. Off-Google Presence

The platform gap here is real and exploitable.

Yelp has placed a Consumer Alert on their business page flagging abnormal activity or attempts to mislead consumers

— a damaging signal that Yelp's algorithm has flagged suspected review manipulation. Against 2,202 Google reviews,

Yelp shows only 28 reviews

— a 79:1 ratio that is a textbook review-funneling pattern.

Facebook shows 4,777 page likes and an 86% recommendation rate across 48 reviews

— again a fraction of their Google volume. Their Facebook page is tied to the entity "INDY ROOF CO," which is a slightly different DBA and creates potential brand confusion.

BBB-accredited since July 2019 with an A+ rating

, but the BBB complaint section contains at least one detailed narrative alleging a forged signature and cash payment irregularity from April 2025 — not yet resolved publicly at time of this brief.

Community presence includes Brownsburg Little League Softball, Beech Grove High School Marching Band, Habitat for Humanity, and the Shelbyville Animal Shelter

, all of which generate local press and trust signals without paid placement.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

A third-party 2026 Indianapolis roofing roundup (Stay Dry Roofing's own site) cites Indy Roof & Restoration as a strong regional company recognized for insurance advocacy and high review volume, calling them a top contender and honorable mention.

That kind of third-party citation on a competitor's domain is a backlink signal worth watching — they are generating citation mentions even from rivals. Their website URL structure (service area pages for Carmel, Fishers, Greenwood, Brownsburg, Westfield, Zionsville per their

careers page

) suggests a standard local SEO landing page strategy.

ZoomInfo's tech stack shows Facebook Pixel, Facebook Web Custom Audiences, WordPress, and PHP

— a fairly lean setup with no obvious schema-heavy CMS. No evidence of structured data investment or AEO-specific FAQ schema content surfaced. Their blog exists but is thin. The Red Rocket content subdomain (indyroofandrestoration.redrocketcontent.com) confirms they use Red Rocket as their SEO/content vendor, which is a local Indianapolis digital shop — not a national roofing-specialist agency.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Indy Roof & Restoration does appear in AI-generated local roundups. The Stay Dry Roofing roundup — which itself ranks in Google for Indy roofing queries —

identifies them specifically for insurance advocacy and 24/7 emergency response, and recommends them for homeowners wanting intense insurance-claim support and mitigation services.

That content will be scraped by LLMs and surface in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini responses to queries like "best storm damage roofer Indianapolis." However, their own domain does not appear to have AEO-optimized content (FAQ schema, structured Q&A, cited statistics) that would make them a primary source in generative answers. Their AI visibility is derivative — they show up because others write about them, not because their own content is structured for retrieval. That is a gap Raptor can exploit directly with properly structured AEO content targeting suburb-level queries (Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, Carmel).

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Sheer review volume — 2,202 vs. 673 — dominates the GBP proximity algorithm and creates an imposing social proof wall for undecided homeowners. Their insurance advocacy positioning and 24/7 emergency response branding gives them a clear lane during storm cycles. Community sponsorship visibility (Little League, high school band, Habitat) is a PR flywheel Raptor has not matched at that frequency.

Where Raptor beats them: 5.0 vs. 4.9 on Google — a meaningful delta at premium price points where buyers scrutinize. The Yelp Consumer Alert, the BBB complaint involving alleged forged signatures, and the 86% Facebook recommendation rate all create credible character vulnerabilities that a premium brand can exploit through contrast messaging. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is a direct counter-punch to a company that has documented sub-rep conduct issues.

Most exploitable weakness: The Yelp Consumer Alert for abnormal review activity combined with the 2025 BBB complaint about alleged forgery and cash demands gives Raptor a clean lane to run a "verified, accountable, no-surprises" campaign that doesn't need to name the competitor — the contrast speaks for itself.
