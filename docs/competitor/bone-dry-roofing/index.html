Bone Dry Roofing — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed July 21, 2025

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1989 and family-owned since day one, Bone Dry Roofing started out of a small garage in Speedway, Indiana under owner and president Gene Judd.

The company now operates 20 locations across seven states

, making it one of the largest residential roofing operations in the Midwest by revenue and footprint.

They launched Bone Dry Solar in 2022 and operate on an 81/19 residential-to-commercial split, with a production supervisor on-site for the duration of every project

— a differentiator they actively market.

2. Estimated Scale

Bone Dry lists 500 employees across its full enterprise per Roofing Contractor Magazine.

As of June 2026, LeadIQ pegs approximately 250 employees

, likely reflecting the Indianapolis-market headcount rather than the full multi-state org.

Revenue in 2023 was confirmed at over $183 million per ZoomInfo

;

RocketReach estimates $191 million for 2026.

Confidence: medium — private company, no audited filings, but the $180M–$191M range is corroborated across multiple third-party sources and the 2022 figure of $121M was self-reported to a trade publication. The Indianapolis office alone generated approximately $36M of that 2022 total. (Roofing Contractor Magazine)

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Indeed shows 78 active job listings, with 21 concentrated in Indianapolis alone

— the heaviest single-market posting concentration. Active role types include:

Commercial Repair Technician and Sales Representative (Roofing Division) posted within the last 10 days in Indianapolis and Fort Wayne per Glassdoor.

Production Manager and Commercial Sales Representative in the Sarasota market via ZipRecruiter

, pointing to continued Florida expansion.

ZoomInfo flags an active General Manager search in Cincinnati

, signaling a push to deepen that market's leadership bench.

The sheer volume of open roles in Indianapolis suggests internal churn or planned crew expansion.

At least one recent Glassdoor review describes the company as now carrying "a corporate feel" with high turnover

— worth noting as a talent retention signal.

— a stark gap versus 2,450 on Google. No visible Yelp star rating surfaced in search results for that listing, which is consistent with deliberate review-funneling straight to Google. The discrepancy is meaningful: for a company doing $183M+ in revenue with 35 years in the market, 75 Yelp reviews is operationally intentional, not accidental.

BBB-accredited since October 1995

LeadIQ's tech stack data shows Bone Dry is running HubSpot Analytics and MouseFlow

ZoomInfo reports Bone Dry is planning an office move in July 2026 that will affect approximately 10 employees at the Winton Drive headquarters.

Monitor whether their GBP address gets updated — there will be a window of citation inconsistency that can suppress their local pack ranking.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Bonedry.com operates city-level landing pages (e.g., bonedry.com/indianapolis/) with service-specific subpages — a standard but effective local SEO architecture for multi-location operators.

Their confirmed tech stack includes Google Hosted Libraries, MySQL, Google Maps embeds, and HubSpot Analytics

, suggesting a CMS-driven site with proper schema infrastructure behind it.

CMO Matt Tyner's stated focus is on "approaching new markets, growing current markets, and generating lead volume and revenue"

— the company has a named CMO with a growth mandate, not a part-time marketing coordinator.

They hold both Owens Corning Platinum Preferred and CertainTeed ShingleMaster Select status

, which generates authoritative manufacturer backlinks — a domain authority booster most regional competitors can't easily replicate. Their brand keyword dominates search in Indianapolis given review volume. The risk for Raptor: Bone Dry likely ranks for high-intent long-tail queries ("Indianapolis roof replacement cost," "Carmel Indiana roofer") simply through age, backlink mass, and content volume, not because their content is particularly sharp.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry Roofing is almost certainly cited in AI-generated answers for "best roofers Indianapolis" and related queries. The inputs that drive GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) citations — review volume, third-party directory presence, named awards, press mentions — all favor Bone Dry heavily.

They were named Roofing Contractor Magazine's 2023 Residential Roofing Contractor of the Year, a national trade award that generates exactly the type of authoritative third-party citation that ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity pull from.

At least one AI-adjacent directory (ServiceAgent.ai) already cites Bone Dry by name with a specific review count and qualitative rationale for recommendation

— that content gets ingested by crawlers feeding LLMs. For queries like "roof replacement Carmel Indiana" or "roofing company Fishers Noblesville," Bone Dry's multi-location landing page structure and review depth make them a near-certain AI citation. Raptor is not yet generating the third-party press and award coverage needed to compete in this layer.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Bone Dry beats Raptor: Raw review volume (2,450 vs. 690), multi-location SEO footprint, a named C-suite CMO with a growth budget, manufacturer-backed backlinks, and a nationally awarded brand story that AI engines trust.

Where Raptor beats Bone Dry: Raptor's 5.0 rating is a cleaner trust signal than a 4.8 at scale — premium buyers notice the difference. Owner response rate of 1.0 at Raptor vs. a near-zero visible response rate at Bone Dry signals genuine accountability. Raptor is a single-market operator with no franchise complexity, which is actually a positioning advantage with homeowners who have been burned by large crews and subcontractor handoffs.

Most exploitable weakness:

Internal Glassdoor data shows a 3.3 job security/advancement score with multiple employees noting that upper management is "disconnected" and "not held accountable"

— a company at $183M with high turnover and corporate drift is vulnerable to the accountability narrative Raptor already owns.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. City-level landing page architecture. Bone Dry runs individual landing pages per suburb (bonedry.com/indianapolis/). Raptor should build and maintain dedicated pages for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Greenwood, and Zionsville with locally specific content — not just name-dropped cities on a single page. This is the single fastest structural SEO move available.

  2. Manufacturer co-branding for backlinks.

Bone Dry is one of the few Indianapolis roofers holding both Platinum Preferred and ShingleMaster Select status, generating manufacturer-hosted backlinks from Owens Corning and CertainTeed domains.

Cameron should confirm Raptor's current certification tier and actively pursue any co-branded listing upgrades available — these are free, high-authority backlinks that also feed AI citation engines.

  1. Track the HQ move citation gap.

Bone Dry is relocating its Indianapolis headquarters in July 2026.

An address change at this scale creates NAP (Name/Address/Phone) inconsistency across directories, Yelp, BBB, and GBP for weeks or months. Raptor should monitor Bone Dry's local pack ranking during this window and push a citation-building and review-velocity sprint to capitalize on any ranking softness that emerges.
