Bone Dry Roofing — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Refreshed August 11, 2026

1. Who They Are

Bone Dry Roofing has been family owned and operated since 1989, founded by Gene Judd, who grew up the son of a roofing contractor in Illinois.

Headquartered in Indianapolis, the company expanded across Ohio, Kentucky, Missouri, and Tennessee, then further into Fort Collins, CO and Sarasota and Punta Gorda, FL, growing through both greenfield offices and acquisitions.

Bone Dry holds both Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor and CertainTeed ShingleMaster Select Contractor status

— the dual-certification angle is a deliberate premium signal they lean on heavily in market messaging.

2. Estimated Scale

As of their 2023 Residential Contractor of the Year profile, Bone Dry reported 20 locations, $121 million in 2022 annual revenue, and approximately 500 employees enterprise-wide.

Revenue crossed $183 million in 2023

, and

RocketReach estimates 2026 annual revenue at $191 million.

For the Indianapolis market specifically,

approximately $36 million — roughly 30% of total revenue — has historically come from the original Indianapolis office.

Confidence: medium on the $36M Indianapolis-local figure (2022 data); the enterprise revenue trajectory to $191M is corroborated by multiple sources.

Note: Bone Dry is planning to move its Indianapolis headquarters from 7735 Winton Dr in July 2026, impacting approximately 10 employees at that location

— worth monitoring for any service disruption signals or brand confusion window.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Active recruiting is broad and ongoing.

Bone Dry is currently seeking multiple Sales Representatives for its Indianapolis team in field-based roles.

BBB rating is A+, and they hold multiple consecutive Angie's List Super Service Awards.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Bonedry.com is a high-authority domain by home-services standards — 20+ locations generating internal links, consistent NAP citations, and a content infrastructure that a single-market operator cannot match organically. They maintain geo-specific landing pages (e.g., bonedry.com/indianapolis/) with service-level sub-pages, which is solid location-page architecture.

Their confirmed tech stack includes HubSpot Analytics and Google Business Profile

, meaning they are running structured lead attribution, not just raw traffic.

Bone Dry University — their in-house training academy launched in 2021 — has logged over 8,734 hours of live training and more than 18,103 e-learning modules completed by employees

, suggesting they can produce blog/content assets from internal expertise at scale. The AEO risk is real: their volume of location pages and long-tail content means they likely capture AI retrieval for multi-city comparison queries more readily than a single-market operator.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on current AI engine behavior for queries like "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," and "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville": Bone Dry is almost certainly cited in generative results due to three compounding factors — (1) their 2,449 Google reviews at 4.8 stars creates a strong entity signal, (2) the 2023 Roofing Contractor Magazine "Residential Contractor of the Year" award generates authoritative third-party press mentions that LLMs are trained on, and (3) their multi-location page structure makes them retrievable for suburb-specific queries.

That national award from Roofing Contractor Magazine is prominently featured on their Indianapolis location page

, which reinforces topical authority signals that AI engines weight heavily. Raptor's 5.0 rating with 697 reviews is less likely to surface in generative results today purely due to review volume gap — this is the single biggest AEO vulnerability to close.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Bone Dry beats Raptor: Review volume (2,449 vs. 697) dominates AI retrieval and creates an asymmetric map pack presence; national press coverage and a multi-location domain authority that a single-market operator cannot replicate without deliberate link-building; Nextdoor Neighborhood Favorite status with 304 Faves builds hyper-local social proof; the DOW-SkillBridge military pipeline is a differentiated recruiting angle. Where Raptor beats Bone Dry: Perfect 5.0 rating (vs. 4.8) is a meaningful premium signal;

active BBB complaints detail shingle buckling and warping issues that remain unresolved nearly 8 months after install

— exactly the accountability gap Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is built to exploit; an owner response rate of 1.0 at Bone Dry's scale feels mechanical, not personal. Most exploitable weakness: The BBB complaint record showing installation quality failures and a GM telling a customer they were being "nitpicky" is a live, documentable credibility crack — Raptor should build content around what a craftsmanship warranty actually means in practice.

and actively posts HVAC/seasonal content to cross-sell across the brand family. Raptor should be running a structured Nextdoor campaign in its core zip codes now — the nomination cycle is community-driven, and a 5.0-rated operator with strong local installs should be winning this in multiple neighborhoods before Bone Dry reclaims it.
