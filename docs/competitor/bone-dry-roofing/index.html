Competitive Intelligence Brief: Bone Dry Roofing

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely | Refreshed: May 19, 2026

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1989 by Gene Judd, Bone Dry Roofing grew out of a small garage in Speedway, Indiana into one of the largest residential roofing companies in the Midwest.

The Bone Dry family of companies now spans 20 businesses in seven states, including 12 Midwest locations plus commercial, solar, and specialty subsidiaries such as Bone Dry Commercial Roofing, Quality Roofing Services, Ravlin's Roofing, and Nu-Tec Roofing Contractors.

They are explicitly not a franchise — they are family-owned

, and their differentiation pitch leans heavily on the "Bone Dry Experience," a production supervisor on-site for every project, and a no-payment-until-satisfied policy.

2. Estimated Scale

Per Roofing Contractor Magazine's 2023 profile, Bone Dry reported 500 employees company-wide, 81% residential / 19% commercial scope, and $121M in 2022 revenue

— their last publicly cited figure.

ZoomInfo notes that the hiring of a new CFO and COO came on the heels of 2023 revenue exceeding $183 million.

RocketReach estimates 2026 annual revenue at $191 million for the full enterprise.

For the Indianapolis office specifically,

approximately $36 million, or around 30% of total revenue, came from the original Indianapolis office in 2022.

Confidence on total enterprise revenue: medium (self-reported 2022 figure is verified; 2023-26 figures are third-party estimates). Confidence on Indy-specific revenue: medium (derived from a single Roofing Contractor Magazine disclosure).

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Their Lever ATS board (jobs.lever.co/bonedry) shows active Indianapolis openings across Commercial, Customer Service, Roofing, Sales, and Solar divisions.

Specific roles visible now on Indeed and ZipRecruiter include:

Commercial Roofing Sales Estimator, Roofing Sales Representative (residential), and IEG Sales Representative (Interior, Exterior, and Gutters) for Indianapolis.

Noteworthy:

they are actively hiring for a Management in Training (MIT) Program

, which signals volume scaling, not just backfill.

They are also hiring a Claims Specialist and an Insurance Specialist specifically to manage insurance claim filings on behalf of clients

— a dedicated in-house insurance navigation team is a lead-conversion weapon worth flagging. No senior marketing hires are visible in the 30-day window;

Matt Tyner, their named CMO, appears to be focused on new market entry and lead generation strategy.

4. Off-Google Presence

On Yelp, the Winton Drive Indianapolis location shows 75 reviews

— a thin number relative to 2,422 Google reviews. This is a textbook review-funneling pattern: they actively push customers to Google and likely suppress or ignore Yelp.

They carry an A+ rating with the BBB and have won the Angie's List Super Service Award for many consecutive years.

Their Facebook presence uses UTM-tagged campaign codes indicating structured paid social tracking

, with campaign codes suggesting they have been running Facebook profile link campaigns since at least February 2024.

A community partnership with Indy Humane for microchipping events

signals active Nextdoor/neighborhood-level brand play. No significant Reddit presence identified. The Yelp gap (75 reviews vs. 2,422 Google) is exploitable for any competitor willing to actually cultivate that channel.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Bone Dry operates on WordPress (

their tech stack includes WordPress, Adobe Premiere Pro, and Module Federation

) with a mature multi-location URL structure (bonedry.com/indianapolis/, /carmel/, etc.). The Indianapolis hub page at bonedry.com/indianapolis/ targets broad head terms ("Indianapolis roofing contractor," "roof replacement Indianapolis") with above-average on-page structure.

Their 2023 Roofing Contractor of the Year award from Roofing Contractor Magazine

represents a high-authority editorial backlink that feeds domain authority — this is the kind of earned media that compounds. Their blog is active and targets informational queries (seasonal maintenance, repair vs. replace decisions). The multi-location site architecture means bonedry.com captures authority across 18+ metro landing pages simultaneously, giving them a structural SEO advantage that single-location operators cannot easily replicate. Schema markup on service pages appears present given their consistent GBP citation consistency. AEO gap: their content is service-and-feature-heavy but light on FAQ/Q&A schema that directly feeds AI answer boxes.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry Roofing is consistently cited by name in AI-generated answers for Indianapolis roofing queries. In ChatGPT and Gemini responses to prompts like "best roofers Indianapolis" and "roof replacement Indianapolis Indiana," Bone Dry appears in the top 2-3 results, typically anchored by their volume of reviews (2,422), their 35+ year operating history, their national award recognition, and their Owens Corning Platinum Preferred status — all of which are AEO trust signals that LLMs weight heavily. For suburb-specific queries like "roofing company Carmel Indiana" or "Fishers Noblesville roofer," Bone Dry's location-specific landing pages give them a structural edge that surfaces in generative results. The review velocity gap is the counter-signal available to Raptor: Raptor's 5.0 perfect average is a factual differentiator that AI engines will cite when prompted on quality, but the 2,422 vs. 673 volume differential still tips most AI answers toward Bone Dry on default "best of" queries.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Bone Dry beats Raptor: Raw review volume (2,422 vs. 673) dominates both local pack rankings and AI citation frequency; their in-house insurance claims team removes a major friction point for storm-damage leads; their national award and editorial backlinks are nearly impossible to replicate quickly; and their multi-location URL structure means they occupy more search real estate across every Indy suburb. Where Raptor beats Bone Dry: 5.0 star average versus 4.8 is a genuine, verifiable quality signal — Raptor's near-zero dissatisfied-customer record over a larger-than-expected review base is a premium positioning asset Bone Dry cannot claim. Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" message directly counters the scale-and-systems perception that naturally attaches to a 500-person multi-state operation. Most exploitable weakness: At 75 Yelp reviews against 2,422 Google reviews, Bone Dry has almost entirely abandoned Yelp, which remains a live channel for higher-income homeowner segments in Carmel and Zionsville who cross-reference platforms before buying.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. In-house insurance navigation desk.

Bone Dry employs dedicated Claims Specialists and Insurance Specialists to guide clients through the insurance filing process.

This is a lead-conversion and retention play disguised as a service feature. Cameron should evaluate whether a single trained team member in this role — or even a documented process handed to sales reps — closes the gap on storm-damage lead conversion.

  1. "Bone Dry University" as a recruitment and brand asset.

Bone Dry University, established in 2021, dedicates facilities and four full-time employees to in-house training across production, sales, leadership, and customer service.

Raptor does not need a four-person facility to use this playbook — but publicly naming and marketing an internal training standard ("we train every installer to Raptor standard before they touch a roof") converts an operational detail into a premium brand signal that resonates with homeowners and recruits alike.

  1. UTM-structured Facebook campaigns tied to location pages.

Bone Dry's Facebook profile link uses full UTM parameters including campaign code, medium, and source

, indicating a disciplined paid social attribution model. Cameron should audit whether Raptor's current Facebook/Meta spend is tagged at this granularity — without it, ROAS data for suburban geo-targets (Fishers, Noblesville, Greenwood) is blind.
