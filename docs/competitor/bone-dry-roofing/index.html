Bone Dry Roofing — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Refreshed: June 30, 2025 | Raptor Roofing Dashboard

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1989 by Gene Judd out of a garage in Speedway, Indiana, Bone Dry Roofing has grown into one of the largest residential roofing companies in the Midwest.

Headquartered in Indianapolis, they have expanded to offices in Ohio, Kentucky, Missouri, Tennessee, Colorado, and Florida.

They have grown further through M&A, with the Bone Dry Family of Companies now spanning eight states and including brands such as G&W Roofing, Roof One, Bone Dry Commercial Roofing, and Nu-Tec Roofing Contractors.

Their public-facing differentiator is scale dressed up as family ownership — not a franchise, but a roll-up operating like one.

2. Estimated Scale

Roofing Contractor Magazine reported 500 employees as of their 2023 Contractor of the Year profile.

LeadIQ pegs approximately 240 employees as of March 2026

, while

RocketReach estimates 181 employees and $191 million in revenue for the enterprise.

The Indianapolis location alone is a fraction of that headcount. Confidence: medium. On revenue,

ZoomInfo notes that the hiring of a new CFO and COO came on the heels of 2023 revenue exceeding $183 million

— the most reliable figure in the data set. Revenue confidence: medium-high (sourced from ZoomInfo and Roofing Contractor Magazine).

3. Recent Hiring Signals

The hiring posture is heavy on revenue-generating roles.

Their Lever ATS board shows active Indianapolis openings across Commercial, Customer Service, Roofing, Sales, and Solar teams.

ZipRecruiter shows active Indianapolis postings for Commercial Sales Representatives and Sales Representatives targeting the Southside/Greenwood market specifically.

Indeed shows open roles for an Insurance Claims Specialist and an Insurance Specialist — both dedicated to helping customers file storm damage claims

, which signals a deliberate push into insurance-driven replacement revenue.

An Assistant Production Manager posting on ZipRecruiter is also active, responsible for material sourcing alongside the General Manager.

No marketing or CMO-level hire detected in the last 30 days;

their current CMO, Matt Tyner, was brought in specifically to drive new market entry and lead volume.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

A November 2025 third-party audit found that Bone Dry's location pages suffer from near-identical duplicate content, creating index inefficiencies and weakening topical authority with search engines.

Their M&A activity has fragmented domain authority by redirecting location pages to separate brand websites (RCV, Whole Sun Solar, G&W), which negatively impacts SEO consolidation.

On site speed:

their mobile speed score is reported at 29 and desktop at only 65

— both well below thresholds that hurt local pack rankings.

GLSAs were not found active for Bone Dry in Indianapolis at the time of a late-2025 analysis

, a significant paid-visibility gap.

Their tech stack includes WordPress, cdnjs, and Adobe Premiere Pro

— nothing indicative of AEO-forward structured data investment. Their blog exists but is thin; no evidence of E-E-A-T-optimized long-form content targeting suburb-level queries (Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville).

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry is almost certainly cited in generic AI answers for "best roofers Indianapolis" purely on volume signals — 2,447 Google reviews and a national trade award create the citation fodder that LLMs index.

Their 2023 Roofing Contractor Magazine "Residential Contractor of the Year" designation

is exactly the type of third-party editorial signal that surfaces in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers. However, their weak suburb-level page structure means they are unlikely to appear in tightly geo-specific queries like "roofing company Fishers Indiana" or "roof replacement Zionsville." Raptor's opportunity: Bone Dry dominates broad Indy queries in AI engines; Raptor can own the high-intent suburb layer (Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, Greenwood) by producing genuinely differentiated location pages with specific project data, named neighborhoods, and permit references — content Bone Dry cannot replicate at scale without exposing their duplicate-page problem further.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where they beat Raptor: Raw review volume (2,447 vs. 681) gives them default AI citation authority and map-pack weight. Their national award, multi-state brand footprint, and dedicated insurance claims staff create institutional credibility that a single-market operator can't match on surface metrics. Their in-house Bone Dry University recruitment machine also builds a talent pipeline Raptor can't ignore as it scales.

Where Raptor beats them: 5.0 vs. 4.8 — that gap is exploitable in paid media headlines and in AI-answer contexts where rating precision matters. Raptor's 0.8 owner response rate is not listed, but Bone Dry's is also low; neither is clean here. Raptor's accountability narrative ("we don't outsource accountability") directly attacks Bone Dry's documented execution failures at scale. Bone Dry has a CMO but a broken paid media engine; Raptor has the ability to be faster and tighter in market.

Most exploitable weakness: Their GLSA gap in Indianapolis combined with a 29 mobile speed score means they are likely losing map-pack clicks to anyone running a tighter technical SEO and paid-local game right now.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Insurance Claims Staffing as a Revenue Play.

Bone Dry is actively hiring dedicated Insurance Claim Specialists to guide customers through storm damage filings.

This is a sales-cycle compression tactic — controlling the adjuster conversation keeps the job in-house. Cameron should evaluate whether a single trained project manager playing this role during storm season creates a measurable close-rate lift before committing to a full hire.

  1. GuildQuality as a Third-Party Trust Layer. Bone Dry uses GuildQuality as an independent survey platform that feeds verified reviews to lenders, realtors, and insurance agents. This is an off-Google trust signal that shows up in B2B referral channels Raptor isn't currently playing in. Worth evaluating as a referral-partner credentialing tool.

  2. "Roofing Contractor of the Year" Award as Paid and Organic Fuel.

Bone Dry has plastered the 2023 national award across every page of their site and in paid creative.

Cameron should be actively pursuing Indiana Roofing Contractors Association recognition or Owens Corning Top of the House award tiers — any third-party award creates the editorial backlink and AI-citation signal that scales Raptor's generative engine visibility without incremental ad spend.
