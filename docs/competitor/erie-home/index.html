Competitor Brief: Erie Home (Indianapolis)

Raptor Roofing Dashboard | Updated April 26, 2025

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1982, Erie Home is a national residential home improvement and re-roofing company headquartered in Toledo, Ohio.

The Indianapolis operation, located at 6925 Corporate Cir, Indianapolis, IN 46278, offers residential roofing and basement waterproofing services including metal roofing, asphalt shingle roofs, foundation repair, and air quality solutions.

Their differentiator is a proprietary stone-coated steel metal roofing product backed by a lifetime warranty — a product-led sale rather than a service-led one — combined with in-home financing, which separates them structurally from most local roofers.

2. Estimated Scale

Erie Home self-reports over $600M in annual revenue with 100+ locations nationwide.

The most recently verifiable number:

in 2022 the company generated $448 million in revenue from 58 offices.

For Indianapolis specifically, the local branch is one of multiple Indiana outposts.

Indeed estimates salary data from 10,700 employees nationally.

Local Indianapolis headcount is not broken out publicly.

Revenue estimate (national): $500M–$650M | Confidence: medium (based on 2022 audited figure + growth trajectory)
Indianapolis branch employee count: 30–70 estimated | Confidence: low (no branch-level data available; derived from office-model norms)

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Erie Home is actively hiring Field Marketing team members in Indianapolis, offering a base hourly wage plus weekly bonuses and uncapped commissions — notably not commission-only, which is a canvassing model shift worth flagging.

On ZipRecruiter, they are also posting immediately for roofing subcontractors seeking consistent, year-round work

— confirming they install via sub-crews, not W-2 installers.

On Indeed, they are recruiting sales representatives nationally with pre-qualified leads and paid training.

No marketing, ops leadership, or digital roles visible for the Indianapolis market specifically in the last 30 days. The canvassing hire is the most strategically relevant signal — it means they are pushing door-to-door lead gen harder than digital.

4. Off-Google Presence

The cross-platform review picture is severely fractured. Google: 274 reviews at 4.6 stars (per Cameron's data).

BBB: 4.69 stars across 3,600-plus reviews nationally.

Yelp tells a completely different story: 1.8 stars nationally from 41+ reviews, with a broader aggregate showing 1.2 stars from 386 reviews across 93 locations.

BBB complaint records show 377 complaints filed in the last three years, with service and repair problems leading at 146 complaints, followed by 47 customer service issues.

Customers have also flagged aggressive marketing practices, with one reporting 116 unsolicited calls within 16 days.

Their BBB Indianapolis profile shows an A+ accreditation — a credibility badge they prominently merchandise — but the Yelp gap is a textbook review-funneling pattern. Facebook Ad Library activity not confirmed in available results; their paid social presence could not be independently verified in this pull.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Erie Home operates a geo-specific location page structure (e.g., eriehome.com/locations/roofing/indianapolis-in/) which is standard franchise-style local SEO. The page is templated and thin on hyper-local content.

Through their Gridiron Capital partnership, Erie unified operations under a single CRM and expanded digital marketing capabilities across all 91 locations.

Their marketing transformation included expanding agency partnerships and managed marketing channels, with new digital initiatives generating an additional $4 million in monthly revenue within 90 days of the Gridiron partnership.

That is a well-funded, agency-managed playbook — not a locally-operated SEO strategy. Their domain authority nationally is strong (eriehome.com is an established domain), but their Indianapolis-specific content depth is shallow. No visible blog content targeting Indy suburb queries (Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville). Schema markup is likely present sitewide but not locally customized. This is a gap Raptor can exploit with deeper suburb-specific content.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on the data available in this pull, Erie Home has the brand name recognition and national review volume that increases their probability of appearing in AI-generated results for broad queries like "best roofers Indianapolis." However, their Yelp signal is actively negative and AI engines increasingly weight multi-platform review consistency. Their BBB-versus-Yelp divergence — 4.69 vs. 1.2 stars — creates a conflicting data signal that weakens their generative engine footprint for trust-based queries. For suburb-specific queries ("roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers"), their templated, thin location pages provide weak AEO signals. Raptor's consistent 5.0 rating across a higher-review-velocity platform, combined with locally-specific content, positions it to outrank Erie in AI answers for suburb-level queries where brand recall is lower and trust signals matter more.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Erie beats Raptor: National brand name, A+ BBB badge they actively advertise, a proprietary metal roofing product with a compelling "last roof you'll ever need" warranty narrative, and a structured in-home sales process with pre-set leads that drives higher close rates.

They are also operating at a scale eclipsing $450M in annual revenue across 40 states and 75+ cities

— meaning their paid media budget dwarfs anything a local operator can match on share-of-voice alone.

Where Raptor beats Erie: 5.0 stars at 664 reviews versus Erie's 4.6 at 274 — a meaningful trust gap. Raptor's reviews are accelerating (9 in 90 days vs. Erie's 9 in 90 days puts them at parity in velocity despite Raptor's lower base). Erie's Yelp profile is a reputation liability a savvy buyer will find. Most exploitable weakness:

Erie's sales presentations run 3–4 hours and the sales team is persistently aggressive on follow-up

— Raptor can win on the "no-pressure, local accountability" message every time a customer has already had an Erie in-home visit.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The "last roof you'll ever need" framing. Erie's metal roofing lifetime warranty is a powerful objection-killer in the sales room. Raptor should develop an equally simple, outcome-focused warranty statement — not product specs, but a one-sentence customer promise — and put it on every paid ad and landing page.

  2. Pre-set lead / in-home demo model.

Erie's sales reps run pre-set, high-interest appointments with a 2-hour in-home presentation, warm leads provided daily, and no cold calling.

Cameron should evaluate whether a structured canvassing or direct-mail-triggered in-home consultation model could accelerate close rates in Raptor's target zip codes without the aggressive follow-up tactics Erie is getting burned on.

  1. Multi-platform review distribution. Erie is actively feeding GuildQuality (5,000+ reviews) and Trustpilot alongside Google and BBB to create a broad review footprint that feeds AI training data. Raptor is concentrated on Google. Seeding GuildQuality and Trustpilot with even 50–75 reviews each would widen the AEO surface area significantly and is a low-cost move Cameron can execute in Q2.
