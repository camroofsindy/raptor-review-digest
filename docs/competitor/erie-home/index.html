Erie Home — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Refreshed: June 30, 2026

1. Who They Are

Erie Home is a Toledo, Ohio-based residential home improvement and re-roofing company founded in 1982. Founder Pat Trompeter has been elevated to chairman of the board, with Jenilee Common serving as CEO and Randy Hamilton as President.

The Indianapolis operation serves homeowners across the metro and nearby communities including Carmel, Greenwood, and Lawrence, installing metal and asphalt roofing systems.

Their core differentiator is a vertically integrated direct-to-consumer model: in-home sales, proprietary product, and company-managed installation under one roof — a national chain playing local.

2. Estimated Scale

Erie Home operates more than 90 locations across 37 states

, making it one of the largest direct-to-consumer roofers in the country.

In 2022 the company generated $448 million in revenue from 58 offices.

With accelerated expansion since, current revenue is likely north of $500M.

Within 90 days of Gridiron Capital's digital marketing investment, new digital initiatives alone generated an additional $4 million in monthly revenue.

Confidence: medium on current revenue (last confirmed figure is 2022).

PitchBook lists Erie Construction Mid-West at 1,042 total employees

— confidence: medium. Notably,

Erie Construction Mid-West was acquired by Leaf Home on September 8, 2025

, meaning the Indianapolis branch now operates under a $1B+ private equity-backed home services conglomerate. This changes the competitive calculus significantly.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

ZipRecruiter currently shows active Indiana postings offering up to a $2,000 sign-on bonus for subcontract roofing crew leads and a "$3,000 Quick Start" incentive for sales representatives.

Glassdoor lists 192 open Erie Home positions nationally.

No marketing, ops leadership, or digital-specific roles were identified for the Indianapolis location specifically in the last 30 days — all visible hiring is sales and install-side. That said, the volume of sales rep postings signals a pipeline-building push, not a maintenance mode.

4. Off-Google Presence

Erie Home holds an A+ BBB Accredited Business rating with a local address at 6925 Corporate Circle, Indianapolis, IN 46278.

Across all BBB locations, they carry a 4.69 average across 3,600-plus reviews, and 4.7 stars across 5,000+ GuildQuality reviews.

However, many Yelp reviews express dissatisfaction with heavy sales tactics and higher-than-average industry quotes

— a sharp discrepancy from their curated platform scores.

On Trustpilot they claim 5 stars across 3,568 reviews.

The gap between Trustpilot/BBB scores and Yelp is the most obvious review-funneling signal in their profile. Facebook ad activity has been confirmed by multiple customer reviews noting they were acquired via Facebook campaign.

At least two separate reviewed customers noted they were acquired through a Facebook campaign

, confirming paid social is a consistent lead-gen channel.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Erie Home operates a dedicated Indianapolis landing page at eriehome.com/locations/roofing/indianapolis-in/ with UTM-parameterized GBP links, indicating structured tracking across channels.

Erie leveraged Gridiron Capital's resources to expand agency partnerships and managed marketing channels

, suggesting a sophisticated national agency stack — not an in-house team.

Their location pages explicitly target Carmel, Greenwood, Lawrence, Noblesville, Avon, Zionsville, and Westfield by name

, covering every major Indy suburb with dedicated keyword copy. Their domain (eriehome.com) carries substantial national authority from backlinks across Roofing Contractor magazine, Qualified Remodeler, and PE firm press releases. That national DA advantage lets even thin local pages rank competitively. No evidence of a local blog content strategy specific to Indianapolis.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Erie Home's national brand scale and the volume of third-party editorial coverage (Roofing Contractor, This Old House, Qualified Remodeler, Trustpilot, GuildQuality) give them strong AI citation signals.

Erie Home, also known as Erie Roofing or Erie Metal Roofing, is described in editorial sources as a home remodeling company "best known for its metal roofs, which are made from stone-coated steel stamped to resemble traditional roofing materials."

That kind of clean definitional language is exactly what LLMs pull when constructing answers to "best roofer in Indianapolis"-type queries. Erie almost certainly surfaces in generative results for metro-wide queries. However, their zero reviews in the last 90 days on Google — and no visible owner response activity — means their local engagement signal for hyper-specific suburb queries (Fishers, Zionsville, Noblesville) is weaker. Raptor's active, high-velocity review profile is a meaningful differentiator in AI answer generation for those suburb-level queries.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Erie beats Raptor: National domain authority, PE-backed marketing budget, multi-channel paid media (Facebook, likely programmatic), a lifetime warranty product story, and a metal roofing niche that commands premium ticket prices with less price shopping. They also have brand name recognition built over 40+ years.

Where Raptor beats Erie: 681 reviews at a perfect 5.0 vs. Erie's 279 Google reviews at 4.6 — Raptor wins the trust signal decisively. Zero Erie reviews in 90 days and no owner response rate is a dead account signal that screams "corporate phone bank, not a local owner who answers." Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is a direct counterpunch to Erie's known Yelp complaints about high-pressure sales tactics,

including reported door-to-door cold prospecting and sales appointments that ran three and a half hours.

Most exploitable weakness:

Multiple reviewers flag Erie's financing partners as "predatory at best" — even customers with 800 credit scores say this.

That is a ready-made conversion talking point for Raptor in any sales situation where Erie has already been in the home.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Metal roofing as a premium upsell category.

Erie's metal roofs are marketed as steel systems rated for hail and winds up to 120 mph.

They use this spec to justify premium pricing. Cameron should evaluate whether adding a metal roofing tier — or at minimum a structured "why we chose not to sell metal" content piece — neutralizes Erie's product story in head-to-head situations.

  1. GuildQuality integration for review diversification.

Erie carries 4.7 stars across 5,000+ GuildQuality reviews

, a platform that AI crawlers and editorial sites like This Old House reference heavily. Raptor's review presence is concentrated on Google. Seeding GuildQuality or Trustpilot would add citation surface area in generative engine results at minimal cost.

  1. Track the Leaf Home acquisition fallout.

Erie was acquired by Leaf Home in September 2025.

Post-acquisition integration almost always creates 6–18 months of service disruption, sales team turnover, and customer confusion. Cameron should watch for upticks in negative Erie reviews locally and be ready to run a targeted "locally owned, always accountable" campaign the moment that churn shows up in their review velocity — which, right now, has already flatlined to zero.
