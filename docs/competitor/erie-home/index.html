1. Who They Are

Watergate Roofing has provided residential and commercial roofing services to the Indianapolis area since 2013, and is a woman-owned and operated business.

The company is led by Millie Hindes, who founded it in 2013 after being in the roofing industry since 2007.

They serve Indianapolis and the greater area — including Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Westfield, Greenwood, Zionsville, and Bloomington — extending as far north as Kokomo and as far east as New Castle.

Their primary differentiator is the woman-owned identity and a boutique, quality-control-first positioning built on long-term crew relationships and a 5-year workmanship warranty on all replacements.
