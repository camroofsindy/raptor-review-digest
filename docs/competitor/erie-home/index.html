Erie Home — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Refreshed: June 9, 2025 | Cameron Blakely Dashboard

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1976, Erie Home is a large residential roofing company serving the Central U.S. and other regions nationwide.

Eclipsing $450M in annual revenue, Erie Home operates across 40 states in over 75 U.S. cities and positions itself as the nation's largest volume roofing replacement contractor, now expanding into basement waterproofing.

Erie Home has also entered into an agreement to be acquired by Leaf Home,

which signals a major ownership and integration transition currently underway — a strategic wildcard that creates internal distraction at exactly the moment Raptor can press.

2. Estimated Scale

Confidence: High — Multiple third-party sources corroborate.

As of October 2025, Erie Home has approximately 1,100 employees across two continents.

The company eclipses $450M in annual revenue

nationally, though the Indianapolis branch's local revenue is not broken out publicly.

Erie Home has been part of the Gridiron Capital portfolio since 2021

, and the pending Leaf Home acquisition layered on top means two ownership transitions in under five years — a meaningful instability signal for a company selling on trust and longevity. The Indianapolis office at 6925 Corporate Circle is one branch in a system of

more than 100 locations across 38 states.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Active postings on LinkedIn include Roofing Installation Crews Wanted (multiple metro markets), Sales Representative (Cedar Park, TX), and Top Sales Opportunity (Round Rock, TX and Springfield, MO).

No Indianapolis-specific hiring postings are visible in the current 30-day window. The national pattern is almost entirely sales reps and installation crew — no marketing, ops leadership, or technology hires surfacing for the Indianapolis market.

Erie Home has grown revenue nearly 10x over the past five years and opened 21 offices in the past 12 months.

The lack of local marketing hiring suggests Indianapolis is being managed by a national marketing machine, not a locally-invested team.

4. Off-Google Presence

The cross-platform review picture is the single most damaging piece of Erie Home's public profile. Their Indianapolis Google number is 277 reviews at 4.6 stars — decent volume, middling rating.

Their Yelp rating sits at 2.1 stars from 43 reviews

— a nearly 2.5-star gap versus Google, which is a textbook review-funneling fingerprint: satisfied customers get pushed to Google, dissatisfied customers self-select to Yelp.

On the BBB, Erie Home holds an A+ rating and is accredited,

but

has approximately 388 customer complaints on file over the past three years.

On Trustpilot, Erie Home has 3,514 national reviews

— a large volume that props up cross-platform optics but dilutes local accountability. Facebook Ad Library: Erie is a national brand running national creative; localized Indianapolis ad spend is not clearly broken out.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Erie Home's domain (eriehome.com) carries the authority of a national brand with 100+ location pages, which gives it structural advantages in any local roofing SERP.

Their Indianapolis location page targets Carmel, Greenwood, and Lawrence

by name — textbook multi-suburb landing page SEO. The site runs UTM-tagged GBP links (utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=roofing&utm_campaign=Indianapolis), confirming a structured tracking layer. However, their content strategy is thin on Indy-specific editorial — blog posts and guides appear national, not localized. No evidence of local press backlinks or Indy-specific media placements.

Their tech stack includes Adobe Analytics, Statcounter, Google Maps, and Adobe Creative Suite

— a reasonably modern but not especially differentiated setup. Schema markup is likely present given the scale of their SEO infrastructure, but AEO-specific FAQ schema on local pages is not confirmed. The 2.1 Yelp rating and 388 BBB complaints are crawlable signals that could weigh against AI citation quality scoring.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Erie Home carries national brand recognition that makes it likely to appear in AI-generated responses to broad queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" — primarily because it is cited by Yahoo Local, This Old House, and other high-DA editorial sources that LLMs pull from heavily.

Yahoo Local lists Erie Home as their top recommendation for Indianapolis homeowners preferring a larger provider, calling it "a large residential roofing company" operating since 1976.

Yahoo Local also names Erie Home the top national recommendation for Carmel and the surrounding area.

That editorial positioning feeds directly into AI answer sourcing. However, the subcontractor use disclosure and Yelp rating create material that could disqualify Erie in AI answers that weight negative signals —

Erie Home uses subcontractors for its roofing crews, and a representative was unable to confirm whether formal background checks are conducted.

Raptor's "we don't outsource accountability" positioning is a direct counter-narrative that, if embedded in structured content, can outcompete Erie in AI answers for trust-sensitive queries.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Erie beats Raptor: National brand recognition, domain authority, multi-suburb landing page coverage, and editorial citations from high-DA national publishers (Yahoo Local, This Old House) that feed AI answers. Their warranty story —

among the strongest of any national roofer reviewed, with a 30-year limited warranty on asphalt and full 10-year labor coverage

— is a credible close in the premium segment.

Where Raptor beats Erie: 677 reviews vs. 277 at the Indianapolis GBP. A 5.0 vs. 4.6 star rating. Zero subcontractor ambiguity. No 2.1 Yelp score dragging brand trust. And Raptor's review velocity is a living signal of operational health — Erie's 8 reviews in 30 days vs. zero in the last 7 days signals a stalled review capture system.

Most exploitable weakness:

Erie Home uses subcontractors for its roofing crews

— and cannot confirm background checks. That is a gift for Raptor's messaging in AI answer optimization, Nextdoor targeting, and any sales conversation where a prospect has done research.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Suburb-specific landing page architecture.

Erie's Indianapolis page explicitly names Carmel, Greenwood, and Lawrence

to capture suburb-level search traffic. Raptor should have distinct, editorially rich location pages for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Greenwood — not just city mentions in a footer. Each page needs localized FAQ schema to compete in both traditional SERPs and AI answers.

  1. Warranty narrative as a close and a content asset.

Erie's stone-coated metal roofing systems are backed by a 50-year limited lifetime transferable warranty covering defects, rust, discoloration, hail, and high winds.

Their warranty is a structured content play — every warranty term is an answerable question in AI search. Raptor should publish a direct warranty comparison page that makes the accountability gap (Erie: subcontractors, unverified background checks; Raptor: in-house accountability) impossible to miss.

  1. Monitor the Leaf Home acquisition integration closely.

Jenilee Common, Erie's CEO, will assume CEO of the combined Erie/Leaf Home organization following acquisition close.

Acquisitions of this type routinely produce service disruptions, brand confusion, and employee churn. Cameron should set a Google Alert for "Erie Home Indianapolis" and "Leaf Home Indianapolis" — any service complaint spike or brand transition noise in the local market is a media and paid opportunity to capture displaced Erie customers in real time.
