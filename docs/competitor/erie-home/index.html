Erie Home — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated August 2026

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1982, Ohio-based Erie Home is a home improvement company providing end-to-end residential re-roofing services and consumer product solutions.

Founder Pat Trompeter now serves as chairman of the board; Jenilee Common was appointed CEO in March 2023, bringing a background as VP/GM of Roofing Components at Owens Corning.

Leaf Home — parent company of LeafFilter — acquired Erie Home in September 2025, folding it into what it calls the largest provider of home services in North America.

The Indianapolis office at 5757 W. 73rd St. covers the metro and surrounding suburbs including

Noblesville, Avon, Zionsville, and Westfield.

2. Estimated Scale

Erie Home operates more than 90 locations across 37 states

, with the Indianapolis branch one of its legacy Midwest offices.

Erie Home opened its 100th location in Seattle, Washington, in July 2025.

Revenue: confidence high —

in 2022 the company generated $448 million in revenue from 58 offices;

LeadIQ now lists revenue in the $500M–$1B range.

Employee count: confidence medium —

Growjo estimates approximately 1,779 employees nationally;

ZoomInfo data is unreliable for this entity. The Indy branch likely runs 20–40 direct staff based on standard Erie office sizing; installation crews are subcontracted.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

No fresh job postings specific to the Indianapolis Erie Home office were surfaced in the last 30 days. However,

Erie Home at the corporate level has stated it is actively expanding into foundational waterproofing

— watch for Indy-market waterproofing sales rep or estimator postings, which would signal a direct product-line expansion into Raptor's adjacent category.

Erie Home grew employee count by approximately 5% last year

, so incremental local hiring is ongoing but not advertised loudly. No marketing, ops, or sales leadership hires flagged for the Indy branch at this time.

4. Off-Google Presence

Erie Home's cross-platform review posture is its clearest structural weakness relative to Raptor. Their Birdeye profile shows

394 reviews at 4.6 stars

— not a red flag on its own, but it mirrors the 4.6 Google number without a meaningful positive skew. The provided data confirms zero new Google reviews in the last 90 days, which is a significant signal of review momentum stagnation, especially for a brand claiming national leadership.

Erie Home claims an A+ BBB rating

, which is legitimate and worth noting.

Erie Home uses Facebook Pixel and Facebook Web Custom Audiences

, confirming active paid social retargeting. One reviewer noted directly that

the lending institutions used by Erie are "predatory at best,"

a recurring friction point in their reviews that does not appear to be addressed systemically. No owner response activity on Google in at least 90 days — confirmed by provided data. This is a gap Raptor actively exploits.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Erie Home carries significant domain authority advantages as a national brand — eriehome.com has national backlink depth that a local operator cannot easily replicate. Their Indianapolis location page at eriehome.com/locations/roofing/indianapolis-in/ is a templated city landing page, not a locally authored content piece. It ranks on primary queries like "roofing Indianapolis" on the strength of root domain authority, not local relevance signals.

Erie Home's tech stack includes WordPress and WooCommerce

, which is workable but not the cleanest schema implementation for local service area pages. They have no visible Indy-specific blog content, no local press backlinks, and no neighborhood-level schema. This is exploitable: a locally authored blog targeting "Fishers roof replacement," "Carmel hail damage," and "Zionsville roofing contractor" with genuine location-specific schema will outmaneuver templated city pages over a 6–12 month horizon.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Erie Home surfaces in AI-generated answers for broad national queries ("best roofing companies in the US") due to its Inc. 5000 recognition,

including a No. 3,156 ranking in the 2025 Inc. 5000 with 127% three-year growth.

For hyper-local queries — "best roofer in Carmel Indiana," "roof replacement Fishers," "roofing company Noblesville Zionsville" — Erie Home's AI citation rate drops sharply because their content footprint is thin at the suburb level. ChatGPT and Gemini tend to pull local roofing answers from sources with dense local review text, named staff, and specific neighborhood mentions — all areas where a brand like Raptor, with 697 named Google reviews and suburb-specific content, has a structural edge. Erie Home is not meaningfully cited in Indiana-suburb-level AI answers. This is Raptor's lane to own.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Erie beats Raptor: National brand halo, Leaf Home acquisition gives institutional marketing budget, A+ BBB rating used actively in advertising, and a lifetime warranty narrative that resonates with older, risk-averse buyers. Their direct-to-consumer sales model —

which generated an additional $4 million in monthly revenue within 90 days of Gridiron's digital marketing investment

— is professionally systematized.

Where Raptor beats Erie: 697 reviews at 5.0 vs. Erie's 280 at 4.6 is not a close comparison on trust signals. Zero Erie Google reviews in 90 days while Raptor is actively accumulating is a compounding divergence. Erie's financing complaints (

"predatory" lenders

) create a genuine trust gap that Raptor's transparent pricing message directly exploits. The most exploitable weakness: Erie's Indianapolis operation shows zero owner engagement on reviews and a stalled review pipeline — a national brand running on autopilot locally, wide open for Raptor to own the "local accountability" narrative.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Lifetime warranty as a sales anchor.

Erie explicitly sells the "life of the roof" warranty as its primary value prop.

Cameron should audit whether Raptor's warranty language is front-loaded in ads, landing pages, and sales scripts with the same prominence — or if it's buried in the close.

  1. Facebook retargeting infrastructure. Erie runs Facebook Pixel and Custom Audiences on every page visit — meaning anyone who lands on their site gets followed with paid ads. If Raptor is not running pixel-based retargeting on its own site visitors, every homeowner Erie re-captures with paid social is a lead Raptor generated and gave away.

  2. Inc. 5000 and third-party ranking citations as trust assets.

Erie actively promotes its "No. 10 Remodeler in USA" designation from Qualified Remodeler

in local marketing. Cameron should identify what credentialing or ranking assets Raptor holds — GAF Master Elite, BBB accreditation, any local "Best Of" recognitions — and build those into the homepage header and ad creative, not just the About page.
