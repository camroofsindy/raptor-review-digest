Erie Home — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard | Updated: April 26, 2025

1. Who They Are

Erie Home was founded in 1976

and operates under the legal entity Erie Construction Mid-West, LLC, with

headquarters in Toledo, Ohio.

The company provides residential roofing and basement waterproofing services, including metal roofing, asphalt shingle roofs, foundation repair, and air quality solutions.

Their differentiation hook is proprietary stone-coated steel metal roofing —

also known as Erie Roofing, Erie Metal Roofing, or Erie Basement Solutions, the company is best known for its metal roofs made from stone-coated steel stamped to resemble traditional roofing materials.

Indianapolis is one of their standard market rollout locations, not a flagship — it's a branch office within a national machine.

2. Estimated Scale

Erie Home eclipses $450M in annual revenue and operates across 40 states in over 75 U.S. cities.

A more recent Indeed job posting self-reports even higher:

"with over 100 locations and $600M+ in annual revenue."

Confidence: medium — these are self-reported figures from recruiting materials and LinkedIn, not audited financials.

LinkedIn lists 725 employees company-wide,

though Apollo.io estimates closer to 840. The Indianapolis branch is a fraction of that headcount — likely 15–30 local W2 staff with subcontracted installation crews. Confidence: low on the local headcount specifically.

ZoomInfo also flagged that Erie Home has entered into an agreement to be acquired by Leaf Home,

which is the most significant strategic development to watch — a post-acquisition integration could disrupt the Indy branch's local responsiveness for 12–18 months.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Current active LinkedIn postings include Sales Representatives (Cedar Park, TX; Round Rock, TX; Springfield, MO) and Roofing Installation Crews Wanted across multiple markets including Carmel, NY, Yonkers, NY, Champaign, IL, and Omaha, NE.

No Indianapolis-specific open roles surfaced on LinkedIn or Indeed in the last 30 days. The dominant hiring pattern across all platforms —

hiring immediately for roofing subcontractors, seeking reliable roofing crews for consistent year-round work

— confirms their install model relies on 1099 subcontractor crews rather than W2 craftsmen. This is worth noting: they are not building local ownership into their labor model. No marketing, ops leadership, or branch manager postings flagged for Indianapolis.

4. Off-Google Presence

The review platform spread on Erie Home is one of the most dramatic discrepancies in the Indy market. On Google (Indianapolis branch): 274 reviews at 4.6 stars — serviceable but unexceptional. The national picture tells a more useful story for Raptor's positioning.

Erie Home's Yelp rating sits at just 1.8 stars from 41 reviews

— a massive gap from their Google number that signals aggressive review funneling to Google and GBP while Yelp collects the bleed-through from dissatisfied buyers.

BBB records show 377 total complaints filed in the last three years nationally, with 136 of those in the past 12 months alone.

The Indianapolis BBB listing shows an A+ rating

, but that grade is largely complaint-response rate, not satisfaction.

Sales presentations run 3–4 hours, and the sales team follows up persistently — one customer documented 116 calls in 16 days.

Facebook Ad Library shows Erie running national creative, not geo-targeted Indy-specific ads, suggesting their paid social is centralized and not locally optimized.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Erie Home's domain (eriehome.com) carries strong national authority built through a network of city-specific landing pages.

The Indianapolis location page targets communities like Carmel, Greenwood, and Lawrence by name

— standard location page SEO play, but not deeply differentiated content. They rank for broad terms like "metal roofing Indianapolis" on the strength of domain authority, not local content depth. Their blog is thin and largely product-focused rather than educational or trust-building. No evidence of active local press backlinks (Indianapolis Star, IBJ, Indy Business) or community partnerships that would build local relevance signals. Their GBP UTM structure (utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=roofing&utm_campaign=Indianapolis) shows they are tracking GBP traffic deliberately — they understand the channel. Schema markup is present on core service pages. The AEO gap is real: their content does not answer specific Indy homeowner questions (hail season timelines, permit requirements, HOA restrictions in Hamilton County), which is exactly where a local operator can out-rank them for long-tail and AI-cited queries.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Erie Home surfaces in AI search results for national queries like "best metal roofing companies" primarily because of their scale, review volume across BBB/GuildQuality/Trustpilot, and editorial coverage from This Old House and Today's Homeowner. For hyper-local queries — "best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville" — Erie Home is not reliably cited by ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini as a named local recommendation. Their local AEO footprint is weak because they have no locally-authored content, no Indy-specific community signals, and no GBP Q&A optimization. This is a direct opening for Raptor: structured, locally-specific FAQ content optimized for AI retrieval will leapfrog Erie's generic national landing pages in generative results.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Erie beats Raptor: National brand recognition, financing infrastructure ($0-down offers, third-party lenders), and a proprietary metal roofing product line that can anchor a premium upsell conversation. Their 274 Google reviews also provide surface-level social proof for homeowners who stop at the star count. Where Raptor beats Erie: Review quality and velocity tell the real story — Raptor's 664 reviews at a 5.0 vs. Erie's 274 at 4.6, with Erie pulling in only 9 reviews in 90 days, signals a stalled or declining customer feedback engine. Erie's 0.97 owner response rate sounds disciplined, but

their BBB responses are cookie-cutter, often starting with "Erie Home apologizes for any inconvenience this may have caused"

— the same boilerplate pattern bleeds into Google. Raptor's authentic, specific responses are a meaningful differentiator. Most exploitable weakness: Erie's Yelp rating of 1.8 stars paired with documented high-pressure 3-4 hour sales tactics is an open door — Raptor should be showing up in "Erie Home complaints Indianapolis" and "Erie Home vs local roofer" search and AI results with trust-based content.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The financing lead magnet.

Erie is actively promoting "$0 down on a new roof" as a 2025 campaign hook.

Whether or not Raptor matches the offer, Cameron should test financing-forward ad creative in Meta and Google — Erie's national data confirms it converts at the top of funnel in this price-sensitive market.

  1. The multi-brand SEO structure. Erie runs parallel landing pages under aliases (Erie Metal Roofs, Erie Construction, Erie Basement Solutions) to capture different search intents. Raptor doesn't need multiple brands, but building separate, deeply-answered service pages for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Greenwood, and Zionsville — each with locally-specific content rather than swapped city names — would replicate the traffic capture without the brand fragmentation risk.

  2. Monitor the Leaf Home acquisition closely.

Jenilee Common is slated to become CEO of the combined Erie/Leaf Home entity post-close.

Acquisitions of this size create 6–18 months of internal chaos — rep turnover, territory confusion, billing disputes. The window to capture Erie's unhappy Indianapolis customers with targeted search and retargeting campaigns will open predictably when the deal closes. Set a Google Alert for "Erie Home Leaf Home acquisition" and have a conquest campaign ready to deploy.
