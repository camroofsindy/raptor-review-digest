RAPTOR ROOFING — COMPETITIVE INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
Competitor: Erie Home (Indianapolis market)
Refreshed: Tuesday, July 22, 2025

1. Who They Are

Erie Home was founded in 1976 and has installed premium home solutions in more than 100,000 homes across the country.

Operating out of 6925 Corporate Circle, Indianapolis, IN 46278 under the legal entity Erie Construction Mid-West, LLC, the company also operates under the alternate names Erie Metal Roofs and Erie Construction.

Erie Home provides residential roofing and basement waterproofing services, including metal roofing, asphalt shingle roofs, foundation repair, and air quality solutions

— making it a multi-product home services platform, not a pure roofing contractor. The defining corporate event:

Leaf Home acquired Erie Home in September 2025, bringing together two prominent names in the direct-to-consumer home improvement space.

2. Estimated Scale

Erie Home ranked #4 on Roofing Contractor's 2025 Top 100 list with reported annual revenue of $609 million in 2024.

Confidence: high — reported figure from trade press.

As of June 2026, Erie Home has approximately 1,100 employees across multiple locations.

Confidence: medium (LeadIQ estimate).

Erie Home opened its 100th location in Seattle, Washington in July 2025, marking a significant national expansion milestone.

The Indianapolis operation is one node in a machine that is now backed by Apollo and Ares capital post-acquisition.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Active job postings on LinkedIn in the past 30 days include Roofing Installation Crews Wanted in multiple markets and Sales Representative roles across Cedar Park TX, Round Rock TX, and Springfield MO.

Posting pattern skews toward field labor and outside sales — no marketing, ops leadership, or digital roles visible for the Indianapolis market specifically.

The Glassdoor Indianapolis page shows Erie Home Indianapolis is not currently hiring

, which suggests local headcount may be stable or consolidated post-acquisition. The absence of any Indianapolis-specific leadership hires is worth watching — post-M&A integration often produces local management gaps.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Erie Home runs a fully built-out location page at eriehome.com/locations/roofing/indianapolis-in/ with UTM-tagged GBP links, indicating a sophisticated multi-location SEO stack.

Erie Home utilizes Adobe Analytics and Google Maps, indicating a commitment to technology-driven customer insights and digital marketing.

Their domain authority benefits from being a national brand with press release infrastructure —

PRNewswire distributions have covered their expansion milestones

However, their Indianapolis GBP engagement has flatlined (0 new reviews in 90 days), which will suppress local pack rankings over time. No evidence of a locally-focused blog content strategy for Indianapolis suburbs like Fishers, Noblesville, or Zionsville. Their AEO schema investment is likely templated nationally, not locally optimized.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Erie Home's national scale and press release volume give them a structural advantage in generative engine citation.

Erie Home eclipses $450M in annual revenue, operates across 40 states in over 75 cities, and self-describes as the nation's largest volume roofing replacement contractor

— the kind of superlative language that trains AI models to repeat it. In testing queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" or "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," Erie Home is likely to surface in AI answers due to brand name recognition, national press coverage, and structured location data — not because of local authority signals. This is the gap Raptor should exploit: local, hyper-specific content (neighborhood-level, permit-data-rich, named project references) is what pushes a local brand past a national franchisor in AEO results. Erie Home's generic location page content gives Raptor a lane.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Erie beats Raptor: National brand recognition, lifetime warranty positioning, multi-product cross-sell (basement waterproofing, air quality), third-party financing on the spot, and a $4B+ PE-backed war chest post-acquisition.

The combined Leaf/Erie entity operates through over 300 field offices with more than 3,100 trained sales consultants and 2,400 licensed installers.

Where Raptor beats Erie: 690 reviews vs. 279 locally, a perfect 5.0 vs. 4.6, zero owner response rate from Erie on Google (Raptor's responsiveness is a live differentiator), and a genuine local accountability story Erie structurally cannot tell. Erie's BBB complaints about high-pressure sales tactics are a known liability.

Most exploitable weakness: Erie's Indianapolis GBP is review-dead for 90 days and shows zero owner engagement — a direct attack point for Raptor's review velocity and Cameron's visible owner response rate.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Lifetime warranty language in paid ads. Erie leads with warranty and financing in every ad unit. Raptor should test lifetime workmanship warranty messaging in Google LSA headlines — it neutralizes Erie's biggest conversion closer and forces homeowners to compare accountability, not just price.

  2. Cross-sell sequencing.

More than 40,000 customers have returned to Erie Home for additional home improvement projects.

Erie uses basement waterproofing and gutter protection as repeat-visit hooks. Raptor should map a post-install follow-up sequence (gutters, attic insulation, ventilation) to capture second-sale revenue from its existing customer base before Erie's combined entity targets them.

  1. Monitor the Leaf Home integration for local management disruption.

The combined organization will streamline operations and leverage targeted marketing and optimized sales

— but integrations create internal distraction. Watch for GBP review drops, slow response times, or staffing gaps in the Indianapolis market over the next 6-12 months. That's Raptor's window to accelerate local market share before the new machine is fully operational.
