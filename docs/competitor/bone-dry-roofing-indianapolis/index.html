Bone Dry Roofing (Indianapolis) — Competitive Intelligence Brief

Refreshed: Tuesday, July 1, 2025 | Analyst: Raptor CMO Dashboard

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1989 by Gene Judd, Bone Dry Roofing is headquartered in Indianapolis and has grown into one of the largest residential roofing companies in the Midwest.

Beginning in Indiana, the company expanded to Ohio, Kentucky, Missouri, Tennessee, Colorado, and Florida.

The Bone Dry Family of Companies now spans eight states and includes 15 Bone Dry Roofing locations plus acquired brands such as G&W Roofing, Ravlin's Roofing, RCV Roofing, Roof One, Bone Dry Commercial Roofing, Quality Roofing Services, and Nu-Tec Roofing Contractors.

Their distinguishing play is scale-through-acquisition dressed up as local family ownership — they explicitly market "not a franchise" while rolling up competitors across the region.

2. Estimated Scale

As of their 2023 Roofing Contractor Magazine profile, Bone Dry operates 20 locations with 500 total employees, generating $121 million in 2022 annual revenue.

A more recent ZoomInfo signal indicates 2023 revenue exceeded $183 million

, representing rapid post-award growth.

As of March 2026, LeadIQ estimates approximately 240 employees

— likely reflecting the Indianapolis HQ entity only, not the full family of companies. Confidence: medium. The $183M figure is enterprise-wide; the Indianapolis location's share is approximately $36–40M based on its historical ~30% contribution to total revenue per the 2022 data.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Active hiring across Indianapolis on Indeed, ZipRecruiter, and their own Lever ATS board (jobs.lever.co/bonedry).

Open Indianapolis roles include Commercial, Customer Service, Roofing, Sales, and Solar positions, with a dedicated "Indy Southside" sales opening.

They are also actively recruiting a Claims Specialist and an Insurance Specialist to facilitate insurance claim filing for roof damage customers

— a direct signal they are building out a storm/insurance channel within Indy.

A posted Assistant Production Manager role is responsible for materials sourcing and procurement working closely with the General Manager

, suggesting ops scaling, not just sales.

ZoomInfo flags that Bone Dry Roofing is planning an office move in July 2026, impacting approximately 10 employees at the existing 7735 Winton Dr location

— worth monitoring for any disruption to their Indy operations mid-summer.

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp:

The main Indianapolis listing at 7735 Winton Dr shows 75 reviews

— a stark gap versus 961 Google reviews. This is a textbook review-funneling pattern: they systematically route customers to Google and neglect Yelp entirely. A 4.9 Google vs. thin Yelp presence is a vulnerability for any homeowner doing cross-platform due diligence.

BBB:

The BBB profile lists Gene Judd as President, with a named COO, CFO, CIO, and Chief Marketing Officer (Matt Tyner).

Recent BBB complaints include documented execution gaps:

at least one complaint cites receiving an invoice for ventilation work that was not actually performed, with shingles lifting shortly after repair.

These are quality-control bleed-through complaints at scale — predictable for a multi-location operator.

GuildQuality:

They actively use GuildQuality for third-party verified survey reviews

, a credibility signal most smaller Indy roofers don't deploy. This is a source aggregation tactic worth noting.

Facebook Ads:

Their Facebook ads are described as very basic, primarily focused on branding rather than aggressive lead generation campaigns.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Bone Dry holds real domain authority at bonedry.com but has documented structural problems.

Their site creates indexation inefficiencies because many location pages are nearly identical, only changing specific city details, which weakens topical authority and confuses search engines about which page should rank.

Acquired company redirects push location authority off-site to separate brand domains, which negatively impacts their SEO.

Technical performance is poor: mobile page speed scores 29 and desktop scores only 65

— both well below the threshold for strong Core Web Vitals performance.

Their Google Local Services Ads presence could not be confirmed in several key locations including Indianapolis, a gap that likely costs significant lead volume.

For Raptor: their bloated multi-location site architecture is an SEO liability that a leaner, single-market operator can beat on relevance signals for city-level queries (Carmel, Fishers, Greenwood, Zionsville, Noblesville).

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry is almost certainly cited in AI-generated answers for broad Midwest roofing queries given the volume of press coverage (2023 Roofing Contractor Magazine Residential Contractor of the Year), their domain age, and their multi-location backlink footprint.

They actively surface the "2023 Residential Roofing Contractor of the Year" award from Roofing Contractor Magazine across their web properties

, which is the type of third-party citation that generative engines weight heavily. For queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" or "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," Bone Dry is likely appearing in ChatGPT and Gemini outputs given their review volume (961 on this location alone,

5,000+ reviews across all locations at a 4.8 average

) and the press authority. Raptor has no equivalent national press citation — this is the gap Cameron should close by pursuing a Roofing Contractor, Remodeling, or local IBJ feature tied to a specific story hook (5.0 star rating, zero-outsource model).

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Bone Dry beats Raptor: Sheer review volume (961 vs. 681), national brand recognition, the 2023 industry award citation that fuels AI engine mentions, multi-channel service offering (solar, commercial, masonry, insulation), and a dedicated in-house training apparatus (Bone Dry University) that supports aggressive hiring at scale.

Where Raptor beats Bone Dry: Perfect 5.0 star rating vs. 4.9, tighter accountability (no outsourced subcontractors to dilute quality), no BBB execution complaints on record, and a more credible "boutique accountability" premium positioning that a 500-person roll-up genuinely cannot replicate.

Even Bone Dry's own marketing acknowledges the production supervisor model as a differentiator

— but at their scale, it gets inconsistent, as GuildQuality complaints confirm.

Most exploitable weakness: Their Indianapolis Google review velocity is stalling — only 9 reviews in the last 90 days on the flagship location — while Raptor should be hammering velocity to close the raw count gap and own the "most reviewed 5-star roofer in Indy" narrative.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. GuildQuality third-party survey integration.

Bone Dry uses GuildQuality's verified survey process

to build a review corpus that sits outside Google's ecosystem and feeds credibility to homeowners who cross-check. Cameron should evaluate GuildQuality or a comparable verified-review platform (Bark, Trusted Pros) to create a second-screen trust signal — especially useful for AEO since AI engines pull from multiple review sources.

  1. Insurance/claims specialist staffing as a sales channel.

They are actively hiring dedicated Claims and Insurance Specialists to assist clients with filing insurance claims for roof damage.

This is a lead-conversion function, not an admin function — it removes friction for storm-damage homeowners who stall on paperwork. Raptor should assess whether a part-time or contracted insurance advocate role could be embedded in the sales process to compete on this.

  1. Discount program for service trades and military.

Bone Dry runs explicit discounts for military members, veterans, senior citizens, first responders, teachers, and healthcare professionals.

This is community-affinity marketing that generates word-of-mouth in exactly the neighborhood Facebook groups and Nextdoor threads where roofing decisions get made. A structured Raptor discount program in the same categories — publicized through Nextdoor ads and hyperlocal Facebook Groups — would generate organic referral volume at low cost.
