Competitive Intelligence Brief: Bone Dry Roofing

Raptor Roofing | Cameron Blakely Dashboard
Refreshed: June 9, 2026

1. Who They Are

Founded in 1989, Bone Dry Roofing has grown into one of the largest residential roofing companies in the Midwest.

The company is family-owned and operated, with owner Gene Judd growing up the son of a roofing contractor in Illinois.

The Bone Dry Family of Companies now spans eight states, including 15 Bone Dry Roofing locations nationwide plus acquired brands such as G&W Roofing, Ravlin's Roofing, RCV Roofing and Siding, Roof One, Bone Dry Commercial Roofing, Quality Roofing Services, and Nu-Tec Roofing Contractors.

Their core differentiator is institutional scale marketed as local family ownership — a direct positioning tension that Raptor can exploit.

2. Estimated Scale

As of their 2023 Roofing Contractor of the Year profile, Bone Dry reported 500 employees company-wide across 20 locations.

More recent data from LeadIQ puts the number lower:

as of March 2026, Bone Dry Roofing has approximately 240 employees.

Confidence: medium — headcount aggregators diverge significantly, likely because subsidiary brands are tracked separately. On revenue:

the hiring of a new CFO and COO at Bone Dry came on the heels of 2023 revenue of over $183 million

, and

RocketReach pegs 2026 annual revenue at $191 million

for the full enterprise. Confidence: medium — these are third-party estimates; the Indianapolis branch alone generated roughly $36M in 2022 per Roofing Contractor Magazine. For competitive purposes, treat the Indy operation as a ~$40–50M annual revenue unit.

Active openings across job boards paint a clear picture of where they're pushing:

Current Indianapolis-area postings on Glassdoor include a Commercial Production Manager, a Sales Representative (Roofing Division), a Construction Assistant to the masonry production manager, and a Field Project Foreman for the Commercial Division.

They are actively recruiting for their Management in Training (MIT) Program

, a pipeline play that signals long-term expansion intent rather than backfill hiring.

ZipRecruiter listings include a Claims Specialist and an Insurance Specialist

— both dedicated to insurance claim facilitation, confirming storm/insurance work remains a core revenue driver.

Per ZoomInfo, their CMO Matt Tyner is focused on "strategic marketing efforts to approach new markets, grow current markets, and generate lead volume and revenue"

— meaning their marketing function is well-staffed and actively resourced at the VP level.

No ops leadership or GM hires flagged specifically for Indianapolis in the last 30 days, but the volume of sales rep postings suggests southside Indy (US-31 corridor) is a priority territory.

4. Off-Google Presence

On Yelp, the primary Indianapolis location at 7735 Winton Dr carries only 75 reviews

— a stark contrast to 954 Google reviews. That is a 92% review-funneling ratio to Google, meaning Yelp is essentially an unmanaged surface for them. Cross-platform rating discrepancy is notable:

a third-party reputation aggregator shows Bone Dry at 4.7 stars across 2,858 combined reviews

— 0.2 stars below their Google profile, suggesting a non-trivial volume of lower ratings exists outside their primary funnel.

On BBB:

they maintain an A+ BBB rating and have won Angie's List Super Service Awards for many consecutive years.

However,

the BBB complaint log shows at least one instance where the business responded to a dispute but failed to make a good faith effort to resolve it, and others where the consumer did not accept the resolution.

A specific December 2025 complaint details billing for work not yet performed.

On Facebook Ad Library: no current paid ad data was surfaced, but their tech stack per LeadIQ includes Facebook — confirming active use. Their partnership with F.C. Tucker Home Services (talktotucker.com) is a notable referral channel that Raptor should map.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Bone Dry operates bonedry.com, a WordPress-based site with dedicated location pages (e.g., bonedry.com/indianapolis/) structured with city-specific service content, reviews, and team pages — a textbook multi-location SEO architecture.

Their tech stack includes Adobe Premiere Pro, WordPress, cdnjs, Module Federation, and Webpack

, indicating investment in both content production and front-end performance. They maintain active blog/article content on the Indianapolis subdirectory and use schema-eligible review content fed directly from their own review page at bonedry.com/indianapolis/reviews/.

Their domain authority is bolstered by:

being named Roofing Contractor Magazine's 2023 Residential Roofing Contractor of the Year

— that national trade press mention generates high-DA backlinks. Their partnership with F.C. Tucker and Indy Humane also produce local backlinks. The combination of brand volume, press coverage, and location-page depth gives them strong domain authority on "Indianapolis roofing company" head terms. Raptor's counter-move is long-tail AEO content targeting specific Indy suburbs where Bone Dry's pages are thin.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry is well-positioned to appear in AI-generated answers for "best roofers Indianapolis" and "roof replacement Indianapolis" for one primary reason: they are the highest-reviewed contractor by volume in the market (954 Google reviews), they have national trade press citations, and their bonedry.com domain has broad topical authority across roofing + Indianapolis keyword clusters. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all weight entity prominence and third-party citation density — Bone Dry checks both boxes.

However, their 4.9-star Google rating trails Raptor's perfect 5.0, and their cross-platform rating bleed (4.7 on aggregators) is a signal that AEO engines may deprioritize them if review sentiment analysis is part of the ranking logic. Raptor's opportunity: create suburb-specific AEO content pages (Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Greenwood, Zionsville) with structured FAQ schema that directly answers "who is the best roofer in [suburb]" — content Bone Dry has not built at the depth needed to dominate those long-tail AI queries.

7. Strategic Read vs Raptor

Where Bone Dry beats Raptor: Raw review volume (954 vs. 677), national brand recognition and trade press credibility, multi-service breadth (solar, commercial, masonry, insulation), an in-house training academy (Bone Dry University) that fuels recruitment, and a dedicated insurance claims team that positions them as a one-stop storm-damage shop.

Where Raptor beats Bone Dry: Perfect 5.0 star rating (vs. 4.9), tighter accountability narrative ("we don't outsource accountability") that directly counters the complexity of a 20-location company with acquired subsidiaries, and a cleaner BBB complaint profile. Bone Dry's BBB complaint log has documented cases of billing for incomplete work — that is a material trust gap.

Most exploitable weakness: At their scale and with their acquisition model, Bone Dry is structurally unable to deliver the single-point-of-accountability experience Raptor can — lean into that asymmetry in every sales conversation and ad creative.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. The F.C. Tucker partnership model.

Bone Dry Roofing is a listed member of F.C. Tucker's Home Services network, which helps homeowners find vendors before, during, and after a move.

That is a systematic referral pipeline tied to the highest-volume real estate brokerage in Central Indiana. Cameron should pursue an equivalent listing or exclusive partnership with a competing brokerage (Carpenter, RE/MAX, Keller Williams Indy).

  1. The on-site production supervisor model.

Bone Dry deploys a production supervisor who remains on-site for the duration of every project to ensure safety, protection, and serve as the main point of contact for homeowners.

If Raptor is not already doing this systematically and documenting it in review requests, start — and call it out explicitly in marketing as proof of the accountability claim.

  1. Military/First Responder discount visibility.

Bone Dry actively promotes discounts for military members, military veterans, senior citizens, first responders, teachers, and healthcare professionals.

This is a low-cost, high-goodwill positioning tactic that plays especially well in south Indy markets and drives word-of-mouth in tight-knit professional communities. Raptor should match or exceed it and make it a homepage-visible offer.
