Competitive Intelligence Brief — Bone Dry Roofing (Loc 2 / Winton Dr, Indianapolis)

Refreshed: May 19, 2026 | Dashboard Cycle: Tuesday

1. Who They Are

Bone Dry Roofing was founded by Gene Judd, who launched it as a true family affair out of a garage in Speedway, Indiana.

Founded in 1989, the company is headquartered in Indianapolis and has expanded to include offices across Ohio, Kentucky, Missouri, and Tennessee

— making Loc 2 (7735 Winton Dr, Indianapolis, IN 46268) one node in a sprawling multi-state platform.

The Bone Dry Family of Companies now spans 15 roofing locations plus acquired brands including G&W Roofing, Ravlin's Roofing, Roof One, and Nu-Tec Roofing Contractors.

Their distinction in the Indy market is sheer brand ubiquity and institutional infrastructure — they are not a single-location operator but a regional roll-up competing locally.

2. Estimated Scale

Per Roofing Contractor Magazine's 2023 profile, Bone Dry operates 20 locations nationally with 500 employees and an 81%/19% residential-to-commercial split.

That is the enterprise figure. For Loc 2 specifically, the Indianapolis office historically accounted for a meaningful slice:

in 2022, Bone Dry reported $121.6M in total revenue, with roughly $36 million — about 30% — attributable to its original Indianapolis operation.

Enterprise-wide revenue has accelerated since:

ZoomInfo notes the hiring of a new CFO and COO followed financial performance in 2023 that included revenue of over $183 million.

Confidence on enterprise revenue: high (confirmed by trade press and ZoomInfo). Confidence on Loc 2 isolated revenue: medium — the $36M Indy figure is the most recent public data point and is now 3 years old.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Bone Dry's ATS (hosted at jobs.lever.co/bonedry) shows active Indianapolis-specific openings across Commercial, Customer Service, Roofing, Sales, and Solar divisions right now. Drilling into what is tactically significant:

Active listings target sales representatives specifically for the Southside of Indianapolis and/or the Greenwood area

— they are pushing into Raptor's southern-suburb turf.

An Insurance Claims Specialist and Insurance Specialist are being recruited to work with clients filing claims for roof damage

— a dedicated insurance-lane sales function, which is a volume play.

A Management in Training (MIT) program is actively recruiting

, indicating pipeline-building for future production and sales leadership.

Bone Dry Solar is recruiting a Solar Systems Electrician for Central Indiana

, signaling continued vertical expansion into solar as an upsell channel.

No VP/Director-level marketing or ops leadership postings were visible at the Indianapolis location specifically in the last 30 days. Marketing appears centrally managed under CMO Matt Tyner (listed on BBB).

4. Off-Google Presence

Yelp:

The Loc 2 listing (7304 US 31 S) has only 1 Yelp review, though Yelp notes roofing was searched 1,684 times within 25 miles of this business last month.

The Winton Dr location (yelp.com/biz/bone-dry-roofing-indianapolis-6) shows 75 reviews with heavy photo presence but a thin overall rating signal. This is a significant discrepancy: 953 Google reviews vs. ~75 Yelp reviews across both Indy locations. Classic review-funneling — their review asks are clearly directed to Google only.

BBB:

The BBB complaint profile covers Bone Dry's headquarters and all corporate-owned locations collectively.

Recent complaint narratives are damaging:

one customer reports being sent to collections after "no follow-up calls, emails, or written notices" for nearly two years following a disputed insurance claim.

Another 2025 complaint details being billed for ventilation work that was not performed, with shingles lifting shortly after repair.

These are workmanship and billing integrity issues — exactly the "accountability gap" Raptor can exploit.

Angi/HomeAdvisor: Present and indexed (angi.com), but no differentiated review strategy visible there.

Facebook Ad Library: Not auditable in this cycle without a direct pull, but their tech stack per LeadIQ includes Facebook as an active platform —

Bone Dry uses Adobe Premiere Pro, WordPress, and Facebook among its confirmed technology products

, indicating active paid social production.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Bone Dry operates bonedry.com — a domain with 35+ years of age, multiple state-level location pages, and a blog content library. The Indianapolis location page (bonedry.com/indianapolis/) is a well-structured hub with service-specific subpages, careers, and an active job board — all positive domain authority signals.

Their tech stack includes WordPress, cdnjs, Module Federation, and Webpack

, suggesting a modern CMS build. They hold dual manufacturer certifications that generate backlink equity:

Bone Dry is both an Owens Corning Platinum Preferred Contractor and a CertainTeed ShingleMaster Select Contractor

— both manufacturer directories link back to bonedry.com.

They were named the 2023 Residential Roofing Contractor of the Year by Roofing Contractor Magazine

, generating high-authority editorial backlinks that Raptor does not yet match. Their AEO posture is stronger than most Indy competitors by virtue of enterprise content volume, named leadership (Gene Judd is a well-indexed public figure), and multi-state press coverage. Schema markup on location pages is likely given their tech investment, though not verified this cycle.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Bone Dry Roofing is almost certainly cited in AI-generated answers for Indianapolis roofing queries. Here is the reasoning: they hold the 2023 national Contractor of the Year award from a major trade publication, operate a high-domain-authority site with 35 years of indexed history, and have BBB, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Yelp, and ZoomInfo profiles all consistently reinforcing the same entity.

Industry reporting confirms that "with AI searches, BBB listings and reviews left on that platform seem to be gaining traction as an authoritative source"

— and Bone Dry's BBB profile is one of the most data-rich contractor profiles in Indiana. For queries like "best roofers Indianapolis" or "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," LLMs pulling from Roofing Contractor Magazine, BBB, and Angi will surface Bone Dry by name. Raptor's current generative visibility is constrained by a thinner third-party citation footprint — no national trade press mentions, no manufacturer directory landing page, and a review corpus concentrated on Google only.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where Bone Dry beats Raptor: Raw review volume (953 vs. 673), enterprise brand recognition, national press citations, dual manufacturer certifications, Bone Dry University as a recruitment and retention moat, and an active insurance-claim specialist function that turns adjusters into a sales channel. Their domain authority and AEO footprint are materially stronger.

Where Raptor beats Bone Dry: A perfect 5.0 star rating vs. Bone Dry's 4.9 — and at 673 reviews, that gap is statistically meaningful to consumers. Raptor's accountability positioning ("we don't outsource accountability") is a direct counter to documented BBB complaints about billing disputes and ghosted follow-ups. Bone Dry is a machine with scale friction; Raptor is a focused operator.

Most exploitable weakness: BBB complaint patterns reveal a recurring billing integrity and follow-through failure — Cameron should be seeding that contrast in sales conversations and review-ask scripts without naming Bone Dry directly.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Bone Dry University as a PR and recruiting asset.

Bone Dry established an in-house training program in 2021 with four full-time employees dedicated to education and development.

This generates press, aids retention, and becomes a brand story. Raptor should build and brand its own internal training standard — even a formalized onboarding program with a name creates a marketable differentiator and feeds AEO citations.

  1. Insurance specialist as a dedicated sales lane.

Bone Dry is actively hiring both a Claims Specialist and an Insurance Specialist to facilitate claim filing for damaged roofs.

This is a volume-multiplier play: every storm event becomes a structured lead pipeline, not just organic inbound. Cameron should evaluate whether a dedicated claims-assist function (even a single person) would accelerate post-storm close rates.

  1. Manufacturer certification as a content and backlink engine.

Being one of the few Indianapolis companies to hold both Platinum Preferred and ShingleMaster Select status generates landing pages on manufacturer directories that link to bonedry.com.

If Raptor is not maximizing OC Platinum or CertainTeed certification pages with Indy-specific geo-content, that is a quick-win AEO gap to close before the next storm season.
