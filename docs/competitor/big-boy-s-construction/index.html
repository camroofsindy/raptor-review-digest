President Mark Andrew leads the company

, and

Their primary differentiator is a heavy insurance-claim/storm-restoration focus wrapped in a GAF Master Elite badge —

a certification held by only the top 2% of roofing companies in the U.S.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

Their site (bigboysconstructioninc.com) runs on WordPress with reCAPTCHA and PHP,

hosted on Nexcess — a managed WP host. The site has dedicated service pages (residential, commercial, repair, metal roofing) and a full areas-we-serve page targeting every major Indy suburb by name, which is solid on-page local SEO hygiene.

They also market drone inspection technology for roof assessments,

which gives them a differentiating content hook. However, the site was established in April 2018 per metadata, the blog appears thin, and no local press backlinks were surfaced. Domain authority is likely in the 20–30 range — competitive but not dominant. Their areas-we-serve page is their strongest SEO asset; it is structured to rank for suburb + "roofing" queries across all nine target counties.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

Based on available research, Big Boy's Construction does not appear to surface prominently in AI-generated responses for key Indianapolis roofing queries ("best roofers Indianapolis," "roof replacement Carmel Indiana," "roofing company Fishers Noblesville Zionsville"). Their low LinkedIn presence (149 followers), thin blog output, and absence of local press citations mean the data sources that LLMs weight — structured citations, news coverage, high-authority directory links — are underdeveloped. Their GAF Master Elite designation and GuildQuality profile are their two best assets for AI citation, but they are not amplifying either in content strategy.
