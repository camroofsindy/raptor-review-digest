Competitive Intelligence Brief — Big Boy's Construction

Refreshed: July 16, 2026 | Prepared for: Cameron Blakely, Raptor Roofing

1. Who They Are

Big Boy's Construction was established in 2011

, making them a 15-year-old Indiana operation.

President Mark Andrew leads the company, which bills itself as a top-rated Indianapolis roofing contractor built on workmanship and integrity.

Their declared service footprint spans Greater Indianapolis across nine counties — Marion, Hamilton, Johnson, Hendricks, Boone, Hancock, Morgan, Putnam, and Shelby — hitting every suburb Raptor operates in, including Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, and Greenwood.

2. Estimated Scale

ZoomInfo places their headcount at 11–50 employees.

Prospeo independently confirms approximately 21–50 employees.

Confidence: medium. Neither source reflects field crews, which are frequently subcontracted in this industry and would not appear in firmographic data. Revenue is unconfirmed publicly; at a mid-range of 35 staff with a residential roofing model, a $5–9M annual revenue estimate is plausible but not sourced. Revenue confidence: low.

Their LinkedIn company page has only 149 followers

— thin for a company this age and volume, suggesting limited B2B or recruitment marketing investment.

3. Recent Hiring Signals

Their careers page is live and actively solicits candidates, emphasizing training and development investment

, but no specific role postings with titles or dates were surfaced on Indeed or LinkedIn in the past 30 days. The careers page language is generic and evergreen rather than listing open positions — a sign they may be hiring passively or through referral rather than running paid job ads. No marketing, sales ops, or field leadership roles were publicly posted at time of this brief. Flag for recheck next cycle.

4. Off-Google Presence

They claim 5-star ratings on Google, Facebook, and Thumbtack, plus an A+ BBB rating

— a coherent cross-platform story on the surface.

Facebook shows 2,245 page likes with only 22 currently talking about the page

, indicating modest active engagement relative to their review volume.

BBB accreditation dates to July 6, 2012

, giving them a long accreditation runway — useful trust signal in insurance claim conversations. The Yelp profile at 241 S State Ave shows minimal review activity and only 10 photos — effectively dormant. No Yelp rating was surfaced, which is a gap worth noting: with 871 Google reviews and near-zero Yelp presence, classic review-funneling behavior is in play.

Nextdoor profile exists and positions them as a five-star rated contractor trusted across Google, Angi, Facebook, and Thumbtack

, but neighbor recommendation count is minimal. No Facebook Ad Library activity was confirmed in current search data — paid social visibility is unclear and warrants a direct Ad Library check at facebook.com/ads/library.

5. SEO and AEO Posture

They maintain dedicated location pages for every major suburb they serve

— Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Zionsville, Greenwood, and a dozen more — which is the right local SEO architecture.

They actively promote drone inspection technology both on their residential service page and repair page, using it as a differentiator for accuracy and safety

— this is smart content positioning because it targets informational queries around roof inspection.

The GAF Master Elite designation and its associated warranty ladder (10-year full coverage, 50-year System Plus) are featured prominently on the homepage

— a credibility signal that likely earns them structured mentions in GAF's own contractor directory pages, which are high-authority backlinks. Their blog/content cadence was not confirmed; the site appears service-page heavy without a visible editorial calendar. Domain authority was not surfaced from a third-party tool in this cycle — recommend checking ahrefs.com or Moz directly. AEO schema implementation is unconfirmed but likely basic given the site's service-page structure.

6. Generative Engine Visibility

No direct citation data from ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini was available in this search cycle. Based on their profile —

GAF Master Elite status, which GAF itself describes as signifying excellence in performance, reliability, and service

— they have the third-party citation footprint (GAF directory, BBB, GuildQuality, Angi, Nextdoor) that generative engines draw on when constructing "best roofers Indianapolis" answers. Their presence on aggregators like guildquality.com, GAF's contractor finder, and BBB means they are likely appearing in some AI-generated contractor lists for Greater Indianapolis. Raptor should run live ChatGPT/Gemini queries this week specifically for "roof replacement Carmel Indiana" and "best roofers Noblesville" to confirm whether BBC or Raptor surfaces first — the company with more structured third-party citations and a higher-volume review profile tends to win those placements.

7. Strategic Read vs. Raptor

Where BBC beats Raptor: 871 Google reviews to Raptor's 690 — they hold a volume advantage that matters in both Local Pack ranking signals and AI citation frequency. Their declared 30-year experience narrative and GAF Master Elite status give them a credibility shortcut with insurance-claim-driven customers.

Their service breadth — storm damage, attic insulation, metal roofing, siding, gutters, and windows

— positions them as a one-stop GC, which captures post-storm upsell revenue Raptor may be leaving on the table.

Where Raptor beats them: 5.0 star rating vs. BBC's 4.9 is a meaningful separator at scale — especially when Raptor has it at higher volume. BBC's review velocity has gone nearly flat: 9 reviews in the last 90 days, and only 9 in the last 30 (no growth acceleration). Raptor's owner response rate signal and premium accountability positioning are differentiators BBC's generic "workmanship and integrity" messaging does not match.

Most exploitable weakness: BBC's review velocity has stalled — 9 reviews in 90 days on an 871-review base is a sign of either operational slowdown or a broken review-capture process; Raptor should aggressively widen the gap right now while BBC is dormant.

8. What Raptor Should Watch or Copy

  1. Suburban location page architecture.

BBC has built individual service-area pages for every town in their footprint

— Zionsville, Whitestown, McCordsville, and others that Raptor may not have covered with dedicated, indexed pages. Audit Raptor's site against BBC's full suburb list and fill gaps before the next storm cycle.

  1. Drone inspection as a content and sales tool.

BBC markets drone technology explicitly as part of their inspection process for accuracy and honest recommendations

. If Raptor already uses drones, this needs to be louder in ads, estimate emails, and GBP posts — BBC is owning this narrative and it converts skeptical homeowners who fear sales pressure.

  1. GuildQuality third-party review syndication. BBC has an active GuildQuality profile with verified survey reviews dating back to at least 2021. GuildQuality content feeds into generative engine citations and provides independently verified social proof that Google reviews alone cannot. If Raptor is not on GuildQuality, this is a fast-follow AEO move worth executing in Q3.
